Brightsund
==========

An island city builder and trading game, made with the Engine (Rust and wgpu).

Start
  Unzip the folder anywhere, open it and double-click Brightsund.exe. Keep the `assets`
  folder next to it. The game opens borderless fullscreen on the title screen; F11 switches
  to a window. The start takes a little while as the islands, the sea and the woods are
  made. A console window shows the log and closes with the game. Needs a graphics card
  with Vulkan or DirectX 12.

The aim
  Your company starts with one barque, the Fair Venture, lying off Haverholm with planks,
  fish and clothes in her hold. Every other island is hidden under fog. Grow Haverholm from a
  landing into a town, raise its people from Fisherfolk to Labourers to Burghers, settle the
  other islands by ship, and bring home the goods of the far lands: Sarrowmere, the Amber
  Reach and Frostholm.

First steps
  1. Place a Harbour on Haverholm's coast (build bar, Harbour and air). The Fair Venture
     sails in, lands her planks and raises it.
  2. Build a Market square, a Woodcutter by the trees and a Sawmill for planks, and a
     Fishery on the shore. Join everything to the harbour with roads.
  3. Lay out houses round the market. Fisherfolk move in and pay rent; a house's card says
     what it is waiting for.
  4. When the town asks for clothes, add a Sheep farm and a Weaver.
  5. A full house with all it needs reads Ready to move up on its card. You choose when it
     moves up a tier, so the works keep their hands. Labourers want bread, clothes, a market
     and a chapel; Burghers a school, a tavern and a paved street too.
  6. Send a ship with planks to settle another island, then tie the islands together with
     trade routes (T).

Good to know
  - Idle works still cost their upkeep. Pause or clear the ones you do not need, and watch
    the balance in the top bar.
  - Happy towns pay more. Angry ones riot. A Fire Watch, a Watch House and an Infirmary keep
    fires, riots and sickness in hand.
  - Each island keeps its own goods in its warehouses, and ships carry them between islands.
  - The game is not saved yet: a game lasts until you quit.

Camera
  W A S D           Pan (arrows too; Shift is faster)
  Right drag        Pan with the ground
  Q  E              Turn (or middle drag)
  Wheel             Zoom, from a street to the whole planet
  H                 Back to the home island

Building
  Left click        Place, or pick a building
  Drag              Lay a road with the road tool
  R                 Turn the building
  Right click       Put the tool down (Esc too)
  Delete            Demolish

Company
  Space             Pause
  1  2  3           Speed: normal, double, four times
  L  T              Ledger, trade routes
  G  I              Fleet, items
  F1  Esc           This page, the menu
  F11               Window or fullscreen

Options (Windows: make a shortcut to Brightsund.exe and add them after the path in Target)
  --quality ultra   low, medium, high (the default) or ultra
  --windowed        start in a window
  --size 2560x1440  the window's size
  --no-title        skip the title screen and start a new game
  --sandbox         start in the sandbox town: four islands and the far lands at work
  --profile         time every system; F3 shows the performance panel
