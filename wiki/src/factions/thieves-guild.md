**The Tangle** is the thieves' guild of [Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md), named after both
the city's rope bridges and its network of favours. It is neither a band of noble thieves nor an
ordinary crime family: it sells burglary, smuggling, forgery, stolen information, illegal debt
recovery and a way into places ordinary people cannot reach.

## Overview

In public, its members are couriers, salvagers, locksmiths, guides and debt brokers, and people
who get your property back without involving the authorities. Its hideout is the **Slipknot**, a
rope-repair shop that is open to the public; members plan their jobs in a back room called **the
Splice**. Many of its members live in [Ward Null](../cities/vantress/ward-null.md), the
neighbourhood the city erased.

Every haul is split three ways: the crew who did the job, the Knotkeeper who sponsored it, and
the guild's communal stores.

## Beliefs and goals

The guild is divided over what it is for, and you will be pulled in every direction:

- **The neighbourhood organiser**: theft is an unofficial tax on people ordinary law cannot reach,
  and stolen wealth should keep the poor districts independent.
- **The professional thief**: theft is a craft. No needless bloodshed, no political causes and no
  betraying a client.
- **The shadow broker**: the guild should own the officials, the routes and the debts, so that
  crime becomes predictable, even if everyone pays tribute.

The older members protect unregistered families and mostly rob officials, predatory lenders and
merchants. Newer leaders want the guild to become the city's invisible government. Nobody asks
you to choose a side: the guild decides what you believe from what you keep doing.

## Professions

Members tend to fall into four trades:

- **Inkfingers**: forgers, accountants, informants and political blackmailers.
- **Chainrunners**: burglars who run the rooftops, crane arms and moving mooring chains.
- **Lowwater crews**: boat smugglers, divers and underwater infiltrators.
- **Collectors**: extortionists who acquire debts, property and people for rich clients.

## Joining

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild's first quest below.

**Before the First Bell.** At sunset, Ressa Vann gathers a handful of hopefuls in the Slipknot and
sets two empty bowls on the counter: one for the single most valuable thing stolen, one for the
greatest value in total. The deadline is the first bell at sunrise. Her rules: no killing, leave
alone any door marked with the guild's black knot, and don't lead the guards back to the shop.

There is no list of targets. You find your own by listening to conversations, earning the trust
of the people who live under the bridges and following clues, while your rivals work their own
jobs around you. At dawn, Maera weighs up what everyone brought back. Win or lose, everyone
capable is offered work as an Outside Hand.

## Questline

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild questline below.

The first jobs are shared by everyone. After that, the story branches by whose work you take on:
Ressa's **Ward Knots**, Tal and Jori's **Clean Work**, Sella's **Quiet Ledger** or Nilo's **Loose
Water**. Every branch ends in the same great finale, **Steal the City Before Dawn**.

- **The House Without Locks**: a wealthy broker hires the guild to steal his own insured
  collection. The house is left open on purpose, some of the valuables are fakes, and the evidence
  is staged to blame a servant.
- **The bank that asked to be robbed**: a bank pays the guild to test its vault. You can leave a
  weakness behind, but if you ever use it you become the first suspect.
