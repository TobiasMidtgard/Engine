**The Accord of Four Keys** is the spiritualists' order of [Orath](../cities/orath.md). It guards
the Hollow Crown and the First Spring, keeps the peace between the city's four faiths, and carries
that work out into the world: its members lead pilgrimages, protect sacred sites, hold funerals,
verify relics and mediate wherever faiths collide.

## Overview

The Accord is named for the agreement that ended Orath's last holy war, 168 years ago: each faith
holds one key to the innermost sanctuary, and all four are needed to open its ceremonial doors.
The order grew from the rescuers of that war, the hospital workers, gravediggers and hostel keepers
who opened the Hollow Crown to the civilians against orders. Its members still take one vow
before any other: **when people are dying outside, the doors open.**

Members may follow any of the four revelations, or none. The order does not ask what you believe,
only what you will do. Outside Orath it is the authority on relics and miracles: a relic with the
Accord's seal is genuine, and a relic without one is worth what someone is willing to believe.

## The four keys

Each faith keeps its key with a **Keybearer**, chosen by its own people, who sits on the Accord's
council:

| Key | Faith | Kept in |
|---|---|---|
| The Dawn Key | [The Solitary Light](../lore/gods.md#the-four-revelations-of-orath) | The Dawn Steps, in a bronze screen facing the sunrise arch |
| The Echo Key | The Deep Choir | The Underchoir, in a chamber where the names of the dead are recited |
| The Open Key | The Returning Road | Never kept: it is carried by the Keybearer at all times, as a guest carries a gift |
| The Ash Key | The Covenant of Ash | Hung in a hospital kitchen, where everyone can see it |

## Ranks

| Rank | What it means |
|---|---|
| Lamp | A novice who guides pilgrims and keeps watch at the shrines. Everyone starts here. |
| Warden | A full member who guards sacred sites, escorts processions and may mediate small disputes |
| Verifier | Examines relics, miracles and holy sites, and may grant or refuse the Accord's seal |
| Keybearer | One of four, each holding a faith's key to the sanctuary |

## People

- **Amsel Dray**, the concord keeper and the Accord's oldest Warden: no single faith may own the
  sacred centre. Shared custody prevents another religious war. Amsel's grandmother was one of the
  rescuers.
- **Brother Cassian Vey** of the Solitary Light, the restorer: one revelation came first, and Orath
  needs a clear doctrine. He is learned, sincere and very persuasive, and the people who agree with
  him are frightening.
- **Oriel Thane**, wayspeaker: people should speak directly with local spirits, ancestors and
  sacred places, without four institutions standing in the way. Oriel travels with the pilgrim
  roads and listens at shrines that nobody else has visited in years.

## What the order gives you

- **Training** in Insight and healing magic, relic lore and the old languages of the inscriptions.
- **Sanctuary**: any Accord hostel will shelter you, and Wardens of the Threshold will not hand
  you to a foreign power.
- **Verification**: bring a relic, an artefact or a holy site, and a Verifier will tell you what it
  is, sometimes.
- **Cleansing**: Accord shrines can wash away
  [corruption](../magic/corruption.md), at a price measured in deeds, not coin.
- **The pilgrim roads**: safe passage and free hostels along the roads to Orath.

## Questline

Your first job is [A Miracle Shared](../quests/a-miracle-shared.md): a new miracle has appeared in a
shared part of the Hollow Crown. Every faith can explain it convincingly, while
[the Spillward](spillward.md) suspects it is simply magic.

The order's longer story runs into Orath's own: the First Spring is weakening, and the city's
council would rather nobody knew (see [Orath](../cities/orath.md#quests-and-rumours)).

## Rivals and allies

- **The Fifth Seat**, Orath's civil government, needs the Accord's blessing and resents its
  authority.
- **[The Spillward](spillward.md)** keeps calling miracles spills.
- **The Wardens of the Threshold** enforce the accord, but answer to the Fifth Seat, not to the
  order.
