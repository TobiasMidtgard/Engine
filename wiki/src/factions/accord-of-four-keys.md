**The Accord of Four Keys** is the spiritualists' order of [Orath](../cities/orath.md). It guards the
Hollow Crown and the First Spring and keeps the peace between the city's four faiths.

## Overview

The Accord leads pilgrimages, protects sacred sites, holds funerals, verifies relics and mediates
between the four revelations.

## Joining

Your first job: a new miracle has appeared in a shared part of the Hollow Crown. Every faith can
explain it convincingly, while [the Spillward](spillward.md) suspects it is simply magic.

## Beliefs and goals

- **The concord keeper**: no single faith may own the sacred centre. Shared custody prevents another
  religious war.
- **The restorer**: one revelation came first, and Orath needs a clear doctrine.
- **The wayspeaker**: people should speak directly with local spirits, ancestors and sacred places.

The Accord can end up defending many faiths together, one dominant orthodoxy, or scattered
spiritual movements.
