**The Keepers' Road** is the guild of animal handlers, based in
[Keth Veyra](../cities/keth-veyra.md) with chapters that travel the roads. Its relationship with
[Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md) is deeply uncomfortable.

## Overview

The Keepers' Road cares for, trains and breeds animals, supplies transport animals, rescues and
scouts, and hires out working creatures.

## Joining

Your first job: recover a trained creature that escaped after being accused of attacking a
caravan. It did real damage, but the evidence suggests it was running from something.

## Beliefs and goals

- **The pactkeeper**: a creature must choose to bond and may refuse orders. Fewer partnerships,
  but stronger ones.
- **The breeder**: responsible ownership and selective breeding under welfare rules.
- **The binder**: magical or mechanical control is needed for defence, dangerous work and war.
- **The Oruvai envoy**: no living creature can be property. The guild should become a sanctuary and
  a diplomatic service.

The side you take decides your companions: bonded partners, bred working animals, controlled war
beasts or independent allies.
