**The Keepers' Road** is the guild of animal handlers, based in
[Keth Veyra](../cities/keth-veyra.md) with chapters that travel the roads. Its members care for,
train and breed animals, supply transport animals, rescue and scout, and hire out working
creatures. Its relationship with [Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md), where animals are citizens, is
deeply uncomfortable.

## Overview

Almost every caravan on the continent travels with animals bred, trained or doctored by the
Keepers' Road. Its **travelling chapters** move with the seasons: wagon camps with forges for
horseshoes, a surgery, pens and a crowd of young apprentices learning hand signals. Where a
chapter camps, sick animals are brought from miles around.

The guild was founded in Keth Veyra by the keeper families who kept the dam's working animals, and
it still carries the city's covenant: authority belongs to those who keep things running. Its
emblem is a cut harness, for the Night of Cut Harnesses eighty-three years ago, when merchants
tried to steal Brinewing eggs and the city learned what happens when animals panic.

Keth Veyra closed its gates to outsiders after that night. The Keepers' Road is the one way
through: a member of the guild may enter the city, and may bring one guest.

## Ranks

| Rank | What it means |
|---|---|
| Groom | Feeds, cleans and walks the animals; learns the signals. Everyone starts here. |
| Handler | Works animals for hire; may take a working animal of their own |
| Keeper | A full member who may train, breed and treat animals and lead a team |
| Roadmaster | Leads a travelling chapter, or one of the keeper families in Keth Veyra |

## People

- **Sere**, keeper of the Brinewing rookery in Keth Veyra, and the guild's pactkeeper: a creature
  must choose to bond and may refuse orders. Fewer partnerships, but stronger ones. Sere has never
  given a Brinewing an order in their life, and the city has only four of them.
- **Holm Agrest**, Roadmaster of the largest travelling chapter: responsible ownership and selective
  breeding under welfare rules. His bloodlines of pack horses and bargebacks are the best on the
  roads.
- **Varo Kett**, binder: magical or mechanical control is needed for defence, dangerous work and
  war. He has a harness that can hold a frightened Brinewing still, and the patience to make it
  feel like kindness.
- **Rain-Back**, the Oruvai envoy, an ancient tortoise who speaks through a listener bloom: no
  living creature can be property. The guild should become a sanctuary and a diplomatic service.
  Rain-Back walks with Holm's chapter, very slowly, and everyone waits.

## What the guild gives you

- **Training** in animal handling, riding, veterinary care and the
  [Wilds branch](../magic/vitalism.md#wilds) of Vitalism.
- **Companions**: a working animal of your own as a Handler, and a creature that chooses you as a
  Keeper.
- **Mounts and pack animals** at guild prices, and stabling in every travelling chapter.
- **Entry to Keth Veyra**, which is otherwise shut to outsiders.
- **Rescue work**: the guild pays for animals brought back alive, and for news of animals mistreated.

## Questline

Your first job is [What the Beast Was Running From](../quests/what-the-beast-was-running-from.md):
recover a trained creature that escaped after being accused of attacking a caravan. It did real
damage, but the evidence suggests it was running from something.

## Rivals and allies

- **[Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md)** buys back animals from the guild and sets them free, which the
  guild calls theft and Oruvai calls rescue.
- **[The Brass Circuit](brass-circuit.md)** hires the guild's beasts for the arena, and returns them
  hurt.
- **The six keeper families** of Keth Veyra control the strongest creatures, and the Unbound want
  the guild to break their hold.
