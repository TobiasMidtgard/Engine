**The Chartered Road** is the guild of merchants and traders, based in
[the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md) and its system of the Five Seals. It is probably the
first great faction you meet.

## Joining

Your first job: a sealed caravan arrives with cargo missing, and its manifest, its ownership
contract and its witnesses all contradict each other.

## Beliefs and goals

- **The Sealkeeper**: the Five Seals bring trust, security and steady advancement. Trade stays
  regulated and tiered.
- **The open-ledger reformer**: prices, ownership and fees should be public. Lesser merchants
  shouldn't need a rich patron to reach the higher floors.
- **The free-route captain**: tariffs, blockades and sanctions hurt ordinary people. Sometimes
  smuggling is the only honest trade.
- **The black-cargo investor**: anything can have a price: contraband, creatures, weapons, debt
  labour and people.

Whoever wins visibly changes who gets in, the guards, the customers and the goods on every tier of
the Gilded Ascent.
