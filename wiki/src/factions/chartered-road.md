**The Chartered Road** is the guild of merchants and traders, based in
[the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md) and its system of the Five Seals. Its caravans
travel every road on the continent, its factors sit in every market, and its seal holders govern
the city at the centre of the world. It is probably the first great faction you meet.

## Overview

The guild grew from the five caravan houses that founded the Gilded Ascent at the Middle Well,
about 420 years ago, and swore to stop robbing each other. Its **charter**, a long and much-amended
document kept in the Charter House, sets out how a caravan is insured, how goods are weighed and
sealed, what a road toll may be and what happens to a merchant who cheats. A copy hangs in every
caravanserai on the continent.

Most of the Gilded Ascent's seal holders belong to the Chartered Road, and the guild's leaders sit
on the Court of Seals. The Road keeps the five trade roads policed by its **outriders**, insures
every caravan that pays, and keeps a **factor**, a resident agent, in every great city.

## Ranks

Your rank in the guild follows your seal in the Gilded Ascent, so rising in the Road means rising
up the spire.

| Rank | Seal | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Drover | Clay | Works a caravan for wages: driving, guarding, loading. Everyone starts here. |
| Trader | Copper | Trades on your own account under the charter, with a share in a caravan |
| Factor | Silver | Runs the guild's business in a city, with a bonded warehouse |
| Charter Captain | Gold | Leads a whole trade road, lends, insures and witnesses contracts |
| Sealholder | Glass | Sits on the Court of Seals |

## People

- **[Hesper Lorn](../people/hesper-lorn.md)**, the Sealkeeper, who chairs the Court of Seals and keeps the master dies: the Five
  Seals bring trust, security and steady advancement. Trade stays regulated and tiered. She has
  unsealed her own brother.
- **[Ivo Tarrant](../people/ivo-tarrant.md)**, Charter Captain of the western road, the open-ledger reformer: prices,
  ownership and fees should be public. Lesser merchants shouldn't need a rich patron to reach the
  higher floors. He started as a drover, and he never lets anyone forget it.
- **Captain [Wenna Sallow](../people/wenna-sallow.md)**, outrider captain, the free-route captain: tariffs, blockades and
  sanctions hurt ordinary people. Sometimes smuggling is the only honest trade. Her outriders are
  the safest escort on the roads, and the best smugglers.
- **[Lucan Prell](../people/lucan-prell.md)**, Gold Seal investor, the black-cargo investor: anything can have a price:
  contraband, creatures, weapons, debt labour and people. He funds half of
  [Arena City](../cities/arena-city.md), and wants the Unsealed List torn up.

## What the guild gives you

- **Seals**: the guild sponsors your Copper Seal, and can help you climb further.
- **Caravan work** on every road: guarding, scouting, driving, and later trading.
- **Insurance**: for a fee, the guild repays goods lost on the road, and sends outriders after them.
- **Loans** and **bonded warehouses** once you are a Factor.
- **Prices**: guild members see the season's prices from the Lantern Table before the market does.

## Questline

Your first job is [Three Accounts of One Caravan](../quests/three-accounts-of-one-caravan.md): a
sealed caravan arrives with cargo missing, and its manifest, its ownership contract and its
witnesses all contradict each other.

Then you climb: driving a caravan along a road nobody wants to insure, sitting your Copper Seal
exam during a Restriking, settling a war of tolls between two cities, and deciding what to do with
a cargo that is legal at one end of the road and banned at the other.

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild's endings below.

| If you follow | The Gilded Ascent becomes | On every tier |
|---|---|---|
| Hesper | Regulated, tiered and safe | The seal gates tighten; the Commons stay poor and the Summit stays rich and calm |
| Ivo | An open market | Prices are posted at every gate; small traders climb, and old houses fall |
| Wenna | A city of free roads | Tolls drop and smuggling flourishes; with the Tangle's mutualists, relief smuggling that also feeds organised crime |
| Lucan | A market for everything | The Unsealed List is torn up; with the Last Writ, one hidden criminal economy |

Whoever wins visibly changes who gets in, the guards, the customers and the goods on every tier of
the Gilded Ascent.

## Rivals and allies

- **[The Makers' Compact](makers-compact.md)** makes what the Road sells, and both think the other
  takes too much.
- **[Sky City](../cities/sky-city.md)**'s Golden Anchor wants the island lowered closer to the
  Road's markets.
- **[The Tangle](thieves-guild.md)** in Vantress robs the Road's warehouses and insures its own
  thefts with the Road's own forms.
