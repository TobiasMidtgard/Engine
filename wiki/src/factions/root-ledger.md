**The Root Ledger** is the guild of herbalists and harvesters, based in
[Rillvault](../cities/rillvault.md) with important chapters near [Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md),
[Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md) and the [Sifting Basin](../cities/sifting-basin.md). Its
ledger is a real book, or rather thousands of them: a record of every harvest ground on the
continent, what grows there, how much was taken and how fast it came back.

## Overview

The Root Ledger maps where things can be harvested, preserves seeds, makes medicine, identifies
poisons and regulates rare living materials. It grew out of the archive of seeds and fungal strains
in Rillvault's fourteenth tunnel, whose keepers began lending knowledge to farmers above ground and
asking, in return, to be told what they found. Over two centuries the lending became a guild, and
the guild became the authority that alchemists, doctors and poisoners all answer to.

Its seal on a medicine means it was made from what it says, at the strength it says. Its **harvest
calendar** says when each wild plant may be picked, and its **red pages** list the plants and
creatures that may not be taken at all. Harvesting from the red pages is a crime wherever the
Root Ledger has a chapter, and the guild has its own wardens to enforce it.

The Root Ledger also decides which kinds of alchemy are legal: what counts as a medicine, a
stimulant, a narcotic or a poison. That makes it the quiet enemy of half the black market.

## Ranks

| Rank | What it means |
|---|---|
| Gatherer | Picks, carries and records for a chapter. Everyone starts here. |
| Sower | A full member who may harvest under licence, brew under the guild's seal and keep seeds |
| Ledger-keeper | Keeps a chapter's ledger and decides its harvest calendar |
| Elder of the Root | One of the seven elders in Rillvault who keep the red pages |

## People

- **[Ilse Moorcroft](../people/ilse-moorcroft.md)**, elder and steward of the harvest grounds: never harvest anything faster than
  it grows back. She has walked every harvest ground in the ledger and can name the plants of a
  valley by its smell.
- **[Benedek Strawn](../people/benedek-strawn.md)**, cultivator: farm and breed at industrial scale, so that medicine is cheap and
  nobody needs to strip a wild valley. His tunnel farms in Rillvault grow healing moss by the
  tonne.
- **[Ottilie Brand](../people/ottilie-brand.md)**, refiner and master alchemist of the Vantress chapter: concentration and
  mutation make stronger medicines, stimulants, narcotics and poisons. Most of what she brews is
  legal. She would like all of it to be.

## What the guild gives you

- **Training** in herbalism, alchemy and the [Herbalism branch](../magic/vitalism.md#herbalism) of
  Vitalism.
- **Harvest licences**: legal picking of rare plants, and the guild's maps of where they grow.
- **The guild's seal** on your potions, which makes them sellable at a far better price.
- **Seed stores** in every chapter: rare seeds and spores to plant, trade or carry to a new place.
- **Identification**: bring anything that grows, or anything that poisoned someone, and a chapter
  will tell you what it is.

## Questline

Your first job is [Not Enough to Go Around](../quests/not-enough-to-go-around.md): a spreading
illness can be treated, but the moss that cures it is vital to a fragile ecosystem and cannot
supply everyone.

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild's endings below.

| If you follow | The guild becomes | In the world |
|---|---|---|
| Ilse | Keepers of wild places | Rare ingredients stay rare and dear; wild valleys recover. With Oruvai's envoy, nonhuman life gains legal standing |
| Benedek | Growers on a vast scale | Medicine gets cheap; farmed strains replace wild ones and old remedies are forgotten |
| Ottilie | Masters of refinement | Potions grow stronger and stranger; new narcotics flood the cities, and poisons become legal goods |

What you choose changes which ingredients are available, what medicine costs, how much damage
harvesting does and which kinds of alchemy are legal.

## Rivals and allies

- **[Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md)** trades medicines with the Ledger and suspects it of treating the
  Chorus as a harvest ground.
- **[The Makers' Compact](makers-compact.md)**'s brewers' lodge fights the Ledger over who decides
  a recipe.
- **The Second City's Hollow Market** sells everything on the red pages.
