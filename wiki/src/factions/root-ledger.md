**The Root Ledger** is the guild of herbalists and harvesters, based in
[Rillvault](../cities/rillvault.md) with important chapters near [Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md),
[Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md) and the [Sifting Basin](../cities/sifting-basin.md).

## Overview

The Root Ledger maps where things can be harvested, preserves seeds, makes medicine, identifies
poisons and regulates rare living materials.

## Joining

Your first job: a spreading illness can be treated, but the organism that cures it is vital to a
fragile ecosystem and cannot supply everyone.

## Beliefs and goals

- **The steward**: never harvest anything faster than it grows back.
- **The cultivator**: farm and breed at industrial scale.
- **The refiner**: concentration and mutation make stronger medicines, stimulants, narcotics and
  poisons.

What you choose changes which ingredients are available, what medicine costs, how much damage
harvesting does and which kinds of alchemy are legal.
