**The Makers' Compact** is the guild of workers and craftspeople: one institution with lodges for
enchanters, brewers, smiths, builders, cooks and the other trades. Its council moves between the
great production cities.

## Overview

The Compact runs apprenticeships, sets quality standards, shares workshops and negotiates wages
and contracts. The Makers decide who owns production; how machines and infrastructure are designed
is a question for [the Pattern Office](pattern-office.md).

## Joining

Your first job: enchanted equipment and brewed goods have started to fail. The cause could be
counterfeits, unsafe quotas, sabotage or a closely guarded trade secret.

## Beliefs and goals

- **The union steward**: workers should own the workshops, share their knowledge and bargain
  together.
- **The master craftsperson**: skill needs hierarchy, long apprenticeships and protected
  techniques.
- **The production organiser**: machines and standard recipes make goods affordable, even if many
  workers are no longer needed.
