**The Makers' Compact** is the guild of workers and craftspeople: one institution with lodges for
smiths, enchanters, brewers, builders, cooks, weavers, glassmakers and the other trades. It has no
capital. Its council moves between the great production cities, a year in each, so that no single
city can own it.

## Overview

The Compact runs apprenticeships, sets quality standards, shares workshops and negotiates wages
and contracts. Its **maker's mark**, stamped beside a craftsperson's own mark, says that an item
was made by someone who passed the lodge's tests, from the materials it claims. In
[the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md) an enchanted item without a registered mark is
"blank work" and cannot be sold at all.

The Makers decide who owns production; how machines and infrastructure are designed is a question
for [the Pattern Office](pattern-office.md).

The Compact was born from **the Long Strike**, about 120 years ago, when the smiths of a dozen
cities laid down their hammers at once and the armies of three kingdoms found they had no new
swords. Two of those kingdoms are gone now. The Compact signed the peace, and the date is still its holiday.

## Ranks

| Rank | What it means |
|---|---|
| Apprentice | Learns a trade under a master, for board and a small wage. Everyone starts here. |
| Journeyman | Has passed the lodge's first test; may work for wages anywhere the Compact is |
| Lodge member | A full member with a vote in the lodge and the right to the Compact's mark |
| Master | Has made a **masterpiece** the lodge accepted; may take apprentices and open a workshop |
| Lodge warden | Leads a lodge in one city and sits on the Council of Lodges in turn |

## People

- **[Hedda Strom](../people/hedda-strom.md)**, steward of the smiths' lodge: workers should own the workshops, share their
  knowledge and bargain together. She led the last strike in Orravell's factories and spent a year
  in its prisons for it.
- **Master [Aldous Pike](../people/aldous-pike.md)**, enchanter: skill needs hierarchy, long apprenticeships and protected
  techniques. His own masterpiece, a sword that cannot be dulled, took him eleven years.
- **[Perrin Haske](../people/perrin-haske.md)**, production organiser: machines and standard recipes make goods affordable, even
  if many workers are no longer needed. He has bought three Tessaran pattern looms, and the
  weavers' lodge has not forgiven him.

## What the guild gives you

- **Training** in every craft: smithing, enchanting, alchemy, cooking, building, weaving and glass.
- **Shared workshops** in every great city, which you may rent by the day once you are a
  journeyman.
- **The maker's mark**, which raises the price of everything you make and lets you sell enchanted
  work in the Gilded Ascent.
- **Contracts**: orders posted in every lodge hall, from horseshoes to bridges.
- **Masterworks**: as a master you can make items above the ordinary limits of your materials.

## Questline

Your first job is [Stamped and Sold](../quests/stamped-and-sold.md): enchanted equipment and brewed
goods carrying the Compact's mark have started to fail. The cause could be counterfeits, unsafe
quotas, sabotage or a closely guarded trade secret, and it might be all four.

## Rivals and allies

- **[The Chartered Road](chartered-road.md)** sells what the Makers make, and both think the other
  takes too much of the price.
- **[The Root Ledger](root-ledger.md)** argues with the brewers over who may decide a recipe.
- **Orravell's factories** depend on the smiths, and the Crown Ascendant has banned the lodge there
  twice.
