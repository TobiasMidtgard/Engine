**The Brass Circuit** is the guild of arena fighters and brawlers, based in
[Arena City](../cities/arena-city.md). By day the city is a market for everything the rest of the
world forbids; by night its trading floor becomes the arena, and the Circuit decides who fights on
it, by which rules, for how much and in front of whom.

## Overview

The Brass Circuit runs sporting events and prize fights, settles disputes by combat when both
sides agree, trains fighters, hires out guards and keeps the crowds coming back. Its fighters are
the most famous people in the south: their names are painted on the balconies, their fights are
sung about in the taverns, and children in [Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md) play at being
them.

The guild began as a ring of dockside brawlers who fought on the cargo barges to settle debts.
When the forbidden market grew rich, its traders paid the brawlers to fight on the auction floor
at night, because a crowd that comes to watch a fight stays to buy. Fighting became the city's
second trade, and the brawlers' brass knuckle tokens became the guild's emblem.

The Circuit's fighters include acrobats, duellists, flying mages, beast tamers and people who fight
in armour made for one night only. The headline act of any season is a fight between an acrobatic
fighter and a flying mage, which never goes the way the betting says.

## Ranks

Rank is won in the arena and nowhere else. A fighter's **ring** is the brass band they wear on the
wrist, re-cast at every promotion.

| Rank | What it means |
|---|---|
| Sand | Unranked. Fights in the open pits and the morning bouts for food and a bed. Everyone starts here. |
| Bronze | A ranked fighter with a trainer, a share of the gate and a bed in the Undercroft |
| Brass | A full member who fights on the main floor at night and may take security contracts |
| Gilt | A star, with a balcony named after them, a share of the betting and the right to choose opponents |
| Champion | One of the four reigning champions, one for each gate of the arena |

## People

- **Dagna Holt, "the Anvil"**, retired champion and the Circuit's arbiter: fair competition and a
  way up for anyone. She wants protection for fighters, honest rankings and a life after
  retirement. Her own knees are ruined, and she tells everyone exactly whose fault it was.
- **Orlo Vance, "the Voice"**, the grand promoter: spectacle pays for the whole city. Rivalries,
  gambling, dangerous formats and celebrity. He has never thrown a punch and is the richest man in
  the guild.
- **Captain Brenn Askew**, the company captain: the arena should find the soldiers, officers and
  mercenaries for the wars to come. She scouts for
  [Orravell](../cities/orravell.md)'s Crown Companies and for anyone else who pays.
- **Mags Rennick**, the pit organiser: formal rules favour the rich. Real brawling allows improvised
  weapons, the terrain and anyone willing to step in. She runs the open pits under the canals,
  where the Circuit's rules do not reach.

## What the guild gives you

- **Trainers** in every weapon and in brawling, with sparring partners of your rank.
- **Prize money**, gate shares and betting shares, which grow with your rank and your fame.
- **Fame**: the higher your rank, the more people recognise you across the south. Fame opens doors,
  and makes disguises harder.
- **Arbitration**: as a Brass fighter you may settle a dispute by combat on someone's behalf, for a
  fee.
- **The Undercroft**: beds, a surgery, a forge for arena armour and the guild's healers, who are
  very good at bones.

## Questline

Your first job is [The Bout That Broke Wrong](../quests/the-bout-that-broke-wrong.md): enter or
support a tournament while finding out why a favourite was badly hurt in a bout he should have won.
You can get through it by fighting, healing, betting, intimidation or sabotage.

Then you climb the ranks, fighting for each of the four leaders' ideas of what a fight is for:
Dagna's honest ladder, Orlo's grand spectacles, Brenn's recruiting tournaments and Mags's pits.

## Rivals and allies

- **The market traders** pay for the arena and want it profitable, not fair.
- **[The Keepers' Road](keepers-road.md)** supplies the arena's beasts, and argues with the Circuit
  over every injured animal.
- **[The Last Writ](last-writ.md)** keeps an eye on fighters who might be worth hiring.
