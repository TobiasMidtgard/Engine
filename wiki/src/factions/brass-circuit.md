**The Brass Circuit** is the guild of arena fighters and brawlers, based in
[Arena City](../cities/arena-city.md).

## Overview

The Brass Circuit runs sporting events and prize fights, settles disputes, trains fighters, takes
security contracts and keeps the crowds entertained.

## Joining

Your first job: enter or support a tournament while investigating a suspicious injury. You can
get through it by fighting, healing, betting, intimidation or sabotage.

## Beliefs and goals

- **The retired champion**: fair competition and a way up for anyone. Protect the fighters, keep
  the rankings honest and look after them when they retire.
- **The grand promoter**: spectacle pays for the whole city. Rivalries, gambling, dangerous formats
  and celebrity.
- **The company captain**: the arena should find the soldiers, officers and mercenaries for the
  wars to come.
- **The pit organiser**: formal rules favour the rich. Real brawling allows improvised weapons, the
  terrain and anyone willing to step in.
