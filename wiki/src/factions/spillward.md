**The Spillward** is the mages' guild, based in [Veyrhold](../cities/veyrhold.md). It is not an
academy and it hands out no robes. Its members are the people you call when magic goes wrong: they
contain magical accidents, repair damaged enchantments, keep the wards that protect a city working
and investigate unstable magic.

## Overview

Magic leaks. A failed enchantment, a cracked rune, a spell cast too hard in a crowded street: the
energy that does not go where it was meant to goes somewhere else, and the Spillward calls that a
**spill**. A small spill turns milk sour and doors stubborn; a large one rewrites a street, so
that stone runs like water, people forget their names or the rain falls upwards for a week.

The guild was founded in Veyrhold after a spill that the quarry's hushstone could not hold. Its
first members were the workers who went back into the shed to drag people out, and they still
wear the grey canvas aprons of quarry labour rather than anything a mage would choose. Today the
Spillward has a **Wardhouse** in almost every city. Its wardens are licensed to enter any building
where a spill is suspected, and that makes them some of the most powerful people in a city, and
some of the most hated.

The Spillward also licenses mages in Veyrhold and certifies spells for sale. Its stamp is the
difference between a spell that is a product and a spell that is a crime.

## Ranks

| Rank | What it means |
|---|---|
| Runner | Carries messages, measures, sweeps up and learns. Everyone starts here, even famous mages. |
| Warden | A licensed member who answers spill calls, inspects wards and certifies small enchantments |
| Containment Lead | Leads a team into a major spill, and may seal a building or a street |
| Spillmaster | The head of a city's Wardhouse; the Spillmaster of Veyrhold leads the whole guild |

## People

- **Inspector Halvard Cray**: magic must be licensed, measured and contained. He has closed more
  workshops than anyone in Veyrhold and can tell which spell caused a spill by the taste of the air.
- **Tamsin Reeve**, a street practitioner on the First Cut who teaches cantrips in the Common
  Hearths for free: restricting knowledge makes magic a monopoly of the elite. Ordinary people
  should learn practical magic, and learn to clean up after it.
- **Ysolde Marr**, field researcher: anomalies should be followed, not erased. Transformation,
  forbidden magic and instability may be the next great discovery. She has been inside more spills
  than anyone alive, and she is not entirely the same as when she started.

## What the guild gives you

- **Training** in every clean subclass, and in **ward work**: reading, repairing and redirecting
  wards.
- **A licence**, which makes casting legal in Veyrhold and opens the Binding Yards to you.
- **Certification**: the guild's stamp on your spells and enchantments, which makes them sellable
  in [the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md).
- **The right of entry**: as a Warden you can enter a building where you suspect a spill. Abuse it
  and you lose it.
- **Spill salvage**: things changed by a spill are strange and valuable, and the guild keeps first
  claim.

## Questline

Your first job is [The Street That Changed](../quests/the-street-that-changed.md): a magical spill
is transforming part of a residential street. An unlicensed practitioner stopped people dying
there, but may also have caused it.

## Rivals and allies

- **The quarry owners** pay for the Wardhouse and want inspections that find nothing.
- **[The Pattern Office](pattern-office.md)** shares the Spillward's dream of understanding every
  system, and its fear of the ones nobody understands.
- **[The Accord of Four Keys](accord-of-four-keys.md)** resents being told that its miracles are
  spills.
