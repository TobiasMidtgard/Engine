**The Spillward** is the mages' guild. It is not an academy: its members contain magical
accidents, repair damaged enchantments, maintain the wards that protect the city and investigate
unstable magic.

## Joining

Your first job: a magical spill is transforming part of a residential district. An unlicensed
practitioner stopped people dying there, but may also have caused it.

## Beliefs and goals

- **The inspector**: magic must be licensed, measured and contained.
- **The street practitioner**: restricting knowledge makes magic a monopoly of the elite. Ordinary
  people should learn practical magic.
- **The field researcher**: anomalies should be followed, not erased. Transformation, forbidden
  magic and instability may be the next great discovery.

Depending on who you follow, magic becomes safer but more exclusive, open to all but volatile, or
the centre of dangerous new magic.
