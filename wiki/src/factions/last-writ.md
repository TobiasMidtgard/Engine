**The Last Writ** is the assassins' guild, hidden throughout the upper criminal society of
[the Second City](../cities/second-city.md). In the rooftop city of the Eaves, every killing needs
a bought **Red Writ** naming its victim, and the Last Writ is the institution that checks each
writ, registers it and, when a client pays for it, carries it out.

## Overview

In public, the Last Writ guards people, recovers fugitives, verifies bounties, protects informants
and investigates suspicious deaths. All of that is true. The guild's real authority is that it
alone can declare a Red Writ **verified**: real, paid for, issued by someone with the right to
issue it, and naming the right person. A verified writ is hung in the Quiet Bell, and from then on
the killing is lawful in the Eaves. That is the writ's last stage, and the guild's name.

Members say the guild was founded by [the Empty Blade](../lore/ascendants.md), a duellist who
could cut without a sword, after the war that ended in the Sunset Compact. The Empty Blade wanted
killing in the Eaves to be rare, paid for and answerable to someone. Whether the guild still
serves that purpose is what its leaders are fighting about.

The Last Writ takes contracts outside the Second City too, and it hunts anyone who kills for money
in the Eaves without a writ.

## Ranks

| Rank | What it means |
|---|---|
| Witness | A trial member who watches, carries messages and keeps quiet. Everyone starts here. |
| Courier | Carries writs between the Red Exchange, the Quiet Bell and clients, and guards witnesses |
| Writ-bearer | Investigates writs, finds the people named in them and may take bodyguard contracts |
| Executor | Carries out verified writs, and hunts rogue killers |
| Hand | One of the guild's few senior members, who choose which writs it accepts |
| The Last Word | The head of the guild, who has the final say on every writ |

## People

- **[Edrin Vasse](../people/edrin-vasse.md)**, Hand and the guild's chief bodyguard: killing is justified only when every
  lawful option has failed. He wants the guild to protect witnesses, rescue marked targets and hunt
  rogue assassins.
- **[Ophel Strand](../people/ophel-strand.md)**, the contract registrar: personal morality turns assassins into political
  servants. Every properly verified writ must be honoured, whoever issued it, or the system
  collapses into ordinary murder.
- **[Sabeth Rook](../people/sabeth-rook.md)**, the master hunter: killing is the purest test of skill. Contracts provide worthy
  prey and keep lesser killers in line. She keeps a small shrine to
  [the Antlered Hunt](../lore/gods.md#the-antlered-hunt) in her rooms.
- **The Last Word**: speaks only through sealed letters. Some members believe the Last Word died
  years ago and the letters are written by committee.

## What the guild gives you

- **Training** in stealth, poisons, disguise, climbing the Eaves and the guild's knife work.
- **Access to the Quiet Bell**, where you can read which writs are hanging, and which name yours.
- **Writ immunity**: while you are a member, nobody can buy a Red Writ on you without the guild
  knowing.
- **A safe room** above the Courts, and passage across the Moon Runs by agreement with the clans.
- **Bounties** from the Boulevard's Magistracy, quietly passed upstairs.

## Questline

Your first job is [A Writ in Question](../quests/a-writ-in-question.md): escort a witness to a
hearing that will decide whether a contract is legitimate. The evidence suggests the target is
guilty, but the writ itself may be forged.

After that you work for all three leaders in turn: guarding a magistrate's daughter for Edrin,
checking a writ issued by a dead man for Ophel, and tracking an Awakened killer across the roofs
for Sabeth. Nobody asks you whose side you are on. The guild watches what you do when a writ is
real but wrong, or wrong but paid for.

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild's endings below.

Depending on who you follow, the Last Writ becomes:

| If you follow | The guild becomes | In the world |
|---|---|---|
| Edrin | A service that protects people from assassins | Writs get rarer and dearer; the Eaves get safer and angrier |
| Ophel | A politically neutral institution of contracts | Anyone with money can buy a lawful death, and the Magistracy starts buying |
| Sabeth | A feared society of predators | Killers come from across the continent to be tested; nobody is safe on the roofs |

The guild's finale can join others: with the black-cargo investors of
[the Chartered Road](chartered-road.md), the Last Writ becomes part of one hidden criminal
economy across the continent.

## Rivals and allies

- **The Night Council** needs the guild and fears it. The Old Blood would like the Quiet Bell to
  ring only for its enemies.
- **The Magistracy** officially does not know the guild exists, and hires it twice a year.
- **[The Tangle](thieves-guild.md)** keeps no assassins of its own and sends lethal work here.
