**The Last Writ** is the assassins' guild, hidden throughout the upper criminal society of
[the Second City](../cities/second-city.md).

## Overview

In public, the Last Writ guards people, recovers fugitives, verifies bounties, protects informants
and investigates suspicious deaths.

## Joining

Your first job: escort someone to a hearing that will decide whether an assassination contract is
legitimate. The evidence suggests the target is guilty, but the contract itself may be forged.

## Beliefs and goals

- **The former bodyguard**: killing is justified only when every lawful option has failed. Protect
  witnesses, rescue marked targets and hunt rogue assassins.
- **The contract registrar**: personal morality turns assassins into political servants. Every
  properly verified contract must be honoured, whoever issued it.
- **The master hunter**: killing is the purest test of skill. Contracts provide worthy prey and keep
  lesser killers in line.

Depending on who you follow, the Last Writ becomes a service that protects people from
assassins, a politically neutral institution of contracts, or a feared society of predators.
