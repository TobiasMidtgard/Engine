**The Pattern Office** is the guild of engineers and technologists of [Tessara](../cities/tessara.md).
It maintains the Patternwork, the ceramic tiles whose channels carry the city's water, power, heat,
information and transport, and it keeps the city's buildings safe. In a city run by the machine
mind Ison, the Pattern Office is the human hand on the machine.

## Overview

Every Patternwork tile carries a pattern, and every pattern can be read. The Pattern Office draws
the patterns, tests them, lays them and repairs them. It also keeps **the Public Book**, the record
of every pattern connected to the city's network, because Tessara's first law says that any
machine with power over a person must reveal its pattern.

Its engineers work in grey tunics with a single glazed tile at the collar, coloured for their
speciality: blue for water, amber for power, red for heat, white for information, green for
transport and black for structure. They are welcome everywhere in Tessara, and nowhere more than
in the middle of the night when the heating fails.

The Office's founders were the students of [the Last Artificer](../lore/ascendants.md), who
laid the first cores of the Mnemeion. Every engineer learns the Last Artificer's one rule:
**build it so that the next person can mend it.**

## Ranks

| Rank | What it means |
|---|---|
| Tiler | Lays and replaces tiles from drawn patterns. Everyone starts here. |
| Pattern-reader | May read, test and repair any public pattern, and sign repair logs |
| Draughtsman | Draws new patterns and submits them to the Certifiers |
| Chief of a Colour | Leads one of the six specialities across the whole city |
| Director of Patterns | Leads the Office and speaks for it to Ison |

## People

- **Thera Kallis**, Chief of Water, the common-pattern engineer: every system should be open to
  inspection, modular and repairable on the spot. Designs belong to the public. Her water patterns
  are so simple that children in the poorest district repair their own fountains.
- **Doros Menti**, Director of Patterns: one coordinated network that predicts demand and prevents
  failures. He trusts Ison more than he trusts people, and he is usually right.
- **Ione Sarakis**, patent architect: money from [Sky City](../cities/sky-city.md) can speed up
  invention, but investors only fund designs they can own and hide. Her black-glazed work for the
  Closed Kilns is the most advanced in the world, and nobody but her can mend it.
- **Corin Ostrander**, a quiet draughtsman who spends his nights in Veyrhold: he believes a pattern
  could learn, and he has drawn one that does.

## What the guild gives you

- **Training** in engineering, Patternwork, the [Artifice branch](../magic/shaping.md#artifice) of
  Shaping, and firearms and energy weapons.
- **Access** to every public pattern in Tessara, and the Menders' tunnels beneath it.
- **Aegis sets**: as a Pattern-reader you may draw an Aegis Sentinel, Iris Strider or Atlas Bastion
  set for Office work (see [Tessara](../cities/tessara.md#arms-and-armour)).
- **Workshops** in the Drafting Halls, with kilns for firing your own tiles.
- **Certification**: a pattern the Office has signed may be sold and connected anywhere in Tessara.

## Questline

Your first job is [Every Repair Has an Owner](../quests/every-repair-has-an-owner.md): a chain of
infrastructure failures threatens Tessara's farms and the terraces where people live. Every repair
on offer would tie the city to a different future.

## Rivals and allies

- **The Closed Kilns** fund the patent architects and despise the Public Book.
- **The Hairline** hides altered patterns inside legitimate mosaics, and the Office hunts them.
- **[Ferris's Tempered Hand](../lore/gods.md#the-maker)**, the priests of the Maker, agree with Thera,
  loudly.
