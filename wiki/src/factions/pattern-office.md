**The Pattern Office** is the guild of engineers and technologists of [Tessara](../cities/tessara.md).
It maintains the Patternwork that carries the city's water, power, heat, information and
transport, and keeps its buildings safe.

## Joining

Your first job: a chain of infrastructure failures threatens Tessara's farms and the terraces
where people live. Every repair on offer would tie the city to a different future.

## Beliefs and goals

- **The common-pattern engineer**: every system should be open to inspection, modular and repairable
  on the spot. Designs belong to the public.
- **The director**: one coordinated network that predicts demand and prevents failures.
- **The patent architect**: money from [Sky City](../cities/sky-city.md) can speed up invention, but
  investors only fund designs they can own and hide.
