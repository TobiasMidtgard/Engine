Overnight, **Cutter's Row**, a lane of bakeries and homes on Veyrhold's sunny rim, began to change.
Its cobbles are soft as bread. Its doors open onto the wrong rooms. A woman woke to find her
staircase going down into a cellar that has never existed, full of warm light. Nobody died,
because someone pulled them all out in the dark, and that someone has no licence.

## The job

The Spillward's inspector, [Halvard Cray](../factions/spillward.md#people), needs runners: people to
carry measuring rods into the spill, mark where it has spread and bring back anything that has
changed. He also wants the unlicensed mage found.

> A spill doesn't care who you are. Walk in straight, touch nothing that hums, and if you start
> forgetting your name, come out at once.

The spill grows slowly towards the Last Step inn and the quarry edge. If it reaches the rim, the
quarry's hushstone will start to drink it, and nobody knows what that will do.

## Inside the spill

- **Doors** lead somewhere else each time you open them; mark them and map the loop.
- **The warm cellar** is where the spill began. Its light makes plants grow and hair turn white.
- **Changed things**: a loaf that never goes stale, a cat that is a little too clever, a kettle that
  boils cold. All of them are valuable, and all of them belong to someone.
- **The street mage**: [Tamsin Reeve](../factions/spillward.md#people) is still inside, holding the
  spill back with a ward she taught herself, and she is getting tired.

## What caused it

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the job below.

Tamsin did not cause the spill; she was teaching a cantrip class in the next street when it began,
and ran towards it. It was caused by a **licensed** spellwright of the Binding Yards, who has been
dumping a failed commission's waste into an old storm drain under Cutter's Row for months to save
the fee for proper disposal. Halvard signed his last inspection. The warm cellar is the drain,
swollen with years of leaked magic, and it has started to be somewhere.

```flow
start: Walk into the spill | Measure it, map it, find the mage.
path: Help Tamsin | Hold the ward together and close the cellar.
path: Follow the drain | Find where the waste comes from.
path: Study the cellar | Ysolde Marr wants it kept open.
join: The spill stops, or it doesn't | Somebody has to answer for it.
end: Report the spellwright | Halvard's own signature is on the inspection.
end: Protect Tamsin | Give her the credit, and a licence.
end: Keep the cellar | Seal it, and give Ysolde the key.
```
