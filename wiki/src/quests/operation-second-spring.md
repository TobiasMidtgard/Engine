Kestrel Vale, a farming region near [Orravell](../cities/orravell.md), hired Orravell's companies
for a long war and now owes them heavily for troops, equipment and loans. An armistice is close,
and Orravell offers to stay on for a while as a guarantor of security.

## The plan

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Orravell's secret operation below.

In secret, the Crown has already decided to take the valley for good: its grain, river tolls,
mills, workshops and government. Classified plans hand its offices to Orravellian officials and
its future revenues to military households, and skilled workers appear on compulsory transfer
lists.

Hushwing Station's glider teams carry sealed orders to seize key officials and the original
financial records when it begins. **Captain [Sera Noll](../people/sera-noll.md)** insists on bringing her crews home; she
can be principled about how her people are treated and still help conquer another country.

## The evidence

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Orravell's secret operation below.

You can find what gives the plan away:

- **A public protection contract** with an end date.
- **Factory contracts** that already treat the valley's future output as Orravell's.
- **Appointments** to offices Orravell doesn't hold yet.
- **Transfer papers** naming people who never agreed to move.

## Your choices

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Orravell's secret operation below.

Support the campaign, expose it, protect particular people, negotiate limited terms, or simply
finish the job you were paid for. Exposing it raises the political cost; it doesn't magically stop
an army.
