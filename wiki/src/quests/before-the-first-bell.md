At sunset, [Ressa Vann](../people/ressa-vann.md) gathers a handful of unknown hopefuls in the
public rope-repair shop at the front of the Slipknot and sets two empty bowls on the counter. You
are one of them. This is how [the Tangle](../factions/thieves-guild.md), the thieves' guild of
[Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md), finds its new hands.

## The contest

Two bowls, two ways to win:

- **The Prize**: the single most valuable thing stolen.
- **The Weight**: the greatest value in total.

The deadline is the first bell at sunrise. Ressa's rules are short:

- **No killing.**
- **Leave alone any door marked with the guild's black knot.**
- **Don't lead the guards back to the shop.**
- **Anything you carry over the threshold counts**, even something you stole from another contestant.

> Bring back the most valuable single item, or the greatest value in total. Finding something
> worth stealing is part of the test.

## Finding a target

There is no list of targets and no markers on the map. Your only goal is to find something worth
stealing and come back before sunrise. Leads arrive in your journal as rumours, such as "A
materials broker is receiving buyers above the Eel", and you find them three ways:

- **Listening**: overheard conversations tell you what is moving, who owns it and when it is
  vulnerable.
- **Trust**: the people who live in the district know the hidden routes and everyone's routines.
- **Looking**: physical clues show what is really valuable and where it is hidden.

Beneath the western bridge, the people of the underbridge share a **communal meal**. Give coin,
food, medicine or stolen supplies and sit down with them, and ordinary conversation turns up what
no guard would tell you: a former crane operator saw the bank testing the ferry crane, a laundry
lift keeps moving after the servants leave, a "guard" changed uniform in an alley. Not every coin
buys a clue.

Your rivals work their own jobs at the same time. Once you find them, you can work together, race
them, steal their plan or rob them on their way back.

## The night

Time moves on when you commit to something big, not while you explore.

| Phase | What the night offers |
|---|---|
| Sunset | Rumours and exploring the district |
| Late evening | The casino and the houses of the wealthy |
| Last bell | Money and goods on the move, and ways in from under the water |
| Sunrise | Getting away, and Maera's appraisal |

## The scores

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the contest's targets below.

Ten jobs are hidden in the district. Five belong to your rivals, who go after them whether or not
you do:

- **The Unpaid Winner** (Tavi "Pin" Rell): a gambler leaves the casino with a wooden claim token
  instead of his winnings, to cash at a guarded window before sunrise. Pick his pocket, follow
  him, take his place, swap a fake or rob the payment chest.
- **The Room Above the Eel** (Orris Vale): a broker is quietly selling a piece of uncut basin glass
  from his rented room. The famous mechanical nightingale under glass is a trap: priceless on
  paper, impossible to sell.
- **The Dawn Chest** (Bram "Kettle" Sorn): the bank moves two identical strongboxes to the dawn
  ferry, and only one holds deposits. Your attempt shows the bank how weak its procedures are.
- **The Ninth Table** (Cala "Twice" Mere): the casino's books hide a ninth table that exists only to
  move untaxed money. Claim its winnings and the casino cannot refuse without admitting it exists.
- **The Weight Below** (Yara Coil): an extra ballast chain under the customs watchhouse holds a
  sealed locker of confiscated valuables, reachable only from the water.

Five more are yours to discover:

- **The Clerk With the Empty Purse**: blank insurance seals that can make stolen goods look insured,
  destroyed or lawfully sold.
- **The Toll Beneath the Toll**: a bridge collector hides his double charges in the bridge's
  counterweight. Take the purse and the bridge jams half open.
- **The Watchman Who Changes Height**: a real guard rents out his uniform and access seal, which
  open doors during the other jobs.
- **Rent on a Rope**: at midnight, a whole building's rent is lowered on a rope to a skiff.
- **The Auction With No Buyers**: a secret salvage auction, where the flashy lot is the hardest one
  to sell.

## Dawn

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the end of the contest below.

As the bell nears, the district wakes up. A thief with clean hands blends into the morning shift;
one carrying a strongbox is chased across roofs, rafts and folding rope bridges. One rival gets
into real danger, and that is the contest's only big decision: keep moving, drop your loot to
help, use them as a distraction, rob them or pool your hauls.

At dawn, Maera weighs every haul by what it will really fetch, not what it is worth on paper:

> Fence value = sale price − laundering cost − heat − damage − partners' shares

You can win both bowls, share a win, lose the money but earn loyalty, win with a rival's stolen
haul and make an enemy, or come back empty-handed after saving someone, because "reliable
accomplices are rarer than burglars". Win or lose, everyone capable is offered work as an
**Outside Hand**.
