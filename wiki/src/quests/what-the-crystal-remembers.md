There are many roads to [Awakening](../play/ascension.md#awakening). This is one of them, and it
begins as a simple theft.

A buyer, who meets you only in the dark, wants a crystal from **the Sunken Works**: a magical
research facility east of [Veyrhold](../cities/veyrhold.md) that collapsed into its own foundations
forty years ago, and that the [Spillward](../factions/spillward.md) has sealed ever since. The
crystal was the heart of whatever the Works were building. The buyer will pay enough to retire
on. You will find out why.

## The job

Get in past the Spillward's seals, go down through the collapsed levels, find the crystal and
bring it out. Nobody who has gone down in the last forty years has come back the same, and
several of them are still down there.

## The Sunken Works

- **The Spillward's seals** on the entrance, renewed every spring by a Containment Lead who is
  quietly paid to renew them badly.
- **The collapsed levels**: workshops folded into each other, staircases that end in solid rock and
  rooms where the air is thick with old spells.
- **The changed**: animals and people who came too close to the crystal and were rewritten. Some
  are dangerous. Some just want to be understood.
- **The heart chamber**, where the crystal hangs in a cage of hushstone that is slowly turning pale.

## The rupture

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the quest below.

When you touch the cage, the crystal ruptures. It is not a weapon and not a treasure: it is a
**fragment of raw Mind**, the core of an artificial Awakening the Works were trying to build, and
it has been alone in the dark for forty years. It begins to rewrite everything near it: stone, air,
the changed, and you.

```flow
start: Touch the cage | The crystal ruptures.
path: Absorb it | Let it rewrite you. It is the most dangerous choice, and the most powerful.
path: Redirect it | Pour it into a machine of the Works, and wear the machine.
path: Bind it | Give it to an animal companion; you Awaken together.
path: Share it | Split it with an ally. Both of you Awaken, a little.
path: Seal it | Lock it in an artefact. You don't Awaken, yet.
path: Bargain | Talk to the mind inside, and find out what it wants.
join: The Works collapse again | Get out before the rock closes.
end: Awakened | You break the human limit, and the price stays with you.
end: Scarred | You survive unchanged, except for what the crystal remembers of you.
```

Each way out carries the four parts of every transformation: the **capacity** you brought with you,
the crystal as the **catalyst**, surviving the rupture as the **deed**, and a **price** that depends
on how you did it:

| If you | You become | The price |
|---|---|---|
| Absorbed it | Awakened, with a mind that sees patterns everywhere | You sometimes remember things that happened to the crystal |
| Redirected it | Awakened through a machine you must keep wearing | The machine needs mending, and only you can mend it |
| Bound it to a companion | Awakened together, sharing senses | When your companion is hurt, so are you |
| Shared it | Both of you partly Awakened | Your ally can feel where you are, always |
| Sealed it | Not Awakened, yet; you carry the artefact | Everyone who knows what it is wants it |
| Bargained | Awakened by the mind's own choice | It asks for one thing in return, later |

And the buyer is waiting outside.
