**Joss Harrow**, a Brass fighter loved by every balcony in Arena City, lost a bout last night to a
nobody and broke his arm in three places. The crowd says he was drunk. Dagna Holt says he was not.
The Spring Tournament goes on, Joss's place in it is empty, and Dagna wants someone in it who will
watch as well as fight.

## The job

[Dagna Holt](../people/dagna-holt.md) offers you two ways in: take Joss's place in the
tournament as an unranked Sand fighter, or work the Undercroft as a healer's helper, a runner or a
second, where you can see everyone and nobody watches you. Either way, find out what happened to
Joss before it happens to someone else.

The tournament runs over three nights: the open bouts, the paired bouts and the final, on the
moving floor of the Grand Exchange.

## Ways through

Every skill gets you somewhere:

- **Fight**: win your bouts and get close to the fighters who are winning too easily.
- **Heal**: Joss's arm tells you more than he does. Mending it shows that the break came from a
  weakened bone, not a blow.
- **Bet**: follow the money. Somebody bet on Joss to lose, and it wasn't one of the usual books.
- **Intimidate**: Joss's opponent is terrified, and not of you.
- **Sabotage**: you can do to someone else what was done to Joss, if you work out how.

## What happened

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the job below.

Joss's bones were weakened by a **slow blight**: a dark Malefice curse, cast on his wrapping cloth
days before the bout, which rots bone from the inside and leaves no wound. It was paid for by
[Orlo Vance](../people/orlo-vance.md)'s chief bookmaker, who had taken a fortune in bets
on the underdog from a buyer in [Orravell](../cities/orravell.md). The buyer was not interested in
the money. He was testing whether a curse could pass the Circuit's healers unnoticed, so that his
army could use it on the battlefield.

```flow
start: Take Joss's place, or work the Undercroft | Three nights to find out.
path: The bones | Heal Joss and find the curse.
path: The money | Follow the bets to the bookmaker.
path: The opponent | Get the nobody to talk.
join: The final | Someone has cursed the champion's wraps.
end: Stop the bout | Expose the curse in front of the whole city.
end: Win clean | Fight the final with your own wraps, and win anyway.
end: Sell it | The buyer pays well for what you have learned.
```

## What it leads to

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild questline below.

You become a **Bronze** fighter of the Brass Circuit if you fought, or its trusted hand if you
didn't. The four leaders each take something different from it:

| If you | You gain |
|---|---|
| Exposed the curse in public | Dagna's trust, and a city that watches the healers more closely |
| Won the final anyway | Orlo's interest, and a crowd that knows your name |
| Traced the buyer to Orravell | Captain Askew's attention: she knows him |
| Settled it in the canal pits | Mags Rennick's respect, and an open invitation |
