[Tessara](../cities/tessara.md) runs on its open mosaics: patterns anyone can read, copy and
repair. Its central quest asks whether the most advanced city in the world should stay that way.

## The problem

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Tessara's main quest below.

Tessara's public network is nearly full. One of the Closed Kilns has made black-glazed plates
powerful enough to modernise the whole city, but only that house can make, inspect or repair them.
It also offers **[Ison](../people/ison.md)**, the machine mind that governs Tessara, a far more capable computing core
whose workings are sealed: accepting it would give a private invention house a hand on the
machinery that runs the city.

## Your choices

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Tessara's main quest below.

- **Protect the open system**, and accept that progress will be slower.
- **Back the Closed Kiln**: a city far ahead of the world, and dependent on one house.
- **Steal and publish the pattern**, breaking the monopoly and Tessara's good name abroad.
- **Help the Menders** build an imperfect open copy.
- **Split the city** into two networks, public and private, that cannot talk to each other.

Whatever you choose changes how the city looks: colourful public mosaics, black sealed bands or
improvised patchwork spread across its walls.

## Getting in

Hacking in Tessara is physical. A thief can slip into a maintenance gallery and swap three tiles
to divert a shipment; an artificer can build new pattern chains; a mage can trace or corrupt the
signals running through a wall. Cutting one route might open a vault, but it might also shut
down a clinic nearby or flood a courtyard of homes.
