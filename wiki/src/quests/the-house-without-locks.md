A wealthy broker hires [the Tangle](../factions/thieves-guild.md) to steal his own insured
collection. It sounds like the easiest job in the city: the house is left open on purpose and the
owner wants you there. That is exactly what should worry you.

## The job

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the job below.

The broker plans to claim the insurance. Several of the valuables on his list are fakes, and the
evidence in the house has been staged so that the blame falls on one of his servants. Somewhere
in the house he keeps his genuine collection, which is not on any list.

## What you can do

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the job below.

- **Do the burglary** exactly as paid for.
- **Change the evidence**, so it points somewhere else or nowhere.
- **Protect the servant** who was set up to take the blame.
- **Steal the real collection** the broker kept hidden.
- **Expose the fraud.**
- **Keep the proof** and use it as leverage.

## What it leads to

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild questline below.

What you do here gives you a different advantage in the guild's finale, **Steal the City Before
Dawn**:

| If you | You gain |
|---|---|
| Protected the servant | An insider at the insurer |
| Completed the fraud | An invitation into the broker's circle |
| Kept the forged claim | Evidence to blackmail him with |
| Took the hidden collection | Possibly an older ownership document |
| Exposed everything | Public support, and wary wealthy clients |
