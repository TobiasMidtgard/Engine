The Valley's road runs down out of the mountains and joins **the Bell Road**, the northern of the
Five Roads, a day's walk south of the village. From there it is four more days to the Gilded
Ascent, with a waystation at the end of each. You can walk it, ride it, or join a caravan.

## The road

This is the quest that teaches you to [travel](../../play/travel.md): reading notice boards, buying
food, sleeping at waystations, paying tolls, and dealing with what lives along the road.

| Stop | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Halfbell**, the junction | A caravanserai where the Valley road meets the Bell Road. A Chartered Road caravan, the **Dunmore & Daughters**, leaves at first bell. |
| **Cold Ford** | A ferry across a fast river, and a ferryman who charges what he thinks you can pay |
| **The Burnt Mill** | A ruined mill where bandits wait for carts that stop to water their horses |
| **Wolfsgate waystation** | Wolves in the woods around it at night; a hunter who wants help |
| **The First Toll Ring** | The outermost of the Gilded Ascent's rings, and the first place anyone asks for your seal |

## The caravan

The caravan master of the Dunmore & Daughters, **[Imke Dunmore](../../people/imke-dunmore.md)**, needs a guard because one of hers
quit at Halfbell. Kit's outrider coat gets you the job if you can answer three questions about the
Chartered Road's charter. If you can't, a Persuasion check, a few marks or a demonstration on the
practice post will do.

Travelling with the caravan is safer and slower. You eat with the drivers, hear the road's gossip
(the chains of Sky City are slack, the Spring in Orath is weak, a Restriking is coming), and learn
that nobody on the caravan has ever seen a Seal Warden north of the Toll Rings before.

## The case

Kit's case is locked with a Chartered Road lock (Lockcraft, Apprentice). You were told not to open
it. If you do:

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the case below.

Inside, wrapped in oilcloth, is a single page of something that isn't paper: a thin leaf of black
stone, flexible as leather, cold as a well. It is covered in tiny lines of a script nobody on the
road can read, and at the bottom, in fresh ink, someone has written a name you can: **the First
Spring, Orath**. There is also a letter from Kit to Ivo Tarrant, which says only: "From the bottom
of the Registry. It's real. They've started."

## On your heels

The two Seal Wardens from the tavern are on the road behind you. They are polite, patient and very
good at asking questions at waystations. At the Burnt Mill or at Wolfsgate, they catch up.

```flow
start: The wardens catch up | At a waystation, in the rain.
path: Fight | Two trained wardens. At level 3, this is a bad idea.
path: Hide in the caravan | Imke Dunmore decides whether to lie for you.
path: Lose them | Leave the road; cross the woods to Cold Ford.
path: Let the bandits have them | Lead the wardens into the Burnt Mill's ambush.
join: You reach the Toll Rings | With the case, and without a seal.
end: Go on to the Gilded Ascent | See [Unsealed](unsealed.md).
```

The wardens are not trying to kill you. They want the case, and they want to know who you are. When
they can't find you in any register, they are more worried than when they started.

## Rewards

- Caravan pay: 1 mark a day, if you guarded the Dunmore & Daughters.
- Imke Dunmore's goodwill: a caravan that will carry you on the Bell Road again for nothing.
- The **Unseen on the Road** observation: Seal Wardens describe you as "nobody we know".
