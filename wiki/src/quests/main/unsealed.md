[The Gilded Ascent](../../cities/gilded-ascent.md) lets anyone into the Commons for one chit, and
nobody onto the tiers above without the right seal. Ivo Tarrant's office is in the Charter House
on the **Ledger Floor**, four tiers up, behind gates that check for a Gold Seal. You have no seal
at all.

## Getting up

Every tier gate is watched by Seal Wardens who check your seal against the Registry. There are many
ways past them, and the city is built to teach you them:

| Way up | How | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| **Buy a Clay Seal** | Register at a gate, pay a chit, and you are a number. You can trade on the Commons, and climb no further. | None |
| **Borrow someone's seal** | A porter on the Weighhouse will lend his Copper Seal for a day, for a price, and get unsealed if you are caught | Persuasion |
| **Forge one** | Sealwrights' Row has people who can. It is the worst crime in the city. | Deception, a lot of money |
| **Climb** | The spire's outer terraces are steep, gilded and patrolled, but the porters' rope-lifts run up the back at night | Climbing, Sneak |
| **Ride the freight** | Hide in a bonded crate on its way up to the Bonded Halls | Sneak, someone to nail the lid |
| **Be invited** | Get a message to Ivo Tarrant that he will want to read, and he will send a White Seal down for you | Persuasion, a runner |

A **White Seal**, recorded three times, lets you climb for a day. If Tarrant sends one, the Registry
writes you down for the first time in your life. Remember that.

## Ivo Tarrant

Ivo Tarrant, Charter Captain of the western road, is a big, tired man who reads Kit's letter twice
and the black page not at all. He knows what it is.

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

> It's a page of the Sixth Register. The oldest book in the Registry, the one they keep below the
> vaults, the one nobody is allowed to read because nobody can. Kit found someone who could. And
> she's been writing in it.

Tarrant tells you what Kit had found:

- The Registry of Seals keeps more than seals. At its bottom, older than the city, is a ledger of
  black stone. Its pages name **wells**: places where the deep currents of the world rise raw.
- In the last two years, a hand in fresh ink has added names to it. The First Spring of Orath. The
  Deepworks of Sky City. Others.
- Every well whose name was written in has started to fail.
- The hand belongs to **[Gisela Quell](../../people/gisela-quell.md)**, Registrar of Seals.

Tarrant can't prove it, can't accuse a Registrar without proof, and can't read the black script.
The page names the First Spring; whoever wrote it there wants it back. He asks you to go to Orath,
where people are trying to keep the Spring alive, and find out what happens when the page is
brought near the well.

## The Registrar

On your way out, a woman in grey and silver holds the gate for you. She is small, neat and kind,
and asks whether you are new to the city. Her seal is glass. Her name is Gisela Quell, and she
notices that you have no number.

## Rewards

- Ivo Tarrant's trust, and a letter of introduction to the Chartered Road (see
  [Three Accounts of One Caravan](../three-accounts-of-one-caravan.md)).
- 20 marks for Kit's delivery fee, which Tarrant insists on paying you.
- If you never took a seal: **the Registry still has no record of you**. Gisela Quell has started a
  page of her own about that.
