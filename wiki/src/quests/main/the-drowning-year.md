[Keth Veyra](../../cities/keth-veyra.md) does not let outsiders in, and you are about as outside as
a person can be. **[Sere](../../people/sere.md)**, the pactkeeper of the [Keepers' Road](../../factions/keepers-road.md),
gets you through the gate anyway, because the animals have started refusing work and Sere believes
they are right.

## The animals know

The story begins when the animals start refusing familiar work. Bargebacks avoid a channel. Gate
Oxen panic in one machinery hall of the Gateworks. A nesting Brinewing moves her eggs away from a
wall. Officials blame foreign sabotage, but the animals are feeling the vibrations of a failing gate
mechanism, and powerful keepers have hidden the maintenance reports.

Investigating means working with the animals as much as the people:

- **Follow the Bargebacks** to the channel they won't enter, and dive it with a Channel Diver.
- **Calm the Gate Oxen** (Animal handling) long enough to see what frightens them in the hall.
- **Ask the Brinewing**, if you have bonded with one (see [A Door Nobody Locked](a-door-nobody-locked.md)),
  or watch where she moved her eggs.
- **Find the hidden reports** in the keeper families' records, or the Old Harness House, where
  retired harnesses keep the stories of generations.

## The Night of Cut Harnesses

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

Eighty-three years ago, visiting merchants tried to steal Brinewing eggs. Their escape started a
fire, panicked the working animals and caused a failed emergency release that drowned a lower
neighbourhood. That is the story Keth Veyra tells. The Old Harness House tells the rest:

- The merchants were not after eggs. They went down into the Gateworks, below the lowest sluice,
  to **black stone** the old kingdom's engineers found when they began the dam and built over
  rather than break.
- They carried a page of black stone, and they tried to write a door shut.
- The door was over a well of raw **Time**. It jammed half open. A whole season's monsoon fell on
  the mountains in one night, and the "failed release" was the dam trying to survive it.
- The flood ran down the great river all the way to the south-west sea. In
  [Vantress](../../cities/vantress/index.md), the council cut a district loose to save the rest:
  that district is [Ward Null](../../cities/vantress/ward-null.md). The continent remembers it as
  **the Drowning Year**.
- The keeper families buried the truth, because a door that can jam once can jam again, and they
  could not admit they didn't know how to fix it.

The gate mechanism failing now is the same door. The Registry has sent someone to try again, and
this time they have the right page.

## Under the dam

Below the Gateworks, the black stone runs down into the riverbed. Time behaves oddly near the
door: water hangs in the air, a dropped coin falls slowly, and you can see a moment before it
happens. The Registry's team is already there, led by a Registry scribe who can read the Builders'
script, guarded by wardens, and in a hurry, because the monsoon is due.

## What it leads to

| If you | Then |
|---|---|
| Stopped the scribe | You take their page: the Registry entry for Keth Veyra's door, and a copy of the **index** of the Sixth Register, which shows where the original is kept |
| Opened the door | Keth Veyra floods, ready. Time runs a little faster there for years: crops grow in weeks, and the old age quickly. |
| Let it close | The Registry has another well. Keth Veyra's river runs strangely slow. |
| Made the keepers publish the reports | Sere becomes the voice of the city's Open Ledger faction, and Keth Veyra opens a gate to outsiders for the first time in 83 years |

The scribe's index says the Sixth Register lies in the Counting Deep beneath the Gilded Ascent,
below the Fifth Reserve's vaults. The only people who have ever robbed the Fifth Reserve are in
Vantress.
