The **Nine Shut Doors** are a side-cut of the quarry of [Veyrhold](../../cities/veyrhold.md), sealed
after a disaster that the city prefers not to discuss. The quarry landlords now want to dig beneath
the oldest workshops beside it. They promise jobs and cheap protective stone, and they have a
backer: an envoy of the Registry, with a Well Charter in one hand and a mining loan from the Fifth
Reserve in the other.

## The people

Everyone in this quarter has a reason to want something different:

| Person | Wants |
|---|---|
| **[Brannagh Fisk](../../people/brannagh-fisk.md)**, rescue forewoman of the lift workers | The surveys published. The lift workers have found fresh cracks. |
| **Master Corvane**, a workshop owner | To trust the new shielding and keep his contracts |
| **The Kiln Street cooperative**, apprentices | Not to be closed for months; they won't survive it |
| **Examiner [Sabra Quint](../../people/sabra-quint.md)** of the Spillward | To know why the original safety report was altered |
| **The landlords' agent** | The dig, on time, on budget |

Help Brannagh get the surveys, Sabra find the altered report, or the cooperative find somewhere to
work; each one opens a way into the Nine Shut Doors.

## Behind the doors

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Veyrhold's main quest below.

Behind the Nine Shut Doors, a survivor of the old disaster, **[Old Tamm](../../people/old-tamm.md)**, has secretly tended a
damaged **containment vessel** for forty years. Someone has paid the rent on his workshop all that
time. Moving the vessel could be dangerous; abandoning it would be worse. The new digging will
expose it, and the people who covered up the original accident.

What Old Tamm knows, if you earn it:

- Forty years ago, a workshop dug too deep and broke into **black stone**. Behind it was a door, and
  behind the door, a well of raw **Energy**: the reason the quarry's stone drinks magic.
- The door cracked. What came out killed the ninth workshop's whole crew in a second. Tamm caught
  a shard of it in the vessel and has been feeding it hushstone ever since, so it stays asleep.
- The safety report was altered by a young Registry clerk sent to "record the damage". Her name in
  the report is **G. Quell**.

The landlords' dig is aimed at the door. The Registry needs to reach it to write it in.

## The vessel

The containment vessel is a battered copper sphere the size of a barrel, humming, warm, and full of
light that hurts to look at. What you do with it matters for the rest of the story:

```flow
start: The digging is coming | And the vessel is in its path.
path: Help Brannagh | Publish the surveys; the dig is halted.
path: Help Sabra | The altered report, with Quell's initial on it.
path: Help Tamm | Keep the vessel asleep, and moved.
join: The door beneath the quarry | Cracked, and waiting to be written in.
end: Seal the crack | With Tamm's vessel and every scrap of hushstone in the quarry. The door holds.
end: Let the Registry dig | Veyrhold signs the Charter; the quarry's magic weakens; jobs for a year.
end: Take the shard | The vessel goes with you. Raw Energy, asleep, in a copper ball.
```

## What it leads to

| If you | Then |
|---|---|
| Sealed the crack | Veyrhold refuses the Charter. Old Tamm can stop. Brannagh owes you. |
| Let the Registry dig | Veyrhold signs. The quarry's hushstone stops drinking; the Spillward has its worst year. |
| Took the shard | The vessel is one of the roads to [Awakening](a-door-nobody-locked.md) later, if you dare. |
| Got Sabra the report | The first document with Quell's own hand on a lie. Sabra takes a copy to the Spillward. |

Old Tamm tells you one more thing: the young clerk who altered his report came back last year, much
older, and asked him whether the vessel was still alive. She seemed glad.
