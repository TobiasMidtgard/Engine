Every seventh year the Gilded Ascent holds a **Restriking**: every Copper and Silver Seal is weighed
again, and its holder must qualify once more. This year the Registrar of Seals, **[Gisela Quell](../../people/gisela-quell.md)**,
has called it early, and called it something new: **the Great Restriking**. Every city that has
signed a Well Charter will receive seals for all its people, struck in one night, in the Five Quiet
Days at the end of the year. Every city that hasn't is invited to.

## The sitting

Ivo Tarrant means to put the evidence in front of the **Court of Seals** at its next sitting at the
**Lantern Table**, on the Summit. You need to get him there with it, and get yourself in:

- **The evidence**: whatever you have from Orath (the black page, the water-sellers' ledger),
  Sky City (the surveyors' notebook) and Rillvault (the rubbing of the Listening Room).
- **Allies**: Tarrant needs three Glass Seal holders to let him speak. Win them over, buy them,
  or find out what they are afraid of.
- **Getting in**: the Summit is for Glass Seals only. A White Seal won't do. You will need a
  disguise, a servant's job, a borrowed seal or a very good climb.

## The Lantern Table

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

Tarrant speaks well. Quell listens, thanks him, and answers in a quiet voice that carries to every
corner of the table.

> The Registry has been keeping the wells for four hundred years, Captain. We have only ever written
> down what is so. The wells are failing because they are old, and because nobody has been keeping
> them as they should be kept. The Great Restriking will keep them. Every city that signs will drink.
> Every city that does not, I am afraid, will learn what its well is worth.

Then she asks why the Captain's witness has no seal, and why no register in the city has ever heard
of them. Within the hour:

- The Court **votes against hearing** Tarrant's evidence, eleven chairs to four.
- Tarrant is **suspended** from his road "pending inquiry".
- **You are added to the Unsealed List**, as a person no seal may carry. From now on, Chartered
  Road caravans, banks and Toll Rings across the continent are closed to you, and Seal Wardens are
  watching for your description.

## Getting out

**Warden-Captain [Ulf Garrick](../../people/companions/ulf-garrick.md)** is waiting at the Summit gate. He is enormous, courteous, and does
not draw his sword, because he doesn't need to: he is one of the [Awakened](../../play/ascension.md#awakening),
and at level 30 you can see the difference in how he moves. He offers you the chance to walk down
under arrest.

```flow
start: The Court turns on Tarrant | And you are on the Unsealed List.
path: Walk down with Garrick | Arrested; break out of the Registry's cells later.
path: Run | Over the Summit's edge, down the gilded roofs.
path: Hide | In the Lantern Table's own service ways.
path: Hesper Lorn | The Sealkeeper voted for Tarrant. She opens a door for you.
join: Out of the Gilded Ascent | Unsealed, in a world that runs on seals.
end: Act II begins | Every city will be offered the Charter. You can be there first.
```

## Hesper Lorn

The Sealkeeper, [Hesper Lorn](../../people/hesper-lorn.md), was one of the four chairs
who voted to hear Tarrant. If you have won her over, she meets you at the bottom of the Summit stair
and gives you something: a list of the cities the Registry's envoys will visit next, in order, and a
warning.

> She asked me for the Sixth Seal last month. To keep it safe, she said. Every Sealkeeper for four
> hundred years has sworn never to use it. I am beginning to wonder what it is for.

## Rewards

- On the **Unsealed List**: a title you didn't want, which makes every Seal Warden on the continent
  your enemy and every smuggler your friend. Arena City's markets open their best stalls to you.
- Hesper Lorn's list: the order in which the Registry will offer its Charters (Veyrhold, the Second
  City, Orravell, Tessara).
- Ivo Tarrant, suspended and furious, as an ally who knows every road on the continent.
