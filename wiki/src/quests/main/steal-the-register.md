The Sixth Register lies at the bottom of the **Counting Deep**, beneath the vaults of the **Fifth
Reserve**, beneath the spire of the Gilded Ascent, behind doors no bank has ever opened. Nobody has
robbed the Fifth Reserve in four hundred years. Nobody has ever been to the bottom of the Counting
Deep and come back. [The Tangle](../../factions/thieves-guild.md) is interested.

## Why the Tangle says yes

[Ward Null](../../cities/vantress/ward-null.md) exists because of the Drowning Year. Thousands of
people were cut loose in the flood, declared dead, and had their homes sold, and the record that
declared them dead was copied into the Registry of Seals, where it is still true. When the Tangle
learns that the Registry's own scribe caused the flood (see [The Drowning Year](the-drowning-year.md)),
the guild stops arguing about what it is for. **Tal Oran** begins to plan.

## The way down

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

The heist has three layers, and the plan changes with whom you brought and what you did earlier:

1. **The Fifth Reserve**: the Ledger Floor's great bank, with Seal Wardens, hushstone vaults, and
   doors that read seals. Sella's clerk, a false bonded cargo, or Nilo's route over the gilded roofs
   gets you in.
2. **The Counting Deep**: the banks' rented vaults, level below level, and then the built levels of
   black stone with no seams. Every door here reads the seal of whoever passes and **writes them
   down**. Your crew can't go further without being recorded. You can: you have no seal, and the
   doors don't know you exist.
3. **The Register room**: at the bottom, behind the last door of the Counting Deep, which opens only
   to the **Sixth Seal**. Hesper Lorn wears it. You can ask her for it, have Jori lift it from her
   neck at the Lantern Table, or persuade Garrick to bring it to you. Beyond the door is the Sixth
   Register on a stand of black stone, open, with fresh ink drying on its last page.

## The last page

The last page of the Register has a name written on it that isn't a well. It is **Gisela Quell**.

Under it, in the Builders' script, a line the Voice Beneath Stone would have recognised: whoever
writes in the Keeper's book is written in it. Quell has written herself into the Register as its
keeper. Every lock in it is hers to turn, and every lock in the world passes a little through her
on its way to the god. She is no longer only a Registrar. She is becoming a door.

## Getting out

```flow
start: The bottom of the Counting Deep | The Register, open.
path: Take the Register | A book of stone heavier than a man.
path: Take the last page | Quell's own entry. Light, and dangerous.
path: Read it | If you can read the script, read everything.
join: The alarm | Quell knows. She can feel her book move.
end: Out through the Middle Well | Yara's way. Wet, dark and fast.
end: Out through the front | Garrick opens the gate, if he has chosen you.
end: Out through the roofs | Nilo's way, with a stone book on a rope.
```

## What it leads to

| If you | Then |
|---|---|
| Took the Register | Quell can't write any new wells in. She brings the Great Restriking forward to the next Five Quiet Days. |
| Took her page | She can't turn her locks without it. She comes for it herself. |
| Read it | You learn the name of every well and door, and the way to write and unwrite them |
| Left anyone of the crew behind | They are in the Registry's cells, and on the Unsealed List |

The Tangle wipes its own records of the job by morning. Ward Null throws a party on every barge.
And Rain-Back, who has somehow reached Vantress, tells you that you are close to something new:
an Ascendant is a person who has made a law of their own, and you have been walking through locked
doors for a long time.
