You have come to the Valley looking for work, and the innkeeper has some: a
[house rat](../../creatures/house-rat.md) the size of a cat has been eating the cheese, and nobody
else will go down there. Two marks and a bed. It is the best offer you have had all month.

## The job

The cellar is dark, damp and full of barrels. The rat is fast, bites hard for its size and knows
the room better than you do. This is where you learn the basics:

- **Light**: take the lantern from the hook, or fight in the dark.
- **Movement**: climb onto the barrels, jump the gap, crawl under the shelving.
- **Combat**: aim with your eyes (see [Combat](../../play/combat.md#aiming-at-the-body)), watch your
  stamina, and dodge the bite.
- **Injuries**: the rat bites. Bandage the bite, or bleed.

There are other ways to deal with it. Shut it in the coal store. Lure it into a barrel with the
cheese. Leave the trap door open for the innkeeper's cat. The innkeeper pays the same either way,
though she has opinions about the cheese.

## The courier

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story's opening below.

When you come back up, the supper crowd has been replaced by silence. A young courier in a
Chartered Road outrider's coat is slumped by the fire, with a crossbow bolt in his side that
somebody has snapped off short. His name is **[Kit Fenn](../../people/kit-fenn.md)**. He has ridden from the Bell Road without
stopping, and he will not ride again.

He looks at you, at your hands, at your empty collar where a seal would be, and makes a decision.

> You're nobody, aren't you. No seal. Good. Then they can't see you. Take this to Ivo Tarrant, at the
> Charter House, on the Ledger Floor. Don't let them seal it. Don't let them seal you.

The case is black leather, locked, with a Chartered Road tag. Kit dies before the innkeeper's
husband finishes running for the healer.

## The night after

Two riders in grey coats arrive at the tavern before dawn, asking politely after a courier and a
case. They wear brass discs on their coats, one for each tier of the Gilded Ascent they may enter:
**Seal Wardens**, a long way from home. What you do now sets the tone of everything that follows:

```flow
start: Grey coats at the door | They want the courier's case.
path: Hide | In the cellar you know better than they do.
path: Talk | Lie, or tell them the truth and keep the case.
path: Run | Out of the window and onto the Bell Road before dawn.
path: Hand it over | They thank you and leave. One of them looks back.
join: The case is still yours, or it isn't | Kit's name was in it.
end: Take the road | Carry the case to Ivo Tarrant.
end: Follow the wardens | If you gave it up, get it back on the road.
```

If you hand the case over, the story does not end: the wardens are ambushed on the Bell Road the
same day, and the case is lying in a ditch with their horses when you pass. Somebody else wants it
too.

## Rewards

- 2 marks and a bed, if you dealt with the rat.
- **Kit's case**, locked, and his outrider's coat, which gets you a seat on a Chartered Road caravan
  if you don't look too closely like a thief.
- The innkeeper's goodwill: Known in the Valley.
