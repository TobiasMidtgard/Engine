You have reached level 60 and the end of being human. To go further you must
[Awaken](../../play/ascension.md#awakening): find a catalyst, survive a deed, and pay a price.
**[Rain-Back](../../people/rain-back.md)** knows of five roads that touch the story you are already in. Any other road to
Awakening in the world works as well; the story will adapt to it.

## The five roads

| Road | Catalyst | Where it starts | The price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The crystal** | A fragment of raw Mind in a collapsed facility | The Sunken Works east of Veyrhold (see [What the Crystal Remembers](../what-the-crystal-remembers.md)) | It rewrites something in you: a memory, a fear, a face |
| **The shard** | The sleeping raw Energy in Old Tamm's copper vessel | Veyrhold, if you took or saved the vessel in [Behind the Nine Shut Doors](behind-the-nine-shut-doors.md) | Your body runs hot for ever; you can't sleep in the cold |
| **The Spring** | Raw Life behind Orath's black door | The fifth passage of Orath, if you hold the First Spring's page or the Accord's trust | You heal everything, and scar nothing, and the Deep Choir hears your name in the caverns |
| **The bond** | A Brinewing that chooses you | Keth Veyra's rookeries, with Sere's leave (the Keepers' Road) | Your life is tied to the Brinewing's: what hurts one hurts both |
| **The Artificer's frame** | The copy of a Builder door in the Mnemeion's deepest gallery | Tessara, if Ison trusts you after [The Open Measure](the-open-measure.md) | A pattern written into your bones that Ison can read, and so can anyone who learns how |

## Each road

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the Awakening roads below.

### The crystal

The Sunken Works are a ruin of the Spillward's own making, and the crystal inside was made by
people trying to build a well. It ruptures when you take it and starts rewriting everyone nearby.
You can absorb it, redirect it, bind it to an animal, share it with an ally, seal it, or bargain with
the mind inside. Absorbing it Awakens you; the other choices Awaken you more slowly, or someone else
instead.

### The shard

Old Tamm's shard has been asleep for forty years. Waking it inside a hushstone chamber, and holding
it while it burns, is a deed most people don't survive. Tamm will help, if he is alive. The
Awakening this road gives is the fastest and the least stable: your first year is full of surges
you don't control (levels 61 to 65 are worse).

### The Spring

Behind Orath's black door, in a cave of glowing water, raw Life rises. Standing in it for one full
night is the deed. You come out unable to be poisoned, quick to heal and slow to age; the Deep Choir
hear your name whispered in their chambers for the rest of your life, and come to ask what you are.
If Orath signed the Well Charter, this door belongs to the Registry, and you will have to break in.

### The bond

A Brinewing chooses its partner; you can't choose it. The Keepers' Road's pactkeeper, **[Sere](../../people/sere.md)**, can
bring you to the rookeries if you have their trust, and then you wait, and work, and are looked at,
for weeks. The deed is the first flight. This road is the one Keth Veyra's story needs most, and it
makes [The Drowning Year](the-drowning-year.md) much easier.

### The Artificer's frame

The Last Artificer built a copy of a Builder door so that someone could understand one. Stepping
through it, with Ison holding the pattern steady, rewrites you as something that can be mended:
your body learns its own pattern. This road suits engineers and enchanters, and gives you the
Builders' script: you can read the Sixth Register.

## Garrick again

Whichever road you take, Ulf Garrick arrives at the end of it. Awakened, you can finally face him on
equal terms. You can kill him, beat him and let him go, or talk to him: he Awakened by a road the
Registry paid for, and he has started to wonder what the price was.


## Rewards

- **Awakening**: level 61, a supernatural ability shaped by your road, and the power to grow past
  the human limit (see [Awakening and Ascension](../../play/ascension.md)).
- A **title** for your road (see [Titles](../../play/titles.md)).
- If Garrick lives and you talked: the Registry's warden-captain, wondering whose side he is on.
