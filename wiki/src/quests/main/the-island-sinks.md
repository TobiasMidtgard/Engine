[Sky City](../../cities/sky-city.md) has floated above the clouds beside the Gilded Ascent for four
centuries, held down by three great chains that have always been taut. This month they are not. The
island is sinking an inch a day, and the city has noticed: the cloud nets hang lower, the lower
caverns are damp, and the airships of the Hanging Market dock at a new level every morning.

## Getting up

Sky City doesn't let just anyone ride the lifts of the Golden chain. You can get up by:

- **A letter from Ivo Tarrant** to the Golden Anchor's trade office, which gets you a seat on the
  lift as a Chartered Road factor.
- **Working your way up** as a servant, a porter or a guard for an estate (easy if your origin is
  a dismissed servant of Sky City).
- **Stowing away** in a cargo basket on the lift, or in an airship's ballast.
- **Gliding in** from the Gilded Ascent's highest terrace, if you are mad and own a folding glider.

## The three anchors

Each chain is held by a faction of the **Council of Three Anchors**, and each has its own theory:

| Anchor | Holder | What they think is happening | What they want |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Iron** | Marshal Joska Kroll | Sabotage: someone has been loosening the winches | Lock all three chains, arrest the Open Anchor |
| **Golden** | Aurenne Lisk | The island is old; it was always going to sink | Sign the Gilded Ascent's **Well Charter**, which promises to "restore buoyancy" |
| **Open** | Captain Faro Wynd | The chains are the problem: the island wants to rise and can't | Cut them |

All three are wrong, and all three are partly right. Each will give you work that serves their
theory, and each piece of work tells you something:

- Kroll sends you to check the winches. They have not been touched.
- Lisk sends you to carry the Charter's terms to the Registry's envoy. The terms include a seal for
  every person on the island, renewed every season by the Registry.
- Wynd sends you to fly a glider under the island at night. From below, the island's oldest rock
  is black, and it has stopped glowing.

## The Deepworks

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

Beneath the Sheltered Rings, the **Deepworks** run down through machinery and ballast chambers into
the island's ancient **buoyancy caverns**. Their walls are seamless black stone: the same stone as
Orath's foundations. At the bottom is a well of raw **Force**, pushing up, which is why the island
floats at all. Over it stands a door of black stone, and the door is nearly shut.

The Deepworks' entry in the Sixth Register has already been written. Without the page, you can't
stop the door. You can learn what it is, though, and what is waiting in the caverns: Registry
surveyors with a warden escort, measuring how long the island has left.

```flow
end: Help Wynd | Cut the Open Anchor's chain. The island tilts, and lives a little longer.
```

## What it leads to

| If you | Then |
|---|---|
| Exposed the Registry's surveyors | The Council refuses the Charter, for now. The island keeps sinking. Kroll owes you. |
| Let the Golden Anchor sign | Sky City joins the seal league. The island rises an inch. The Registry has its second city. |
| Cut the Open Anchor's chain | The island tilts, a district floods, and the Open Anchor becomes your friend for life |

Whatever you choose, one of the surveyors' notebooks says where the door network was first written
down: "the Echo Galleries, Rillvault, where the Voice sang". Ivo Tarrant knows a Root Ledger
steward who can get you into the deep caverns.
