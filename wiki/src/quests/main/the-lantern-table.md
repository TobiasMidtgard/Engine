Gisela Quell has invited you to the Lantern Table for the last sitting before the Great Restriking.
She means it: she would like you to sign. Ivo Tarrant, still suspended and now openly in rebellion,
would like you to bring enough of the continent with you that the Court of Seals has to listen.

## Gathering the cities

Each city remembers what you did there. Visit them again, or send word, and each gives you what its
story allows:

| City | If you helped it refuse | If it signed |
|---|---|---|
| **Orath** | The four Keybearers send the Wardens of the Threshold, and the First Spring's page | Orath's envoy votes with Quell, unhappily |
| **Sky City** | The Iron Anchor sends airships; the Open Anchor sends gliders | The Golden Anchor's fleet guards the Summit for the Registry |
| **Rillvault** | The Confluence Compact stops food exports to every signing city | Rillvault's grain feeds the Registry's wardens |
| **Veyrhold** | Hushstone, the Spillward, and Brannagh Fisk's lift workers | Veyrhold's licensed mages serve the Restriking |
| **The Second City** | The Last Writ honours its black feather; the Pack-Speaker sends the clans | The Eaves' sealed vampires vote with Quell, and some of them are glad |
| **Orravell** | The Still Bough keeps the Companies at home, or turns them | The Crown Companies march on the Gilded Ascent as the Restriking's guarantors |
| **Tessara** | Ison publishes the seal's pattern to every city at once | A sealed Ison advises the Court |
| **Keth Veyra** | Brinewings over the Summit, and Sere | Keth Veyra stays behind its gates |
| **Vantress** | The Tangle, Ward Null and every smuggler on the Salt Spur | The Gilded Ledger lends the Registry money |
| **Arena City** | Its champions, for a price, and everything on the Unsealed List | Arena City sells to both sides |

## The guilds

Your standing with each [guild](../../factions/index.md) matters too. A guild you rose high in sends
its best; a guild you betrayed sends someone to stop you. The Chartered Road splits: Hesper Lorn's
faction of old seal holders, Ivo Tarrant's reformers, Wenna Sallow's free-route captains and Lucan
Prell's black-cargo investors all choose sides.

## The sitting

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

The last sitting of the Court of Seals is a battle fought with words, at a round table of glass under
a lantern of flawless basin glass that throws five colours across the room. Quell argues for order:
locked wells mean no spills, no wars over springs, no sinking islands, and every city drinks as long
as it keeps its seal. She is persuasive because much of it is true.

You argue with what you brought: evidence (Ison's pattern, the Drowning Year, Kit Fenn's testimony,
Old Tamm's report), allies at the table, and your own presence as an Ascendant the Register cannot
hold. Each piece of evidence you bring moves a chair; each city you brought moves several.

```flow
start: The last sitting | Fifteen chairs of glass.
path: Evidence | Every proof you gathered, in front of the Court.
path: Allies | The cities and guilds who came.
path: Threat | An Ascendant at the table, and armies at the gates.
join: The vote | On the Great Restriking.
end: The Court refuses | Quell doesn't need its permission. She goes down to the Register.
end: The Court agrees | The Restriking goes ahead with the Court's blessing.
end: The Court splits | Half the city fights the other half.
```

Whatever the vote, Quell rises, thanks the Court, and goes down. The Five Quiet Days begin at
midnight, and with them the Great Restriking.
