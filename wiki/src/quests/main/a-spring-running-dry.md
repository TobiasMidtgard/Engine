You reach [Orath](../../cities/orath.md) three days before **the Unbarred Night**, when every door in
the city stands open and a hundred thousand pilgrims come to drink from the First Spring. The
Spring has started to weaken. The four faiths have not announced it. Somebody is already selling
"miraculous water" from barrels in the Fifth Quarter.

## The water engineer

**Ilyan Dareth**, a water engineer of the Fifth Seat, has found an abandoned channel beneath the
**Underchoir**, the Deep Choir's echoing chambers below the Spring. Later generations built burial
galleries inside its old reservoir, and the Deep Choir will not let anyone dig through its dead.
**Sister Avara**, a physician of the Covenant of Ash, wants to prepare the city for a shortage at
once; several officials fear that announcing it will start hoarding before the pilgrims arrive.

Your first job is small: escort a maintenance crew through a disputed burial gallery. By the time you
come back, a custodian of the Deep Choir has withdrawn permission, one of the crew has vanished,
and the barrel-sellers have doubled their prices.

## What you can do

- **Find the missing worker**: she followed the old channel further than she meant to, and found a
  door.
- **Find who is selling the water**, and where they are getting it. It is not from the Spring.
- **Win the Deep Choir's leave** to dig through the galleries: recite the right names, find a lost
  family's record, or persuade a custodian whose own family is buried there.
- **Prepare the city**: help Sister Avara set up water stores without starting a panic, or let the
  panic start and manage it.
- **Bring the black page near the water**, as Ivo Tarrant asked.

## The fifth passage

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Orath's main quest below.

Beyond the blocked channel lies **the fifth passage**, an archive older than all four faiths,
recording how earlier communities kept the Spring alive together. It cannot say whether anyone met
a god here. It can show who has altered the waterworks, hidden failures, or claimed ownership they
never had, and the newest entry is in fresh ink.

At the passage's end stands a **door of black stone**, as seamless as the Hollow Crown's
foundations, set into the rock over the place where the Spring rises. It is closing, a hair's width
a day. When you bring the black page near it, the script on both lights up, and the door stops.

The page is the First Spring's entry from the **Sixth Register**: whoever holds it holds the door.
The missing worker is on the far side of it, alive and terrified, in a cave full of water that
glows.

## The envoy

On the Unbarred Night itself, an envoy of the Gilded Ascent's Registry arrives with an offer for
the Accord of Four Keys and the Fifth Seat: a **Well Charter**. Join the seal league, register every
citizen of Orath under a seal, and the Registry "will restore the Spring". The envoy does not say
how it could. The four Keybearers argue all night.

```flow
start: The Spring is failing | Three days to the Unbarred Night.
path: Ilyan's channel | Through the burial galleries to the fifth passage.
path: The water-sellers | Follow the barrels to the people behind them.
path: Sister Avara's stores | Keep the city calm and watered.
join: The black door | The page stops it closing.
end: Keep the page | The door holds while you carry it. Orath's Spring is in your pocket.
end: Leave the page in the door | The Spring recovers. Whoever comes for the page will come here.
end: Give it to the Accord | The four Keybearers hold it together, and argue about it for ever.
```

## What it leads to

| If you | Then |
|---|---|
| Kept the page | The Registry's agents follow you, not Orath. Orath's Spring stays steady while the page is safe. |
| Left the page in the door | The Spring flows again; Seal Wardens visit Orath within a month |
| Gave it to the Accord | Amsel Dray's trust; the Accord refuses the Well Charter |
| Exposed the water-sellers | They were buying water from a Registry clerk's warehouse: the first proof with Quell's office on it |
| Let the panic happen | Orath signs the Well Charter at once, out of fear |

Wherever the page ends up, Ilyan Dareth's survey shows one more thing: the Hollow Crown's
foundations, the Counting Deep beneath the Gilded Ascent and the rock under Sky City are the **same
black stone**. Ivo Tarrant sends word: Sky City's chains have dropped another inch.
