**The Unsealed** is the main story. It begins with a rat in a tavern cellar and ends at the top of
the Gilded Ascent, with the keys to every well in the world within reach of your hand. In between,
it takes you to almost every great city, through both of the transformations that make a person
more than human, and to the edge of the question of whether a god can die.

You are not the chosen one. Nobody chose you. That is the point: in a world where everyone who
matters carries a seal, and every seal is written in one ledger, you are the one person the ledger
has never seen.

## The story so far

The wells of the world are failing. Orath's First Spring has begun to weaken just before the
Unbarred Night; Sky City's chains, which have been taut for four hundred years, have started to
slacken; Rillvault's deepest springs run thinner each month. Every city blames something different:
weather, sabotage, sin, the Spillward. And in the Gilded Ascent, the Registry of Seals has
announced that this year's Restriking will be unlike any before it.

## How it can end

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the whole main story below.

The First Builders, long before any of the present cities, served the
[God of Locked Doors](../../lore/gods.md#the-god-of-locked-doors). Wherever a
[Fundament](../../lore/fundaments.md) rose raw into the world, they built a door of black stone over
it, and they kept the keys in one ledger: the **Sixth Register**, the oldest book in the Registry of
Seals. Gisela Quell found it. She learned that a well written into the Register is a well the
Registrar holds, and she has been writing them in, one by one.

Closed wells mean scarce magic, failing springs and sinking islands, and a Registry that can open
them again for any city that signs its **Well Charter**: join the seal league, put every citizen
under a seal, and your well will flow. Every seal is a small lock, and every lock feeds the god. At
the **Great Restriking**, Quell means to seal the whole continent at once, and stand in the god's
door when it opens.

At the end you can:

| Ending | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Break every door** | Every well opens at once and for ever. Magic floods the world: spills everywhere, Sky City rises, the Spring overflows, and the Spillward is overwhelmed. The god is weaker than it has been in a thousand years. |
| **Give each city its key** | Each well's key goes to the people who live above it. The continent becomes a patchwork of wells kept by their own cities, and nobody owns them all. |
| **Keep the keys** | You hold every well. Every city needs you. You may not need a god any more; you may be on your way to becoming one. |
| **Seal the world** | You let the Restriking finish, or finish it yourself. Order, peace and a locked world, with a god at the bottom of it that is stronger than it has ever been. |

Whatever you choose, the god notices. The way to its temple, and to the question of whether it can
die, is [The Temple of Locked Doors](../the-temple-of-locked-doors.md).
