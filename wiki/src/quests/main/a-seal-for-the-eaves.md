You come to [the Second City](../../cities/second-city.md) because Hesper Lorn's list says the
Registry's envoy is coming here next. You find that the Registry got here first. A **Red Writ** with
your description on it (no name: the buyer didn't have one) hangs in the bell tower of the
[Last Writ](../../factions/last-writ.md), verified and paid. In thirty nights, anyone in the Eaves may
kill you, lawfully.

## The offer to the Eaves

The Registry's envoy is offering the **Night Council** something the Eaves have never had: seals.
Under a Well Charter, every vampire, werebeast and necromancer of the Eaves would carry a seal of
the Gilded Ascent, which would make the Eaves a lawful city in the eyes of every bank, court and
caravan on the continent. The Boulevard would be furious. The Sunset Compact would not survive it.

The five seats of the Night Council each see it differently:

| Seat | What they want |
|---|---|
| **The Old Blood** | Respect, after centuries of hiding. Tempted. |
| **The Pack-Speaker** | Nothing that puts a seal on a werebeast. Against. |
| **The Registrar of the Dead** | To know what the Registry wants the dead for. Curious. |
| **The Warden of Wards** | To know what kind of lock a seal really is. Suspicious. |
| **The Broker** | The deal. The Broker brought it here. |

Meanwhile drained bodies keep turning up on the Boulevard, and the Magistracy has begun hiring
climbers. Somebody wants the Compact to break before the Council votes.

## Lifting the writ

A verified Red Writ can't be refused, but it can be **voided**: by the buyer withdrawing it, by
proving it was bought with false papers, or by the Last Word deciding the writ itself was a
crime. You need to know who bought it. The Last Writ's registrar,
[Ophel Strand](../../factions/last-writ.md#people), won't tell you. Somebody else might:

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

- **The Pale Archive** questions the dead. Kit Fenn was buried in the Valley. Bring his body to the
  Registrar of the Dead (dig him up, buy him from the Valley's sexton, or persuade the Archive to
  send its own collectors) and Kit will testify about the night he died.
- Kit's testimony: the bolt was fired by a hired crossbowman on the Bell Road, under a Red Writ
  bought in the Red Exchange **by the Broker**, on behalf of a client with a glass seal.
- The same Broker bought the writ on you, and has been paying for the drained bodies on the
  Boulevard, to frighten the Eaves into needing a seal.

## The vote

```flow
start: A writ on your name | Thirty nights to void it.
path: Raise the dead | Kit Fenn testifies in the Pale Archive.
path: Work the Last Writ | Edrin Vasse or Sabeth Rook, for a price.
path: Find the drainer | Catch whoever is leaving bodies on the Boulevard.
join: The Night Council votes | On the Charter, and on the Broker.
end: Expose the Broker | The writ is void; the Council refuses the Charter; the Broker's seat is empty.
end: Let the Eaves take the seal | The Night Council signs. The Compact breaks. The writ stands.
end: Make a deal | The Broker withdraws the writ, and owes you; the vote goes the Broker's way.
```

## What it leads to

| If you | Then |
|---|---|
| Exposed the Broker | The Night Council refuses the Charter. The Last Word voids your writ and sends you a gift: a single black feather, the Last Writ's mark of a debt it owes. |
| Let the Eaves sign | The Sunset Compact breaks. Street fighting on the Boulevard for a month. The Eaves trade on the Five Roads for the first time, and every vampire in them carries a seal. |
| Made a deal with the Broker | Your writ is gone and the Broker is your friend, which is worse than being their enemy |

Either way, the Registrar of the Dead has one more thing to tell you: the Registry asked the Pale
Archive, last year, how to register the dead. The Archive said it couldn't be done. The Registry
said it would find a way.
