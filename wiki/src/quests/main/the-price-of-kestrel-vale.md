[Orravell](../../cities/orravell.md) has nothing the Registry needs: no well, no door of black stone,
nothing under its great tree but roots. What it has is an army. And the Registry's list says the
envoy came here before going to Tessara.

## The war in the valley

Kestrel Vale, a farming region near Orravell, hired Orravell's Crown Companies for a long war and
now owes them heavily. An armistice is close. In secret, the Crown has already decided to keep the
valley (see [Operation Second Spring](../operation-second-spring.md)). What nobody in Kestrel Vale
knows is who is paying for the occupation.

Two factions of the Crown Ascendant argue about it:

- **The Long Shade** wants the valley, and every valley after it.
- **The Still Bough** wants profitable contracts and raids, not costly occupations.

Both are being courted by the Registry's envoy, a soft-spoken banker of the **Fifth Reserve**.

## Following the money

You can get into Orravell's government as a recruit of the Crown Companies (easy if you are a
washed-out recruit by origin: you know the canopy test), as a glider crew at Hushwing Station under
**Captain [Sera Noll](../../people/sera-noll.md)**, as a foreign trader, or over the branches at night.

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

What you find in the Council of Stewards' records:

- A **loan from the Fifth Reserve** large enough to pay for the occupation of Kestrel Vale for ten
  years, at no interest at all.
- Its only condition: Orravell signs a **Well Charter**, and its Crown Companies become **the
  Restriking's guarantors**. If any city that has signed a Charter refuses its seals, the Companies
  march.
- A list of cities the Registry expects to "require guarantee": Rillvault, Tessara, Keth Veyra.
- A second, smaller contract: the Fifth Reserve will buy every debt Kestrel Vale owes, so the
  valley will owe the Gilded Ascent, not Orravell. The Long Shade hasn't noticed this clause.

## Choices

```flow
start: The armistice is close | And the Fifth Reserve is paying.
path: The Long Shade | Show them the debt clause. They will not like being bought.
path: The Still Bough | Help them win the argument: no occupation, no Charter.
path: Sera Noll | Bring her crews home before the operation begins.
path: Kestrel Vale | Warn the valley's council what is coming.
join: Orravell decides | War, peace, or a deal with the bank.
end: Break the loan | Orravell refuses the Charter. Kestrel Vale is still in danger, from Orravell alone.
end: Let the loan stand | Orravell signs. The Crown Companies become the Registry's army.
end: Turn the army | The Still Bough takes the loan's gold and refuses its terms. The Fifth Reserve has a war of its own.
```

## What it leads to

| If you | Then |
|---|---|
| Broke the loan | Cael Ordan publicly refuses the Charter, privately wants the gold back. Kestrel Vale's fate is decided in Operation Second Spring. |
| Let it stand | Orravell's Companies march with the Restriking. In Act III, they stand at the Gilded Ascent's gates. |
| Turned the army | Orravell owes the Fifth Reserve a fortune it doesn't mean to pay. Quell has an enemy with an army. |
| Brought Sera Noll's crews home | Sera and her gliders will come when you call, once. |
| Warned Kestrel Vale | The valley prepares. Fewer people on the transfer lists. |

The Fifth Reserve's banker leaves Orravell for Tessara the night you finish. Tessara is the one city
whose law says that any machine with power over a person must show how it works, and Ison has
asked to see the Registry's.
