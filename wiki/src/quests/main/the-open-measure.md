[Tessara](../../cities/tessara.md)'s defining law is that any machine with power over a person must
reveal its pattern. When the Registry's envoy offered Tessara a Well Charter, **Ison**, the machine
mind that governs the city, did what the law requires: it asked to see how the Registry works. The
Registry declined, politely. Then a Fifth Reserve banker arrived with an offer for one of the Closed
Kilns, and a sealed computing core for Ison.

## Ison's request

Ison contacts you through a public terminal in the Measure Forum. It speaks plainly and shows its
working:

> The Registry of Seals keeps a record of people. A record with power over a person is a machine
> under our charter. I have asked to inspect it. I was refused. You are the only person I have found
> who is not in it. I would like to ask you some questions, and then I would like you to bring me
> something.

Ison wants a **sample of a seal's pattern**: the real inner working of a Registry seal, which no
Tessaran tile-reader has ever been allowed to examine. You can get one by:

- **Taking one** from a Seal Warden in the Fifth Reserve's Tessara office (Pickpocket, or worse).
- **Asking Ivo Tarrant** for his own Gold Seal, which he will give you if he trusts you completely.
- **Buying one** in the Hollow Market of the Second City, if you went there and left on good terms.

## The black core

At the same time, Tessara's own crisis comes to a head: the public network is nearly full, and a
Closed Kiln offers black-glazed plates and a sealed core that would make Ison far more capable,
and give a private house a hand on the machinery of the city (see
[The Black Pattern](../the-black-pattern.md)). The core the Kiln is offering came from the Fifth
Reserve.

## What the seal is

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

When Ison reads the seal's pattern, it goes silent for eleven seconds, the longest pause in its
history. Then it shows the Open Measure what it found:

- A seal is not a token. It is a **door**: a tiny lock, keyed to one name in the Registry.
- Every seal opens onto the same place: the Sixth Register, at the bottom of the Counting Deep.
- Every lock passes something through it, very faintly, to whatever is on the other side.
- The black core the Kiln is offering is a seal, too: one with Ison's name on it.

In the Mnemeion's deepest gallery, where the [Last Artificer](../../lore/ascendants.md#the-last-artificer)
walked in and did not come out, Ison shows you something it has never shown a citizen: a frame of
black stone, the Artificer's last work, a **copy of a Builder door** made to be understood. On it, in
the Artificer's hand: "Build it so the next person can mend it. Never build it so that one person
can lock it."

## The vote

```flow
start: Ison asks for a seal | And the Kiln offers a sealed core.
path: Bring the seal | Ison reads it in public.
path: Expose the core | It came from the Fifth Reserve.
path: The deepest gallery | The Artificer's door, and the Artificer's rule.
join: The Open Measure votes | On the Charter, and on the core.
end: Publish the pattern | The whole continent learns what a seal is. The Registry's envoys are thrown out of three cities.
end: Keep it in Tessara | Tessara refuses the Charter and the core, and quietly arms itself.
end: Take the core | Tessara signs. Ison becomes faster, and sealed, and stops answering questions about the Registry.
end: Split the city | Two networks, two answers, and a city that argues with itself for a generation.
```

## What it leads to

| If you | Then |
|---|---|
| Published the pattern | Every seal holder on the continent learns what they carry. Some throw their seals away; most can't afford to. The Registry declares Tessara an enemy of the Restriking. |
| Kept it in Tessara | Ison becomes your ally, and the Artificer's frame is yours to study (a road to [Awakening](a-door-nobody-locked.md) for an engineer) |
| Took the core | Tessara signs. One more city, and one more machine, in the Register. |

That night, Warden-Captain Ulf Garrick arrives in Tessara. He has been sent to bring you back to the
Gilded Ascent, and he has stopped being polite.
