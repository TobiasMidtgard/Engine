[Rillvault](../../cities/rillvault.md)'s deepest springs have been running thinner every month, and
the Flow Delegates are frightened: less water means slower crops, and slower crops mean less food
for half the world. **Ilse Moorcroft**, steward of the Root Ledger, has heard from Ivo Tarrant what
you found in Orath and Sky City, and she has a place to show you that the Confluence Compact keeps
closed.

## The Echo Galleries

Below the spring headworks and the performance grotto of the Lantern Gardens lie the **Echo
Galleries**: smooth, curving tunnels that were never dug. Rillvault's oldest story says
[the Voice Beneath Stone](../../lore/ascendants.md#the-voice-beneath-stone) sang the rock open, and
that the springs came after. The galleries carry sound for kilometres, and they do something else:
they **remember**. Sing the right note in the right chamber, and the rock sings back what it heard
long ago.

Exploring them is a puzzle of sound:

- **The Mouth**: the grotto shaped like an open mouth, where the galleries begin. A tuning stone
  sets the note for everything below.
- **The Choir of Pillars**: each pillar rings at a different pitch; strike them in the order the
  carvings show and a passage opens.
- **The Drowned Stair**: half flooded, where the water is thinning and the steps are reappearing
  for the first time in centuries.
- **The Listening Room**: where the oldest echo lives.

Something lives down here too: **echo-crawlers**, blind cave hunters that find you by sound (see
[Creatures](../../creatures/index.md)). Walking quietly matters more than walking fast.

## The oldest echo

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

In the Listening Room the rock sings back a voice that is not the Voice Beneath Stone, but someone
far older, speaking a language nobody alive knows. The Voice herself left a translation, cut into
the walls, the way she understood it:

> We built a door over every place the deep rises. We gave every door a name, and every name to the
> Keeper, and the Keeper keeps them, and is kept by them. Let no one write in the Keeper's book who is
> not willing to be written.

The **Keeper** is the [God of Locked Doors](../../lore/gods.md#the-god-of-locked-doors). The First
Builders were its servants. Every door they built over a well is a lock, and every lock is the god's,
and the god grows with every lock in the world. The **book** is the Sixth Register.

At the bottom of the Drowned Stair stands Rillvault's own door, over a well of raw **Matter**. It is
not yet written in: the springs are thinning because the doors are connected, and each one closed
pulls the others a little shut.
