An Awakened person has gone past human limits. An **Ascendant** has gone past the limits of the
world, and claimed a Legend, a Domain, a Principle or a law of their own (see
[Awakening and Ascension](../../play/ascension.md#ascension)). At level 80 you have reached the known
limit of the Awakened. Quell is already beyond it.

## What you can claim

What you claim grows from how you have played the story, and the story offers several claims of its
own. Any other claim that fits how you have played works as well.

| Claim | Concept | How the story offers it |
|---|---|---|
| **Trespass** | Doors, Passage | You have walked through every locked door on the continent unrecorded. Walk through the last one: a door that has never been opened. |
| **The Unwritten** | Absence | You are the one person no register has ever held. Make it a law: nothing can record you, and nothing you protect can be recorded. |
| **Exchange** | Exchange | Make a fair trade with something that can't be traded with: a god, a well, the Crownless King's ghost. |
| **Protection** | Protection | Hold a city's door against the whole Registry, alone, for one night. |
| **Return** | Return | Die in the Counting Deep, and come back. |
| **Severance** | Severance | Cut a seal from a person without hurting them, and then cut a hundred. |
| **Pattern** | Pattern | Read the Sixth Register in full and understand every door in it. |
| **Partnership** | Partnership | With your Brinewing, refuse a command no creature has ever refused. |

## The deed

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

Every claim needs a deed the world can witness, and the story gives each one a place:

- **Trespass**: the door at the very bottom of the Counting Deep, behind the Register room, which
  the Builders shut and never opened. It leads toward the [Temple of Locked Doors](../the-temple-of-locked-doors.md).
  You open it, step through and step back, and the world agrees that no door can stop you.
- **The Unwritten**: Quell writes your name into the Register, at last, with all the power she has.
  The ink won't take.
- **Exchange**: below the Commons, the Middle Well still holds the Crownless King's bargain. Trade
  something you love for something the world needs, fairly, in the well's presence.
- **Protection**: choose a city that refused the Charter: Rillvault, Tessara, Keth Veyra or Orath.
  When the Registry's wardens come to seal it, stand at its door.

## The price

Ascension has a price, always: a lasting weakness, obligation or loss. Trespass leaves you unable to
be welcome anywhere until you are invited twice. The Unwritten leaves you forgotten by anyone who
looks away from you for too long, unless they love you. Exchange takes the thing you traded for ever.
The price is yours to choose from what the claim offers; you can't refuse to pay one.

## Recognition

When you Ascend, the world notices. The other Ascendants (fewer than ten known by name; see
[Legends and Ascendants](../../lore/ascendants.md#the-living)) come to look at you, one at a time:
the woman in Sky City's Crown who stopped a storm, somebody in the Last Writ who hasn't aged in fifty
years, and Rain-Back, who has been an Ascendant all along and never thought it worth mentioning.

```flow
start: Level 80 | The limit of the Awakened.
path: Trespass | The door that was never opened.
path: The Unwritten | The ink that won't take.
path: Exchange | A fair trade in the Middle Well.
path: Protection | One door, one night, against everyone.
join: Ascended | Level 81, and a law of your own.
end: The world recognises you | And so does Quell.
```

## Rewards

- **Ascension**: level 81, your claimed concept and its first authority (see
  [Awakening and Ascension](../../play/ascension.md)).
- A **Legend** in the making: people start calling you by the name of your claim.
- Quell's attention, in full. She sends you a letter, in her own neat hand, inviting you to the
  Lantern Table.
