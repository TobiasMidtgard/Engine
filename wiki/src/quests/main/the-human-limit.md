**Warden-Captain [Ulf Garrick](../../people/companions/ulf-garrick.md)** is the best fighter the Gilded Ascent has, and one of the few hundred
people on the continent who have [Awakened](../../play/ascension.md#awakening). He has been sent to
bring you back to the Registry alive, if possible. He has stopped expecting it to be possible.

## The hunt

Garrick doesn't come alone, and he doesn't come at you head on. He has read everything the Registry
knows about you, which is very little, and everything the wardens who failed to catch you have
written, which is a great deal. He comes at the time and place your habits make likely:

| If you mostly | Garrick |
|---|---|
| Fight | Brings a shield line and archers, and fights you on open ground |
| Sneak | Comes at dawn with dogs and lanterns, and watches the roofs |
| Talk | Arrests the people who would help you, and waits for you to come for them |
| Cast | Brings hushstone manacles and a Spillward-trained null mage |
| Run | Closes the roads first, then the rivers |

## The fight

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

Garrick is not a fair fight. He is faster than you can follow, his skin turns blades that would kill
anyone else, and his blows crack the ground when they miss. At the human apex, with everything you
have learned, you can **survive** him: hold him long enough, break away, use the land, or make him
choose between you and something he has sworn to protect.

What happens when you can't is part of the story. If he beats you, you wake in a Registry prison
cell in the Gilded Ascent, and breaking out is the next thing you do.

During the fight, Garrick talks. He is not cruel, and he is not a fool:

> You're good. You're as good as a person gets. That's the trouble, isn't it. I was as good as a person
> gets once, too, and then I wasn't a person any more. She paid for that. Who's going to pay for yours?

## The ceiling

Whatever happens, by the end of this quest you reach **level 60**, the human apex, and the game
shows you the ceiling: experience no longer raises your level. You can still grow in skill, and
you will, but you will not become more than you are without something rare.

> **The Human Limit.** You have mastered being human. Experience can no longer raise your level.
> To go further, you must Awaken.

## The tortoise

On the road afterwards you meet **[Rain-Back](../../people/rain-back.md)**, an old tortoise envoy of [Oruvai](../../cities/oruvai.md)
travelling with the [Keepers' Road](../../factions/keepers-road.md), who has been walking towards
you, very slowly, for some months. Rain-Back is much older than any tortoise should be. It speaks
through the Chorus, in a voice that sounds like a forest agreeing with itself.

> You have reached the edge. Good. Most never see it. There are doors past it, and none of them is
> locked, because nobody thought anyone would walk through them. That is why they are open to you.

Rain-Back tells you the roads to Awakening it knows of, and where each one starts (see
[A Door Nobody Locked](a-door-nobody-locked.md)).

## Rewards

- **Level 60** and the **Human Apex** breakthrough, if you weren't there already.
- Garrick's respect, whatever happened. He will remember how you fought.
- If you escaped the Registry's prison: the prison's layout, and the start of a plan for later (see
  [Steal the Register](steal-the-register.md)).
