> Corven Auld is showing the Gloamglass Collar tonight. At first bell, it enters the Gilded Ledger.
> Bring it to Maera before then. Intact.

**The Gloamglass Collar** is nine matched stones that change colour together as the wearer moves,
from dusk blue to ember red. Maera has a collector who will pay four times what the stones are worth,
as long as the setting survives. Broken up, it is just nine nice stones.

## Auld House

Corven Auld's house is four tightly packed square rafts, joined by bridges that can be pulled in:

| Raft | What is there |
|---|---|
| The receiving raft | The public gallery and the cloakroom, where the guests arrive |
| The service raft | Kitchens, wine stores and the servants' passages |
| The dining raft | The dining rooms and an open atrium beneath the stars |
| The private raft | The family's apartments, and a rooftop greenhouse linked to the neighbouring roofs |

A small vault hangs in the central support frame between the four, over the water.

## The evening

The collar moves through the night, and so must you:

| When | Where the collar is |
|---|---|
| Arrivals | In a display case in the receiving gallery |
| Dinner | Around the neck of Auld's daughter, at the head of the table |
| After the toast | In the hands of a jeweller, who examines it for the bank |
| First bell | Sealed in a deposit cylinder by a bank runner, and carried away |

## Opportunities

Everything below is just something true about the evening. What you do with it is up to you.

- The jeweller is losing at [the Seven Lanterns](../cities/vantress/lantern-ward/seven-lanterns.md),
  and has sent an assistant nobody has met before.
- A wine merchant delivers six barrels and collects seven empties.
- The display case weighs what is in it.
- The greenhouse vents must stay open: one of its plants releases dangerous spores when it gets too
  warm.
- The bank runner carries the cylinder's key; Corven carries the vault's.
- A servant, **Nel Ash**, knows every blind spot in the house, but won't go into the private
  apartments.
- [Cala "Twice" Mere](../factions/thieves-guild.md#outside-hands) is at the dinner under a false
  name, stealing something else.
- The daughter hates the collar, and takes it off whenever her father isn't looking.
- If you are strong enough, you can pull in the bridges and tow the whole vault raft away.

## When it goes wrong

The house doesn't fail you; it reacts. Suspicion keeps the collar on the daughter all night. A
confirmed intruder seals the upper bridges. A visible robbery sends the runner off early. Then
comes a chase across the bridges and roofs, and a last chance on the water. The job is lost only if
the collar reaches the Gilded Ledger and you stop chasing it.

## Inside the guild

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild questline below.

Nel Ash has sold part of the floor plan to [Ressa Vann](../people/ressa-vann.md), and an invitation to
Cala. [Tal Oran](../people/tal-oran.md) calls him untrustworthy; Ressa calls it normal thief
behaviour. [Sella "Scrip" Dask](../people/sella-dask.md) wants the guests' signet rings copied from the
cloakroom while you are there.

You can steal the collar intact, break it up, swap in a replica, copy it and return it, or use it
to deceive Maera or her buyer.
