Before it takes on a huge new collection of the city's debts,
[the Gilded Ledger](../cities/vantress/knot-square/the-gilded-ledger.md) wants to know how safe its
vault really is, so it hires the people who would know: [the Tangle](../factions/thieves-guild.md).
The job is legal, signed and paid. It is also the most dangerous gift the bank could give a thief.

## The contract

Enter after closing, reach the reconciliation vault, remove one numbered glass token and leave,
without injuring anyone or taking anything that belongs to a customer.

## Plan the entry

Several ways in, one vault. Mix them, or bring a crew and use several at once.

```flow
start: Take the contract | The bank signs you in for one night.
path: Talk your way in | Pose as an overnight audit, or win over someone on the staff.
path: Through the walls | Service shafts and the bank's message tubes.
path: Magic and machines | Copy a magical signature, or rework the bank's patternwork.
path: Crack the vault | Patience, tools and nerve.
join: Reach the reconciliation vault | Take the numbered glass token.
end: Close every gap | Report what you found, or fix it quietly.
end: Leave the Ghost Seal | Keep a secret way back in.
```

## The Ghost Seal

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the end of the job below.

For the test, the bank gives you a temporary **White Seal**, its authorisation recorded by the
bank, [the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md) and an outside notary. One plate in the
vault's reconciliation mechanism can be set slightly out of line, so that the cancelled seal still
has a hidden way through. You can:

- **Correct it and report it.**
- **Correct it quietly.**
- **Leave it**, and tell nobody.
- **Leave it and tell one person** in the guild.
- **Leave it now and remove it later.**

Using the Ghost Seal opens the vault or rewrites the ledgers, but the signature belongs to your
test credential: the moment you use it, you are the prime suspect. Your accounts are frozen, your
friends are questioned and the whole city hunts you. Handing the method to someone else doesn't
help.

Closing the gap has a cost too: it strengthens a bank that may foreclose on the raft settlement.
The guild's leaders disagree. Jori may walk away from the finale if you use the seal, because the
guild promised to repair every weakness it found; Sella would rather keep it unused as a threat;
Nilo wants it published for everyone. After this job you become **Knotted**, a full member of the
guild.
