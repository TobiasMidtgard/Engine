A crane on the Vantress docks dropped a cargo of glass when its enchanted brake failed. The same
week, a barrel of preserving brine turned three hundred meals rotten on a ship to Rillvault, and a
Keth Veyra harness snapped on a ramp. Every one of them carried the **Makers' Compact's mark**. If
the mark can't be trusted, nothing the Compact makes can be sold for what it is worth.

## The job

[Hedda Strom](../people/hedda-strom.md) needs someone outside the lodges, who has no
reason to protect anyone, to follow the failed goods back to where they were made. Start with the
crane brake, the brine and the harness. Bring the Council of Lodges an answer it can act on.

Each failed item has a mark, a maker and a story:

| Item | Mark | Where it leads |
|---|---|---|
| The crane brake | Enchanters' lodge, Veyrhold | A workshop that cut its enchanting time in half last spring |
| The preserving brine | Brewers' lodge, Rillvault | A brewery on a new quota from its buyers |
| The harness | Builders' and weavers' lodges, Vantress | A ropewalk that buys its fibre from a new supplier |

## What you find

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the job below.

All four suspected causes are real, and each explains only one failure:

- **Counterfeits**: the harness was never made by the Compact. Its mark was struck on Sealwrights'
  Row in the Gilded Ascent, by a forger who sells marks by the hundred.
- **Unsafe quotas**: the brewery's buyers demanded twice the barrels for the same price, and the
  brewers skipped a week of curing to meet it.
- **Sabotage**: the crane brake was weakened on purpose, by a worker who wanted the dock's new
  pattern-driven cranes to look dangerous.
- **A trade secret**: the enchanter who made the brake had been told by his master never to write
  down the last step of the binding, and he died before teaching it. His apprentices have been
  guessing.

```flow

path: The quota | Find who set it, and who profits.

path: The lost secret | Find out what the dead master knew.
join: The Council of Lodges | Everyone wants a different culprit.
end: Blame the bosses | Hedda's answer: quotas and secrets kill workers.
end: Blame the shortcuts | Aldous's answer: skill was cut, and skill must be protected.
end: Blame the old ways | Perrin's answer: standard recipes and machines don't forget.
```

## What it leads to

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild questline below.

You may take an apprenticeship in any lodge. The three leaders each hear the answer they wanted,
or don't:

| If you | You gain |
|---|---|
| Exposed the quota and the buyers behind it | Hedda's trust, and the dock workers' gratitude |
| Recovered the dead master's last step | Aldous's trust, and a technique no one else knows |
| Proved the new cranes were safe | Perrin's trust, and a share in the pattern-crane company |
| Caught the forger on Sealwrights' Row | The Chartered Road's notice, and an enemy in the Gilded Ascent |
