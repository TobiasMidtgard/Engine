A Red Writ has been bought on **Arno Kesk**, a Boulevard shipping clerk, for the murder of a
child. Before the Last Writ will hang a writ in the Quiet Bell it must be verified, and tonight a
hearing will decide whether this one is real. You are hired to bring the only witness there alive.

## The job

[Edrin Vasse](../factions/last-writ.md#people) hands you a lantern, a guild token and a name:
**Lisbet Arrow**, a laundress who says she saw the writ being signed. She lives in the Arcades, at
street level, and the hearing is on the roofs. Between the two lies the third floor, and above it
anyone may kill her if they can afford the writ, or are willing to answer for not having one.

> Get her there breathing. What she says when she arrives is her business.

Three things are certain: Kesk has money, somebody else wants the writ to stand, and the hearing
starts at midnight with or without her.

## Getting her there

Routes up and across the city, all of which Lisbet can manage if you help:

- **The servants' stair** of a Courts magistrate's house, if you can talk your way past the cook.
- **The laundry lift** behind Lisbet's own workplace, which runs up into the Red Exchange.
- **The Moon Runs**, the fastest way across, if the werebeast clans let you through.
- **The night-sails**: climb onto the canvas and cross under the stars, quietly.
- **Hire a guard**: a Writ-bearer will come along for half your fee.

On the way you find out more about the writ than you wanted to.

## The writ

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the job below.

Kesk is guilty: there is a child's shoe in his strongbox and a ledger of payments to a man who
"found" the body. The writ, however, is forged. It carries the signature of the child's mother,
and she cannot write. It was bought by **Kesk's own business partner**, who wants Kesk dead and the
partnership's ships for himself, and who has a second writ in his pocket: one naming Lisbet.

At the hearing, [Ophel Strand](../factions/last-writ.md#people) presides. If the writ is forged, it
cannot be honoured, whatever Kesk has done.

```flow
start: Bring Lisbet to the Quiet Bell | Alive, by midnight, across the roofs.
path: Prove the forgery | The writ falls, and Kesk walks free.
path: Prove both | Show the forgery and Kesk's guilt; ask the mother to buy a true writ.
path: Stay silent | Let the forged writ stand. Kesk dies; so might Lisbet.
join: The hearing ends | Ophel rules. Sabeth Rook is watching you.
end: Edrin's way | Protect Lisbet, hand Kesk to the Magistracy below.
end: Ophel's way | The rules are followed exactly, whatever they cost.
end: Sabeth's way | Somebody hunts tonight. You choose who.
```

## What it leads to

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild questline below.

You become a **Witness** of the Last Writ whatever happens, but the three Hands remember how you got
there:

| If you | You gain |
|---|---|
| Kept Lisbet alive and her name out of it | Edrin's trust, and a friend in the Arcades |
| Followed the rules, even when they freed Kesk | Ophel's trust, and access to the registry of writs |
| Tracked down the partner yourself | Sabeth's interest, and an invitation to her next hunt |
| Told the Magistracy about the child | The Boulevard's gratitude, and the Eaves' suspicion |
