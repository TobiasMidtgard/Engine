A slow **grey fever** has reached the garden warrens of [Rillvault](../cities/rillvault.md). It
starts as tiredness and a grey tint to the lips, and over weeks it turns lungs to stone. The Root
Ledger knows the cure: **lamplight moss**, which grows only on the walls of the deepest springs,
where its glow feeds the blind fish that feed the river that waters the farms. There is enough of
it to cure perhaps a third of the sick.

## The job

[Ilse Moorcroft](../factions/root-ledger.md#people) hands you a gathering knife, a ledger page and a
lamp. Go down to the springs with her gatherers, count the moss, and bring back what can be taken
without killing the spring. The rest is a question for the Confluence Compact, and they would like
your count first.

Everyone has a different answer before you even start:

- **The Flow Delegate** of the springs says taking more than a tenth will kill the spring, and
  with it the farms of three tunnels.
- **The Hearth Delegate** of the warrens says people come first, and will strip the walls if you
  don't.
- **[Benedek Strawn](../factions/root-ledger.md#people)** says he can grow the moss in a tunnel farm,
  given a season, and the sick don't have a season.
- **[Ottilie Brand](../factions/root-ledger.md#people)** says she can make one sprig of moss do the
  work of ten, and will not tell you how.

## In the springs

The deep springs are cold, beautiful and dangerous: flooded passages, glowing walls, blind fish
that panic at lamps, and cave-ins where old harvesters dug too far. The moss grows highest where
the water runs fastest. Counting it means climbing; taking it means choosing which walls go dark.

## The choice

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the job below.

There are four answers, and none of them is free:

```flow
start: Count the moss | Bring the number to the Compact.
path: Take a tenth | The spring lives; two thirds of the sick wait for Benedek's farm.
path: Take it all | Everyone is cured; the spring dies, and three tunnels' farms with it.
path: Ottilie's way | Her concentrate cures everyone from a tenth of the moss, and it is addictive.
path: Look further | An older spring, sealed for a century, may have moss of its own.
join: The Compact decides | It follows your count, and your advice.
end: The steward's answer | Slow, careful, and some people die waiting.
end: The cultivator's answer | Farmed moss in a season, and a new industry.
end: The refiner's answer | Quick cures, and a new drug in the warrens.
```

The sealed spring is sealed for a reason: its moss is there, and so is whatever made the first
harvesters seal it.

## What it leads to

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild questline below.

You become a **Gatherer** whatever you advise. Each elder remembers what you counted:

| If you | You gain |
|---|---|
| Kept the harvest to a tenth | Ilse's trust, and the red pages to read |
| Helped Benedek start a moss farm | Benedek's trust, and a share in the first harvest |
| Brought Ottilie her tenth | Ottilie's trust, and the recipe, almost |
| Opened the sealed spring | The Elders' anger, a cure for everyone and something new in the dark |
