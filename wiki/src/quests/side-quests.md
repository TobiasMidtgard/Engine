# Side quests

Most of the work in the world isn't on any guild's board. It starts as a **rumour**: something an
innkeeper mentions, a notice nailed to a waystation door, a caravan driver who won't stop talking,
or a person standing in the road who needs help. Each [region](../world/regions/index.md) page lists
the rumours you hear on its roads, and your journal keeps the ones you have heard.

A side quest has no marker until somebody tells you where to go. Most can end more than one way, and
many touch the main story, a guild or a companion. The levels below are where a quest is meant to
be done; you can try it sooner.

## Four of them in full

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for four side quests below.

### Seals on the Washing Line

Clay Seals are worth a chit each, and somebody is stealing them from washing lines in **Weighford**,
where farmers leave their coats out to dry with their seals still pinned on. The farmer who asks
you to catch the thief wants a hanging. The thieves are children: the unsealed children of the
Lowmarket, who wear the stolen seals to get through the Ring's gate to the Commons school, where a
teacher asks no questions as long as a seal is pinned on.

```flow
start: Seals on the washing line | A chit each, and a farmer who wants a hanging.
path: Catch them | Wait by the lines at night.
path: Follow the seals | Find the school in the Commons.
path: Ask in the Lowmarket | Somebody's mother knows.
join: Twelve children | And a teacher who asks no questions.
end: Turn them in | 8 marks. The children are fined; their families can't pay.
end: Pay for the seals | One chit each, a season at a time. The farmer is satisfied.
end: Get them sealed | Take them to the gate and pay for honest Clay Seals: 12 chits, and the Registry writes twelve more names down.
```

The last ending is the kindest, and it puts twelve more people in Gisela Quell's ledger. Tamsin Reeve
approves of the second, and Teodor Voss of the third, for different reasons.

### A Pie Without a Seal

**Annis Crale** has sold pies from a stall in the [Lowmarket](../cities/gilded-ascent.md) for thirty
years. The Seal Wardens arrested her this morning for selling food without a seal, which she has
never had, and which nobody in the Lowmarket has. Her stall goes on sale at noon tomorrow. The
buyer is a Copper-sealed caterer from the Commons who has wanted her recipe for a decade, and who
reported her.

- **Buy the stall** yourself: 40 marks at the sale, or less if you out-haggle the caterer.
- **Free Annis**: a fine of 10 marks at the Weighhouse, or a forged release, or a wall.
- **Prove the caterer** has been selling in the Lowmarket too, without paying the Lowmarket's dues.
- **Learn the recipe** from Annis, if she trusts you, before any of it.

If Annis keeps her stall, she teaches you Lowmarket pie (see [Cooking](../crafting/cooking.md#dishes-of-the-cities)),
feeds you for nothing whenever you're in the Lowmarket, and you are **Known** to the Lowmarket: its
people warn you when the Seal Wardens are coming. Hesper Lorn's brother eats at her stall every day.

### Hollow Jen's Toll

**Hollow Jen** is a river pirate with two boats and a grudge, and she is charging 1 mark a hull on
the Salt Spur above Spur Ferry. Nobody gave her the right. The ferry inn wants her gone; the Tangle
wants a share; the Chartered Road wants an example made. Jen says the Spur's old toll was the Drowned
Families' before the Tally took their deeds, and she is the last of them collecting it.

```flow
start: A toll on the Salt Spur | One mark a hull, and nobody's right to charge it.
path: Sink her boats | Two crews, on the water, at night.
path: Talk to her | She tells you about the Drowning Year.
path: Find the old toll-roll | It is in the Tally, with the deeds.
join: Jen's claim | True or not.
end: Jen is gone | The Spur is free again. The Chartered Road pays 30 marks.
end: Jen takes the Tangle's share | The toll stays, the Tangle gets a cut, and you are Knotted a little tighter.
end: Jen's toll is lawful | With the toll-roll from [the Tally](../world/dungeons/the-tally.md), a Vantress court gives the Spur back to the Drowned Families.
```

### Before the Moon Is Full

A girl of **Saltgrave**, nine years old, was bitten on the moor by a [werebeast](../creatures/werebeast.md),
and the town means to drown her before the moon is full in three nights. Her mother has locked her in
the smokehouse and is standing outside it with a gaff. Nobody in Saltgrave knows a cure, because
nobody in Saltgrave has ever looked for one.

- A bite can be cured before the first change. [Sister Avara](../people/companions/sister-avara.md)
  knows how: a clean-blood tonic of Masterwork quality, brewed with wolfsbane and silver, drunk under
  the full moon (see [Alchemy](../crafting/alchemy.md#poisons)).
- The werebeast that bit her is still on the moor. It is a fisherman of Saltgrave who has been hiding
  what he is for six years, and the town will drown him too, if it finds out.
- If the moon rises before you're done, the girl changes in the smokehouse, and you'll have to get
  her out of town before the town gets in.

Curing her makes you **Respected** in Saltgrave, which is a useful thing to be in a smugglers'
harbour that trades with both halves of the Second City.
