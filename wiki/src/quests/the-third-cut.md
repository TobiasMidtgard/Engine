Every haul the Tangle takes is split three ways: the **crew** who did the job, the **Knotkeeper**
who sponsored it, and [Maera "Cask" Orro](../people/maera-orro.md)'s **communal stores**, which feed
Ward Null in a bad winter. The last big job came in worth eleven hundred marks. By the time the
shares were counted, three hundred were gone, and the crew think someone above them took it.

## The job

Maera wants the missing money found, and she wants it found quietly. A guild that thinks its own
leaders are stealing from it stops being a guild. Follow the haul from the moment it left the
target's house to the moment it reached the stores, and rebuild the cut.

## Following the money

The haul passed through five pairs of hands, and each of them is a lead:

- **The crew**, who carried it out and swear every chit came home.
- **The fence**, who turned the goods into coin, and quietly raised his fee.
- **A Knotkeeper's debt**: the sponsor of the job had an emergency debt to settle, and settled it
  from the haul before anyone counted.
- **A concealed mistake**: a crew member dropped a strongbox in the canal and paid a diver to bring
  it up, from the haul.
- **The ledger**: [Sella "Scrip" Dask](../people/sella-dask.md)'s records of the job, which are
  correct, and which nobody but Sella can read.

## What happened

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the job below.

Nobody stole the three hundred marks. A little went to each: the fence's quiet fee rise, the
Knotkeeper's emergency debt, and the diver's fee for the dropped strongbox. Each was small and
each had a reason, and together they took more than a quarter of the haul.

You can rebuild the cut without blaming anyone, or name every person who took a share and let the
guild sort it out. Either way, you now know how Maera's stores and Sella's records work, and both of
them know you do.
