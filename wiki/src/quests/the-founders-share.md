> The Knucklebone Exchange is auctioning a black coffer called the Founder's Share. Steal it
> before Lot Twelve leaves the Exchange. Bring it to the Mooring unopened.

Years ago, the guild's quartermaster vanished with a reserve of gemstones, foreign coin and the
unclaimed shares of members who died or disappeared. The coffer has reappeared at a private
criminal auction. It holds **twenty-four sealed purses**, each labelled with a former member's
name, and it is worth more than every job you have done for the guild put together.

## The Knucklebone Exchange

A decommissioned customs pontoon, dressed up as a salvage market and designed by retired burglars
to be robbed by nobody:

- **The receiving room**, where lots are checked in.
- **A masked bidders' gallery**, where every mask matches a weighted token on the bidder's wrist.
- **Negotiation booths**, an **appraisal chamber** and **watcher galleries** overhead.
- **A vault cage**, hanging under the water, and a **dispatch dock** of anonymous skiffs.

Its rules are traps. Unlocked doors are bait. The auctioneer, the appraiser and the courier each
control part of every handover. The escape boats stay chained until the auction closes. The staff
leave tempting valuables lying around to see who takes them.

## Opportunities

- [Maera](../people/maera-orro.md) has a fence's invitation, but is known by sight.
- A bidder has sold his mask, his entry phrase and his credit token.
- Large consignments are admitted unopened, if their owner accepts liability.
- Coloured knots on the dispatch cages decide which skiff carries which lot.
- The vault's inspection bell is big enough to hide a diver.
- The appraiser rechecks any seal that looks out of line.
- The buyer of Lot Twelve means to carry it away personally.
- A rival crew will rob the deposits during Lot Eleven.
- The vault cage hangs on four pins, and each can be replaced.

## Inside the guild

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild questline below.

Nobody trusts anybody with this one. [Ressa](../people/ressa-vann.md) has sent Tavi "Pin" Rell to find out
which named purses are still there. [Sella](../people/sella-dask.md) has sent Cala "Twice" Mere to copy
the records. [Tal](../people/tal-oran.md) has hidden Bram "Kettle" Sorn under the dispatch bridge as a
secret back-up. If you do nothing, their plans collide in the middle of the auction.

## Inside the coffer

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the end of the job below.

The coffer holds the named shares, and beneath them a much larger operational reserve that no list
mentions. It also holds records showing that **Isca Dorn**, the quiet bookkeeper who collects the
fees in the Slipknot's shop, and who is the Mooring, always knew the quartermaster had escaped. And
it holds one purse that is empty, labelled with Isca's own name.

You can return it unopened, open it and reseal it, copy the records, pay out the shares to the
families of the dead, skim it, swap the stones, give it to one of the Three Weights, or sell it and
hide the greatest theft of your career.
