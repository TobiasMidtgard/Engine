Every god has a law that keeps it alive. To become a [Godslayer](../play/progression.md#godslayer)
you must find that law, and break it. **The God of Locked Doors** cannot be harmed while any door
in its temple stays closed. Its temple is at the bottom of the Counting Deep, under the richest
vaults in the world, and it has more doors than anyone has counted.

## Finding the law

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the Godslayer path below.

Nobody tells you the god's law. You have to learn it, the way every Godslayer does: by
understanding what the god is, and what it cannot survive.

- **The Sixth Seal**: old vault-keepers in the Gilded Ascent whisper of a seal no house struck. It
  is the key to the temple's first door, and it is hanging around the neck of the Sealkeeper.
- **The locksmiths' shrines**: [Jori "Latch" Pell](../people/jori-pell.md) keeps a tiny shrine to the
  god behind a loose board in his workshop, as every master locksmith does. He knows the prayer that
  locksmiths say before they open a lock they were not given the key to. It is the god's law, in
  verse.
- **The vault records**: the Fifth Reserve's oldest ledgers show rooms rented below the deepest
  level of the Deep, by a tenant who has paid for four hundred years.

## The doors

The temple is a maze of thresholds, and every kind counts:

| Doors | What they are | How they open |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical** | Vault doors, gates, hatches and chests by the thousand | Locks, keys, force, patience, and help |
| **Magical** | Wards and sealed portals, one of them shut for centuries | Ward work, Passage magic, or someone else's magical signature |
| **Symbolic** | An oracle who has sworn never to speak, a contract nobody may read | Persuasion, law, or a promise you must keep |
| **Your own** | A memory you have locked away, of the worst thing you ever did | Remembering it, out loud, in front of the god |

Opening them is not enough: they must **stay open for the whole fight**. Doors close by themselves,
the god's servants close them, and the god closes them with a word. Allies who hold doors open for
you are worth more than any weapon, and every one of them is at risk.

Killing a god changes the world. When the God of Locked Doors dies, locks across the continent
begin to fail, vaults spring open, prisons empty, and every secret anybody ever locked away starts
to come out. Some people will thank you. Most will not.
