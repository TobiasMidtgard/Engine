The caravan of the **Harrowgate house** rang its bell at the Caravanserai of a Thousand Bells this
morning, with every seal on every crate intact. When the crates were opened in the Bonded Halls,
four of them held sand. The insurers will pay out at sunset unless someone can show what happened,
and three very respectable people have three very different explanations.

## The job

[Ivo Tarrant](../factions/chartered-road.md#people) hires you as a drover for the day, the lowest
work in the guild, because a drover can walk into the Caravanserai, the stables and the Bonded Halls
without anyone noticing. Find out where the cargo went. Ivo doesn't care who is to blame. He cares
that the guild can say what happened.

## The three accounts

| Account | Says | What it leaves out |
|---|---|---|
| **The manifest** | Twelve crates of Tessaran pattern lamps, sealed at the Sifting Basin | That the lamps were bought on credit from a Closed Kiln |
| **The ownership contract** | Lucan Prell owns the cargo from the moment it passes the Toll Rings | That he insured it the day before for twice its value |
| **The witnesses** | The drovers swear no crate was opened on the road | That they stopped for a night at an inn that wasn't on the route |

## Finding out

- **The crates**: the seals are real, but one has been lifted and pressed back with a warm knife.
  Only a sealwright, or a forger, can do that cleanly.
- **The inn**: a roadside house half a day east, where the drovers drank, slept and were paid
  extra to sleep late.
- **The Weighhouse**: the Great Balance weighed the caravan at the gate. The weight was right,
  because sand is heavy.
- **The Registry**: Lucan Prell's insurance was witnessed by a Gold Seal notary who owes him money.

## What happened

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the job below.

Everyone is lying a little, and nobody is lying about everything. The caravan master swapped four
crates for sand at the inn on Lucan Prell's orders, so that Lucan could claim the insurance and
sell the lamps again in [Arena City](../cities/arena-city.md). But the forger who resealed the
crates was paid by **Captain Wenna Sallow**'s outriders, who stole the real lamps from Lucan's own
smugglers on the road and gave them to the frozen villages of the northern road, where they now
heat a school.

## What it leads to

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild questline below.

The Road sponsors your **Copper Seal** however you end it, because you were useful. Its leaders
watch what you chose:

| If you | You gain |
|---|---|
| Told the Court of Seals everything | Hesper Lorn's trust, and a reputation as someone who can't be bought |
| Kept it inside the guild | Ivo's trust, and a place on his next caravan |
| Protected the village | Wenna's trust, and a seat in her outriders' smuggling runs |
| Sold your silence | Lucan's trust, and a first look at the Unsealed List's black market |
