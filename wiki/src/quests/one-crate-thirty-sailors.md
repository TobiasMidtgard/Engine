> The merchant cutter *Saint Lume* carries a cedar case marked L-12. Bring us the case before the
> ship clears the outer chain. The rest of the vessel is none of our concern.

The case holds nine unregistered bars of **colourglass**: compact, easy to divide and easy to
fence. The *Saint Lume* leaves on the morning tide, and between her berth and the open sea she must
pass the bridges, the cranes and the customs boards of the chain inspection.

## The voyage out

| Stage | What it offers |
|---|---|
| **The loading berth** | Cranes, cargo nets and a crowd of porters |
| **The inner channel** | Close to the bridges, cranes and walkways; you can drop on board |
| **The chain inspection** | Customs boards the ship; the captain is nervous |
| **The outer basin** | Open water, and only boats can reach her |

## The ship

The *Saint Lume* has passenger and crew decks, officers' cabins, a galley and an infirmary, a hold
with a hanging loading cage, bilge and ballast spaces, the captain's safe, a signal mast, two
cargo tenders that can be cut loose, and a locked weapons cabinet. There are **three cases marked
L-12**: the colourglass, a decoy full of brass fittings, and a case of worthless shards that marks
whoever opens it with an alchemical dye.

## Opportunities

- The pilot is late, and has never met the captain.
- Six deckhands haven't been paid.
- The first mate is hiding a passenger who isn't on the list.
- A quarantine officer is expected on board.
- The cargo lift passes under an upper bridge.
- A swimmer can get in through the bilge outlet.
- A signal error would stop the ship right under the loading cranes.
- If customs really searches, the captain will dump the contraband overboard.
- One tender carries empty crates away, barely guarded.

## Piracy

Taking the case is the job. Taking the ship is a choice: fly a false flag, pin the rudder, grapple
and board. A ship is a small moving city. Keep her and you must control thirty sailors, find a
navigator, sell registered cargo that everyone will recognise and pay a share to everyone who
helped.

independent pirate ally or a resentful rival, depending on what you did.
