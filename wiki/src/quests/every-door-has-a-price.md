A customs inspection order will be read at the evening tide, and when it is, the guild's
smuggling barge will be searched to the keel. Your job as an Outside Hand is to walk into the
customs watchhouse while it is open and full of clerks, and replace the order with one that names a
different barge.

## The job

The order sits in a ledger on the duty clerk's desk on the watchhouse's second floor. The
replacement page is already written; [Vessa Har](../factions/thieves-guild.md#the-bench) forged it on
a real registry number, so the barge it names really exists and really is due. All you have to do
is make the swap without anyone seeing.

This is the first job where the senior members of the guild offer to help, and each of them offers
their own way in. Nobody tells you that they are watching which one you take.

## Ways in

| Way | Who offers it | How it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **A key** | [Jori "Latch" Pell](../people/jori-pell.md) | A copy of the records room key, if you bring him a wax impression of the lock |
| **The roof** | [Nilo "Eel" Sare](../people/nilo-sare.md) | A route over the roofs and down a rope to a window that sticks |
| **A clerk** | [Sella "Scrip" Dask](../people/sella-dask.md) | A junior clerk who owes money; he can take the ledger to the privy for five minutes |
| **A delivery** | [Maera "Cask" Orro](../people/maera-orro.md) | A crate of lamp oil for the watchhouse, with a false bottom big enough for you |
| **Improvise** | Nobody | A watchhouse full of clerks, inspectors, smugglers paying their fines and one bored cat |

The watchhouse has a rhythm: the bell for the tide report, the inspectors' tea, the fines queue,
the moment the duty clerk goes to argue with the harbourmaster. Learn it and the room is yours for
a minute at a time.

## What it leads to

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild questline below.

The guild's leaders are watching how you work, not whether you succeed. Whoever's help you took,
and how you used it, is the first thing each of them remembers about you. Get caught and the job
fails forward: the barge is searched, the guild loses a cargo, and you owe [Maera](../people/maera-orro.md)
its value. The next job is [The Third Cut](the-third-cut.md).
