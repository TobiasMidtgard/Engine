**Mossback**, a bargeback bred and trained by the Keepers' Road, broke its harness on the northern
road, overturned three wagons, gored a drover and vanished into the forest. The caravan's insurers
want it shot. [Holm Agrest](../factions/keepers-road.md#people), who bred it, wants it back alive,
because in twelve years it has never so much as stepped on a foot.

## The job

Track Mossback through the forest below Keth Veyra, calm it and bring it home before the insurers'
hunters find it first. Holm gives you a lead rope, a pouch of salt and a whistle Mossback knows. He
also gives you the drover's account, which does not quite add up.

## The trail

- **The wreck**: the wagons were overturned from the downhill side. Mossback was pushing away from
  something in the trees, not charging the drovers.
- **The tracks**: deep, panicked prints, then calm ones, then prints of something else walking
  alongside, very large and very light.
- **The forest**: wolves, boar and a herd of red deer that won't stay in one place. All of them are
  heading the same way: away.
- **The drover**: says Mossback went mad. His bandages say he was burned, not gored.

## What it was running from

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the job below.

The caravan was carrying a **Brinewing egg**, hidden in a crate of salt fish and stolen from Keth
Veyra's rookery. The egg's mother followed it down the mountain, and every animal on the road knew
before any person did. Mossback panicked when she landed beside the wagons; the drover was burned
when he tried to drive her off with a torch.

The egg is now in the drover's saddlebag, and the Brinewing is circling the forest looking for it.

```flow
start: Track Mossback | Calm it, bring it home.
path: Follow the animals | They know where the danger is.
path: Question the drover | His story falls apart.
path: Find the Brinewing | She is grieving, and she is dangerous.
join: The egg | Somebody has to carry it back up the mountain.
end: Sere's way | Return the egg, and let the mother choose whether to trust you.
end: Varo's way | Bind the mother and lead her home in a harness.
end: Rain-Back's way | Let the mother take her egg wherever she wants to go.
```

[Sere](../factions/keepers-road.md#people) and [Varo Kett](../factions/keepers-road.md#people) both
arrive on the road before you finish, and they want different things.

## What it leads to

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild questline below.

You become a **Groom** of the Keepers' Road if Mossback comes home alive. The guild remembers the
rest:

| If you | You gain |
|---|---|
| Returned the egg and let the mother lead | Sere's trust, and a Brinewing that remembers you |
| Bound the mother and brought her home | Varo's trust, and a harness that can hold anything |
| Let her take the egg away | Rain-Back's trust, and Keth Veyra's fury |
| Handed the drover to the Closed Hand | Keth Veyra's gratitude, and a name the merchants won't forgive |
