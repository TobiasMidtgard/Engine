In **the Chamber of Meeting**, a shared hall where the four waterways of the Hollow Crown cross
beneath the bridges, a thread of water has begun to rise from the basin into the air, climb to
the ceiling and fall back as light rain. Pilgrims have started sleeping on the floor beneath it.
All four faiths have explained it, persuasively, and the hearing that decides who may tend it is
in four days.

## The job

[Amsel Dray](../factions/accord-of-four-keys.md#people) needs a Lamp who belongs to no faith to
gather what every side says and what the chamber shows, so the hearing has something to argue about
besides itself. Keep the pilgrims safe, keep the four faiths from coming to blows, and find out
what is happening.

## Four answers and a fifth

| Who | Says the rising water is |
|---|---|
| **The Solitary Light** | Light made visible: the divine shining through water, as it once shone through the spring at sunrise |
| **The Deep Choir** | The voices of the dead below, rising at last to be heard |
| **The Returning Road** | A sign that the stranger is coming back, and the city must open its doors |
| **The Covenant of Ash** | A gift to the sick: the rain beneath it heals small wounds, and the Covenant has the records |
| **[The Spillward](../factions/spillward.md)** | A spill: something is leaking into the water from below, and it could get worse |

Every one of them has evidence. The rain does heal small wounds. Voices do sound in the chamber at
night. The rising water does catch the sunrise. And [Halvard Cray](../factions/spillward.md#people)'s
rods do twitch.

## What is happening

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the job below.

The spring is weakening (see [Orath](../cities/orath.md#quests-and-rumours)), and as its water level
falls in the old channels, a thin stream of **raw Life** from deep beneath the city, which the
spring's weight has always pressed down, has begun to rise with the water. The Spillward is right
that it is a leak. The Covenant is right that it heals. Whether a rising
[Fundament](../lore/fundaments.md) is a miracle is a question the Accord has never had to answer.

```flow

end: Shared custody | Amsel's answer: all four tend it together, and the Spillward watches.
end: One faith's miracle | Cassian's answer: the Light came first, and the chamber is theirs.
end: The pilgrims' miracle | Oriel's answer: nobody tends it; anyone may come.
end: Seal it | The Spillward's answer, and every faith's enemy.
```

## What it leads to

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the Accord's questline below.

You become a **Lamp** of the Accord. The rising water is your first sign that the First Spring is
failing, and the start of Orath's own story.

| If you | You gain |
|---|---|
| Kept the chamber shared | Amsel's trust, and the four Keybearers' attention |
| Gave it to the Solitary Light | Cassian's trust, and three faiths' resentment |
| Opened it to the pilgrims | Oriel's trust, and a chamber nobody can control |
| Sealed it | The Spillward's gratitude, and a city that blames you when the spring weakens |
