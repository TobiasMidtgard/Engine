Dark magic makes wounds that will not heal, and somewhere something drinks from every one of them.
When your [corruption](../magic/corruption.md) reaches its last stage, **Claimed**, that something
notices you. This questline begins the first night you dream of a door made of skin, standing
slightly open.

## The calling

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the questline below.

The Waiting Wound does not command. It offers. Over a series of dreams, and then in the waking
world through its whisperers, it shows you what it could give: dark spells without their price,
wounds that never close on your enemies, and the ability to feel every wound you have ever made,
everywhere, at once.

Its whisperers are ordinary people: a surgeon in [Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md) who keeps
her patients just ill enough, a Pale Archive necromancer in [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md),
a quiet officer of Orravell's Office of Civic Integrity. Each of them carries a wound that has been
open for years, and is proud of it.

## Three ways it can end

```flow
start: The first dream | A door of skin, slightly open.
path: Accept | Become the Wound's champion, and keep one wound open forever.
path: Refuse | Cleanse your corruption the hard way, before it claims you.
path: Hunt it | Learn the Wound's law, and break it.
join: The Wound answers | It does not take refusal well.
end: Champion | Dark magic costs you nothing, and you can never heal again.
end: Cleansed | The long road through Orath, and a scar that remembers.
end: Godslayer | Heal every wound it has tasted, all at once.
```

| Path | What it asks | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Accept** | Choose one wound, your own or someone else's, and keep it open for the rest of your life | Dark spells without their cost in health, and power that grows with every wound you make |
| **Refuse** | Walk the cleansing road: the Covenant of Ash's wound-houses, the First Spring of Orath, and the people you hurt | Your corruption falls to nothing. The Wound sends its whisperers after you |
| **Hunt it** | Discover its law: it cannot die while any wound it has tasted stays unhealed | The hardest Godslayer path in the game, and an army of healers to build |

To kill the Waiting Wound, every wound it has ever tasted must be healed at once, including the ones
you made. You will need every healer of the Covenant of Ash, every Vitalist who will follow you, and
a reason for each of the people you hurt to let you mend them.
