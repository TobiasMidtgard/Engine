Three days ago, the water patterns of an upper farm terrace in [Tessara](../cities/tessara.md)
started sending water the wrong way. The next day the heat patterns of the terraces below went
cold. Today the trams to the farm districts stopped, and the farms tended by their own machines
have begun to harvest crops that aren't ripe. Each failure has caused the next one. If the
terrace pumps stop, a fifth of Tessara's food goes with them.

## The job

[Thera Kallis](../people/thera-kallis.md) takes you on as a Tiler, the lowest rank in the
Office, and sends you into the Menders' tunnels with a pattern-reader and a bag of spare tiles.
Find where the chain of failures began. Keep the pumps running while the Office decides how to
mend it.

## The cascade

- **The upper terrace**: a water tile, laid last spring, carries a pattern that isn't in the
  Public Book.
- **The heat line**: the warm water from the Mnemeion's computing arrays has been rerouted, by
  hand, to somewhere the maps don't show.
- **The trams**: stopped by Ison, which decided the farm districts were unsafe to reach. Nobody
  told it to.
- **The underground district**: the Menders know a shortcut through the old tunnels, and the
  Hairline knows who laid the unlisted tile.

## Three repairs

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the job below.

The unlisted tile was laid by the Hairline for a Closed Kiln that wanted to test a black-glazed
pump on the farms in secret, using the city's own water. The test went wrong, and the faults spread
through the network faster than anyone could follow. Now the Office's three leaders each offer a
fix:

| Repair | Who | What it does to the city |
|---|---|---|
| **Common tiles** | Thera | Replace the whole terrace with simple, open patterns any farmer can mend. Slow: some crops are lost. |
| **Network control** | [Doros Menti](../people/doros-menti.md) | Give Ison control of every farm pattern, so this can never happen again. Fast, and nobody else can touch them afterwards. |
| **The Kiln's pump** | [Ione Sarakis](../people/ione-sarakis.md) | Finish the Closed Kiln's black-glazed pump: twice as good, and owned by the Kiln forever. |

```flow
start: Follow the failures | From the farms down to the tunnels.
path: Through the Menders' tunnels | Find the rerouted heat line.
path: Through the Hairline | Find who laid the unlisted tile.
path: Through the Mnemeion | Ask Ison why it stopped the trams.
join: The pumps are failing | The Office must choose a repair.
end: Common tiles | Open, mendable, and slow.
end: Network control | Safe, fast, and Ison's alone.
end: The Kiln's pump | Brilliant, and owned.
```

## What it leads to

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the guild questline below.

You become a **Tiler** of the Pattern Office. What you found and who you told decide who trusts you:

| If you | You gain |
|---|---|
| Laid the common tiles with the farmers | Thera's trust, and the Menders' friendship |
| Gave Ison the farms | Doros's trust, and a pass to the Mnemeion's inner galleries |
| Finished the Kiln's pump | Ione's trust, and a Closed Kiln that pays very well |
| Exposed the Kiln and the Hairline in the Open Measure | Tessara's gratitude, and two dangerous enemies |
