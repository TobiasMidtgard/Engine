**Amsel Dray** is the **concord keeper** of the [Accord of Four Keys](../factions/accord-of-four-keys.md)
and its oldest Warden. His grandmother was one of the rescuers in the story that made the Accord:
people of four faiths who saved each other when Orath burned and the Spring kept flowing under the
ruins. Amsel believes no single faith may own the sacred centre, and that shared custody of the
Spring is the only thing standing between Orath and another religious war.

He is seventy, white-bearded and tireless, with a Warden's sword he has drawn exactly twice and a
habit of answering questions with stories about his grandmother.
