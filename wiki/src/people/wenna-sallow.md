**Captain Wenna Sallow** commands the Chartered Road's **outriders**, the riders who police the
Five Roads, escort the caravans and chase down stolen cargo. She is forty-five, weathered, short and
quick, with a crossbow on her saddle and a road map tattooed on the inside of her left forearm. She
believes tariffs, blockades and sanctions hurt ordinary people more than anyone, and that a free
road is worth more than a legal one. Her outriders are the safest escort on the continent and the
best smugglers, and she sees no contradiction.

[Kit Fenn](kit-fenn.md) was one of her riders. She has not stopped looking for whoever fired the bolt.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Escort** | Outriders for you or a caravan, on any of the Five Roads | 2 marks a rider a day |
| **Training** | Riding and Bows, up to Expert | See [Training](../play/skills.md#training) |
| **Free routes** | Once she trusts you: the smugglers' paths around every Toll Ring (see [Travel](../play/travel.md)) | A favour |

## In quests

- [Three Accounts of One Caravan](../quests/three-accounts-of-one-caravan.md): her outriders found the
  caravan, and her account is one of the three.
- After [The Restriking Is Called](../quests/main/the-restriking-is-called.md) puts you on the
  Unsealed List, her free routes are the only way most caravans will carry you.
- At [The Lantern Table](../quests/main/the-lantern-table.md) her free-route captains choose a side.
  They choose yours if you found Kit's killer.
