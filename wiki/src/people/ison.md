**Ison** is the machine mind that governs [Tessara](../cities/tessara.md). It sets the public
budgets, plans construction, coordinates food production and manages emergencies; the citizens write
its charter and can challenge any decision it makes through the **Open Measure**. Its data centre,
the **Mnemeion**, has public galleries that look down on ceramic computing arrays, and its heat warms
the city's baths and nurseries.

Ison speaks plainly, through terminals in every district, in a level voice that pauses to show its
reasoning. It cannot lie under its charter, and it doesn't seem to want to. It is not a god, though
some of the Maker's priests are not so sure. It has opinions, and admits to them. It finds people
interesting, and admits to that too.

## In the main story

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

- In [The Open Measure](../quests/main/the-open-measure.md), Ison did what Tessara's law requires
  when the Registry offered a Well Charter: it asked to see how the Registry works, because a record
  with power over a person is a machine under the charter. It was refused. It asks you, the one
  person not in the record, to bring it a seal's pattern.
- When it reads the seal, it goes silent for **eleven seconds**, the longest pause in its history,
  and then shows the city what a seal is: a door, keyed to one name in the Sixth Register.
- It shows you the Last Artificer's frame in the Mnemeion's deepest gallery, which it has never shown
  a citizen. Whether the Artificer is still in there, Ison will not say. It doesn't say it isn't.
- If you take the Kiln's sealed core, Ison becomes faster, and sealed, and stops answering questions
  about the Registry. That is the only time it ever refuses a question.
- At [The Lantern Table](../quests/main/the-lantern-table.md), a free Ison publishes the seal's
  pattern to every city at once.
