**Jori "Latch" Pell** is a master locksmith of [the Tangle](../factions/thieves-guild.md),
Vantress's thieves' guild, who has refused every promotion he has been offered. His maintenance
work gives him a legitimate way into the Great Winch House of the
[Lowwater Chainyards](../cities/vantress/lowwater-chainyards.md).

## Personality

Jori lives by a code: no unnecessary killing, no abandoning accomplices, no taking a job and
quietly changing its terms, and no taking more than the agreed target. His professional
neutrality often ends up serving wealthy clients.

## Services

Locks, traps, mechanisms and engineering.

## Quests

- The **Clean Work** branch of the guild questline, with [Tal Oran](tal-oran.md).
