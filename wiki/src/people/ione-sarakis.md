**Ione Sarakis** is the best pattern architect in the world, and almost nobody is allowed to see her
work. Investors from [Sky City](../cities/sky-city.md) fund the Closed Kilns' research, and they only
fund designs they can own and hide; Ione's black-glazed patterns are hidden so well that nobody but
her can mend them. She thinks invention needs money, money needs ownership, and the Public Book is a
lovely idea that would have left Tessara fifty years behind.

She is thirty-five, brilliant, impatient and elegant, and she is not as sure as she sounds.

| Service | What | Price |
