**Marshal Joska Kroll** holds the **Iron Anchor**, the chain of soldiers, engineers and
traditionalists who want [Sky City](../cities/sky-city.md) fixed in place for good. He is sixty,
grey, rigid and honourable, and he has checked the Iron chain's winches every morning for thirty
years. When the chains went slack, he assumed sabotage, because in his experience everything that
goes wrong is somebody's fault.

| Service | What | Price |

| **Training** | Heavy armour and Spears, up to Expert | See [Training](../play/skills.md#training) |

## In the main story

In [The Island Sinks](../quests/main/the-island-sinks.md), Kroll sends you to check the winches. They
have not been touched. If you expose the Registry's surveyors in the Deepworks, the Council refuses
the Charter for now, and Kroll owes you. At [The Lantern Table](../quests/main/the-lantern-table.md),
the Iron Anchor sends airships if Sky City refused.
