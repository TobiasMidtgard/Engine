**Hadrin Vale** owns several lacquer works in [Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md) and a
profitable share in the city's bridge-inspection contracts. He is rich, but still trying to join
the city's true ruling class.

## Overview

He lives at [the Vale](../cities/vantress/argent-heights/the-vale.md) in Argent Heights with his
wife, their two children and his widowed sister, a steward, three servants and two hired guards.
