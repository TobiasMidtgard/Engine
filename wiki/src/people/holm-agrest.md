**Holm Agrest** leads the [Keepers' Road](../factions/keepers-road.md)'s largest travelling chapter: forty
wagons, two hundred animals and an old tortoise, moving slowly along the Five Roads selling, stabling
and treating animals as it goes. He believes in responsible ownership and selective breeding under
welfare rules, and his bloodlines of pack horses and bargebacks are the best on the roads.

He is sixty, weathered, patient and fussy, and he has carried [Rain-Back](rain-back.md) across three
rivers and complained every time.
