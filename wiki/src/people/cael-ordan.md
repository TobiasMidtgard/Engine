**Cael Ordan** rules [Orravell](../cities/orravell.md) as **First Gardener** and **Commander of the
Crown**. He rose out of the crisis that followed a long regional peace (unemployment, unpaid
military debts, collapsing investment) promising national renewal through discipline, permanent
mobilisation and expansion, and he has delivered all three. Under him Orravell is a one-party state:
the Council of Stewards carries out his decisions, the Office of Civic Integrity watches everyone
else, and the Doctrine of the Greater Canopy says the lands around Orravell will only know order
under its branches.

He is sixty, lean, silver-haired and gentle-voiced, and he really does like children. He carries
**the Gardener's shears**, said to be forged from the first raider's blade, and uses them at every
ceremony to cut one green shoot.

## In the main story

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

In [The Price of Kestrel Vale](../quests/main/the-price-of-kestrel-vale.md), the Fifth Reserve offers
Ordan a loan large enough to occupy Kestrel Vale for ten years at no interest, if Orravell signs a
Well Charter and its Crown Companies become **the Restriking's guarantors**. If you break the loan,
he publicly refuses the Charter and privately wants the gold back. If you let it stand, his
Companies march on the Gilded Ascent as the Restriking's army.

The loan has a second clause he hasn't read: the Fifth Reserve buys every debt Kestrel Vale owes, so
the valley will owe the Gilded Ascent, not Orravell. Showing him this clause is the surest way to
turn him, because the one thing Cael Ordan cannot bear is to be pruned.
