**Mags Rennick** runs the open pits under Arena City's canals, where the Brass Circuit's rules don't
reach. Formal rules favour the rich, she says: real brawling allows improvised weapons, the terrain,
and anyone willing to step in. Anyone can fight in her pits, and anyone does.

She is forty, wiry, gap-toothed and loud, and fights in her own pits once a month to prove she can.
