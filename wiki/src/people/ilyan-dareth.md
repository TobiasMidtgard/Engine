**Ilyan Dareth** is a water engineer of the **Fifth Seat**, [Orath](../cities/orath.md)'s civil
government, and the person who understands the First Spring's plumbing better than anyone alive. He
is thirty-five, earnest and badly organised, with ink on his cuffs and a surveyor's chain in every
pocket. He is not religious. He is very careful about saying so in Orath.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Training** | Engineering, up to Expert | See [Training](../play/skills.md#training) |
| **Surveys** | Maps of the channels, galleries and old waterworks under Orath | 5 marks each, or free if you bring him back alive |

## In the main story

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

In [A Spring Running Dry](../quests/main/a-spring-running-dry.md), Ilyan has found an abandoned
channel beneath the Underchoir, behind burial galleries that the Deep Choir won't let anyone dig
through. One of his crew follows it further than she means to and finds the fifth passage, and a
door of black stone. Whatever happens to the page, Ilyan's survey shows that the Hollow Crown's
foundations, the Counting Deep beneath the Gilded Ascent and the rock under Sky City are the same
black stone.

His survey is evidence at [The Restriking Is Called](../quests/main/the-restriking-is-called.md), and
it is one of the things that can tell [Ulf Garrick](companions/ulf-garrick.md) what his Awakening cost.
