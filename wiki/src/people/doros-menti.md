**Doros Menti** directs the Pattern Office, and he would like Tessara to be one machine: a single
coordinated network, run with Ison, that predicts demand and prevents failures before anyone
notices them. He trusts Ison more than he trusts people, and he is usually right, which makes him
very hard to argue with.

He is sixty, soft-spoken and tireless, and he has not taken a day off in eleven years.
