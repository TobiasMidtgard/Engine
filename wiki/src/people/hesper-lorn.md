**Hesper Lorn** is the **Sealkeeper**: she chairs the Court of Seals at the Lantern Table, keeps the
master dies from which every seal on the continent is struck, and oversees the Mint of Seals. She
believes in the Five Seals completely. They bring trust, security and steady advancement, she says,
and a world without them is a world where the strong take what they like and nobody keeps count.

She is seventy, tall, straight-backed and severe, with white hair cut short and a voice used to
ending arguments. Thirty years ago her younger brother forged a Silver Seal to cover a debt, and she
presided over his unsealing herself. She has not spoken to him since. She is the most honest person
on the Summit, and the least forgiving.

Around her neck, on a chain of plain iron, she wears the **Sixth Seal**: a seal of black stone that
every Sealkeeper for four hundred years has worn and sworn never to use. Nobody living knows what it
opens.

## In the main story

- In [The Restriking Is Called](../quests/main/the-restriking-is-called.md) she is one of the four
  chairs that vote to hear Ivo Tarrant. If you have won her over, she meets you at the bottom of the

- In [Steal the Register](../quests/main/steal-the-register.md), the last door of the Counting Deep
  opens only to the Sixth Seal. You can ask her for it (she will make you swear what you'll do with
  it), have Jori Pell lift it from her neck at the Lantern Table, or persuade Garrick to bring it.
- At [The Lantern Table](../quests/main/the-lantern-table.md) she leads the old seal holders. She
  votes against the Great Restriking if you have shown her what a seal is.

## Winning her over

Hesper respects law, honesty and evidence, and despises flattery and forgery. She can be won by:

- Bringing her proof she can hold: the Rillvault rubbing, the water-sellers' ledger from Orath, or
  the Sky City surveyors' notebook.
- **Not** forging a seal, at any point. If you have, she knows, and that is the end of it.
- Telling her about her brother, who is alive, unsealed, and working the Lowmarket outside the walls.
  (She will not thank you. She will listen.)
