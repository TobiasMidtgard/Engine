**Examiner Sabra Quint** keeps and reads the [Spillward](../factions/spillward.md)'s records: every
spill, every licence and every inspection in Veyrhold for two hundred years. She is fifty, thin,
precise and dry, wears spectacles of tinted hushstone glass that let her see the faint residue of old
spells on paper, and has a reputation for finding the one wrong line in a thousand pages.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Spill records** | Who was licensed, inspected or blamed for any spill in Veyrhold | A reason she believes |
| **Reading residue** | Teaches you to read old spells on paper, cloth and stone (+5 Attunement-based sensing) | A quest, below |

## In the main story

In [Behind the Nine Shut Doors](../quests/main/behind-the-nine-shut-doors.md), Sabra wants to know
why the original safety report on the disaster at the Nine Shut Doors was altered. Help her, and
she opens a way into the side-cut.

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

The report was altered by a young Registry clerk sent to "record the damage", and the clerk initialled
her change: **G. Quell**. It is the first document with Quell's own hand on a lie. Sabra takes a copy
to the Spillward, and another, quietly, to Tessara's Pattern Office, so that it can't be lost.
