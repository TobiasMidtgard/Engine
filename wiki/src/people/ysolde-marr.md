**Ysolde Marr** studies spills from the inside. Transformation, forbidden magic and instability, she
argues, might be the next great discovery, and the Spillward's habit of sealing and erasing them
throws away the most interesting things that ever happen in Veyrhold. She has been inside more spills
than anyone alive. Her left eye is a different colour from her right, her shadow is a little late,
and she hums at a pitch that makes hushstone warm.

She is forty, delighted by everything and impossible to frighten, and her notebooks are the best
record of what magic does when nobody is in charge of it.
