**Benedek Strawn** grows medicine like grain. His tunnel farms in [Rillvault](../cities/rillvault.md)
grow healing moss by the tonne, and he argues that farming and breeding at industrial scale is the
only way to make medicine cheap and leave the wild valleys alone. Ilse Moorcroft thinks his moss is
weaker than wild moss. He thinks that's what a second dose is for.

He is fifty, round, sunless and kind, and he gives his moss away in famine years.
