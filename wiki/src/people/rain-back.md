**Rain-Back** is an old tortoise of [Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md), much older than any tortoise
should be, with a shell as wide as a cart wheel and grown over with moss, lichen and one small,
persistent fern. It travels with Holm Agrest's chapter of the [Keepers' Road](../factions/keepers-road.md)
as Oruvai's envoy, and it speaks through a listener bloom in a voice that sounds like a forest
agreeing with itself. It believes no living creature can be property, and that the guild should
become a sanctuary and an embassy. It is in no hurry to win the argument.

Rain-Back is the only creature of Oruvai known to have walked the whole continent and stayed in the
Chorus all the way. It carries the city with it, somehow, in the fern.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Training** | Animal handling and the [Wilds branch of Vitalism](../magic/vitalism.md#wilds), up to Master | Not money: a need of the city's, met |
| **Listener bloom** | One, grown for you, so you can hear Oruvai anywhere | A seed planted somewhere it will grow |
| **The roads** | Where each road to Awakening starts (see [A Door Nobody Locked](../quests/main/a-door-nobody-locked.md)) | Patience |

## In the main story

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

- In [The Human Limit](../quests/main/the-human-limit.md) Rain-Back has been walking towards you for
  months, and meets you on the road after Garrick: "You have reached the edge. Good. Most never see
  it."
- In [A Door Nobody Locked](../quests/main/a-door-nobody-locked.md) it tells you five roads past the
  human limit, and where each one starts.
- In [Steal the Register](../quests/main/steal-the-register.md) it has somehow reached Vantress, and
  tells you that you are close to something new.
- In [A Law of Your Own](../quests/main/a-law-of-your-own.md) the world's Ascendants come to look at
  you, and Rain-Back is among them: it has been an **Ascendant** all along, of **Patience**, and never
  thought it worth mentioning. Its law: nothing that waits for Rain-Back is ever too late.

## Relationships

- **[Moss-Under-Stone](companions/moss-under-stone.md)**: Rain-Back is the only creature who can tell
  Moss how to stay in the Chorus so far from home.
- **[Sere](sere.md)**: they agree about almost everything, and argue about the rest, slowly.
- **[Varo Kett](companions/varo-kett.md)**: Rain-Back finds him sad, and tells him so.
- **[Holm Agrest](holm-agrest.md)**: has carried Rain-Back across three rivers, and complains every time.
