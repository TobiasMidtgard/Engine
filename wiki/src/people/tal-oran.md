**Tal Oran** is the Weight of Work of [the Tangle](../factions/thieves-guild.md), Vantress's
thieves' guild, and its greatest heist planner.

## Personality

Tal wants the guild to leave neighbourhood politics behind and become the finest criminal
organisation in the world: jobs that are non-lethal, carefully planned and fairly paid. His jobs
take no side, which in practice means they mostly serve the rich.

## Services

Planning, timing, coordinating crews and pulling off several objectives at once.

Trains Sleight of hand up to Expert (see [Training](../play/skills.md#training)); Tangle members pay half.
