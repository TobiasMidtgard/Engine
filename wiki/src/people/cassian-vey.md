**Brother Cassian Vey** of the Solitary Light believes one of Orath's four revelations came first,
and that Orath needs a clear doctrine to survive what is coming. He is learned, sincere, gentle and
very persuasive. The crowds on the Dawn Steps grow every week, and the people who agree with him
most loudly are frightening.

He is forty, thin, bright-eyed and humble, and he does not see the danger in himself.
