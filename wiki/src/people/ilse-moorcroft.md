**Ilse Moorcroft** is one of the seven Elders of the [Root Ledger](../factions/root-ledger.md) and the
steward of its harvest grounds: never harvest anything faster than it grows back, she says, and she
has walked every harvest ground in the ledger to make sure nobody does. She is seventy-five, stooped,
sharp-eyed and impatient with fools, and she can name the plants of a valley by its smell.

## In quests

- [Not Enough to Go Around](../quests/not-enough-to-go-around.md): the Root Ledger's own quest.
- [What the Builders Buried](../quests/main/what-the-builders-buried.md): Ilse opens the **Echo
  Galleries** below Rillvault's springs for you. If you keep what you find quiet, she is
  disappointed in you.
