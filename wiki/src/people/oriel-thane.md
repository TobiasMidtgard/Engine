**Oriel Thane** is a wayspeaker of the Accord of Four Keys: they travel the pilgrim roads into and out
of Orath, and listen at shrines that nobody else has visited in years. People should speak directly
with local spirits, ancestors and sacred places, Oriel says, without four institutions standing in
the way. The Keybearers find Oriel inconvenient and very hard to dislike.

Oriel is fifty, weathered, barefoot in all weathers, and laughs easily.
