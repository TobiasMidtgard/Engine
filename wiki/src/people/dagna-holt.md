**Dagna Holt, "the Anvil"**, held the Champion's belt of the [Brass Circuit](../factions/brass-circuit.md)
for nine years and has refereed every major bout since. She wants a fair fight and a way up for
anyone: protection for fighters, honest rankings, and a life after retirement. Her own knees are
ruined, and she tells everyone exactly whose fault it was (a promoter's, and a sand floor that
should have been raked).

She is fifty-five, enormous, slow-moving and very fast when she needs to be.
