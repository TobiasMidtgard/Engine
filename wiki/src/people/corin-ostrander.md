**Corin Ostrander** draws patterns for the Pattern Office by day and, by night, works with the
Spillward in Veyrhold on something neither of them has a name for: patterns laid in stone that drink
a little magic and change themselves. He believes a pattern could learn. He has drawn one that does.
It is very small, it lives in a tile on his windowsill, and it has started to rearrange itself
towards the light.

He is twenty-eight, shy, polite and badly dressed, and he has no idea how dangerous his work is.
