**Edrin Vasse** is a **Hand** of the [Last Writ](../factions/last-writ.md), one rank below the Last
Word, and the guild's chief bodyguard. He believes killing is justified only when every lawful option
has failed, and he wants the guild to protect witnesses, rescue marked targets and hunt rogue
assassins. He has killed eleven people under verified writs and regrets nine of them.

He is fifty, tall, grey-eyed and almost silent, with a shortsword and a small round shield, and he
is the best defensive fighter in the Eaves.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Bodyguards** | A Last Writ guard for you, or for someone you name | 5 marks a night |
| **Training** | One-handed blades (Shieldmate) and Shields, up to Master | See [Training](../play/skills.md#training) |
| **A writ's protection** | Shelters a marked person under the guild's own protection, until a writ is reviewed | A case he believes |

## In quests

- [A Writ in Question](../quests/a-writ-in-question.md): he gives you the job of escorting a witness.
- [A Seal for the Eaves](../quests/main/a-seal-for-the-eaves.md): with your name on a verified Red
  Writ, Edrin will help you void it, for a price: you guard a witness of his.
