**Perrin Haske** organises production for the Compact, and he thinks most of the lodges are fighting
the last war. Machines and standard recipes make goods affordable, he says, even if many workers are
no longer needed, and a coat everyone can buy is worth more than a coat a master spent a month on.
He has bought three Tessaran pattern looms. The weavers' lodge has not forgiven him.

He is forty, neat, energetic and cheerful, and he is right about the prices.
