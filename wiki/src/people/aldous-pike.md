**Master Aldous Pike** is the Compact's most respected enchanter, and its most stubborn defender of
the old ways: skill needs hierarchy, long apprenticeships and protected techniques, and a craft
that anyone can learn in a season is a craft nobody can trust. His own masterpiece, the sword
**Patience**, cannot be dulled. It took him eleven years. Every enchanter leaves an **accent** in
their work (see [Enchanting](../magic/enchanting.md)), and Pike's is so precise that forgers study it.

He is seventy, small, white-haired, courteous and utterly certain, and he takes one apprentice a
decade.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Training** | Enchanting up to Master, to Compact members of Journeyman rank or above | See [Training](../play/skills.md#training) |
| **Commissions** | A custom enchantment on your gear | From 20 marks to many ascents |
| **Appraisal** | Who enchanted an item, when, and whether it is blank work | 1 mark |
