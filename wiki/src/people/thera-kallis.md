**Thera Kallis** runs Tessara's water, and she runs it in the open: every pipe, pump and fountain
pattern she designs is published in the Public Book, and is simple enough to be mended on the spot by
anyone who can read a tile. Her water patterns are so simple that children in the poorest district
repair their own fountains, and she counts that as her life's work.

She is forty-five, tall, cheerful and relentless, with tile-dust under her nails and a laugh you can
hear across the Measure Forum.
