**Captain Sera Noll** commands the glider crews of **Hushwing Station**, the launch ledges high in
Orravell's great tree where the Crown Companies' silent gliders take off for scouting and raids. She
is thirty-eight, small and wiry, with a flier's squint and a scar under her chin from a bad landing.
She is loyal to her crews first, the Companies second and the Crown a distant third.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Flights** | A glider ride to anywhere within a day's flight of Orravell | 2 marks, or a favour |
| **Training** | Piloting, Flyer branch, up to Expert; gliders only | See [Training](../play/skills.md#training) |

## In quests

- [Operation Second Spring](../quests/operation-second-spring.md): Sera insists on bringing her crews
  home when the operation begins.
- [The Price of Kestrel Vale](../quests/main/the-price-of-kestrel-vale.md): you can get into Orravell's
  government as one of her glider crew. If you help bring her crews home before the operation
  begins, she and her gliders will come when you call, once.
- [The Company Ledger](companions/brenn-askew.md#the-company-ledger): Brenn Askew's company can walk
  out of Kestrel Vale through Hushwing Station at night, with Sera's help.
