**Gisela Quell** is the **Registrar of Seals** of [the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md):
the keeper of the ledger that records every seal ever struck, and so every person on the continent
who matters to a bank, a court or a caravan. She has held the office for nineteen years and has
never been found to make a single mistake in it.

She is small, grey-haired, tidy and soft-spoken, in grey and silver with a Glass Seal on a fine
chain. She remembers the names of porters' children and asks after them. She writes everything
down, in a neat, slanted hand that every clerk in the Registry learns to imitate. People who meet
her come away thinking she is the kindest official in the city, and they are not entirely wrong.

## What she wants

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

Quell believes that the cruelty of the world comes from things that are not kept: wells that run
wild and spill, springs that cities fight over, people who vanish because nobody wrote them down,
magic that is cheap for whoever happens to live on top of it. She has spent her life making a city
where nobody disappears, and she wants to make a world like it.

Forty years ago, as a young Registry clerk sent to Veyrhold to "record the damage" of a quarry
disaster, she altered a safety report to hide a door of black stone. She initialled it **G. Quell**
(see [Behind the Nine Shut Doors](../quests/main/behind-the-nine-shut-doors.md)). She has been
looking for doors ever since.

What she found, at the bottom of the Counting Deep, was the **Sixth Register**: the First Builders'
ledger of every well in the world, and the key to each. She learned to read it. She learned that a
well written into it is a well the Registrar holds. Then she wrote herself in as its keeper.

- She is closing the wells one by one, and offering each city a **Well Charter** to open them again:
  sign the seal league, put every citizen under a seal, and drink.
- Every seal is a small lock keyed to the Register. Every lock passes something through it to what
  is on the other side: the [God of Locked Doors](../lore/gods.md#the-god-of-locked-doors).
- At the **Great Restriking** she means to seal the continent in one night and stand in the god's
  door when it opens: its voice, its keeper, or its replacement. She is not sure which, and she is
  not afraid.

## As an enemy

Quell is an **Ascendant of Keeping**. She doesn't fight like a warrior: she **locks** things. Your
doors, your abilities one at a time, your companions behind walls that weren't there a moment ago,
and, if she holds Keth Veyra's door, time itself. Every seal the Mint strikes above heals her.

| | The Great Restriking |
|---|---|
| **Level** | 100 |
| **Health** | 8,000, and every seal struck above restores 40 |
| **Poise** | Can't be staggered while she holds a lock |
| **Locks** | Doors, then your strongest ability, then a companion, then time, then your name |
| **Weak to** | Trespass, the Unwritten and Severance (see [A Law of Your Own](../quests/main/a-law-of-your-own.md)); a broken die above; the Register's stand |
| **Drops** | Her Glass Seal; whatever is left of the Sixth Register; the **Keeper's Pen** |

## What she says

> Nobody drowns in a locked world, nobody sinks, nobody starves for want of a spring they don't own.
> Every door I close is one fewer place to fall.

> I have written down every person in this city. I have never once written down a lie. You are the
> only thing in the world I can't write down, and I find I would very much like to.

## Relationships

- **[Hesper Lorn](hesper-lorn.md)**, the Sealkeeper: an old friend. Quell asked her for the Sixth
  Seal and was refused.
- **[Ulf Garrick](companions/ulf-garrick.md)**: her warden-captain. She bought his Awakening, and
  never told him with what.
- **[Ivo Tarrant](ivo-tarrant.md)**: a reformer she rather likes, and had suspended.
- **[The Broker](the-broker.md)** of the Second City's Night Council: paid, through the Fifth Reserve.
- **[Old Tamm](old-tamm.md)**: the man whose report she altered, forty years ago.

## If she lives

Some endings let Quell live: as your prisoner, your enemy, or (if you **Keep the keys**) a very
strange adviser who knows the Register better than you do and would like it back.
