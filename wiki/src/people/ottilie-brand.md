**Ottilie Brand** is the best alchemist in [Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md), and probably on the
continent. She believes in concentration and mutation: refine a herb far enough, cross it with
something stranger, and you get medicines, stimulants, narcotics and poisons stronger than anything
that grows. Most of what she brews is legal. She would like all of it to be, and she argues the point
in court twice a year.

She is sixty, sharp-nosed, stained to the elbows and gleeful, and she tests everything on herself first.
