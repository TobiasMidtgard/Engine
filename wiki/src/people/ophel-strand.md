**Ophel Strand** keeps the Last Writ's register: every Red Writ bought in the Second City passes
across her desk to be checked, sealed and hung in the Quiet Bell. She believes personal morality is
what turns assassins into political servants, and that every properly verified writ must be honoured,
whoever bought it, or the whole system collapses into ordinary murder. She has never killed anyone
and never will.

She is sixty, small, iron-haired and exact, and she has not made a mistake in a writ in thirty years.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Verification** | Checks a Red Writ: buyer, papers, fee and term | The guild's fee |
| **Voiding** | A verified writ can be voided only by the buyer, false papers, or the Last Word | Proof |

## In quests

- [A Writ in Question](../quests/a-writ-in-question.md): the hearing is hers.
- [A Seal for the Eaves](../quests/main/a-seal-for-the-eaves.md): she verified the writ on your name,
  and won't tell you who bought it. If you bring her proof of false papers, she voids it herself, at
  once, and apologises.
