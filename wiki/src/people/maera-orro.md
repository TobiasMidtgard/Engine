**Maera "Cask" Orro** runs [the Drunken Sailor](../cities/vantress/lantern-ward/drunken-sailor.md)
tavern in Vantress and keeps the stores of [the Tangle](../factions/thieves-guild.md), the
thieves' guild: its fences, its stolen goods, its emergency supplies and the community's share of
every job.

## Personality

Maera wants one central fund that shares wealth out between the neighbourhoods. That also gives
her the power to decide who gets food and medicine.

## Services

Fencing, appraisal, equipment, supplies and trade. At the end of a job she weighs up what a haul
is really worth:

> Fence value = sale price − cost of laundering − heat − damage − partners' shares
