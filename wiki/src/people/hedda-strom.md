**Hedda Strom** is the steward of the [Makers' Compact](../factions/makers-compact.md)'s smiths' lodge,
and its fiercest voice for the idea that the people who make things should own the places they make
them in, share what they know and bargain together. She led the last strike in
[Orravell](../cities/orravell.md)'s factories, and spent a year in its prisons for it. She came out
with a cough, a list of names and no regrets.

She is fifty, broad, burn-scarred and loud, and she is the best smith in the Compact, which is the
only reason the masters put up with her.
