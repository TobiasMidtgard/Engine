**Orlo Vance, "the Voice"**, promotes the Brass Circuit's fights, and he is very good at it.
Rivalries, gambling, dangerous formats and celebrity, he says, are what fill the terraces, and the
terraces pay for everything else in Arena City. He has never thrown a punch and is the richest man
in the guild. His voice, magically carried, can be heard in every tier of the arena at once.

He is forty-five, glittering, warm and shameless.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Bookings** | A billed fight on the night floor, with a purse | The purse is the house's share |
| **Betting** | Bets on any bout | The house takes a tenth |
| **Fame** | Billed fights raise your renown in Arena City (see [Reputation](../play/reputation.md)) | Win |
