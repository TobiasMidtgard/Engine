**Nilo "Eel" Sare** is an independent bridge-runner in Vantress and a former member of
[the Tangle](../factions/thieves-guild.md). He moves across roofs and hidden bridges that most
people never see.

## Personality

Nilo believes the guild has made crime a monopoly. He wants independent crews and knowledge that
is shared rather than owned.

## Services

Parkour, hidden routes, rooftop travel and contacts outside the guild.

Trains Acrobatics and Climbing up to Expert (see [Training](../play/skills.md#training)); Tangle members pay half.

## Quests

- The **Loose Water** branch of the guild questline.
