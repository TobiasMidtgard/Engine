**Ivo Tarrant** is the Charter Captain of the western road: he runs the Chartered Road's caravans,
insurance and outriders from the Gilded Ascent to Vantress, and he sits on the Court of Seals as
one of its loudest voices for an **open ledger**. Prices, ownership and fees should be public, he
says; lesser merchants shouldn't need a rich patron to reach the higher floors. He started as a
drover on a Clay Seal at fourteen.

He is a big, tired man of sixty with a farmer's hands and a ledger clerk's eyes, who reads every
letter twice and every contract three times. He laughs loudly and rarely. He likes people who tell
him things he doesn't want to hear, and he has no patience at all for people who flatter him.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Caravan contracts** | Guard, scout and drive jobs on the western road | Pays 1 to 3 marks a day |
| **Insurance** | Your goods insured on any road the Chartered Road polices | A tenth of their value |
| **Training** | Trade and Persuasion, up to Expert | See [Training](../play/skills.md#training) |
| **Sponsorship** | A Copper Seal, if you want one (he will be disappointed if you take it) | Free to guild members |

After [The Restriking Is Called](../quests/main/the-restriking-is-called.md) he is suspended, and
his services move with him: he can still find you caravan work, but only on roads the Registry
doesn't watch.

## In the main story

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

- In [Unsealed](../quests/main/unsealed.md) he reads Kit Fenn's letter twice and the black page not
  at all, because he knows what it is: a page of the Sixth Register.
- He sends you to Orath, Sky City and Rillvault to find out what the page does, and takes your
  evidence to the Court of Seals in [The Restriking Is Called](../quests/main/the-restriking-is-called.md).
  The Court votes eleven to four not to hear it, and suspends him.
- In Act II he becomes your network: he knows every road, every waystation and every factor on the
  continent, and he finds you work and allies wherever the Registry's envoys go.
- In [The Open Measure](../quests/main/the-open-measure.md), if he trusts you completely, he gives
  you his own Gold Seal for Ison to read.
- At [The Lantern Table](../quests/main/the-lantern-table.md) he leads the reformers of the
  Chartered Road against Quell, openly in rebellion.

## Relationships

- **[Hesper Lorn](hesper-lorn.md)**: his oldest rival on the Court, and one of the four chairs that
  voted to hear him.
- **[Wenna Sallow](wenna-sallow.md)**: her outriders carry his letters when the Registry reads the
  rest. Kit Fenn was one of hers.
- **[Gisela Quell](gisela-quell.md)**: he liked her, once. He still can't quite believe it.
- **[Lucan Prell](lucan-prell.md)**: a man he would like to see unsealed.
