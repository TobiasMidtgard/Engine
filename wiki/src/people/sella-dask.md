**Sella "Scrip" Dask** is the Weight of Names of [the Tangle](../factions/thieves-guild.md),
Vantress's thieves' guild. She controls its informants, its blackmail, its compromised officials
and its false identities.

## Personality

Sella offers safety through watching everyone and keeping corruption under control: a quiet city
where nothing happens that she doesn't know about.

## Services

Intelligence, surveillance, blackmail and access to officials who have already been bought.

- The **Quiet Ledger** branch of the guild questline.
