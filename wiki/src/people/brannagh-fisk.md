**Brannagh Fisk** leads the **rescue crews** of Veyrhold's lift workers: the people who work the
great cable lifts down into the quarry, and the ones who go down when a lift jams, a ledge falls or a
spill swallows a workshop. She is forty, square, red-haired and scarred, and she has brought more
people up out of the quarry alive than anyone in the city's history. She doesn't trust anything she
hasn't measured.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Lift passage** | Down to any level of the quarry, including ones that are closed | 2 chits, or a favour |
| **Training** | Climbing, Rope-work branch, up to Expert | See [Training](../play/skills.md#training) |

## In the main story

In [Behind the Nine Shut Doors](../quests/main/behind-the-nine-shut-doors.md), Brannagh wants the
landlords' surveys published: her crews have found fresh cracks beside the Nine Shut Doors, and the
new dig is aimed straight at them. Help her get the surveys, and she opens a way into the sealed
side-cut.

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

- If you publish the surveys, the dig is halted, and Brannagh owes you.
- If you seal the crack, she and her lift workers bring every scrap of hushstone in the quarry.
- At [The Lantern Table](../quests/main/the-lantern-table.md), if Veyrhold refused the Charter, her
  lift workers come with Veyrhold's hushstone and the Spillward.
