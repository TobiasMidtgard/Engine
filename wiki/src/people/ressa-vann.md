**Ressa Vann** is a Knotkeeper of [the Tangle](../factions/thieves-guild.md), Vantress's thieves'
guild, and your first employer. She works from the public rope-repair shop at the front of the
Slipknot.

## Personality

Ressa believes every neighbourhood needs criminals who answer to the people who live there, not
to merchants, distant rulers or corrupt guards. She protects her raft district fiercely and
expects loyalty in return: those who turn her down can find that protection, food and safe
passage are suddenly hard to come by.

## Services

Persuasion, reputation, neighbourhood support and safehouses.

## Quests

- **Before the First Bell**: the contest that brings you into the guild (see

- The **Ward Knots** branch of the guild questline.
