**Tamsin Reeve** grew up in the Commons of [the Gilded Ascent](../../cities/gilded-ascent.md) under a
Clay Seal, and left at fifteen with three cantrips, a stolen primer and a strong opinion about who
magic belongs to. Now in her thirties, she teaches anyone who turns up at the Common Hearths on
[Veyrhold](../../cities/veyrhold.md)'s First Cut: apprentices, porters, children, the occasional
quarry landlord's daughter in a borrowed coat. She charges nothing, licenses nothing and cleans up
after every lesson, because a cantrip taught badly is a spill waiting for a street.

She is quick, funny, impatient with rules that exist to keep people out, and very patient with
people who are trying. She has a burn scar on her left hand from the first ward she ever taught
herself, and she still holds wards with that hand.

## Recruiting

You meet Tamsin inside the spill on Cutter's Row in [The Street That Changed](../../quests/the-street-that-changed.md),
holding the street together with a ward and getting tired. If she survives, and you make sure
the Spillward knows she saved Cutter's Row rather than caused it, she is at the Common Hearths
afterwards, and she will come with you if you ask:

> You walked into a spill to help a stranger. That's either very brave or very stupid, and I can
> teach either. Where are we going?

If you missed her in Act I, she is still at the Hearths during
[Behind the Nine Shut Doors](../../quests/main/behind-the-nine-shut-doors.md), teaching the Kiln Street
cooperative's apprentices in a borrowed loft, and she asks to come with you into the Nine Shut Doors.

## How she fights

Tamsin is a mage of [Evocation](../../magic/evocation.md) (Frost and Fire) and the Warding branch of
[Vitalism](../../magic/vitalism.md), and she only ever casts clean magic. She stays behind you, keeps your back warded and turns the ground
against anything that gets close.

| Ability | What it does | At |
|---|---|---|
| **Kitchen ward** | A ward on you or an ally that soaks up the next big hit, or the next spill | Always |
| **Frost and flint** | Two cantrips she can cast all day: a frost bolt that slows, and a spark that lights oil | Always |
| **Hold the line** | A wall of hardened air across a doorway or a bridge for ten seconds | Approval Friendly |
| **Drink the spill** | Pulls a magical spill, a runaway spell or an enemy's ward into a ward of her own, and lets it go somewhere safe | Her quest's first part |
| **Lesson** | Out of combat, she teaches you one Novice or Apprentice spell effect she knows, for free | Approval Loyal |

She starts at your level with Evocation and Vitalism at Adept. Give her a staff or a focus and light
armour; she won't wear heavy armour ("I can't feel the Ethereal through a kettle").

## Approval

| Tamsin approves of | Tamsin disapproves of |
|---|---|
| Teaching someone something, or letting her | Licences, seals and permissions that exist to keep people out |
| Helping the poor, the apprentices and the unsealed | Dark magic anywhere near bystanders |
| Cleaning up a spill, or a mess you made | Selling knowledge that should be free |
| Telling the truth about magic, even when it's frightening | Siding with the Registry, the landlords or Halvard Cray |
| Clean magic used cleverly | Taking credit for someone else's work |
| Mercy to people who were only trying to survive | Keeping a dangerous thing because it's useful |

She has opinions about corruption. If yours reaches **Marked**, she asks you about it once, kindly.
If it reaches **Tainted**, she asks again, not kindly.

## Free Hearths

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Tamsin's personal quest below.

When Tamsin is **Friendly**, a runner brings her a letter at camp. One of her students, a Kiln Street
apprentice called **Pim Arden**, has caused a spill in the Binding Yards with a ward Tamsin taught
him, and a journeyman's arm has turned to glass. Halvard Cray has arrested Pim and posted a notice:
every free class in Veyrhold is to be licensed, inspected and charged for, starting with hers.

Tamsin wants to go back. She wants to know what went wrong with a ward she has taught three hundred
times, and she doesn't want you to tell her it wasn't her fault until you are sure.

- **The ward**: Pim cast it correctly. Tamsin can show you, in the Common Hearths, on herself.
- **The Binding Yards**: the spill started on a workbench reinforced with the landlords' new cheap
  protective stone, the same stone they are promising in Behind the Nine Shut Doors. It isn't
  hushstone. It **reflects** magic instead of drinking it.
- **[Halvard Cray](../halvard-cray.md)**: he knows the stone is bad. He signed the landlords' certificate before he knew.
  He is trying to close the classes because it is easier than admitting it.

```flow
start: Pim Arden is in a cell | And every free class is to be licensed.
path: Prove the stone | Test it in front of the Spillward, with Tamsin's ward.
path: Free Pim | Out of the hushstone cells under the Spillward hall.
path: Talk to Halvard | He is ashamed. Shame can be useful.
join: The hearing | Tamsin, Pim and Halvard, before the Spillmaster.
end: The classes stay free | Halvard admits the certificate. The stone is pulled out of the Yards. Tamsin keeps teaching.
end: Tamsin takes a licence | The classes survive, inspected and charged for, and Tamsin hates it.
end: Tamsin takes the blame | Pim goes free. Tamsin loses the Hearths and is in the cells for a month, unless you break her out.
```

What she becomes:

| Ending | Tamsin |
|---|---|
| **The classes stay free** | Writes down every cantrip she knows in a book anyone can copy: *Free Hearths*. Her wards grow stronger (Drink the spill heals you). |
| **She takes a licence** | Is quieter for a while. She becomes a Spillward Warden and can open its sealed archives for you, and resents every page. |
| **She takes the blame** | Comes back harder and stranger. She stops caring about rules at all, including the ones about bystanders. |

## Romance

Tamsin likes people who say what they mean, who are kind to strangers, and who can be taught. A
romance opens after *Free Hearths* if she is Loyal and you have flirted at least twice without
lying to her. She will end it if your corruption reaches Tainted, or if you sign your name into
the Registry by choice.

> Don't look at me like that in the middle of a lesson. They'll all want to learn it.
