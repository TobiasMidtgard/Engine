Eight people in the world can become your **companions**: not hired swords, but people who travel
with you because they have decided you are worth following. Each one has a home, a way of fighting,
things they approve of and things they won't forgive, and a **personal quest** that opens when they
trust you. How companions work in general (orders, approval, how many you can take, what happens
when they fall) is on [Companions](../../play/companions.md).

## Who gets along

Companions talk to each other on the road, at camp and in the middle of fights, and some of them
don't get along at all. A few pairs have quests that cross: helping one may cost you the other.

| | Gets along with | Clashes with | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tamsin** | Avara, Yara | Garrick | She was born under a Clay Seal and has hated the Registry ever since |
| **Brenn** | Garrick, Yara | Teodor | She buries her dead and doesn't like to see them stand up again |
| **Avara** | Tamsin, Moss | Teodor | Necromancy is the wound she spends her life closing |
| **Moss** | Avara, Rain-Back | **Varo** | Varo binds animals. Moss is an animal, and remembers it. |
| **Varo** | Brenn | **Moss** | He thinks the Chorus is a cage of its own |
| **Teodor** | Yara | Avara, Brenn | He thinks the dead are owed a use, and a record |
| **Yara** | Tamsin, Teodor | Garrick | Her family's deed went down with Ward Null, and a Registry clerk signed it away |
| **Garrick** | Brenn | Tamsin, Yara | He used to arrest people like them, and he is not yet sure he was wrong |

## Personal quests that cross

- **Moss-Under-Stone and Varo Kett**: Varo's quest, *The Harness Order*, ends with a hundred
  harnesses that can hold a Brinewing still. If you let him build them, Moss leaves you, and the
  Chorus remembers your name in a way you won't like. If you help Moss in *Too Far from the
  Chorus* first, Varo asks for a harness for a fox.
- **Sister Avara and Teodor Voss**: Teodor's quest, *The Unregistered*, raises a crypt of the
  Registry's dead. Avara will not stay in a camp with a raised corpse in it, and Teodor will not
  stay with someone who burns them.
- **Ulf Garrick and Yara Coil**: the Registry clerk who signed away Ward Null's deeds was Garrick's
  grandfather, and Garrick knows it.

## Romance

Five companions can become more than friends: Tamsin, Brenn, Varo, Teodor and Yara. A romance
opens after their personal quest's first part, if they are **Loyal** and you have said the right
things along the way. Each has a scene at the Five Quiet Days before the Great Restriking, and an
epilogue together if you both live.
