**Sister Avara** keeps the Covenant of Ash's wound-house in [Orath](../../cities/orath.md)'s Fifth
Quarter, where the pilgrims who arrive too sick to reach the Spring are brought. The Covenant's
faith holds that surviving catastrophe creates a duty to others, and Avara has turned that into a
working day: stitching, setting bones, burning out infections, sitting with the dying and, more
often than she would like, cleansing the [corrupted](../../magic/corruption.md).

She is in her fifties, small, grey-haired and quick, with ash-grey sleeves rolled to the elbow and
a voice that is calm in every situation, which people find either comforting or alarming. She
does not kill. She has been very close to it.

## Recruiting

You meet Avara in [A Spring Running Dry](../../quests/main/a-spring-running-dry.md), trying to
prepare Orath for a shortage the officials won't announce. If you help her set up the water stores,
or carry the injured worker from the fifth passage to her wound-house, she asks to come with you
when you leave Orath:

> The Spring is not the only thing failing. If you are going where I think you're going, you will
> need someone to sew you up, and I would rather it was me than a stranger.

## How she fights

Avara fights to end fights. She keeps you alive, treats injuries in the middle of a battle, and
puts enemies on the ground without killing them.

| Ability | What it does | At |
|---|---|---|
| **Field surgery** | Runs to a downed or injured ally and treats one [injury](../../play/injuries.md) in seconds | Always |
| **Mend** | Clean [Vitalism](../../magic/vitalism.md): heals you over time, and stops bleeding | Always |
| **Sleep** | Puts one person to sleep until they are hit or woken | Approval Friendly |
| **Ashlight** | Burns a stage of corruption out of an ally, or a curse off them, at a cost in her own stamina | Approval Loyal |
| **Triage** | Gets everyone in the fight who is down back up at once, with a quarter of their health | Her quest's first part |

She starts with Vitalism and Alchemy at Expert and Maces at Apprentice. She carries a short iron
mace, uses only its flat, and wears light armour under her ash-grey coat.

## Approval

| Avara approves of | Avara disapproves of |
|---|---|
| Mercy, especially when it's expensive | Killing anyone who has surrendered |
| Preparing people for a disaster rather than hiding it | Dark magic of any kind, and necromancy most of all |
| Telling people hard truths kindly | Letting panic happen because it's useful |
| Saving a life you didn't have to | Selling water, food or medicine in a shortage |
| Treating your injuries instead of ignoring them | Corruption on you (she will offer to treat it, every time) |
| Asking for help | Torture, for any reason |

Avara never leaves you over corruption. She leaves if you **raise the dead** in front of her more
than twice, or if you kill a prisoner she asked you to spare.

## The Seventh Bed

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Avara's personal quest below.

There are seven beds in the wound-house's back room, where the corrupted are cleansed. Eight years
ago Avara cleansed a pactbinder called **Orrin Hask** in the seventh bed: a young man who had made
a bargain with [the Patient Creditor](../../magic/pactbinding.md) and wanted out. She burned out
everything she could find, and let him go. She missed something.

When she is **Friendly**, word reaches her that Orrin has come back to Orath, **Hollowed**, and that
the people who drink from the Fifth Quarter's barrel-sellers have started to dream of a creditor
with very exact accounts. He is paying his debt with them.

- **The barrels**: the water-sellers from A Spring Running Dry are still selling. Orrin is in their
  cellar, and something in the water carries his mark.
- **The ledger**: Orrin owes the Patient Creditor a number of years of life, and he has been paying
  it in other people's. Avara can read the account, if you can get her his.
- **Avara herself**: she believes the debt is hers, because she let him go.

```flow
start: The seventh bed is empty | And the Fifth Quarter is dreaming.
path: Follow the barrels | To a cellar under the water-sellers' warehouse.
path: Read the account | Steal Orrin's pact, and learn what he owes.
path: Ask the Spring | If the First Spring flows, it can cleanse what Avara can't.
join: Orrin Hask | Hollowed, frightened, and still paying.
end: Cleanse him | Avara takes his corruption into herself. He lives; she is Marked.
end: Pay the debt | Make a new bargain with the Creditor, in your own name.
end: Let him die | Avara asks you to do it, so she doesn't have to. She never asks again.
end: The Spring | If you left the page in Orath's door, the Spring cleanses Orrin, and Avara weeps.
```

What she becomes:

| Ending | Avara |
|---|---|
| **Cleanse him** | Carries a mark she can't remove, and is gentler about everyone else's. Ashlight costs her nothing. |
| **Pay the debt** | Is furious with you and grateful at once. She keeps a ledger of your debt for you, page by page. |
| **Let him die** | Stops cleansing the Hollowed. She tells you why once, and never again. |
| **The Spring** | Believes, for the first time in years, that some things can be mended completely. Triage heals in full. |

## Not a romance

Avara is fond of you and says so, in the way of a woman who has sewn you up a dozen times. If you
try to romance her, she laughs, kindly, and tells you to drink some water.
