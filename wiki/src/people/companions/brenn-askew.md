**Captain Brenn Askew** has been in the arena since she was sixteen and out of it since she was
thirty, when a Gilt-rank axe took two fingers from her left hand and she found she liked choosing
fighters better than being one. Now she is the [Brass Circuit](../../factions/brass-circuit.md)'s
company captain: she watches the pits and the sand for people who can hold a line, signs them to
companies, and sells the companies to whoever is paying, mostly
[Orravell](../../cities/orravell.md)'s Crown Companies.

She is big, plain-spoken and dry, with a voice that carries across a battlefield and a laugh that
carries further. She keeps a ledger of every fighter she has ever signed, with the date they
signed and, more and more often, the date they died.

## Recruiting

Brenn doesn't join anyone. She **contracts**. If you have won a Bronze-rank bout in the arena, she
finds you in the Undercroft and offers terms: 40 marks, a share of any loot that isn't personal, and
a clause that she leaves if you ever leave a wounded fighter behind. You can pay, or you can fight
her for it on the practice sand. If you win, she signs for nothing:

> Anyone who can put me on my back is worth following for free. Anyone who can't, pays.

If you never went to Arena City, she is in Orravell during
[The Price of Kestrel Vale](../../quests/main/the-price-of-kestrel-vale.md), at Hushwing Station,
counting the fighters she sold to the Crown Companies, and she offers you the same terms.

## How she fights

Brenn is a wall. She stands in front of you, holds the enemy's attention, and takes the blows you
would rather not.

| Ability | What it does | At |
|---|---|---|
| **Shield wall** | Raises her shield beside you: arrows and blows from the front hit her instead | Always |
| **Here, you** | Bangs her spear on her shield and calls an enemy out; it fights her for eight seconds | Always |
| **Hold the gap** | Plants herself in a doorway or a bridge; nothing walks past her while her stamina lasts | Approval Friendly |
| **On me** | Rallies you and other allies: stamina back, fear and stagger shaken off | Approval Loyal |
| **Last of the company** | When you go down, she stands over you and can't be staggered until you are up | Her quest's first part |

She starts with Shields, Spears and Heavy armour at Adept. She will carry any spear or shield you
hand her, and complain about its balance.

## Approval

| Brenn approves of | Brenn disapproves of |
|---|---|
| Keeping your word, especially when it costs you | Breaking a contract, even a bad one |
| Paying people what they're owed, promptly | Leaving the wounded behind |
| Facing a fight head on | Cruelty to prisoners and people who have surrendered |
| Courage in other people, noticed out loud | Sneaking about when you could just knock |
| Training: practice bouts, drills, a good spar | Raising the dead, especially soldiers |
| Fair fights in the arena | Spending fighters' lives to save money |

## The Company Ledger

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Brenn's personal quest below.

When Brenn is **Friendly**, she shows you her ledger: forty names on one page, a company she signed
out of the pits and sold to the Crown Companies two years ago as the **Sand Lions**. She has just
learned where they are: in Kestrel Vale, attached to **Operation Second Spring**, and listed as
"guarantors of transfer", which is the Crown's way of saying they will be clearing farms.

Brenn signed them for a war, not for that. Their contracts have four years to run.

- **The contracts**: the Council of Stewards in Orravell holds them. They can be bought out (a
  fortune), forged (a crime the Crown hangs people for), or voided by finding a clause Orravell
  has already broken.
- **The Sand Lions**: forty people, some of whom like the work. Their sergeant, **Tolly Marsh**,
  thinks Brenn has no right to ask.
- **The Long Shade**: a commissar of the Crown Ascendant who will execute the whole company for
  desertion if they walk.

```flow
start: Forty names in a ledger | And Kestrel Vale is about to be cleared.
path: Buy them out | 3 ascents a head, paid to the Council of Stewards.
path: Find the broken clause | The contracts promised wages Orravell hasn't paid in a year.
path: Walk them out | Through Hushwing Station at night, with Sera Noll's help.
join: The Sand Lions choose | Brenn can't make them. She can only ask.
end: The company comes home | Brenn tears out the page. The Sand Lions follow you at the Lantern Table.
end: Half of them stay | Brenn loses some she signed, and keeps the page.
end: The commissar | Brenn kills him in front of the company. They follow her anywhere; Orravell wants her head.
```

What she becomes:

| Ending | Brenn |
|---|---|
| **The company comes home** | Stops selling fighters to wars she hasn't read. She can bring the **Sand Lions** to one battle of your choosing. |
| **Half of them stay** | Keeps signing companies, and starts reading the wars first |
| **The commissar** | Becomes an outlaw in Orravell, and the Brass Circuit's hero. Gains *Last of the company* for you and every ally. |

## Romance

Brenn likes people who are brave without showing off, who pay their debts and who don't flinch at
her laugh. A romance opens after *The Company Ledger* if she is Loyal and you asked her about the
fingers (and didn't make a fuss about the answer). She ends it if you leave someone wounded behind.

> I don't do this. I sign people. I don't, you know. Keep them.
