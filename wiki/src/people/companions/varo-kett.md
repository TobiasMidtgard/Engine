**Varo Kett** is the [Keepers' Road](../../factions/keepers-road.md)'s best binder: the person a
chapter sends for when an animal is too big, too frightened or too dangerous to handle any other
way. He makes harnesses, part leather, part mechanism, part ward, that can hold a panicking
Brinewing still without hurting her, and he has the patience to sit with a bound animal for days
until it stops fighting. He believes, with complete sincerity, that control is the kindest thing
you can give a creature that might otherwise be killed for being dangerous.

He is in his forties, broad and soft-voiced, smells of saddle oil, and travels with **Grey**, an
enormous grey [wolf](../../creatures/wolf.md) in one of his own harnesses, who was going to be put
down for killing sheep and now sleeps across his feet. He is warm, practical and very good company,
which is what makes him difficult.

## Recruiting

Varo turns up in [What the Beast Was Running From](../../quests/what-the-beast-was-running-from.md)
wanting the beast brought in alive and bound. If you help him do it, or do it better than him, he
offers to come with you, because he wants to see the animals of the cities you are going to.

If you haven't done that quest, he is at **Wolfsgate waystation** on the Bell Road in Act I, with a
problem: the wolves around the waystation have started coming into the yard at night, and the
hunter wants them dead. Varo wants them collared and moved. Help him, and he comes with you.

> People talk about freedom like it's a meal. Freedom is how a wolf ends up shot in a sheepfold.
> Grey's alive. Ask him which he'd rather.

## How he fights

Varo controls a fight. He snares, holds and pins, and Grey does the biting.

| Ability | What it does | At |
|---|---|---|
| **Grey** | His bound wolf fights beside him, with its own health; it can be ordered to hold a target | Always |
| **Snare** | Throws a weighted line that wraps an enemy's legs; it falls and must cut itself free | Always |
| **Harness** | Binds a beast or monster (not a person) for ten seconds: it stops and can't act | Approval Friendly |
| **Turn it** | A bound beast fights for you until the harness wears off | Approval Loyal |
| **The long hold** | Harness lasts until the beast is hurt, and works on Awakened creatures | His quest's first part |

He starts with Animal handling at Master, Spears at Adept (his hooked staff counts as a spear) and
Light armour at Apprentice.

## Approval

| Varo approves of | Varo disapproves of |
|---|---|
| Bringing a dangerous animal in alive | Setting a dangerous animal free where it can hurt people |
| Taking care of your mount and your animals | Killing an animal you could have held |
| Practical answers to hard problems | Sentimental ones that get people killed |
| Keeping order: in a camp, a city or a fight | The Chorus of Oruvai, which he finds unsettling |
| Hard work, and people who do it | Sere's "partnership": "a word for a harness you can't see" |
| Paying the Keepers' Road its due | Breaking a contract with the guild |

## The Harness Order

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Varo Kett's personal quest below.

When Varo is **Friendly**, a letter reaches him from the Gilded Ascent: an order for **one hundred
harnesses** of his Brinewing pattern, from the Registry of Seals, for "the care of animals in
signatory cities", with a fee large enough to set up a chapter of his own. Varo is delighted, and
then he is curious, because there are only four adult Brinewings in the world.

- **The specification**: the Registry wants the harnesses made to fit people. Not animals. People.
- **The workshop**: in Keth Veyra's harness workshops on the Wet Face, a Registry agent waits with the first payment and a
  sample of what the harnesses are to carry: a seal of the Gilded Ascent, set in the buckle.
- **Grey**: Varo's wolf won't go near the sample seal. Grey has never refused him anything.

Varo needs to decide what his work is for, and he wants you there when he does.

```flow
start: A hundred harnesses | For animals, the letter says.
path: Read the specification | They are cut to fit a person.
path: Ask Grey | The wolf won't go near the seal.
path: Follow the agent | To the Registry's warehouse in Keth Veyra.
join: Varo decides | What a harness is for.
end: Build them | Varo is rich. The Registry's wardens can hold anyone still, Awakened or not.
end: Burn the order | Varo loses his fee and his guild's favour, and keeps his name.
end: Build them wrong | A hundred harnesses with a flaw only Varo knows. They fail on the night they matter.
end: Free Grey | Varo takes off Grey's harness. Grey stays anyway, and Varo doesn't understand why.
```

What he becomes:

| Ending | Varo |
|---|---|
| **Build them** | Stays with you, richer and quieter. At the Great Restriking, Seal Wardens bring his harnesses. Moss-Under-Stone leaves you for good. |
| **Burn the order** | Turns against the Registry with a binder's thoroughness. *The long hold* works on people. |
| **Build them wrong** | At the Great Restriking, every harness the wardens carry falls apart at once |
| **Free Grey** | Starts to understand what Sere meant. He makes his peace with Moss, and Grey fights harder than ever. |

## Romance

Varo likes people who are steady, who work with their hands, and who argue with him without
getting angry. A romance opens after *The Harness Order* if he is Loyal and you have argued with him
about Grey at least once. He ends it if you let something he harnessed be killed.

> I don't know how to do this without holding on. You'll have to tell me if it's too tight.
