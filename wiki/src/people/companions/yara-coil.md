**Yara Coil** was born in [Ward Null](../../cities/vantress/ward-null.md), the district of
[Vantress](../../cities/vantress/index.md) that the city declared dead, and she has never been
registered anywhere: no birth record, no seal, no census line. She grew up under the decks, diving
for things the rafts dropped, and now she is the [Tangle](../../factions/thieves-guild.md)'s best
diver: she can hold her breath for four minutes, open a lock by feel in black water and find a
lost ring in the swamp mud by the way the silt lies.

She is in her late twenties, wiry, brown from the sun on the salvage rafts, with her hair cut short
so it doesn't catch. She laughs at almost everything, including things that aren't funny, and swears
in four raft dialects. Her grandmother, **Nan Coil**, was a child in the Drowning Year, and Yara has
heard the story of the house Nan lost so many times she could draw it.

## Recruiting

Yara is the diver in [Before the First Bell](../../quests/before-the-first-bell.md), where her job,
*The Weight Below*, reaches a sealed locker under the customs watchhouse from the water. If you do
that job with her, she finds you on the salvage rafts of the
[Lowwater Chainyards](../../cities/vantress/lowwater-chainyards.md) afterwards:

> You don't exist either, do you? I can tell. You walk like nobody's ever stopped you at a gate.
> Come on. I'll show you what's under the city.

If you didn't, she is the diver of the crew in [Steal the Register](../../quests/main/steal-the-register.md),
and after the job she stays, because the Middle Well's water was the strangest she has ever been
in and she wants to know where else you go.

## How she fights

Yara is a thief. She fights dirty, fast and close, and she is happiest when the fight is near water.

| Ability | What it does | At |
|---|---|---|
| **Net** | Throws a weighted net: the target is tangled and can't attack until it cuts free | Always |
| **Knife work** | Two quick stabs at an opening; she aims for the hands | Always |
| **Under** | Dives into any water deeper than her waist and comes up behind an enemy | Approval Friendly |
| **Long breath** | Shares her breath with you underwater: you can stay down as long as she can | Approval Loyal |
| **Harpoon** | Spears an enemy and pulls them into the water, or off a ledge | Her quest's first part |

She starts with Daggers, Lockcraft and Athletics (swimmer) at Expert and Sneak at Adept. She wears
light armour, or nothing heavier than oilskin.

## Approval

| Yara approves of | Yara disapproves of |
|---|---|
| Stealing from the rich, banks and the Registry | Stealing from people who have nothing |
| Swimming, diving and getting wet for no reason | Seals, registers and anyone who wants your name |

| Taking a risk for someone who can't pay you | Killing when a knife to the hand would do |

## What the Water Kept

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Yara Coil's personal quest below.

When Yara is **Friendly**, she tells you what she has been looking for: the **Tally**, a census barge
that sank under the Lowwater Chainyards in the Drowning Year. The Tally carried Vantress's original
roll of the dead: the list that declared Ward Null drowned, and the deeds of every home the
council sold afterwards, before they were copied out for the Gilded Ledger and sent on to the
Registry of Seals. The copies are the law. Nobody has ever seen the originals.

Yara has found the wreck. It is deeper than she can dive alone, and it is not empty.

- **The wreck**: thirty metres down in the swamp mud under the Chainyards, in the dark, where
  things live that like dead census clerks (see [Creatures](../../creatures/index.md)).
- **The roll**: in a sealed iron chest, the original roll of the dead, and four hundred deeds. Nan
  Coil's house is on the third page.
- **The signature**: every deed is countersigned by the Registry clerk who certified the roll,
  **Osric Garrick**. If Ulf Garrick travels with you, he goes very quiet.
- **The difference**: the original roll lists fewer than half the people the copy does. The rest
  were alive when the copies were made, and were written dead on purpose so their homes could be
  sold.

```flow
start: The Tally | Thirty metres down, under the Chainyards.
path: Dive it with Yara | Long breath, a lantern and a rope.
path: Raise it | Hire the Great Winch House to lift the wreck. Everyone will see.
path: Ask the Tangle | Tal Oran can plan it, for a cut.
join: The original roll | Fewer than half of them drowned.
end: The Salvage Court | Yara takes the roll to court. Ward Null's dead sue to be alive.
end: Sell it to the Gilded Ledger | It pays a fortune to make the roll disappear.
end: Burn it | Yara decides she doesn't want a name on anyone's page, not even a true one.
end: Give it to Garrick | He reads every page, and asks you what his grandfather was for.
```

What she becomes:

| Ending | Yara |
|---|---|
| **The Salvage Court** | The roll is proof that a Registry clerk wrote living people dead. Ward Null rises out of the underdecks, slowly, and Yara is registered, for the first time, under her own name. She hates it, and keeps it. |
| **Sell it** | Rich, and cheerful, and she never mentions Nan's house again |
| **Burn it** | Stays nobody, on purpose. *Under* works in any water, including a bath. |
| **Give it to Garrick** | Garrick and Yara stop clashing. If you then take it to court together, Garrick testifies. |

## Romance

Yara likes people who make her laugh, who take risks for strangers, and who don't ask her to be
anyone. A romance opens after *What the Water Kept* if she is Loyal and you have dived with her
for no reason at least once. She ends it if you sign your name into the Registry by choice.

> Neither of us exists. Makes it easier. Nobody to tell.
