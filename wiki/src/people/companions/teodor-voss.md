**Teodor Voss** is a clerk of the **Pale Archive**, the necromancers' tower of the
[Second City](../../cities/second-city.md)'s Eaves, where every corpse in the city is registered and
the dead are questioned on behalf of their families, their creditors and the courts. He has spent
fifteen years writing down who died, how, and what they had to say about it. He likes the dead.
They are honest, unhurried and grateful to be asked.

He is thin, pale (from working nights, not from anything else), precise, and much funnier than he
looks, in a voice so quiet that people lean in and then wish they hadn't. He wears ink-stained
gloves, carries a ledger everywhere and writes in it after every fight: who fell, where, and what
they might be good for.

## Recruiting

In [A Seal for the Eaves](../../quests/main/a-seal-for-the-eaves.md) you need Kit Fenn to testify
in the Pale Archive. Teodor is the clerk who registers Kit's body, prepares him, and asks him your
questions, gently. Afterwards, he asks if he can come with you:

> I registered your courier. It is the first time I've had to leave the "sealed by" line blank. I'd
> like to see where that goes. Also, you will be leaving bodies behind you, and somebody ought to
> write them down.

If you dug Kit up without leave instead, Teodor still joins you, but he starts Wary and doesn't
let you forget it.

## How he fights

Teodor is a dark mage of [Necromancy](../../magic/necromancy.md). He raises the dead on the field to
fight for you, weakens the living and walls off the worst of it in bone.

| Ability | What it does | At |
|---|---|---|
| **Raise the fallen** | One corpse on the field gets up and fights for you until it falls again | Always |
| **Grave chill** | A touch of cold that saps an enemy's strength and slows its swings | Always |
| **Bone ward** | A ring of bones that blocks arrows and spells around you for a few seconds | Approval Friendly |
| **Speak with dead** | Out of combat, asks any corpse three questions for you | Approval Loyal |
| **A full ledger** | Every enemy that dies in the fight stands up for him, for twenty seconds | His quest's first part |

He starts with Necromancy at Expert and Malefice at Adept, and his corruption at **Marked**. Each
dark spell he casts adds to his own corruption, not yours, but Sister Avara notices either way.

## Approval

| Teodor approves of | Teodor disapproves of |
|---|---|
| Treating the dead with respect, and asking before using them | Desecrating graves for fun or profit |
| Keeping records: names, dates, what happened | Burning a body nobody has registered |
| Curiosity about death, magic and how things end | The Registry of Seals, in a professional way |
| Dark magic used carefully, with the bystanders protected | Cruelty for its own sake |
| Paying a corpse's family for its use | Wasting the dead: leaving a useful body to rot |
| Asking him about his work | Being called a ghoul |

## The Unregistered

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Teodor Voss's personal quest below.

In A Seal for the Eaves, the Registrar of the Dead tells you that the Registry of Seals asked the
Pale Archive how to register the dead, was told it couldn't be done, and said it would find a way.
When Teodor is **Friendly**, he finds out how.

Pages are missing from the Archive's ledgers: two hundred and twelve deaths, all in the last two
years, all paupers, all registered and then removed. The bodies are gone too. Teodor follows the
missing pages down into the **catacombs** under the Pale Archive, and finds them: two hundred and
twelve corpses on shelves, every one of them wearing a seal of the Gilded Ascent.

They are not raised. They are not at rest either. They are written in.

- **The catacombs**: below the Archive's lowest tier, older than the Eaves, where Teodor has never
  been allowed to go. Something down there is guarding the shelves (see
  [Undead](../../creatures/undead.md)).
- **The seals**: each one keyed to a name in the Sixth Register, and each corpse a lock the dead
  can't open. The Registry has found a way to seal people who can't refuse.
- **The clerk who did it**: a junior registrar of the Archive, paid by the Broker, who believes
  he was helping the city become lawful.

```flow
start: 212 missing pages | And 212 sealed dead in the catacombs.
path: Down the catacombs | Past what guards the shelves.
path: The junior registrar | Who sold the pages, and to whom.
path: Ask the dead | Speak with them. Some of them remember being sealed.
join: The shelves | Two hundred and twelve locks the dead can't open.
end: Lay them to rest | Break the seals and bury them. Teodor registers every name, by hand.
end: Raise them | Unseal them by raising them. Teodor has an army, and the dead have a grudge.
end: Leave them sealed | And follow the seals to the Register. Teodor will never forgive you.
end: Burn them | Fire breaks a seal. Teodor writes "burned" two hundred and twelve times.
```

What he becomes:

| Ending | Teodor |
|---|---|
| **Lay them to rest** | Becomes gentler with his own magic, and his corruption falls a stage. He writes *The Book of the Unregistered*. |
| **Raise them** | Keeps the dead as a company of his own. They can march at the Lantern Table. Sister Avara leaves you. |
| **Leave them sealed** | Leaves you. He goes to the Gilded Ascent on his own to find the Register, and you will meet him again. |
| **Burn them** | Stays, colder. He never raises anyone without asking you first again. |

## Romance

Teodor likes people who listen, who are curious about death without being frightened of it, and who
remember names. A romance opens after *The Unregistered* if he is Loyal and you have asked him about
his ledger at least twice. It is slow. He ends it if you burn a body he asked you to leave.

> I've written down a great many people. I would like, very much, not to write you down.
