**Moss-Under-Stone** is a red fox of [Oruvai](../../cities/oruvai.md), the city that is one mind. Most
of Oruvai's animals step out of the Chorus for a while and come back. Moss stepped out to see the
sea, and kept walking: through the marsh woodlands, over the Five Roads and further than any
creature in the valley's long memory. Without the chorus-vines under their paws, the voice of the
city is getting fainter, and something else is getting louder: a single fox's own thoughts.

Moss speaks through a **listener bloom**, the flowering tendril every visitor to Oruvai wears, and
says "we" out of habit, then corrects it to "I", then forgets again. They are curious about
everything: doors, coins, boots, the reason people lock things. Moss doesn't kill; Oruvai's people
never do. They will bite a tendon, though, and they have very sharp teeth.

## Recruiting

Moss finds you. Somewhere on the roads near the marsh woodlands, or in the woods around the Valley
if you came that way, a fox follows you for a day at a distance, then sits in the road in front of
you holding a listener bloom in their mouth. Wear it, and they ask:

> We have never seen a person with no number on them. I have. Where are you going? Can I see it?

If you have killed an animal in front of them (not a monster: an animal), Moss leaves before you
can answer and doesn't come back for a long while.

## How they fight

Moss is a scout. They see what you can't, go where you can't, and make enemies look the wrong way at
the wrong moment.

| Ability | What it does | At |
|---|---|---|
| **Chorus sight** | Marks hidden enemies, traps and tracks within 40 m; birds nearby tell Moss what they see | Always |
| **Look at me** | Darts across an enemy's path; it turns to follow the fox for a few seconds | Always |
| **Hamstring** | Bites the back of a knee: the enemy is slowed and can't sprint or dodge | Approval Friendly |
| **Ask the animals** | Out of combat, asks the local animals a question: where someone went, what came this way, where the water is | Approval Loyal |
| **Gone to ground** | Vanishes into grass, roots or rubble, and reappears behind an enemy | Their quest's first part |

Moss can't wear armour or carry weapons. You can give them a **collar** with one enchantment, and a
silk harness from Oruvai's White Galleries that carries a few small things.

## Approval

| Moss approves of | Moss disapproves of |
|---|---|
| Being kind to animals, and to plants | Killing animals for sport, or needlessly |
| Leaving a place as you found it | Burning forests, fields or gardens |
| Curiosity: going somewhere new, asking a question | Harnesses, cages and binding magic used on animals |
| Sharing food | Fire magic near trees, and near them |
| Walking instead of riding | Cities, slightly, except the parts with roofs to sit on |
| Saving something no one else would bother with | Locking things for no reason |

Moss has a running fascination with doors, and asks about every locked one you pass.

## Too Far from the Chorus

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Moss-Under-Stone's personal quest below.

When Moss is **Friendly**, they stop on a road one evening and can't hear the Chorus at all. For a
whole night, they are completely alone in their own head for the first time in their life, and they
are terrified, and then, by dawn, they aren't. They ask you to take them back to Oruvai, to see
what the city thinks of a fox who has become a **one**.

The Chorus doesn't know. It has never had to decide.

- **The Listening Bowers**: in the Stillwater Memory, Moss can pass their memories back into the
  Chorus, which would make them part of the city again, and take away the one who walked.
- **[Rain-Back](../rain-back.md)**: the old tortoise envoy, if you have met it, has walked further than Moss and is
  still in the Chorus. It explains how, slowly.
- **The Quiet Verge**: glades cut off from the vines, where anyone can rest apart for a day or a
  lifetime. Some animals there have been ones for years, and some of them are not well.

```flow
start: Moss is alone in their head | And Oruvai has never had a one.
path: The Listening Bowers | Give the memories back.
path: Rain-Back | Learn how to carry the Chorus with you.
path: The Quiet Verge | Meet the ones who stayed apart.
join: The Chorus decides | Slowly, and all at once.
end: Moss goes home | Their memories go back into the Chorus. A fox stays in Oruvai; it isn't Moss.
end: Moss stays a one | The first individual in Oruvai's history. The city is uneasy, and curious.
end: A bloom of their own | Rain-Back grows a listener bloom that carries the Chorus wherever Moss goes.
```

What they become:

| Ending | Moss-Under-Stone |
|---|---|
| **Moss goes home** | Leaves your company. The Chorus remembers you warmly, and Oruvai opens every district to you. |
| **Moss stays a one** | Says "I" from now on. Their bite grows sharper and their Chorus sight goes; they learn to rely on their own nose. |
| **A bloom of their own** | Keeps both. Chorus sight works anywhere on the continent, and once a day Moss can borrow any animal's eyes. |

If you let Varo Kett build his harness order (see [Varo Kett](varo-kett.md)), Moss leaves before
this quest can finish, and the Chorus remembers you as the person who let it happen.

## Not a romance

Moss is your friend, deeply, and has no idea what romance is. If you try, they think you are
offering them food.

## Lines

- On the Gilded Ascent: "So many doors. Every one of them is a no."
- On Varo Kett: "He is kind to animals the way a fence is kind to a field."
- On the sea, the first time: nothing at all, for a long time.
