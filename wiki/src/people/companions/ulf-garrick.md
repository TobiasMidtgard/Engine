**Warden-Captain Ulf Garrick** commands the **Seal Wardens**, the grey-coated guards who check
seals at every gate of [the Gilded Ascent](../../cities/gilded-ascent.md) and carry the Registry's
warrants across the continent. He is fifty-eight, enormous, courteous, and the best fighter the city
has. He is also one of the [Awakened](../../play/ascension.md#awakening): eleven years ago, at the
human limit and dying of a wound, he was offered a road past it by the Registrar of Seals, and took
it.

He believes in the seals. He believes a city where everyone is written down is a city where nobody
disappears, and he has spent his life making sure nobody does. He is honest, patient and kind to
prisoners, and he does not let them go.

## In the main story

Garrick is the main story's wall at the human limit:

- In [The Restriking Is Called](../../quests/main/the-restriking-is-called.md), he waits at the
  Summit gate and offers to walk you down under arrest.
- In [The Human Limit](../../quests/main/the-human-limit.md), he hunts you, at the time and place your
  habits make likely, and he can't be beaten by a human. You can only survive him.
- At the end of [A Door Nobody Locked](../../quests/main/a-door-nobody-locked.md), you are Awakened
  too, and you can finally face him as an equal: kill him, beat him and let him go, or talk.

## Recruiting

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

If you spared Garrick in The Human Limit (or ran instead of killing him), and spare him again in
A Door Nobody Locked, he lowers his sword and asks you a question:

> Eleven years ago she offered me the limit, and I took it, and I never asked what it cost. You've
> walked every road I was told was closed. Tell me what she paid for me.

If you can tell him (Ilyan Dareth's survey, Ison's reading of the seal, Rain-Back's knowledge of
the Awakening roads, or the Tally's roll if you have it), Garrick resigns his commission on the
spot, in writing, and asks to come with you. He is the last companion who can join.

## How he fights

Garrick is a warrior, and the only companion who is Awakened from the moment he joins.

| Ability | What it does | At |
|---|---|---|
| **Awakened strength** | His blows break shields and stagger anything his size | Always |
| **Warden's word** | Seal Wardens who see him hesitate for a few seconds before attacking | Always |
| **Ground slam** | Brings Oathkeeper down: everything within 4 m is knocked off its feet | Always |
| **Unbreakable** | Once a fight, shrugs off a killing blow | Approval Friendly |
| **Oath** | Chooses one ally to protect: every blow aimed at them, he takes | Approval Loyal |

He starts at your level with Two-handed blades, Heavy armour and Shields at Master.

## Approval

| Garrick approves of | Garrick disapproves of |
|---|---|
| Keeping order where people are frightened | Petty theft and pickpocketing |
| Protecting people who can't protect themselves | Lying to him, about anything |
| Fair fights, and sparing a beaten enemy | Cruelty to prisoners |
| Honesty, especially when it hurts | Killing Seal Wardens who are only doing their job |
| Duty, done well | Breaking the seals of ordinary people who didn't ask |
| Admitting you were wrong | Gisela Quell, more each day |

## What She Paid For

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Ulf Garrick's personal quest below.

Garrick's Awakening was paid for with a **well**. Eleven years ago, the Registry wrote a small
spring into the Sixth Register: the spring under **Harrowmere**, a hill town of the Bell Road where
Garrick was born. Its well's strength was drawn up through the seals of the town's people and poured
into a dying warden-captain. Harrowmere's spring has been dry ever since. Its people moved away,
or stayed and grew old fast.

- **Harrowmere**: a hollow town two days off the Bell Road, with a dry fountain and an old woman
  who remembers Garrick as a boy.
- **The door**: under the fountain, a small door of black stone, fully shut.
- **The page**: Harrowmere's entry in the Sixth Register, which you can find if you stole the
  Register or read it.

```flow
start: Harrowmere is dry | Because a warden-captain had to live.
path: Go home with him | The dry fountain, and the old woman.
path: Find the page | In the Sixth Register.
path: Ask Rain-Back | Whether a bought Awakening can be given back.
join: The black door under the fountain | Fully shut.
end: Give it back | Garrick opens the door with his own strength. The spring flows; he is no longer Awakened.
end: Keep it | Garrick stays Awakened, and spends it on stopping her.
end: Make Quell pay | Garrick wants her alive, at the Lantern Table, to say it in front of the Court.
```

What he becomes:

| Ending | Garrick |
|---|---|
| **Give it back** | Falls back to the human apex. He is still the best fighter you know, and lighter than you have ever seen him. |
| **Keep it** | Fights at the Great Restriking as he never has. *Oath* covers every ally. |
| **Make Quell pay** | At the Lantern Table, his testimony moves four chairs on its own |

## Not a romance

Garrick was married once, to a Copper Seal porter called Ilka, who died the year before he Awakened.
He talks about her sometimes, at night, and makes it clear that this is all there will ever be.
