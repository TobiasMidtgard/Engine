**The Broker** holds the mortal seat on the **Night Council** of the
[Second City](../cities/second-city.md)'s Eaves, beside the vampire Old Blood, the werebeast
Pack-Speaker, the Registrar of the Dead and the Warden of Wards. The seat belongs to whoever brings
the Eaves the most profitable bargains, and the present Broker has held it for eleven years. Nobody
uses their name. They are a soft-voiced, middle-aged person in a grey coat, ordinary in every way
except that they never seem to be in a hurry, and never seem to be surprised.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Deals** | Anything in the Eaves bought, sold, arranged or forgotten | A share, always |
| **Red Writs** | Buys a verified Red Writ in the Red Exchange on your behalf (see [The Last Writ](../factions/last-writ.md)) | The writ's fee, and the Broker's |
| **Introductions** | To any seat of the Night Council, or to someone in the Hollow Market | A favour |

## In the main story

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

In [A Seal for the Eaves](../quests/main/a-seal-for-the-eaves.md):

- The Broker brought the Registry's **Well Charter** to the Night Council, and wants it signed.
- Kit Fenn's testimony in the Pale Archive names the Broker as the buyer of the Red Writ that killed
  him, on behalf of a client with a glass seal.
- The same Broker bought the writ on you, and has been paying for drained bodies on the Boulevard,
  to frighten the Eaves into needing a seal.
- The Broker paid a junior registrar of the Pale Archive to sell the pages of the dead (see
  [Teodor Voss](companions/teodor-voss.md)).

You can expose the Broker, and the seat is empty; let the Eaves sign, and the Broker's power grows;
or make a deal, and the Broker becomes your friend, which is worse than being their enemy.
