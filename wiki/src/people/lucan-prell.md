**Lucan Prell** is a Gold Seal investor: he lends, insures and backs ventures, and the ventures he
backs are the ones nobody else will touch. Contraband, exotic creatures, weapons, debt labour,
people. Half of [Arena City](../cities/arena-city.md)'s stalls and pits are his money. He thinks the
Unsealed List is a tax on honesty: everything is traded somewhere, so why pretend?

He is fifty, handsome, perfectly dressed and pleasant to everyone, including people he is ruining.
He never raises his voice and never forgets a debt.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Loans** | Up to 50 ascents, with no questions and very exact interest | A fifth a season |
| **Black cargo** | Anything on the Unsealed List, delivered anywhere | Whatever he thinks you can pay |
| **Fencing** | Buys stolen goods in bulk, at a third of their worth | — |
| **Training** | Trade, Fence branch, up to Expert | See [Training](../play/skills.md#training) |

He lends to people on the Unsealed List. He lends to them especially.

## In quests

- [Three Accounts of One Caravan](../quests/three-accounts-of-one-caravan.md): the missing cargo was his.
- [The Lantern Table](../quests/main/the-lantern-table.md): his black-cargo investors choose a side.
  He sells to both until the last moment, then picks the one that owes him more.

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

Prell bankrolled the Broker of the Second City's Night Council for years, and knows exactly who paid
for the Red Writ on Kit Fenn. He will sell you the name. The price is a favour, to be named later.
