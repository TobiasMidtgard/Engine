**Aurenne Lisk** holds the **Golden Anchor**, the merchants of [Sky City](../cities/sky-city.md) who
want the island lowered closer to the trade routes, and their chain comes down at the Anchorage
beside the Gilded Ascent. She is forty-five, elegant, quick and genuinely practical; she thinks the
other two anchors are sentimental, and that a city should take a good deal when it is offered one.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Trade licence** | Lets you buy and sell in the Hanging Market at local prices | 5 marks a season |
| **Merchant** | Stormglass, cloudwater, spore medicines and precision instruments | Sky City prices, high |

## In the main story

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

In [The Island Sinks](../quests/main/the-island-sinks.md), Lisk sends you to carry the Charter's
terms to the Registry's envoy: a seal for every person on the island, renewed every season by the
Registry. If you let her sign, the island rises an inch and Sky City becomes the Registry's second
city. At [The Lantern Table](../quests/main/the-lantern-table.md), the Golden Anchor's fleet guards
the Summit for the Registry, if Sky City signed.
