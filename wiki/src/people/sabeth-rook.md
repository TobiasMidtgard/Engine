**Sabeth Rook** is the Last Writ's master hunter. For her, killing is the purest test of skill, and
contracts provide worthy prey and keep lesser killers in line. She keeps a small shrine to
[the Antlered Hunt](../lore/gods.md#the-antlered-hunt) in her rooms, and she will only take a writ on
someone who can fight back.

She is thirty-five, lean, sun-browned (unusual in the Eaves), and quietly cheerful. She has a bow
of black yew, and she has never missed a target she chose.
