**Imke Dunmore** runs the **Dunmore and Daughters**, a caravan of eleven wagons that has worked the
Bell Road for three generations. She is sixty, loud, broad and practical, and two of the daughters
are hers (the third is her niece, and Imke doesn't like it mentioned). She knows every waystation
cook, every toll keeper and every bandit gang on the road by name, and she pays them all exactly
what she has to and not a chit more.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Guard work** | A place with the caravan, guarding the wagons | Pays 1 mark a day |
| **Passage** | A seat on a wagon, Halfbell to the First Toll Ring | 3 marks, or free if she owes you |
| **Trader** | Road food, rope, lamp oil, bandages, cheap blades and wool | Bell Road prices |

## In the main story

In [The Bell Road](../quests/main/the-bell-road.md), one of Imke's guards has quit at Halfbell, and
Kit Fenn's outrider's coat gets you the job, if you can answer three questions about the Chartered
Road's charter (or persuade her, pay her, or show her what you can do on the practice post).

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

When the Seal Wardens catch up at the Burnt Mill or Wolfsgate, Imke decides whether to hide you in a
wagon and lie for you. She does if you have been honest with her, worked hard, and not stolen from
the wagons. Afterwards, the Dunmore and Daughters will carry you on the Bell Road for nothing, for
the rest of the game, Unsealed List or not.
