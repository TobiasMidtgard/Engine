**Sere** keeps the rookery of [Keth Veyra](../cities/keth-veyra.md), home of the four adult
[Brinewings](../creatures/brinewing.md) in the world, and is the Keepers' Road's **pactkeeper**: the
voice in the guild for partnership over ownership. A creature must choose to bond, Sere says, and
must be free to refuse an order. Fewer partnerships, but stronger ones. Sere has never given a
Brinewing an order in their life.

They are in their forties, weathered by wind and spray, with scarred forearms from a decade of
catching hatchlings and a habit of standing very still for a long time. They speak little, mostly
to animals, and when they speak to people it is usually to tell them they are standing somewhere a
Brinewing would like to land.

## In the main story

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

- In [A Door Nobody Locked](../quests/main/a-door-nobody-locked.md) Sere can bring you to the
  rookeries for **the bond**, one of the five roads to Awakening.
- In [The Drowning Year](../quests/main/the-drowning-year.md) Sere gets you into Keth Veyra because
  the animals have started refusing work, and Sere believes they are right. The keeper families
  have hidden the gate reports, and when Sere learns what really happened on the Night of Cut
  Harnesses, they are angrier than anyone has ever seen them.
- If you make the keepers publish the reports, Sere becomes the voice of the Open Ledger faction,
  and Keth Veyra opens a gate to outsiders for the first time in 83 years.
- At [The Lantern Table](../quests/main/the-lantern-table.md), Sere and the Brinewings fly over the
  Summit, if Keth Veyra refused.
