**Kit Fenn** was a free-route courier of the [Chartered Road](../factions/chartered-road.md), one of
Captain Wenna Sallow's outriders: twenty-two, fast, cheerful and too curious for his job. He
carried letters and small cases between the caravan houses of the Five Roads, and he had a habit of
reading what he carried when he thought it mattered.

## In the main story

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

- In [A Rat in the Cellar](../quests/main/a-rat-in-the-cellar.md), Kit staggers into the tavern in
  the Valley with a crossbow bolt in his side, looks at your empty collar and gives you his case:
  "You're nobody, aren't you. No seal. Good. Then they can't see you."
- His case holds a page of the **Sixth Register** naming the First Spring of Orath, and a letter to
  Ivo Tarrant: "From the bottom of the Registry. It's real. They've started."
- Kit had been carrying letters for a Registry clerk with a conscience, who passed him the page and
  then disappeared. The bolt was fired by a hired crossbowman on the Bell Road, under a **Red Writ**
  bought in the Second City by the Broker, for a client with a glass seal.
- In [A Seal for the Eaves](../quests/main/a-seal-for-the-eaves.md) you can bring his body to the
  Pale Archive, where [Teodor Voss](companions/teodor-voss.md) asks him your questions. His
  testimony names the Broker.
- At [The Lantern Table](../quests/main/the-lantern-table.md), his testimony is evidence.
- In the epilogue, his grave in the Valley has fresh flowers, or it doesn't.

## What he leaves you

- **Kit's case**, black leather with a Chartered Road tag and lock.
- **His outrider's coat**, which gets you a seat on a Chartered Road caravan and a job with
  [Imke Dunmore](imke-dunmore.md).
- A debt nobody will ever ask you to pay.

> Take this to Ivo Tarrant, at the Charter House, on the Ledger Floor. Don't let them seal it. Don't
> let them seal you.
