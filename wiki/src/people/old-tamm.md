**Old Tamm** was a hushstone cutter in the ninth workshop of the Nine Shut Doors forty years ago,
when the workshop dug too deep, broke into black stone, and found a door. What came out killed his
whole crew in a second. Tamm caught a shard of it in a copper vessel meant for slag, and has been
feeding it hushstone ever since, so that it stays asleep.

He is in his seventies, bent, deaf in one ear and very gentle, with hands that are hard as the stone
he cuts. He talks to the vessel. He has not left the side-cut in twenty years: food comes through a
hatch, and every season, for forty years, someone has paid the rent on his workshop.

## In the main story

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the main story below.

In [Behind the Nine Shut Doors](../quests/main/behind-the-nine-shut-doors.md), the landlords' new dig
is aimed at the door Tamm's vessel came from. If you earn his trust, he tells you everything: the
black stone, the well of raw **Energy** behind the door, and the young Registry clerk sent to "record
the damage", who altered his report. Her name in it is **G. Quell**.

He also tells you that she came back last year, much older, and asked him whether the vessel was
still alive. She seemed glad.

- **Seal the crack** and Tamm can stop, at last. He asks to see the sea.
- **Take the shard** and he helps you carry it, weeping.
- In [A Door Nobody Locked](../quests/main/a-door-nobody-locked.md), if he is alive, he helps you
  wake the shard inside a hushstone chamber: **the shard road** to Awakening.
- His report is evidence at [The Lantern Table](../quests/main/the-lantern-table.md).

## Who paid the rent

The rent was paid, every season for forty years, through a Fifth Reserve account in the name of a
Registry clerk who retired long ago. The account's signature is a neat, slanted hand that every
clerk in the Registry learns to imitate. [Gisela Quell](gisela-quell.md) kept Tamm fed and housed
for forty years, and kept him exactly where he was: the only keeper of a shard she might one day
need, and the only witness to a report she had to alter. Whether she did it out of guilt or out of
use, she has never decided.
