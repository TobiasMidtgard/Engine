**Captain Faro Wynd** leads the **Open Anchor**: pilots, explorers and radicals who believe
[Sky City](../cities/sky-city.md)'s chains should be cut so the island can roam free. He is thirty,
loud, charming and reckless, flies a patched black glider called *Ballast* and has been arrested by
the Iron Anchor four times for flying under the island without leave.

## Services

| Service | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Gliders** | Sells and repairs folding gliders | 12 marks for a used one; 4 ascents new |
| **Airship berths** | A berth on an Open Anchor airship, anywhere it is going | 3 marks a day |
| **Training** | Piloting, Flyer branch, up to Master | See [Training](../play/skills.md#training) |

## In the main story

In [The Island Sinks](../quests/main/the-island-sinks.md), Wynd sends you to fly a glider under the
island at night. From below, the island's oldest rock is black, and it has stopped glowing. If you
help him cut the Open Anchor's chain, the island tilts, a district floods, and the Open Anchor
becomes your friend for life. At [The Lantern Table](../quests/main/the-lantern-table.md) the Open
Anchor sends gliders, if Sky City refused.
