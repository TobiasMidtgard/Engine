**Inspector Halvard Cray** has spent thirty years walking into the [Spillward](../factions/spillward.md)'s
worst messes with a measuring rod and coming out with a report. Magic, he believes, is like the
quarry: useful, beautiful and always about to fall on someone, so it must be licensed, measured and
contained. He has closed more workshops than anyone in Veyrhold, and he can tell which spell caused
a spill by the taste of the air.

He is fifty-five, gaunt, grey and scrupulous, with a voice like a door closing. He is not cruel.
He is frightened, and he has good reasons to be, and he is more honest than he finds comfortable.

## In quests

- [The Street That Changed](../quests/the-street-that-changed.md): the Spillward's opening job. His
