**Cruising** takes your ship from the sky over the [home world](../world/planets/home-world.md)
to other planets and stars in seconds.

## How it works

Climb above 12 km and press **J**. Your speed grows with your distance from the nearest
planet or star, so you pass [the Moon](../world/planets/moon.md) in seconds and leave the solar
system in under a minute. **W** and **S** set how fast you go; **J** drops you out anywhere.

## Arriving

Point within 2° of a planet or star that looks small, and the ship homes in on it, slows down
by itself and drops back to normal flight 30 km above it. The gauges name your target and its
distance. Alpha Centauri, home of [Proxima b](../world/planets/proxima-b.md), is about 80 seconds
from the home world.

## Going home

Out in space, point the nose anywhere at the planet you left, and the ship flies you back to
the spot where you pressed **J**, around the planet if it has to, and comes out level at
300 m/s a few kilometres away.
