**Planes** fly where you look: point the mouse and the plane banks into the turn and follows.

## Where to find them

A plane is parked on the airfield's west apron, on the third stand, nose toward the taxiway.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Mouse | Aim |
| W / S | Throttle lever up and down |
| Shift | Full throttle |
| A / D | Roll; steer on the ground |
| Space | Wheel brakes |
| G | Landing gear |
| Hold C | Look around without steering |

The gauges under the prompt show speed, altitude, throttle and gear.
