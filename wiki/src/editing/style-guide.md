# Style guide

```meta
summary: How pages are written here, borrowed from the best game wikis.
```

A few rules keep the wiki easy to read, however many people write it. They come from the
style guides of the best game wikis (listed at the end).

## Pages

- **Start with one sentence that says what it is**, with the page's name in bold: "The
  **wolf** is a pack hunter of the forests and meadows." Then why a player should care.
- **Facts at a glance go in the infobox**; the text explains them.
- **Keep each kind of page in the same order.** Use the [templates](templates.md); leave out a
  section you have nothing for rather than writing "unknown".
- **Short paragraphs, tables and lists** beat long prose. A game wiki is looked up in the
  middle of playing.
- **One topic per page.** If a section grows long enough to need its own sections, give it its
  own page and link to it.

## Voice

- **Describe the world from inside it, in the present tense**: "Vantress floats on rafts", not
  "Vantress will float on rafts" or "the developers made Vantress float".
- **Behaviour, Lore and Overview sections state facts.** Advice goes in **Strategy**, written
  to the reader as "you".
- **British spelling**: armour, colour, neighbourhood.
- **Sentence case for titles**: "Wild boar", not "Wild Boar". Names keep their capitals:
  "Knot Square".
- **Mark spoilers** at the start of the section that has them:

  ```text
  > [!WARNING]
  > Spoilers for the Counterseal questline below.
  ```

## Links and pictures

- Link the first mention of anything that has its own page.
- Every picture gets a caption that says what it shows.
- Only pictures made for the game.

## Unfinished pages

Mark pages whose details aren't settled as **draft**, and pages that are only a start as
**stub** (see [How to add a page](how-to-add-a-page.md#draft-and-stub)). A stub with a good
first sentence is better than no page.

## Where these rules come from

- Pikipedia's [enemy article guidelines](https://www.pikminwiki.com/Pikipedia:Enemy_article_guidelines):
  a fixed section order, objective Behaviour, advice only under Strategy, written to "you".
- wiki.gg's guides to [articles](https://support.wiki.gg/wiki/Articles) and
  [infoboxes](https://support.wiki.gg/wiki/Infoboxes): tables, pictures and lists over long
  text, and at-a-glance facts in a box at the top right.
- wiki.gg's [main page guide](https://support.wiki.gg/wiki/Meta_pages): the front page leads to
  the main topics and says how to help.
- The [Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/) and [Minecraft Wiki](https://minecraft.wiki/)
  infobox and style conventions: one infobox per thing, named in bold in the first sentence.
