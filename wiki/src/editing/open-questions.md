The city pages were written from the design chats, keeping only the latest version of each idea.
Where two chats disagree, the wiki picks one for now and the question is listed here. Decide,
fix the pages, and delete the question.

## Maps

- **Keth Veyra**: the world map draws a ring of walls in the snowy northern mountains, but the
  final city is a dam across a monsoon river. Where is the dam?
- **Sky City**: its first design chained the island between mountain peaks, but the world map
  floats it beside the Gilded Ascent in the middle of the continent. The wiki now brings the Golden
  chain down at the Anchorage, on the Gilded Ascent's eastern edge, and leaves the other two chains'
  anchors unnamed. Its first design also had fortified chain forts, which the garden look replaced;
  the wiki leaves them out.
- **Rillvault**: the world map still shows the rejected single-cavern city; the final city is many
  tunnel systems.
- **Vantress's anchors**: the lore ties the city to nine stone anchor towers (the bank's vault is
  chained to the third), but the later pictures replaced the towers with low anchor posts. The wiki
  just says "stone anchors".
- **Ward Null**: the lore puts it beneath the tavern, the casino, the Hall of Seals and the south of
  Knot Square; the labelled map marks it in the underdecks of the Lowwater Chainyards. The wiki
  says both.
- **Sifting Basin**: the world map draws a moat around it, but its design keeps water covered or
  confined to cisterns, gardens and canals.
- **Vantress's raft yard**: the Cradle Yard was on the south-east edge, but the labelled map has
  Raftworks in the south-west. The wiki only mentions Raftworks.

## Rules

- **Two ways to grow.** The levelling chat has 100 character levels in three states (Human,
  Awakened, Ascendant), with Godslayer earned separately. The magic draft has every skill rise to
  100 and then ascend. The wiki now joins them: a skill can't pass 100 until you are Awakened, and
  can't reach Ascended II until you have Ascended (see [Skills](../play/skills.md) and
  [Awakening and Ascension](../play/ascension.md)). Is that the rule you want?
- **Skill ranks.** The wiki uses the game's ranks, Novice to God (Novice, Apprentice, Adept,
  Expert, Master, Ascended I to III, God). The levelling chat had Untrained, Familiar, Competent,
  Expert, Master, Transcendent.
- **How automated is Tessara?** The first version said nothing builds or repairs itself; the later
  one added repair robots, drones and an AI. The wiki follows the later one.
- **Eighty-three years ago** both Vantress cut Ward Null loose and Keth Veyra had its Night of Cut
  Harnesses. The wiki now makes them one disaster, **the Drowning Year**, a monsoon flood across the
  continent (see the [Timeline](../lore/timeline.md)).
- **Maera "Cask" Orro** runs the Drunken Sailor in the Vantress chat and keeps the guild's stores in
  the guild chat. The wiki gives her both jobs.
