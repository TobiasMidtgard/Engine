**Sky City** is a city on a vast floating island, held above a sea of clouds by three colossal
chains. It is home to the rich and powerful: a garden city of layered estates, promenades, canals,
pools, glasshouses, waterfalls and lush terraces. It hangs beside
[the Gilded Ascent](gilded-ascent.md), because the rich want to live close to the trade.

## Overview

The city wraps around the whole island in broad, continuous terraces, each ring separated from the
next by bare rock and deep shadow. The chains are not decoration: the island is not free to drift,
and great winches can raise, lower or tilt it to dodge storms, catch seasonal winds and control who
can reach it from below. From underneath, hundreds of lamps make the island look like an
upside-down constellation.

Sky City is where the world's investors live. Its money funds invention across the continent, but
only designs its investors can own and keep secret (see
[the Pattern Office](../factions/pattern-office.md)).

## Layout and districts

| District | What is there |
|---|---|
| The Crown | Towers, government estates, observatories and gardens on the island's upper surface, beautiful and exposed to the storms |
| The Sheltered Rings | Dense streets of homes on the island's protected sides |
| The Hanging Market | Shops and walkways hung beneath the island, where the airships dock. Its merchants control foreign trade and are among the richest in the city. |
| The Deepworks | Machinery, ballast chambers and ancient buoyancy caverns inside the rock |

## Law and government

The city is ruled by **the Council of Three Anchors**, and each of the three chains is held by a
different faction:

- **The Iron Anchor**: soldiers, engineers and traditionalists who want the city fixed in place for
  good.
- **The Golden Anchor**: merchants who want the island lowered closer to the trade routes.
- **The Open Anchor**: pilots, explorers and radicals who believe the chains should be cut so the
  city can roam free.

The island cannot move while all three chains are locked, but releasing even one tilts it, and that
changes its streets, its water and which districts can be reached.

## Economy

Huge woven nets strung through the clouds catch the city's water. Farms grow on sunlit terraces
under glass, and fungi and pale root crops fill the warm caverns inside. Sky City sells cloudwater,
medicines made from airborne spores, precision instruments and **stormglass**, an electrically
charged glass harvested during thunderstorms.

## Getting around

Airships dock at the Hanging Market. Raising or lowering one side of the island opens new docks,
exposes hidden caverns, floods the lower tunnels and changes the gliding routes. Criminals use
folding gliders to move between the hanging buildings, and smugglers hide goods in the cargo
ballast thrown overboard as the island climbs.
