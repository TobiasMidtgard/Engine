**Sky City** is a floating island trailing hanging roots, with a smaller floating islet beside it.
It drifts in the centre-east of the continent, right beside [the Gilded Ascent](gilded-ascent.md),
because the rich want to live close to the trade.

## Overview

Investors from Sky City fund invention across the world, but only designs they can own and keep
secret (see [the Pattern Office](../factions/pattern-office.md)).
