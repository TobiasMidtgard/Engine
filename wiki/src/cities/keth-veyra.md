**Keth Veyra** is a city built into a colossal dam across a monsoon river. Its homes, workshops,
streets, animal pens and defences climb the dam's stepped face and fill its inner galleries. Its
people train animals, including the dragon-like **Brinewings**, and they keep outsiders away.

## Overview

Above the dam lies a vast reservoir of floating farms and aquatic training grounds. Below it,
controlled waterfalls tumble through orchards, mills and terraces wrapped in mist. From a
distance the city is a monumental wall covered in neighbourhoods, waterfalls, greenery, animal
ramps and dragon towers.

Training animals decides your standing here. A respected keeper's animals work calmly, and
children learn the hand signals early. Every year, on the **Day of Loose Ropes**, the city honours
its retired animals with a procession in which nothing carries a load.

## History

Keth Veyra was founded by the workers who were abandoned halfway through building a kingdom's
greatest dam. When civil war ended their wages and supplies, the engineers, animal keepers and
their families stayed. They survived the first monsoon together, finished the dam over
generations and built their homes between its enormous buttresses. Their founding covenant gave
authority to those who kept the city running. Three centuries later, six keeper families control
most apprenticeships and the strongest creatures.

Eighty-three years ago, visiting merchants tried to steal Brinewing eggs. Their escape started a
fire, panicked the working animals and caused a failed emergency release that drowned a lower
neighbourhood. **The Night of Cut Harnesses** is why the city shuts outsiders out.

## Layout and districts

| District | Where | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| Rookery Crown | The dam's crest | Three broad dragon towers, home to the four adult Brinewings. Heated chimneys make reliable updrafts for take-off, and apprentices watch from the galleries as keepers check wings, prepare food and teach flight. |
| Wet Face | The outer, downstream face | Terraced homes, harness workshops, fruit stalls and broad animal ramps between the spillways. Talk stops whenever a gate signal sounds. |
| Deep Streets | Tunnels inside the dam | Cool, dry galleries of homes, granaries and the animal infirmary, and the **Old Harness House**, where retired harnesses keep the stories of generations. |
| Gateworks | The sluices | Machinery halls of capstans, counterweights and enormous chains, turned by Gate Oxen and held by brakes and ratchets |
| Reservoir Range | The reservoir above the dam | Floating pastures, fisheries and breeding islands, with Bargebacks towing cargo between them. Vets and fodder stores are as busy as the docks. |
| Dry Foot | Downstream, outside the walls | The inspected market for foreigners. At the **Broken Halter** tavern, merchants can look up at a city they may never be allowed to enter. |

## Factions and guilds

Politics splits three ways:

- **The Closed Hand** protects the city's isolation.
- **The Open Ledger** wants supervised trade and medicine from outside.
- **The Unbound** fight restrictive apprenticeships and the mistreatment of working animals.

Downstream, the farmers demand a say in decisions about the water that decide their harvests.
Every gate opened for the lower farms costs power, reservoir depth or protection somewhere else,
so keepers, engineers and farmers are always fighting over the same water.

Keth Veyra treats foreigners less as soldiers and more as risks: one poisoned animal, one
contaminated reservoir or one sabotaged gate could endanger the whole city.

## Defence

Invaders must climb the dam's wet face while the defenders hold every bridge, sluice and
waterfall. The city can sweep sheets of water across chosen streets, strand attackers by pulling
in the animal bridges, flood the inner galleries behind sealed doors, launch Brinewings from the
towers straight above the fighting, and send swimming creatures behind enemy boats to cut their
anchor and steering lines. Breaking the dam would unleash the reservoir on the whole valley below,
which makes a siege of Keth Veyra dangerous for everyone.

The story begins when the animals start refusing familiar work. Bargebacks avoid a channel. Oxen
panic in one machinery hall. A nesting Brinewing moves her eggs away from a wall. Officials blame
foreign sabotage, but the animals are feeling the vibrations of a failing gate mechanism, and
powerful keepers have hidden the maintenance reports. Ending the crisis means the city's best
handlers must accept that their animals noticed something they refused to hear.
