**Veyrhold** is a city built down the terraces of an enormous abandoned quarry. Its dark stone
dampens magical explosions, which makes it the safest place in the world for dangerous enchanting.
The deeper you work, the more dangerous and the more profitable your craft.

> Trust the wall. Check the wall.
>
> (Veyrhold's oldest saying)

## Overview

Homes and markets line the sunny upper rim. Workshops step down the quarry in tiers according to
how risky their work is, and experimental weapons are tested on the sealed quarry floor. Cranes,
lifts and guarded stairways join the levels.

The streets themselves are dusty stone, timber and iron; every vivid colour in Veyrhold comes
from magic at work, and it grows stronger the deeper you go. Cyan ward tests, amber binding work,
emerald alchemical vessels and violet discharges light the lower terraces.

Mages learn through paid apprenticeships, working beside carpenters, instrument makers, hauliers
and stoneworkers. A famous spellwright may earn a fortune and still have to wait for the lift crew
to bring up their materials. Quarry owners grow rich while the workers on the lower tiers suffer
magical illnesses.

## History

Veyrhold began as an ordinary quarry settlement. It changed when an enchanter's failed commission
destroyed a cutting shed but left the workers behind its back wall unharmed: the quarry's dark
stone had absorbed the escaping magic. Within a generation, renting out protected workshops earned
more than selling building blocks.

## Hushstone

The quarry's **hushstone** is what makes so much magic in one place possible. Thick, unbroken
walls weaken any uncontrolled spell that passes through them, but open doorways, cracks and
careless digging undo that protection. Long exposure slowly damages the stone and leaves pale,
branching seams that inspectors learn to spot. Raw hushstone is exported for prisons, vaults and
fortifications.

## Layout and districts

| District | Where | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| Sunledge | The sunny rim | Homes, bakeries, gardens, markets and merchants' courtyards, and **the Last Step**, an inn overlooking the quarry |

| Chainmarket | Middle tiers | Freight lifts, warehouses, canteens and shift lodgings. Its lift crews can bring the richest industries to a halt. |
| The Binding Yards | Lower tiers | Enchanting chambers divided by thick rock walls, and **the Assay Court**, where buyers watch independent demonstrations |
| The Rest Houses | Sheltered ledges | Clinics, bathhouses and recovery gardens for magical exposure and ordinary injuries |
| The Nine Shut Doors | A sealed side-cut | Abandoned after a devastating accident. Someone is still paying the rent on one workshop. |
| The Deep Court | The quarry floor | Restricted testing grounds for dangerous commissions and for taking apart unstable artefacts, watched from protected galleries |

## Daily life

Workers eat filled breads at the shift canteens, drink orchard cider and go to the terrace
theatres. At the yearly **Small Wonders Fair**, craftspeople compete to solve everyday problems,
and a charm that keeps a baby's milk warm can win more hearts than a spectacular weapon.

- **[The Street That Changed](../quests/the-street-that-changed.md)**: the Spillward's first job for
  you. A spill is rewriting a street of homes on the rim, and the unlicensed mage who saved its
  people may be the one who caused it.
- **[What the Crystal Remembers](../quests/what-the-crystal-remembers.md)**: steal a crystal from the
  Sunken Works, a collapsed ruin in the hills east of the city, and survive what it does when it
  breaks.

Much of Veyrhold's trouble runs through its lifts: controlling them, smuggling creations between
terraces, and using hushstone to weaken a spell.

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers below.

The landlords want to dig beneath the oldest workshops. They promise jobs and cheap protective
stone; the lift workers have found fresh cracks; independent mages fear that closing the old
chambers will hand every affordable workshop to the biggest firms. A rescue forewoman wants the
surveys published, a workshop owner trusts new shielding, an apprentice cooperative cannot survive
months of closure, and an examiner has found an altered safety report.

Behind the Nine Shut Doors, a survivor of the old disaster has secretly tended a damaged
containment vessel for decades. Moving it could be dangerous; abandoning it would be worse. The
new digging will expose it, and the people who covered up the original accident.
