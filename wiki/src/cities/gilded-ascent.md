**The Gilded Ascent** is the trading heart of the world. It stands at the exact centre of the
continent, where the five great trade roads meet, around **the Ascent**: a stepped market spire
of five tiers, roofed in gilded copper, that you can see for a day's ride in every direction. At
sunset the roofs catch fire one tier after another, from the bottom up, and the whole city looks
as if it is climbing.

Everything here runs on trust, and trust here has a shape: the **Five Seals**. Which seal you
carry decides which tier you may trade on, who will lend to you, which doors open and which guards
salute. Getting a better seal is the dream of every merchant on the continent. Forging one is the
worst crime the city knows.

## Overview

The Gilded Ascent is not a capital: no king rules it, and no army has ever needed to take it,
because every army needs what it sells. It is a merchant republic, governed by the few dozen
people who hold its highest seal, and it is probably the first great city you will see. Caravans
from [Rillvault](rillvault.md) bring food, the [Sifting Basin](sifting-basin.md) sends basin
glass, [Veyrhold](veyrhold.md) sends hushstone, [Tessara](tessara.md) sends Patternwork and
[Oruvai](oruvai.md) sends medicines, and all of it is weighed, sealed, taxed and sold on again
here.

What the Gilded Ascent refuses to sell is just as important. Every banned good is written on
**the Unsealed List**, and most of it ends up in [Arena City](arena-city.md), which was founded
to sell exactly that.

## The Five Seals

Each seal is a small disc, struck from its own material and stamped with the holder's number. A
seal is not money and cannot be sold: it is recorded in **the Registry of Seals** against one
name, and anyone can ask the Registry whether a seal is real.

| Seal | Tier | Who carries it | What it allows |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clay Seal** | The Commons | Anyone who registers at a gate, for one chit a season | Selling from a stall or a blanket; staying in the Ring |
| **Copper Seal** | The Weighhouse | Traders with a guild or a sponsor and a clean record for a year | Trading by registered weight; hiring porters; borrowing small sums |
| **Silver Seal** | The Bonded Halls | Wholesalers, foreign trading houses and consuls | Storing goods tax-free until sold; importing in bulk; a seat in a trade hall |
| **Gold Seal** | The Ledger Floor | Bankers, insurers, notaries and the richest houses | Lending, insuring and witnessing contracts; entering the Registry |
| **Glass Seal** | The Summit | About sixty holders, the Court of Seals | Setting tolls, prices and laws; entering the Lantern Table |

The Glass Seal is cut from a single flawless piece of uncut basin glass from the Sifting Basin,
so clear that it bends the light of any lamp held behind it into five colours. No two are alike,
and the Registry keeps a drawing of each one's light.

**The White Seal** is a temporary seal that a bank or the Registry can issue for a single job, such
as an audit or a test of a vault. It is recorded three times: by whoever issued it, by the Registry
and by an outside notary, and it is cancelled the moment the job ends. In theory.

> A seal is a promise that the city keeps for you. Break the promise and the city forgets your
> name.

Being **unsealed** is the city's harshest punishment: your seals are broken in public, your name
is posted at every gate, and no one holding any seal may trade with you again. Some unsealed
merchants vanish; most go to Arena City.

## The Ascent

The spire is about 300 metres tall. Each tier was added when the one below grew full, so the
lowest is the oldest and the widest, and the Summit is barely larger than a palace garden. Broad
ramps wind up the outside for porters and animals; inside, lifts, stairs and goods shafts link the
floors, and every landing has a **seal gate** where a clerk checks your disc against the Registry.

| Tier | What is there |
|---|---|
| **The Commons** | A roaring covered market of stalls, food, animals and anything cheap. Caravans unload in its yards, porters wait for work and fortune-tellers read seals for luck. Pickpockets love it. |
| **The Weighhouse** | The **Great Balance**, a scale big enough to weigh a loaded wagon, and the **Assayers' Hall**, where coins, metals and glass are tested. Every sale here is by registered weight, and false weights cost a hand of fines. |
| **The Bonded Halls** | Vast, cool warehouses where goods wait tax-free until sold, the trading halls of foreign houses, and the consulates of other cities. The Keth Veyra hall keeps no animals, and the Oruvai hall is a garden. |
| **The Ledger Floor** | Banks, insurers and notaries, the **Registry of Seals** and the great bank of **the Fifth Reserve**. It is quiet, carpeted and guarded by people who never seem to look at you. |
| **The Summit** | The **Court of Seals** and the **Lantern Table**, a round table under a lantern of basin glass where prices for the season are set. Its garden looks across the air to Sky City. |

## Layout and districts

Around the spire the city spreads in rings.

| District | Where | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| **The Ring** | Around the base | Inns, stables and pens, and the **Caravanserai of a Thousand Bells**, where every arriving caravan rings its own bell |
| **The Toll Rings** | Where the five roads enter | Gatehouses, customs yards and the **Hall of Contraband**, where seized goods are kept and sometimes sold back |
| **Sealwrights' Row** | West of the spire | Engravers licensed to cut seal dies, and the best forgers on the continent, often the same people |
| **The Mint of Seals** | South of the spire | Strikes the continent's chits, marks and ascents behind triple doors |
| **The Anchorage** | The eastern edge | A great iron collar sunk into the bedrock, where Sky City's Golden chain comes down, and the lifts that ride it |
| **The Lowmarket** | Outside the walls | Traders who can't afford a Clay Seal or have lost theirs. Unlicensed, unlit and very cheap |
| **The Counting Deep** | Beneath the spire | Vaults cut into the rock, the deepest of them older than the city (see below) |

## Law and government

The **Court of Seals**, the holders of the Glass Seal, governs the city. It meets at the Lantern
Table, sets the tolls on the five roads and the season's prices on the Summit, and appoints the
city's officials. Its chair is the **Sealkeeper**, who guards the master dies that strike every
seal; the present Sealkeeper is [Hesper Lorn](../factions/chartered-road.md#people) of the
Chartered Road.

The city's guards are the **Seal Wardens**, in grey coats with a brass disc for each tier they may
enter. They are polite, well paid and almost impossible to bribe, because a warden caught taking
money is unsealed with their whole family.

**The Unsealed List** names the goods no seal may carry: dark magic reagents and soul gems, glow,
unregistered enchantments ("blank work"), unlicensed firearms, Brinewing eggs, marrowfruit
cuttings, human beings and debts sold with a person attached. The Chartered Road argues about the
list every season. Its black-cargo investors would like it much shorter.

## History

The city began at **the Middle Well**, a deep well at a crossroads where five caravan roads met
and where, about 420 years ago, five rival caravan houses agreed to stop robbing each other. Each
house kept one die, and each struck one kind of seal; to trade with all five, you needed all
five to trust you. Legend says a sixth founder stood with them: a king who had sold his own crown
to buy the well, and who is remembered only as [the Crownless King](../lore/ascendants.md).

Over the centuries the five dies became five tiers, the market became a spire, and the five houses
became the Chartered Road. The Middle Well still exists, sealed beneath the Commons. Its water is
drawn once a year, on the day of **the Restriking**, and drunk by every new Glass Seal holder.

## Daily life

- **The Restriking**: every seventh year, every Copper and Silver Seal is struck again and its
  holder must qualify once more. For a month the city is chaos: queues at the Registry, fortunes
  lost and made, and more forged seals than in the other six years together.
- **Bells**: each caravan's bell rings as it arrives at the Caravanserai, and old porters can tell
  where a caravan comes from by its bell alone.
- **The sunset climb**: as the light climbs the roofs, traders on each tier stop work and look up.
  Superstitious merchants never close a deal while the Summit still shines.
- **Sky City**: from the eastern terraces you can watch Sky City's lifts climb the Golden chain all
  day, carrying investors up and goods down.

## The Counting Deep

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the Counting Deep below.

The vaults of the Ledger Floor go down into the rock, level below level, and the banks rent them by
the room. The lowest levels are not cut but **built**: walls of black stone with no seams, like the
foundations beneath Orath's Hollow Crown. Banks have broken into them for centuries and found
only more doors.

Vault-keepers whisper about a **Sixth Seal**, struck by no house, that opens doors no tier shows.
They also whisper about a temple at the very bottom, every door of which is shut, and about the god
who lives behind them: [the God of Locked Doors](../lore/gods.md#the-god-of-locked-doors).

## Factions and guilds

- **[The Chartered Road](../factions/chartered-road.md)**: the merchants' guild that grew from the
  five founding houses. Most seal holders belong to it, and its four leaders disagree about what
  the seals are for.
- **The Seal Wardens**: the city guard.
- **The Assayers**: test every coin, metal and stone that changes hands on the Weighhouse. Their
  word decides whether a fortune is real.
- **The Sealwrights**: licensed engravers of seal dies, a small, proud and watched guild.
- **The Fifth Reserve**: the bank of banks, which lends to cities and holds the reserves of the
  Gilded Ledger in [Vantress](vantress/index.md) and most other banks on the continent.
- Every great guild keeps a hall in the Bonded Halls, and [the Makers' Compact](../factions/makers-compact.md)
  holds its council here one year in five.

## Quests and rumours

- **[Three Accounts of One Caravan](../quests/three-accounts-of-one-caravan.md)**: the Chartered
  Road's first job for you. A sealed caravan has arrived with cargo missing, and nobody's story
  agrees.
- **The Restriking**: a forger on Sealwrights' Row offers you a Silver Seal of your own, a month
  before the Registry checks every one.
- **The White Seal that wasn't cancelled**: an auditor's temporary seal still opens doors a year
  after the audit ended, and three people want it.
- **The Sixth Seal**: an old vault-keeper swears she has seen it, and wants someone to go down
  with her.
- **[The Temple of Locked Doors](../quests/the-temple-of-locked-doors.md)**: the bottom of the
  Counting Deep, for those who have learned how gods die.
