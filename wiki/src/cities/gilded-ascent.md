**The Gilded Ascent** is the trading heart of the world. It stands at the exact centre of the
continent, where every great trade route meets, around a tall stepped market spire of five tiers.

## Overview

Trade here runs on the **Five Seals**, which decide which merchants may reach which tier of the
spire. It is home to [the Chartered Road](../factions/chartered-road.md), the merchants' guild.
The rich of [Sky City](sky-city.md) live close by.
