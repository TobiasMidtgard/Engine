Every city is built around one idea that decides who holds power, what the people make, what
they fear and how you move through the streets: a dam, a tree, a quarry, a market spire. Each
city page covers its districts, landmarks, people, services and the trouble a visitor can find
there.

Thirteen great cities stand on the continent; see [the world map](../world/index.md#the-world-map)
for where each one lies.
