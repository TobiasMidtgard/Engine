Every city is built around one idea that you can feel as soon as you arrive: who holds power,
what the people make, what they fear and how you move through the streets. Each city page
covers its districts, landmarks, people and the trouble a visitor can find there.

Thirteen great cities stand on the continent; see [the world map](../world/index.md#the-world-map)
for where each one lies.
