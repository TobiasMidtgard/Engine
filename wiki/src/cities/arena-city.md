**Arena City** is built into the seating of a colossal arena in the south. By day its floor is a
vast market for everything banned in the world's main trading hub; by night the same floor is
cleared, the gates seal and the fights begin. Its tiers of seats are hotels, bars, restaurants and
homes, all facing the centre.

## Overview

Every tier of the city looks down on the same central platform. During the day the tiers are
bidding galleries, shops, restaurants and viewing terraces; at night they are the stands. Every
shop has two faces: a back door onto the city street and a front balcony over the arena. Hotels and
restaurants rent their balconies out as private bidding boxes during the day.

A protective wall rings the floor, solid at the bottom with open observation galleries and
transparent magical wards above, so that nobody watching gets hurt. Eight broad avenues run toward
the centre, the stalls stay low so everyone can see, and large goods are shown on platforms or
lifted into the air rather than built up in tall structures.

## The Grand Exchange

At the centre stands a wide, low mechanical platform. By day it is **the Grand Exchange**:

- a rotating auction floor;
- appraisal tables that rise for inspecting rare goods;
- a lifting plinth that raises a single lot for the whole city to see;
- four bridges out to the platforms for each kind of goods;
- trapdoors and hoists that bring goods straight up from the secure vaults below;
- coloured bidding lanterns, so hotels, restaurants and private balconies can bid from afar.

At night the auction machinery sinks underground. The disc becomes the main fighting platform, and
its bridges, lifts and moving sections become part of the arena.

## Layout and districts

| Area | By day | By night |
|---|---|---|
| Central platform | The great auctions and big reveals | The main fighting platform |
| Four outer platforms | Relics, alchemy, creatures and machinery | Second fighting platforms |
| Radial bridges | Market avenues and goods transport | Routes across the arena and chokepoints |
| Water channels | Cargo boats and floating merchants | Obstacles, and room for flying mages |
| Inner wall | Open gates and inspection points | Sealed gates and active wards |
| City tiers | Bidding galleries, shops and dining | Terraces for the crowd |
| South-west quarter | Storage, appraisal and merchants' services | Nightlife and trading after the fights |

The rest of the city spreads around the arena: the great south gate where visitors arrive, the
hotel terraces in the north, homes and artisans' courts in the east, the canal quay on the lowest
ring, restaurant terraces and the fighters' and guards' quarter.

## A day in Arena City

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| Dawn | The four arena gates open and cargo boats come in along the canals. |
| Morning | Stalls unfold from compartments in the floor and goods rise from the vaults. |
| Midday | Demonstrations, auctions and trading fill the arena. |
| Late afternoon | A closing bell starts the pack-down; goods move to the south-west quarter. |
| Dusk | The bridges move, the stalls retract, the gates seal and the wards come on. |
| Night | An acrobatic fighter and a flying mage walk onto the floor that held the market hours before. |

Trade pays for the arena, and the arena brings the crowds that make the market worth trading in.

## Economy

The market sells what the rest of the world forbids, and more than auctions happens on its floor:
demonstrations of forbidden spells and machines, relics authenticated by rival experts, rare
creature parts and specimens unveiled, live enchanting and alchemy, contracts for smugglers,
mercenaries and specialist craftspeople, public price-setting for scarce materials, products tested
against each other, and hearings where fake goods are destroyed in public.

## Factions and guilds

Arena City is a crossroads rather than anyone's headquarters. Merchants, thieves and fences,
fighters, mages who build wards and brokers for assassins all meet in its daytime market. It is
home to [the Brass Circuit](../factions/brass-circuit.md), the guild of arena fighters.

## Quests and rumours

Whether you want to trade, steal or fight with magic, blades or bows, Arena City has work for you.
