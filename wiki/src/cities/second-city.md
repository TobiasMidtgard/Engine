**The Second City** is two cities stacked on top of each other. At street level it is an ordinary,
lawful city of boulevards. Above it, across the rooftops and upper floors, runs one continuous
rooftop street: a criminal metropolis controlled by vampires.

## Overview

The city stands on the north-west coast. Its upper city is home to black magic, vampires,
werewolves, necromancers and murder for hire, and the assassins of
[the Last Writ](../factions/last-writ.md) hide throughout its upper criminal society.
