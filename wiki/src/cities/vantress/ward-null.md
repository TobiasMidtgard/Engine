**Ward Null** is a whole neighbourhood of [Vantress](index.md) that exists but appears on no
official map. It is home to the [thieves' guild](../../factions/thieves-guild.md) and to
everyone the city has declared dead.

## History

Eighty-three years ago, during a catastrophic flood, the council cut a working-class district
loose so it would not drag the wealthy moorings under. Thousands were left in the swamp. When the
survivors returned, they found that they had been declared dead and their homes sold. So they
forged themselves back into existence.

## Overview

Ward Null is not one big underground district. It is put together from unregistered service
platforms, hidden rooms beneath legitimate buildings, hanging walkways and small barges that
change position every night. Its rooms run beneath the tavern, the casino, the Hall of Seals and
the southern half of [Knot Square](knot-square/index.md), and down into the underdecks of the
[Lowwater Chainyards](lowwater-chainyards.md).

The guild's hall is an old census vessel. Its outer plates are swapped so often that, depending
on the day, it looks like a tannery, a shrine, a quarantine station or a tax office. It moves
between moorings, so even members may not know where it will lie tonight.

## Housing and residents

**Underhomes** shelter people declared drowned, escaped debtors, refugees, unregistered
children and families the guild protects.

## Getting around

Abandoned buoyancy chambers beneath Knot Square lead down into the ward.
