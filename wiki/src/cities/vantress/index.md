**Vantress** is a city of floating rafts in a blackwater swamp, where the streets, the
districts and even the legal borders move with the water. It is home to the
[thieves' guild](../../factions/thieves-guild.md), and the most corrupt city in the world: here
the law still works, but only for whoever has bought it.

> In Vantress, an address is often worth more than the building attached to it.

## Overview

Vantress stands where five rivers dissolve into one enormous blackwater basin. It is not a
town on an island but thousands of small square rafts lashed together, with two to five floors
stacked on nearly every one. Rope bridges and gangways are its streets. The rafts sit so close
that most people walk the bridges, while anyone acrobatic can jump the narrow seams between
them and run from deck to balcony to rooftop. A few wider channels stay open for boats.

The city is darker than any other: amber lanterns over black water, with blue and turquoise
light glowing in the depths. Its colours come from black lacquered wood, pale ceramic water
towers, oxidised copper roofs, brilliant market awnings, turquoise algae and red-painted
mooring chains.

## The moving city

Twice each day, as the water rises and falls, enormous winches pull the districts into new
positions. The movement follows a public schedule: bells ring before the chain crews start, and
coloured markers show where each district will go. The wealthy buy safe inner moorings; poorer
neighbourhoods are pushed out toward floodwater, predators and river raiders.

Anyone who learns the schedule can use it:

- **Escape the guards** by crossing into another ward's jurisdiction.
- **Bribe the chain crews** to move a building somewhere it shouldn't be.
- **Use the water level**: low water exposes entrances and routes that high water hides.

## Law and corruption

Every home, warehouse and shop carries a numbered brass **mooring seal**. That number decides
who owns the building, what it pays in tax, which guards patrol it and whether it gets clean
water. Criminals rarely need to break into a building when they can bribe someone to change
what the building legally is. The right forged papers can:

- move a warehouse outside a guard captain's jurisdiction;
- declare stolen cargo to be lawful flood salvage;
- register a living person as drowned and share out their property;
- make someone's home the collateral for a debt they never took on;
- erase a street from the patrol maps for one night;
- hand a shop to a new owner by swapping its brass seal.

Government posts are auctioned openly. Officials then win back what they paid through
"administrative gifts", fines and favourable rulings.

## History

Vantress survives because it is too valuable to abandon: it controls the rivers that link
several inland kingdoms to the coast.

Eighty-three years ago, during a catastrophic flood, the council cut loose a whole
working-class district so it would not drag the wealthy moorings under. Thousands were left in
the swamp. When the survivors came back, they found they had been declared dead and their homes
sold. They forged themselves back into existence, and their erased district lives on as
[Ward Null](ward-null.md).

## Layout and districts

North is upstream. The main current runs from north to south, so the rich live in the north
where the water is cleanest and the anchorage most stable, while smoke, waste, salvage and heavy
industry drift downstream to the south. The districts are not single giant platforms but areas
where one kind of small raft has clustered together.

## Homes

About two in every three rafts are homes, or homes and workplaces together; the famous
buildings stand between them. A typical home keeps work or storage on its lowest floor, receives
visitors at bridge level, lives on the floors above and uses the roof to catch rainwater and dry
clothes.

| Home | Where | Who lives there |
|---|---|---|
| Eelstep Houses | Reedwalk Yards | Families who have run eel pens, gardens, ferries and net-making for generations |
| Bridgebacks | Lantern Ward, Lowwater Chainyards | Rope-splicers, casino workers, porters, laundresses and junior guards in four- and five-storey tenements |
| Shopstacks | Around Knot Square | Merchant families: shop at bridge level, storage below, flats above |
| Rainjar Courts | Rainworks | Potters, water testers, apothecary assistants, teachers and craftspeople doing well |
| Pylon Rooms | Argent Heights | Bank clerks, advocates, senior guards, government scribes and minor merchants |
| Lowwake Bunks | Lowwater Chainyards | Salvage divers, seasonal labourers, new arrivals and boat crews renting beds by the shift |
| Roof Cots | All over the centre | Couriers, performers, young workers and bridge-runners in tiny rooftop cabins |
| Underhomes | Ward Null | The officially drowned, escaped debtors, refugees and unregistered children |

## Economy

Vantress lives on the river trade, and on what only the swamp can make:

- **Blackwater lacquer** that stops ships from rotting.
- **Fever medicines and antivenoms** from swamp plants.
- **Waterproof rope and sailcloth** woven from marsh fibre.
- **Dyes** of brilliant crimson, turquoise and violet.
- **River salvage**, ship repair and the transfer of cargo between river and sea.
- **Glow**, refined in [Fossil Reach](fossil-reach.md): a sun-charged light that is also a
  potent hallucinogen.

People eat rice, lotus roots, mussels, eels, ducks and vegetables from floating compost gardens.
Drinking water is rain, caught in sealed rooftop cisterns, and who gets it is one more tool of
power (see [Rainworks](rainworks.md)).

## Getting around

Four bridge routes meet in [Knot Square](knot-square/index.md), the heart of the city:

| Route | Leads to |
|---|---|
| Brasswalk | North to the bank and the government buildings |
| Lantern Run | West to the casino and the night market |
| Greenline | East to Rainworks and Reedwalk Yards |
| Lowchain | South to the docks and the industrial rafts |

Every street has four layers: the underdecks at the waterline, the bridges, the balconies and
upper walkways, and the rooftops. Between districts you can walk the bridges, balance along the
ropes strung between buildings, or jump across the boats passing in the channels.
