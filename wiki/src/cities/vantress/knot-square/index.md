**Knot Square** is the heart of [Vantress](../index.md): a busy, solid square of rafts where the
city's four great bridge routes meet.

## Overview

By day the square is packed with food stalls, public auctions and travelling traders. At night
it becomes a meeting place, a labour market for anyone looking for work with no questions asked,
and a stage for public challenges. The Central Market and the Concourse of Laurels, a plaza
around a central monument, fill its middle.

| Bridge | Leads to |
|---|---|
| Brasswalk | North to the bank, the government buildings and [Argent Heights](../argent-heights/index.md) |
| Lantern Run | West to the casino and night market of [Lantern Ward](../lantern-ward/index.md) |
| Greenline | East to [Rainworks](../rainworks.md) and [Reedwalk Yards](../reedwalk-yards.md) |
| Lowchain | South to the docks and industry of the [Lowwater Chainyards](../lowwater-chainyards.md) |

## Landmarks

- **[The Gilded Ledger](the-gilded-ledger.md)**: the bank on the north side of the square, which
  decides who owns what.
- **The Hall of Seals**: on the east side. It holds every address, ownership record and mooring
  registration in the city, which makes it the most important target in Vantress for forgers and
  anyone buying a ruling.
- **The Central Market** and **the Concourse of Laurels**, in the middle of the square.
- **The Ward Captain**: the post of the captain who commands the square's guards.
- **The Dry Step**: beneath the eastern edge of the square, a shelter and communal kitchen for
  people without a registered address. Those who donate earn trust, and hear rumours that no rich
  informant could sell them.
- **The thieves' guild headquarters**, on the south-western edge.

## Housing and residents

**Shopstacks** ring the square: the shop at bridge level, storage below and the merchant
family's flats above.

## Secrets

Beneath the square, abandoned buoyancy chambers lead down into [Ward Null](../ward-null.md).
