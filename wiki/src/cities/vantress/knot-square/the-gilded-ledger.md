**The Gilded Ledger** is the bank of [Vantress](../index.md), on the north side of
[Knot Square](index.md) at the end of the Brasswalk bridge. Because it decides who owns what, it
is one of the most powerful institutions in the city.

## Overview

The Gilded Ledger handles far more than money. It stores property deeds, insurance contracts,
debt records and bearer notes.

## Layout

The bank stands on three closely joined rafts:

- **Bridge level**: the public banking hall.
- **Upper floors**: clerks' offices, insurance rooms and the archives.
- **Roof**: guard quarters and a semaphore station.
- **Below**: a watertight vault caisson, chained straight to a stone anchor beneath the water.
  Inspection tunnels run from the vault to the city's flood-control works.
