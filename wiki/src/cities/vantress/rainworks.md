**Rainworks** is the north-eastern district of [Vantress](index.md), built around the cisterns
that hold the city's drinking water.

## Overview

Vantress drinks rain. It is caught in sealed cisterns on the rooftops and gathered here, in a
cluster of tall, pale ceramic water towers. The Greenline bridge leads east from
[Knot Square](knot-square/index.md) toward Rainworks and [Reedwalk Yards](reedwalk-yards.md).

## Landmarks

- **The Rainwater Works**: where the water is tested and rationed. Officials can punish a whole
  neighbourhood simply by delaying its share.

## Housing and residents

**Rainjar Courts** house potters, water testers, apothecary assistants, teachers and
craftspeople who are doing well.
