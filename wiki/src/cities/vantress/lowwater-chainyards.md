The **Lowwater Chainyards** are the docks and industrial rafts of [Vantress](index.md),
downstream in the south and south-west, where the smoke, waste and salvage of the city collect.

## Overview

The Lowchain bridge leads south from [Knot Square](knot-square/index.md) to the docks and the
industrial rafts. Here the city is built, repaired and moved: the chains that hold every district
in place are worked from these yards.

## Landmarks

- **The Great Winch House**: controls the chains that tighten, loosen and move whole sections of
  the city.
- **The Cargo Docks**, where river and sea cargo change hands.
- **Raftworks**, the raft-builders' yards.
- **The Ember Barge**: the city's forge, glass kiln and communal ovens, on the outer edge of the
  yards. If a fire starts it can be cut loose and towed away.
- **The Salvage Court**: inspects and auctions recovered cargo. Bribe its assessors and stolen
  goods become lawful "flood salvage".
- **Dockside Prison**: a prison raft on the southern edge with bridges that pull in. During a riot
  or a rescue, the Watch simply tows it out into open water.
- **The underdecks of [Ward Null](ward-null.md)**.

## Housing and residents

**Lowwake Bunks** rent beds by the shift to salvage divers, seasonal labourers, new arrivals and
boat crews. **Bridgebacks** tenements house rope-splicers, porters and laundresses.
