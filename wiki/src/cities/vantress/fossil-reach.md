**Fossil Reach** is the eastern district of [Vantress](index.md), home of the fossil divers and
of the glow trade.

## Overview

**Glow** is refined here: a sun-charged light that is also a potent hallucinogen.

## Landmarks

- **The Fossil Divers' Hall**
- **The Fossil Exchange**
- **The Glow Workshop**
