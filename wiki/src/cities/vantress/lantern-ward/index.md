**Lantern Ward** is the western district of [Vantress](../index.md): homes and workshops by day,
and after dark the city's casino, theatre and night market.

## Overview

The Lantern Run bridge leads west from [Knot Square](../knot-square/index.md) to the casino and
the night market. Brilliant awnings hang over the market rafts, and the lanterns that give the
ward its name burn late.

## Landmarks

- **[The Seven Lanterns](seven-lanterns.md)**: the casino, a tall tower hung with lanterns two
  bridges west of Knot Square, at the entrance to the night market.
- **[The Drunken Sailor](drunken-sailor.md)**: the tavern where the ward meets Knot Square, and
  the best place in the city to hear rumours.
- **The Hanging Theatre**: its stage, audience platforms and backstage rooms stand on separate
  rafts joined by suspended galleries, which makes it one of the best places in the city for a
  rooftop chase.

## Housing and residents

**Bridgebacks**, four- and five-storey tenements, house rope-splicers, casino workers, porters,
laundresses and junior guards.
