**The Drunken Sailor** is a tavern in [Vantress](../index.md), where
[Lantern Ward](index.md) meets the south-western corner of
[Knot Square](../knot-square/index.md). It is run by
[Maera "Cask" Orro](../../../people/maera-orro.md).

## Overview

Labourers, traders, ferrymen, gamblers, clerks and members of the
[thieves' guild](../../../factions/thieves-guild.md) all drink here, yet it is not an obvious
criminals' den. It is the best place in the city to pick up work: talk at the bar, drunken
boasts, the guests in the rented rooms and the boat crews coming in all point to jobs, without a
quest board in sight.

## Layout

- **Bridge level**: the crowded public room.
- **Upstairs**: cheap rented rooms.
- **Roof terrace**: used by musicians and messengers.
- **Kitchen**: on a smaller raft tethered alongside.
- **Below**: a boat entrance hidden behind floating casks.
- **The back room**: a private room whose wall opens onto a neighbouring raft that appears in no
  register.
