**The Seven Lanterns** is the casino of [Vantress](../index.md), a tall, narrow tower hung with
lanterns in [Lantern Ward](index.md), two bridges west of
[Knot Square](../knot-square/index.md) at the entrance to the night market.

## Layout

The casino stands across two rafts:

| Floor | What is there |
|---|---|
| Upper floors | Private rooms and the high-stakes tables |
| Bridge level | The main gaming hall |
| Lower floor | Cheap dice, card games and betting on the river races |
| At the back | The counting room, staff passages and an office for laundering money |
| Below | Ballast chambers that keep the gaming tables level as the raft moves |

## Secrets

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers below.

The ballast is the casino's weakness. Working alone, you can join the inspection staff and
tamper with a table's levelling marks. With an accomplice below deck, you can shift the raft's
balance in the middle of a dice or wheel game.
