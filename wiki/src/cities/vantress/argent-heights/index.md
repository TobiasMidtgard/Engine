**Argent Heights** is the wealthy northern quarter of [Vantress](../index.md). It sits upstream,
where the water is cleanest and the anchorage most stable, so its rafts are the last to be
pushed out when the city moves.

## Overview

The rich buy safe inner moorings, and the safest of all are here. The Brasswalk bridge runs
north from [Knot Square](../knot-square/index.md) to the bank and the government buildings and
on into the Heights. Everything that the rest of the city fears, from floodwater and predators to
the smoke of the Chainyards, ends up downstream.

## Landmarks

- **[The Vale](the-vale.md)**: the home of Hadrin Vale, a rich man still trying to join the
  city's true ruling class.
- **The Grand Hotel**
- **The Argent Walk**
- **Maison Verrel**: a manor.

## Housing and residents

**Pylon Rooms** house the people who keep the city's money and law running: bank clerks,
advocates, senior guards, government scribes and minor merchants.
