**The Vale** is the house of [Hadrin Vale](../../../people/hadrin-vale.md) in
[Argent Heights](index.md). It stands north-east of the bank, between the wealthy north and the
cisterns of [Rainworks](../rainworks.md).

## Overview

The Vale is built across three private rafts:

- **The residence**: four storeys, the family's home.
- **The garden raft**: a small water garden and a private cistern.
- **The service raft**: kitchens, servants' rooms and storage.

## Residents

Hadrin Vale lives here with his wife, their two children and his widowed sister, along with a
steward, three servants and two hired guards.

## Getting in

Only one official bridge reaches the Vale. Its balconies, delivery cranes, garden trellises and
rooflines offer plenty of other ways in for anyone who can climb.

## Valuables

What the Vale keeps is worth stealing because it can be sold: silver trade weights, bearer
notes, sealed dye concentrates and imported coins, rather than one famous treasure that every
fence would recognise.
