**Reedwalk Yards** is the south-eastern district of [Vantress](index.md): eel pens, floating
gardens and tanneries, home to families who have lived on the water for generations.

## Overview

The Greenline bridge leads east from [Knot Square](knot-square/index.md) toward Reedwalk Yards.
Much of the city's food grows or swims here.

## Landmarks

- **The Reed-Walker Tannery**
- **The House of Returned Names**: part shrine, part undertaker and part memorial archive.
  Families leave tokens here for relatives lost to the water.

## Housing and residents

**Eelstep Houses** belong to families who have run eel pens, gardens, ferries and net-making
businesses for generations.
