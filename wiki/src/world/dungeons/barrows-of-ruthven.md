The kings of **Ruthven** ruled the central plains before the Gilded Ascent, and they were buried in
**barrows**, long mounds of earth over stone passages, on the low hills of what are now the Tollands.
There are nine. Eight have been robbed. The ninth, the **Long Barrow**, half a kilometre long, has a
door that no one has opened in four hundred years, and last month it was found standing open, with
the earth heaped outside it, as if something had pushed its way out.

## Getting there

Half a day west of the Gilded Ascent, on the hills above the Middle River. Grave robbers camp at the foot of the Long Barrow by night.

## The Barrow King

The **Barrow King** is the last king of Ruthven but one: the father of the Crownless King, who was
buried without his crown because his son had already sold it. He has been awake, in the dark, since
his son's bargain, and he wants his crown back. He fights with a broad sword and calls his soldiers
up from the Hall of Shields, and he cannot be killed while he believes he is a king.

## Secrets

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the secrets of this place below.

- The empty bed in the Kings' Rest is the Crownless King's own. He was never buried here, and his body was never found.
- Give the Barrow King a crown (any crown: a paper one from a festival will do) and he lies down and sleeps, and the soldiers with him.
