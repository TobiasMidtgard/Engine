A hundred years ago the Chartered Road built six toll forts along the Black Track to police the
steppe, and lost all six within a generation. **The Old Toll Fort**, the largest, is held by the
**Wind's Teeth**, a raider band who take caravans on the Track and, lately, let the drivers go. Their
chief has started paying people. Nobody pays raiders to stop raiding unless something worse is coming.

## Getting there

Two days out on the Black Track from the Tollands, on a low ridge with a view of the whole steppe. They will see you coming.

## Marrok Teague

**Marrok Teague** leads the Wind's Teeth. He is forty, scarred and clever, rides a black steppe horse
and fights with a great axe, from the saddle if he can. He would rather talk. He has been paid, by an
agent of the Fifth Reserve, to keep the Black Track open for one cargo in the Five Quiet Days, and he
doesn't like the cargo.
