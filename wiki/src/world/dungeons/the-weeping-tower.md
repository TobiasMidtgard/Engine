**The Weeper** is the tallest sea stack off the Grey Coast, and on top of it stands a tower that
weeps: salt water runs down its walls in every weather, and at night the sea below it is full of
lights. **Silas Morrow**, a necromancer of the Pale Archive, built it thirty years ago, after his wife
drowned off Fogwick. He meant to bring her back. The tower went dark, and everyone assumed he had
failed. Its light has come back on.

## Getting there

At low tide, a causeway of rocks runs from the Wreckers' Steps to the Weeper's foot. It floods in two hours. Or by boat from Fogwick, if anyone will take you.

## Silas Morrow

**Silas Morrow** succeeded, in a way: he raised his wife from the sea. She was not his wife. He has
spent thirty years trying to mend what came back, and he has become what necromancers become when
they raise themselves to keep working. He fights with dark magic from the lantern room, raising the
drowned from the sea below, and he begs you to leave between every spell.
