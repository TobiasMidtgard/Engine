Before the desert, there was a river here, and on the river was **Saffra**, a city of the old
kingdoms that traded glass with Ruthven. The river moved, the sand came, and Saffra was buried in a
generation. A sandstorm this year uncovered the top of its tallest tower, with a window, and a lamp
burning in the window, and the people of Sweetwater would like to know who is keeping it lit.

## Getting there

A day and a half south of Sweetwater across open dunes: carry water for three days. The tower top is the only way in, unless you dig.

## Enemies

- [Glass worms](../../creatures/glass-worm.md) in the sand around and under the city
- [Dune runners](../../creatures/dune-runner.md) on the surface
- Glass thieves, who got here first (see [Human enemies](../../creatures/human-enemies.md))
- The city's keepers: three old people, descendants of Saffra's last librarians, who have lived down here all their lives and are not hostile

## The Great Glass Worm

The **Great Glass Worm** has lived in Saffra's glassworks for three hundred years, eating sand and
breathing heat, and the fused streets are its tunnels. It is forty metres long. It swims through
sand as through water, and it comes up under you. The keepers feed it, and it lets them live. It
doesn't know you.

- The library holds a book in the Builders' script, which Saffra's librarians copied without being able to read: a list of wells, older than the Sixth Register.
