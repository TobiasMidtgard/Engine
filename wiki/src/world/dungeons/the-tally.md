**The Tally** was a census barge of Vantress: a floating office where the city counted its people.
In the Drowning Year it sank under what are now the Lowwater Chainyards, with the original roll of
the dead and the deeds of every home the council sold afterwards in an iron chest in its hold. In
[Yara Coil](../../people/companions/yara-coil.md)'s quest, *What the Water Kept*, she has found it.

## Getting there

Dive from the salvage rafts with a lantern and a rope (Gill-drink, or Yara's Long breath, helps); raise the wreck with the Great Winch House (everyone will see); or let the Tangle plan it.

## The Census Clerk

The **Census Clerk** was the Tally's chief clerk, and he is still counting. He drowned with the roll,
and he rose with it, and he will not let it go until every name in it is accounted for. He fights by
counting: every name he reads aloud raises one of the drowned. He can be beaten, or he can be
answered: tell him the names of the people who lived, and he crosses them off.
