The world is older than its cities, and most of what is underneath them is not on any map. These
are the **dungeons and ruins** you can explore: the tombs of the old kingdoms, the First Builders'
black stone, workshops that went wrong, wrecks, prisons and towers. Each page has its layout, its
enemies, its boss with full numbers, its treasure and its secrets (hidden behind a spoiler cover).

## The black stone

Six of these places stand on the **black stone** of the First Builders: seamless, uncuttable, and
older than every faith. Each one has a door of black stone over a **well**, a place where one of the
[Fundaments](../../lore/fundaments.md) rises raw, and each door has a **doorwarden**, a guardian of
the same stone that wakes when anyone not written in comes near.

| Door | Well | Where |
|---|---|---|
| The First Spring | Life | [The Fifth Passage](the-fifth-passage.md) |
| The Deepworks | Force | [The Deepworks](the-deepworks.md) |
| The Drowned Stair | Matter | [The Echo Galleries](the-echo-galleries.md) |
| The ninth workshop | Energy | [The Nine Shut Doors](the-nine-shut-doors.md) |
| The Gateworks | Time | [Under the Dam](under-the-dam.md) |
| The Register room | The god's door | [The Counting Deep](the-counting-deep.md) |

What the black stone is, who built it, and what it was built for is the main story,
[The Unsealed](../../quests/main/index.md).

## How dungeons work

- **Levels are a guide.** Nothing stops you walking in early, and very little stops you from
  dying there.
- **There is more than one way in.** Every dungeon has a front door, and most have a back one: a
  drain, a roof, a river, a bribe.
- **Bosses can often be talked to.** Old Sorrel surrenders; the Barrow King wants his crown; Silas
  Morrow wants to be stopped. Fighting is one answer.
- **What you take, someone owns.** Grave goods belong to families, salvage to the Salvage Court, and
  the banks' vaults to the banks. Taking them is a [crime](../../play/crime.md) if anyone can prove it.
