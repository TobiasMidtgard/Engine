**The Burnt Mill** was a watermill on the Bell Road until a fire twenty years ago. Its waterwheel
still turns, its trough still fills, and carts still stop to water their horses there, which is why
a gang of bandits lives in its shell. In [The Bell Road](../../quests/main/the-bell-road.md), the Seal Wardens
on your heels can catch up with you here, and you can lead them into the bandits' ambush.

## Getting there

A day south of Halfbell on the Bell Road, where the road dips to cross the mill race. You can't miss it; that is the point.

## Layout

| # | Area | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The trough** | A stone horse trough by the road. Anyone who stops here is watched from the mill. |
| 2 | **The yard** | Burnt carts, a cookfire and two dogs. The ambush starts here. |
| 3 | **The wheelhouse** | The waterwheel, still turning. The wheel can be jammed with a beam to flood the cellar. |
| 4 | **The grinding floor** | The bandits' beds, a lookout at the broken window and an archer |
| 5 | **The cellar** | Stolen cargo, and Old Sorrel, who sleeps on it |

## Enemies

- Bandits with clubs, spears and knives, levels 3 to 5 (see [Human enemies](../../creatures/human-enemies.md))
- A bandit archer at the window
- Two dogs, which bark at anything that moves (see [Wolf](../../creatures/wolf.md) for how a pack moves)

## Old Sorrel

**Old Sorrel** has run the Burnt Mill's gang for twelve years. She is sixty, lame in one leg, and
fights with a long boathook from behind a barricade of sacks, while her people try to get round you.
She surrenders when half her gang is down, if you let her, and tells you everything she has heard on
the road for her life.

## Secrets

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the secrets of this place below.

- The letter in Sorrel's strongbox is from a Seal Warden sergeant, paying her to watch the road for a courier in an outrider's coat. It is dated a week before Kit Fenn died.
- Jam the waterwheel with the beam by the wheelhouse and the cellar floods: Sorrel comes up the stairs to you.
