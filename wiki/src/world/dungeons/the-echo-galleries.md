Below Rillvault's spring headworks and the performance grotto of the Lantern Gardens lie the **Echo
Galleries**: tunnels so smooth they look poured. Rillvault's oldest story says
[the Voice Beneath Stone](../../lore/ascendants.md#the-voice-beneath-stone) sang the rock open. The
galleries carry sound for kilometres, and they remember: sing the right note in the right chamber and
the rock sings back what it heard long ago (see [What the Builders Buried](../../quests/main/what-the-builders-buried.md)).

## Getting there

Through the Mouth, the grotto behind the Lantern Gardens' stage, with the Root Ledger's leave.

## Layout

| # | Area | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Mouth** | A grotto shaped like an open mouth. A tuning stone sets the note for everything below. |
| 2 | **The Choir of Pillars** | Pillars that ring at different pitches; strike them in the carvings' order and a passage opens |
| 3 | **The Drowned Stair** | Half flooded, with the water thinning and steps reappearing for the first time in centuries |
| 4 | **The crawlers' nest** | A dome of pale shells, and the Matriarch |
| 5 | **The Listening Room** | Where the oldest echo lives, and the Voice's translation is cut into the walls |
| 6 | **The door of Matter** | At the bottom of the Drowned Stair, over a well of raw Matter. Not yet written in. |

## Enemies

- [Echo-crawlers](../../creatures/echo-crawler.md), which hunt by sound: walk, don't run
- [Lantern spiders](../../creatures/lantern-spider.md) on the high ceilings
- The galleries themselves: shout and the echo comes back loud enough to knock you down

## The Matriarch

The **Matriarch** is the oldest echo-crawler in the galleries, as big as a cart, blind, and able to
hear a heartbeat across a cavern. She fights by sound: every noise you make tells her where you are,
and her shriek, focused by the galleries, can stun. Silence is your weapon. So is the Choir of
Pillars: ring a pillar and she turns on it.
