Beneath Orath's **Underchoir**, beyond burial galleries built inside an old reservoir, runs an
abandoned water channel, and beyond the channel lies **the fifth passage**: an archive older than all
four faiths, recording how the communities before them kept the First Spring alive together. At its
end stands a door of black stone over the place where the Spring rises (see
[A Spring Running Dry](../../quests/main/a-spring-running-dry.md)).

## Getting there

Through the Underchoir and the burial galleries, with the Deep Choir's leave or without it, then along Ilyan Dareth's channel.

## Layout

| # | Area | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The burial galleries** | Shelves of the dead inside the old reservoir walls; the Deep Choir's custodians guard them |
| 2 | **The channel** | A dry stone channel, waist-deep in places with seepage; the missing worker's lantern |
| 3 | **The fifth passage** | Tablets cut into the walls in five scripts, the oldest of which no one can read. The newest entry is in fresh ink. |
| 4 | **The well cave** | A cave full of water that glows. The missing worker is here, alive and terrified. |
| 5 | **The black door** | A seamless door of black stone over the Spring's source, closing a hair's width a day |

## Enemies

- The water-sellers' thugs, who use the channel to fill their barrels from a cistern (see [Human enemies](../../creatures/human-enemies.md))
- [Gloomwings](../../creatures/gloomwing.md) in the channel
- A [doorwarden](../../creatures/doorwarden.md) of the First Builders at the black door

## The doorwarden of the Spring

A **doorwarden** is a guardian of black stone that the First Builders set at their doors: a tall,
faceless figure without seams that wakes when anyone not written in comes near. The Spring's warden
has slept for a thousand years. The black page wakes it, and it will not let you pass with the page,
until it understands that the page is what keeps the door open.

## Secrets

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the secrets of this place below.

- The newest entry in the fifth passage is in a neat, slanted hand, and adds the First Spring to a list.
- If you hold the page against the doorwarden's chest, it kneels, and lets you through for the rest of the game.
