The **Pale Archive**, the necromancers' tower of the Eaves, registers every corpse in the Second City.
Below its lowest tier lie catacombs older than the Eaves themselves, where the Archive's clerks are
not allowed to go. In [Teodor Voss](../../people/companions/teodor-voss.md)'s quest, *The Unregistered*,
they follow two hundred and twelve missing pages down there.

## Getting there

Through the Pale Archive's lowest tier, past a locked grille (Lockcraft, Adept), or up through the Boulevard's sewers, which the Magistracy pretends don't reach this far.

## Layout

| # | Area | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The registry stair** | A spiral stair below the Archive, lined with the ledgers of three centuries |
| 2 | **The old tombs** | Burials from before the Eaves, under the first Boulevard |
| 3 | **The guardians' gate** | A gate of fused bone, with two bone golems |
| 4 | **The new shelves** | 212 corpses on fresh shelves, every one wearing a seal of the Gilded Ascent |
| 5 | **The Shelf-Keeper's hall** | A vaulted hall where the bones of the catacombs have been gathered into one shape |

## Enemies

- [Undead](../../creatures/undead.md): skeletons, zombies and wraiths of the old tombs
- Bone golems at the guardians' gate
- The Broker's hired men, guarding the new shelves (see [Human enemies](../../creatures/human-enemies.md))

## The Shelf-Keeper

The **Shelf-Keeper** is a bone golem of the catacombs, made centuries ago to keep the old tombs in
order, and remade by the Broker's junior registrar to guard the new shelves. It is made of hundreds
of skeletons and it keeps adding to itself from the fallen. Kill what it fights beside before you
fight it, or it grows.
