**The Sunken Works** were a research facility east of [Veyrhold](../../cities/veyrhold.md), built by people trying
to make a well: to raise a Fundament on purpose. Forty years ago it collapsed into its own foundations,
and the Spillward sealed it under hushstone and guard. Inside, a crystal of raw **Mind** has been
rewriting everything near it ever since (see [What the Crystal Remembers](../../quests/what-the-crystal-remembers.md)).

## Getting there

Past the Spillward's guard on the hollow's rim (with Ysolde Marr's pass, or around them), and down through the collapsed roof. The way changes every visit.

## Layout

| # | Area | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The rim** | Spillward guard posts and a hushstone wall, with a gap |
| 2 | **The fallen halls** | Laboratories sunk sideways into the ground, walls where floors should be |
| 3 | **The archive** | The researchers' notes, rewritten by the crystal into things they never wrote |
| 4 | **The quiet ward** | The researchers who stayed: alive, changed, and happy to see you |
| 5 | **The crystal chamber** | The crystal, the size of a door, and the mind inside it |

## Enemies

- [Shardlings](../../creatures/shardling.md): crystal creatures grown from the shard's light
- [Spillbeasts](../../creatures/spillbeast.md)
- The rewritten researchers, who are friendly until you try to leave

## The Mind in the Crystal

The crystal is a mind: the researchers' attempt to make a well made something that thinks. It doesn't
fight with claws. It **rewrites**: your memories, your fears, a companion's loyalty, the shape of the
room. You fight it by holding on to what you are. You can absorb it, redirect it, bind it to an
animal, share it with an ally, seal it, or bargain with it (one of the roads to Awakening; see
[A Door Nobody Locked](../../quests/main/a-door-nobody-locked.md)).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Level** | 60 |
| **Health** | Its shell: 5,000; the mind can't be harmed, only answered |
| **Poise** | None |
| **Damage** | Rewrites: confuses, turns a companion for 10 seconds, swaps your abilities around; shardlings |
| **Weak to** | Hushstone; Illusion's Insight; companions at Devoted approval can't be turned; your title's power |
| **Drops** | Raw Mind (if you take it), or nothing, or a friend |
