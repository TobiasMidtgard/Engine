Beneath Sky City's Sheltered Rings, **the Deepworks** run down through winches, counterweights and
ballast chambers into the island's ancient **buoyancy caverns**, whose walls are seamless black stone.
At the bottom, a well of raw Force pushes up: the reason the island floats at all. In
[The Island Sinks](../../quests/main/the-island-sinks.md), Registry surveyors with a warden escort are already down
there, measuring how long the island has left.

## Getting there

Through the maintenance hatches of the Sheltered Rings (an engineer's pass, or Lockcraft), or up from below the island on a glider, through a ballast vent.

## Layout

| # | Area | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The ballast halls** | Huge iron weights on chains, moving slowly as the island trims itself. Get between two and you are flattened. |
| 2 | **The winch galleries** | The roots of the three anchor chains, and their brakes |
| 3 | **The buoyancy caverns** | Black stone caves where things fall upwards; you can walk on the ceilings near the well |
| 4 | **The surveyors' camp** | Registry surveyors' tents, instruments and a notebook |
| 5 | **The door of Force** | A door of black stone over the well, nearly shut; the air around it pushes you away |

## Enemies

- Seal Wardens and Registry surveyors (see [Human enemies](../../creatures/human-enemies.md))
- The ballast mechanisms, which kill the careless
- A [doorwarden](../../creatures/doorwarden.md) at the door of Force

## Warden-Sergeant Hollis

**Warden-Sergeant Hollis** commands the surveyors' escort: a veteran of the Seal Wardens with a tower
shield and a spear, and six wardens who fight in a line. She will let you leave if you leave the
notebook. In the caverns near the well, gravity turns: fight her on the ceiling and her shield wall
is useless.
