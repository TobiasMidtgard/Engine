Below the lowest sluice of Keth Veyra's **Gateworks**, the dam's foundations stand on black stone that
the Kingdom of Esk's engineers found and built over rather than break. Behind it is a door over a well
of raw **Time**, jammed half open since the Drowning Year. Time behaves oddly near it: water hangs in
the air, a dropped coin falls slowly, and you can see a moment before it happens (see
[The Drowning Year](../../quests/main/the-drowning-year.md)).

## Getting there

Through the channel the Bargebacks won't enter, with a Channel Diver; down the hidden stair in the keeper families' records; or down the spillway on a Brinewing.

## Enemies

- Seal Wardens with harnesses (see [Human enemies](../../creatures/human-enemies.md))
- Time echoes: yourself, a few seconds ahead, which the wardens can see and you can use
- A [doorwarden](../../creatures/doorwarden.md) at the door of Time

## The Registry scribe

The **Registry scribe**, Ansel Voight, can read the Builders' script, and he has the right page this
time. He fights from behind his wardens with the page itself: every line he reads aloud slows you, or
speeds them, and if he finishes the page, the door begins to close. Behind him, the doorwarden of Time
wakes when the page is read, and it doesn't care whose side anyone is on.
