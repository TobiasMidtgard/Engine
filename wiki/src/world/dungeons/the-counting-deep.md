Beneath the Ledger Floor, beneath the vaults of **the Fifth Reserve**, the **Counting Deep** goes down:
first the banks' rented vaults, level below level, and then the built levels of black stone without
seams, which the banks don't rent because nobody built them. Every door in the black levels reads the
seal of whoever passes and writes them down. At the bottom, behind a door that opens only to the
Sixth Seal, is the Register room (see [Steal the Register](../../quests/main/steal-the-register.md)).

## Getting there

Through the Fifth Reserve (Sella Dask's bought clerk, a false bonded cargo, or Nilo Sare's route over the roofs), or up the Middle Well from the Commons with Yara Coil. The door at the bottom needs the Sixth Seal.

## Layout

| # | Area | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Fifth Reserve** | The Ledger Floor's great bank: Seal Wardens, hushstone vaults, doors that read seals |
| 2 | **The rented vaults** | Thirty levels of banks' vaults, each with its own guards, locks and traps |
| 3 | **The Middle Well** | The old well of the Crownless King's bargain, running down beside the vaults |
| 4 | **The black levels** | Seamless black stone; every door writes down who passes. You have no seal, and they don't know you exist. |
| 5 | **The Register room** | The Sixth Register on a stand of black stone, open, with fresh ink drying on its last page |
| 6 | **The god's door** | Behind the Register room: a door the Builders shut and never opened |

## Enemies

- Seal Wardens of the Fifth Reserve (see [Human enemies](../../creatures/human-enemies.md))
- Vault constructs of the banks: brass guardians that only stop for the right seal
- [Doorwardens](../../creatures/doorwarden.md) of the black levels

## Gisela Quell

At the end of the story, [Gisela Quell](../../people/gisela-quell.md) waits in the Register room with the
Sixth Register open, as an Ascendant of Keeping who locks doors, abilities, companions, time and your
name. Her full fight is on her page and on [The Great Restriking](../../quests/main/the-great-restriking.md).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Level** | 100 |

| **Poise** | Can't be staggered while she holds a lock |

| **Drops** | Her Glass Seal; the Keeper's Pen; whatever is left of the Sixth Register |
