The roots of **Avarren**, Orravell's great tree, go down further than the tree goes up, and between
them lies **the Rootdark**: a maze of root-tunnels, underground rivers and caves, glowing faintly
with lantern spiders. The first raiders of Avarren hid their plunder here. The Office of Civic
Integrity hides people here now, in cells cut into the roots, and Orravell officially has no idea
what you are talking about.

## Getting there

Through the root-gardens at the tree's foot, through a drain from the Council of Stewards' cellars, or by swimming up the underground river from the Kestrel.

## Layout

| # | Area | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The root-gardens** | The tree's foot, where fallen bark is gathered; the way down is behind the compost heaps |
| 2 | **The spider halls** | Caves strung with glowing silk; walk through without touching a thread |
| 3 | **The raiders' hoard** | A cave where the first raiders left offerings to the tree, still there |
| 4 | **The cells** | The Office of Civic Integrity's prison: forty cells in the roots, and their prisoners |
| 5 | **The Root Mother's nest** | A cavern of silk around the oldest root, and the oldest spider |

## Enemies

- [Lantern spiders](../../creatures/lantern-spider.md), and their mother
- [Canopy stalkers](../../creatures/canopy-stalker.md) that come down the roots to hunt
- Civic Integrity guards and interrogators (see [Human enemies](../../creatures/human-enemies.md))

## Commissar Vellen Harsk

**Commissar Vellen Harsk** of the Long Shade runs the cells. He is courteous, thorough and devoted to
the Greater Canopy, and he has a key to every cell on a ring at his belt. He fights with a sabre and a
pistol from Tessara, and calls the guards. If you free the prisoners first, they fight him for you.
Beyond his cells, the **Root Mother**, the lantern spiders' queen, is optional, and much worse.
