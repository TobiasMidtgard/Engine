The **Nine Shut Doors** are nine workshops cut into a side-branch of Veyrhold's quarry, sealed with
hushstone after a disaster forty years ago that the city prefers not to discuss. The landlords now
want to dig beneath the oldest workshops beside them. Behind the ninth door, someone has been
tending something all that time (see [Behind the Nine Shut Doors](../../quests/main/behind-the-nine-shut-doors.md)).

## Getting there

Down Brannagh Fisk's lifts to the closed levels, through Sabra Quint's records to the old survey tunnel, or through the Kiln Street cooperative's loft and down its drain.

## Layout

| # | Area | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The sealed gallery** | Nine bricked doors in a row, each with a workshop's mark |
| 2 | **Doors one to eight** | Empty workshops, each a little stranger than the last: frost in summer, rooms longer inside than out |
| 3 | **The landlords' cut** | A new tunnel coming in from below, with diggers and fresh cracks |
| 4 | **The ninth workshop** | Old Tamm's home: a bed, a stove, a hatch for food, and the humming copper vessel |
| 5 | **The black crack** | A seam of black stone in the ninth workshop's floor, split open a finger's width, breathing heat |

## Enemies

- [Spillbeasts](../../creatures/spillbeast.md) in the empty workshops
- The landlords' diggers and guards (see [Human enemies](../../creatures/human-enemies.md))
- Spill echoes: shapes of the ninth crew, repeating their last minute

## The Ninth Crew

When the landlords' dig reaches the crack, what is left of the energy that killed the ninth crew
forty years ago comes out with it, and it remembers them. **The Ninth Crew** are six figures of light
in the shapes of the cutters who died, repeating the last minute of their lives, and burning anything
they touch. They can't be hurt, only contained: with hushstone, with Old Tamm's vessel, or by sealing
the crack.

| | |
