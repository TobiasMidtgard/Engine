**The Canopy Marches** are the borderlands around [Orravell](../../cities/orravell.md), the city in a single
colossal tree. The court maps draw the great symbolic **canopy** of the Doctrine of the Greater Canopy
over all of them, and the edge of the canopy moves outward every year. The Crown Road runs through
them paved and patrolled; off the road are old forest, raiders' memorials, Companies' training
grounds, and the farms of **Kestrel Vale**, which hired the Crown Companies for a long war and now
owes them heavily (see [Operation Second Spring](../../quests/operation-second-spring.md)).

## Geography

The great tree **Avarren** stands at the centre, so tall that its shadow is a district of its own.
Around it, the old forest grows dense and dark; to the east, the river **Kestrel** opens into a broad
farming valley of terraced barley and orchards, with villages on every bend. Checkpoints of the
Crown Companies stand at every bridge.

## Settlements

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Hollin** | Kestrel Vale's market town: a town hall, a granary, a free inn (the Kestrel, 8 chits) and a council meeting in secret |
| **Merrow Ford** | A village on the Kestrel, half empty since the last transfer list |
| **The transfer camps** | Fenced fields where families from the valley wait to be moved. Orravell calls them resettlement. |
| **Hushwing Station** | Orravell's glider ledges, high in the tree; Captain Sera Noll's crews |
| **Cohort Hill** | A training ground and garrison of the Bough Cohorts, with a quartermaster who sells surplus |

## Points of interest

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **The raiders' stones** | Standing stones in the forest where the first raiders of Avarren are buried |
| **The fallen limb** | A branch of Avarren that fell a century ago, as big as a hill, hollowed into a village of charcoal burners |
| **The canopy line** | Painted posts that mark where the court maps say Orravell ends this year |

## Rumours and work

- Hollin's council wants someone to carry a letter to the Gilded Ascent without the Crown reading it (see [The Price of Kestrel Vale](../../quests/main/the-price-of-kestrel-vale.md)).
- Two deserters from the Bough Cohorts are hiding in the fallen limb. The Companies pay 5 marks a head.
- The canopy line moved three kilometres overnight. A farm that was free yesterday is Orravell's today.
- Brenn Askew's Sand Lions are posted at Merrow Ford (see [Brenn Askew](../../people/companions/brenn-askew.md)).
