**The Whitecrowns** are the snow mountains along the north of the continent. The Bell Road climbs
into them for its last three days and ends at the gate of [Keth Veyra](../../cities/keth-veyra.md), which
rarely opens. Beyond the city, the mountains run down to a coast of ice floes and the
[Ice Spires](../landmarks/ice-spires.md). This is the hardest country on the continent to cross:
the passes close in winter, avalanches take whole caravans, and the cold kills anyone who isn't
ready for it (see [Attributes and vitals](../../play/attributes.md#hunger-sleep-and-weather)).

## Geography

The great river of the north rises here and runs through a gorge that the Kingdom of Esk's engineers
closed with a dam three centuries ago; behind it lies the **Reservoir Range**, a lake of floating
pastures and breeding islands. Above the dam the peaks rise white to the sky; below it the river
falls through the Gateworks and runs south and west across the whole continent, all the way to the
sea at [Vantress](../../cities/vantress/index.md).

## Settlements

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Dry Foot** | Keth Veyra's inspected market for foreigners, outside the walls. The Broken Halter tavern (2 marks a night) looks up at a city most will never enter. |
| **Last Bell** | The Bell Road's highest waystation: a stone hut, a fire, a bell for lost travellers, and a keeper who has rescued two hundred people |
| **Highcairn** | A herding village of ridge-goat shepherds on the pass above the reservoir; they sell goat's wool coats that keep out anything |
| **The Shepherds' Huts** | Stone shelters along the passes, stocked with wood and food by custom. Taking without leaving is the worst crime in the mountains. |

## Rumours and work

- The keeper of Last Bell hasn't rung the bell in three days. Someone should check on him.
- A Brinewing egg has been seen in a shepherd's hut on the pass. The shepherd says it was left there on purpose.
- Highcairn's goats won't go near one of the high pastures, where the snow melts in a perfect circle.
- In Dry Foot, a foreign merchant offers 10 ascents for a single Brinewing scale, no questions asked. The keepers would like to know who he is (see [The Drowning Year](../../quests/main/the-drowning-year.md)).
