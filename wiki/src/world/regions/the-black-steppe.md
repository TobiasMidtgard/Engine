**The Black Steppe** is the great grassland of the south, named for its grass, which grows almost
black in the summer heat. Nobody governs it. The Chartered Road gave up policing it a century ago,
and the **Black Track**, the route across it to [Arena City](../../cities/arena-city.md), is the road for
everything the Gilded Ascent bans and everyone the Gilded Ascent is looking for. Raiders, herders,
smugglers and fugitives share it, and the wind.

## Geography

The steppe is flat to the horizon except where long low ridges of black rock run east to west. Dry
riverbeds cut across it and fill for a day after rain. The Black Track has no waystations, only a
line of old **toll forts** from the days when the Chartered Road tried, every one of them ruined or
taken.

## Rumours and work

- A raider band called the Wind's Teeth is taking caravans on the Black Track and leaving the drivers alive, which is strange.
- Ninewells' market has appeared out of season, and its merchants won't say who invited them.
- Lucan Prell's agents are buying every steppe horse at twice the price. For what?
- Something in the Bone Field is digging up the old kingdoms' dead, carefully.
