**The Mirewood** is the great marsh woodland of the west, and [Oruvai](../../cities/oruvai.md), the city that is
one mind, lies at its heart. The Root Road skirts its eastern edge on causeways; everywhere else
the land is half water, and the water moves. The animals of the Mirewood are not part of the Chorus,
mostly, but they live alongside it, and visitors who harm them tend to find the paths closing behind
them.

## Geography

Dozens of rivers braid through the woods here, rising and shifting with the monsoon; beaver dams
and fallen trees make new lakes every year. The **Lantern Fen** glows at night with marsh lights.
The **Bloomward**, Oruvai's flower belt of ivory blooms and rust-red pods, closes the valley's
southern approaches; its haze drives away anyone the city doesn't want.

## Settlements

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **The Giving Orchard** | Oruvai's entrance, where visitors receive listener blooms and merchants wait to be invited further in |
| **Lantern Ferry** | The Root Road's waystation, a ferry and an inn on stilts (the Dry Boots, 1 mark) |
| **Reedholm** | A village of reed-cutters and eel-trappers who trade with Oruvai and pay its price |
| **The Keepers' camp** | A Keepers' Road chapter that buys animals from the woods, and argues with Oruvai about it |

## Rumours and work

- Reedholm's eel-traps are being emptied every night, and the trappers have found footprints with four toes.
- A Keepers' Road handler has been taking animals out of the Mirewood without paying Oruvai's price. The Chorus has noticed.
- A red fox has been seen on the Root Road holding a flower in its mouth (see [Moss-Under-Stone](../../people/companions/moss-under-stone.md)).
- Lights in the Lantern Fen have started following the Root Road at night.
