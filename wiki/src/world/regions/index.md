Between the great cities lie the **regions**: the farmland, hills, swamps, deserts and mountains
you cross to reach them, and where most of the world actually lives. Each region page lists its
villages and inns, its points of interest, its dungeons and ruins, the creatures that live there,
what you can gather or mine, and the rumours you will hear on its roads.

The levels are a guide to how dangerous a region is at its worst. The roads are always safer than
the wilds, and the wilds are always safer by day.

## Before the seals

Many of the ruins in the regions are older than the cities. Before the Gilded Ascent, the continent
was ruled by many small kingdoms (see [Timeline](../../lore/timeline.md)). Two of them left the most
behind:

- **Ruthven**, the kingdom of the central plains, whose last king sold his crown to buy the Middle
  Well and became [the Crownless King](../../lore/ascendants.md#the-crownless-king). Its kings lie
  under the barrows of the Tollands.
- **Esk**, the kingdom of the northern hills, whose engineers built the dam at Keth Veyra and the
  bell towers on every ridge of the Bell Foothills, and were abandoned there when Esk's civil war
  ended their wages.

Older still is the black stone of the First Builders, which lies under at least six cities, and
which nobody can cut.
