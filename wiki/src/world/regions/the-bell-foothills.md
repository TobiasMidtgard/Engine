**The Bell Foothills** rise north of [the Tollands](the-tollands.md) and climb, for four days on the
Bell Road, towards the snow. They are the first wild country most travellers see: woods that close
over the road, rivers too fast to wade, villages a day apart, and the old mine towns of the
**Kingdom of Esk**, whose engineers once built the great dam at [Keth Veyra](../../cities/keth-veyra.md).
[The Valley](the-valley.md) lies a day's walk west of the road, up its own river. The main story
begins here (see [The Bell Road](../../quests/main/the-bell-road.md)).

## Geography

The Bell Road follows the river **Cold** up through pine and oak, crossing it at Cold Ford and
climbing away from it at Wolfsgate. Side valleys run off to old mine towns, logging camps and
shepherds' summer pastures. Every valley has a bell tower on its ridge, from the days when Esk rang
warnings down the road; most are silent, and some ring on their own.

## Settlements

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Halfbell** | The caravanserai where the Valley road meets the Bell Road. The Dunmore and Daughters caravan leaves at first bell. Inn: the Cracked Bell, 1 mark. |
| **Cold Ford** | A ferry across the Cold, and a ferryman, Dace Arno, who charges what he thinks you can pay (2 chits to 2 marks) |
| **Wolfsgate waystation** | A walled waystation in the deep woods; wolves around it at night; a hunter, Tobin Reiss, who wants them dead, and Varo Kett, who wants them moved |
| **Harrowmere** | A hollow hill town two days off the road, with a dry fountain and an old woman who remembers Ulf Garrick as a boy |
| **Esk Stair** | A mine town that still works its silver, barely, with a smith who trains Smithing to Adept |

## Rumours and work

- Tobin Reiss at Wolfsgate wants the wolves dead; Varo Kett wants them collared (see [Varo Kett](../../people/companions/varo-kett.md)).
- A bell tower above Esk Stair rang three times last night. Nobody has been up there in twenty years.
- Harrowmere's spring dried up eleven years ago, the week the town's seals were struck (see [Ulf Garrick](../../people/companions/ulf-garrick.md)).
- The ferryman at Cold Ford has a daughter who wants to go to the Gilded Ascent, and no seal.
