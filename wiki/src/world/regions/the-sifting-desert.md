**The Sifting Desert** spreads south-east of the Glass Road's end, a red desert of mesas, salt
flats and wind-carved stone, and the [Sifting Basin](../../cities/sifting-basin.md) sits in its lowest hollow,
sifting wealth from the sand. The desert is where basin glass comes from, and so where much of the
continent's money comes from; it is also where caravans disappear in sandstorms and come back, years
later, without their drivers.

## Geography

The Glass Road ends at the Basin; beyond it, there are only caravan tracks between oases. The desert
is famous for its stone: the [Hoodoo Fields](../landmarks/hoodoo-fields.md), the
[Window Arches](../landmarks/window-arches.md) and the [Layered Buttes](../landmarks/layered-buttes.md).
Latticebacks build their mineral mounds on the flats, and the wind uncovers something new every
season.

## Settlements

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **The Sifting Basin** | The city in the hollow, which fires latticeback mounds into basin glass |
| **Sweetwater** | An oasis caravanserai with palms, a cistern and an inn (the Cistern, 2 marks); water costs 1 chit a cup |
| **The glass camps** | Camps of mound-cutters who follow the latticebacks, and their guards |
| **Red Mesa** | A village on top of a mesa, reached by one rope lift, that has never been taken by raiders |
