**The Hushlands** are the grey hills east of the Gilded Ascent, where the Quarry Road runs five days to
[Veyrhold](../../cities/veyrhold.md). The stone here has drunk the Ethereal for so long that it drinks
magic: spells cast in the old quarries fade faster, and enchantments carried through them grow
dimmer. It is the safest place in the world to have a magical accident, which is why the mages came,
and the most dangerous place in the world when the stone is full, which is why the Spillward exists.

## Geography

Low grey hills of hushstone and pine rise towards the coast, scarred by old quarries, some flooded,
some still worked. The Quarry Road is lined with the Spillward's **warning posts**, iron poles that
ring when a spill is near. East of Veyrhold, the land runs down to the sea, and to the hollow where
the Sunken Works collapsed forty years ago.

## Settlements

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Veyrhold** | The city of mages in the quarry that swallows magic |
| **Greystair** | A quarry village of hushstone cutters on the Quarry Road, with an inn (the Quiet Room, 1 mark: it has hushstone walls and mages sleep well there) |
| **Post Nine** | A Spillward watch post with a runner on duty, a hushstone cell and a medic |
| **Seaward** | A fishing village below the Sunken Works, where the fish have started to remember things |

## Rumours and work

- A warning post near Greystair has been ringing for a week, and there's no spill anywhere near it.
- The fish of Seaward remember the faces of the people who caught them.
- A spill-scavenger is selling changed things in the Lowmarket that came from inside the Sunken Works (see [What the Crystal Remembers](../../quests/what-the-crystal-remembers.md)).
- Post Nine's runner has not reported in for two days.
