**The Pilgrim Hills** lie between the Gilded Ascent and [Orath](../../cities/orath.md), three days down the
Glass Road, and for most of the year they are full of pilgrims: walking, praying, singing, selling
relics, buying relics and being robbed. Every crossroads has a shrine, every village has a hostel,
and every hostel has a story about a miracle. The wayspeaker [Oriel Thane](../../people/oriel-thane.md) walks
these roads listening at shrines nobody else has visited in years.

## Geography

The hills are terraced with vineyards and olive groves, cut by dry valleys full of scrub and wild
thyme. The Glass Road climbs over them in long bends, with waystations at the top of each climb;
the old pilgrim paths go straighter and steeper, from shrine to shrine.

## Settlements

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Saints' Rest** | The biggest pilgrim hostel, run by the four faiths in turn, with free bread and a fight every evening about whose turn it is |
| **Vinegate** | A wine town with a market, an inn (the Pressed Grape, 1 mark) and relic-sellers on every corner |
| **The Threshold** | The Wardens of the Threshold's post at the last hill before Orath, where pilgrims are counted |
| **Ashwell** | A village of the Covenant of Ash, rebuilt three times after fires, that runs a wound-house for pilgrims |

## Points of interest

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **The Shrine of the Fallen Cup** | A wayside shrine where pilgrims leave cups. Oriel Thane says someone is buried under it. |
| **The Pilgrims' Stair** | Six hundred steps cut into the last hill, climbed on the knees by the devout |
| **The Glass Carts' road** | The Glass Road's carts, carrying basin glass north to the Gilded Ascent, under heavy guard |

## Resources

| Resource | Where | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Ashroot** | Burnt ground around Ashwell | Ash-water, for burns and smoke |
| **Pilgrim's balm** | Scrub on the dry valleys | Clean-blood tonic |
| **Grapes, olives, thyme** | Every terrace | Wine, oil, cooking |
| **Relics** | Every market | Mostly fake; sometimes not |

## Rumours and work

- A relic-seller in Vinegate is selling water from the First Spring. It isn't.
- A pilgrim has collapsed at the Pilgrims' Stair, and the four faiths are arguing over whose hostel should take her.
- The Shrine of the Fallen Cup has started to fill its cups with water overnight.
- A glass cart was robbed on the Glass Road, and the thieves left the glass and took the driver.
