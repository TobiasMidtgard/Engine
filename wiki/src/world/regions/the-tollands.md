**The Tollands** are the farmland around [the Gilded Ascent](../../cities/gilded-ascent.md): the plain the
spire stands on, and everything within a day's ride of its Toll Rings. Every road on the continent
runs through them, and so does most of its money. The fields are rich, the villages are prosperous
and nervous, and every farm has a seal-holder in the family, or wants one.

Before the seals, this was the heart of the old **Kingdom of Ruthven**, whose last king sold his
crown to buy a well at a crossroads (see [the Crownless King](../../lore/ascendants.md#the-crownless-king)).
His ancestors are still here, under the barrows on the low hills.

## Geography

A wide, gently rolling plain, crossed by the slow **Middle River** and its canals, rises towards the
spire at its centre. The Five Roads leave the Toll Rings like spokes; between them lie wheat and
barley, apple orchards, market gardens that feed the Commons, and the long low hills of the Ruthven
barrows. Sky City's Golden chain comes down to the ground at the Anchorage, and on clear days the
island's shadow crosses the fields at noon.

## Settlements

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **The Lowmarket** | The market outside the walls, for anyone without a seal: stalls, cheap inns, unlicensed healers and the best street food in the world |
| **The Caravanserai of a Thousand Bells** | The great caravanserai below the First Toll Ring: stables, bonded warehouses, Wenna Sallow's outriders and a notice board as long as a street |
| **Weighford** | A prosperous farm town on the Middle River with a flour mill, an inn (the Full Measure, 1 mark a night), a smith and a Chartered Road factor |
| **Five Wells** | A hamlet with five wells, four of them dry. The fifth is very old, and the villagers don't drink from it |
| **The Anchorage** | Where Sky City's Golden chain is moored: winch houses, lifts, warehouses and the Golden Anchor's customs house |

## Points of interest

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **The First Toll Ring** | The outermost ring of the Gilded Ascent's walls, where anyone with a cart pays and anyone without a seal is asked why |
| **Crownless Cross** | A stone cross at the old crossroads where the Crownless King is said to have stood. People leave coins on it, and swear deals on it |
| **The Long Barrow** | The largest of the Ruthven barrows, half a kilometre long, with a door |
| **The drowned orchard** | A flooded apple orchard on the Middle River where the trees still fruit underwater |

## Rumours and work

- A farmer in Weighford wants a thief caught: someone has been stealing seals from the washing lines. Clay Seals, worth a chit each. Why?
- The fifth well at Five Wells has started to hum at night.
- The Lowmarket's best cook has been arrested for selling food without a seal. Her stall is being sold tomorrow (see [Side quests](../../quests/side-quests.md)).
- Something has opened the door of the Long Barrow from the inside.
