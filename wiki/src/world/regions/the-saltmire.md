**The Saltmire** is the great swamp where the rivers of the west reach the sea. [Vantress](../../cities/vantress/index.md)
floats on its delta, and the **Salt Spur** reaches it from the Root Road on causeways and ferries
that cost more than they should. Eighty-three years ago the flood of the Drowning Year came down
every river at once and drowned the swamp's villages; some were rebuilt on stilts, some were left
under the water, and their bells still ring when the tide turns. The edges of the Saltmire are
smugglers' country and easy going. The deep swamp is not.

## Geography

The Saltmire is a maze of brackish channels, mangrove islands and mudflats, changing with every
tide and every monsoon. The Salt Spur's causeway crosses it on old stone piles; off the causeway,
the only ways through are by boat or by knowing the swamp. Towards the sea the water turns salt and
the mud gives way to the **Fossil Reach**, where the swamp's ancient bones lie in the open.

## Settlements

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Spur Ferry** | The last ferry on the Salt Spur before Vantress, with a toll (3 chits) and a ferry inn (the Dry Plank, 1 mark) |
| **Heronwick** | A stilt village rebuilt after the Drowning Year, of eel-fishers and lacquer-makers |
| **Low Bell** | A village that wasn't rebuilt. Its church tower stands out of the water, and its bell rings at the turn of the tide. |
| **The Smugglers' Islands** | Mangrove islands where cargo waits for the night tide into Vantress, and nobody asks your name |

## Rumours and work

- The bell of Low Bell rang twice last night, and the tide only turned once.
- Heronwick's lacquer-makers are being undercut by a new works on the Smugglers' Islands, and their best cook has gone missing.
- A river pirate called Hollow Jen is collecting a toll on the Salt Spur that nobody gave her the right to collect.
- Yara Coil has been seen diving off the Chainyards at night, alone (see [Yara Coil](../../people/companions/yara-coil.md)).
