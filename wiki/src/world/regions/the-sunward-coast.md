**The Sunward Coast** is the warm, fertile shore around [Tessara](../../cities/tessara.md), the most advanced
city in the world, and Tessara's patterns reach far beyond its walls: tram lines run along the coast,
terraces plant and harvest themselves, and every village fountain is a public pattern that its
children can mend. It is the safest country on the continent outside the Tollands, and the strangest
to anyone from the north.

## Geography

White towns climb the hills above coves and beaches, between olive groves and citrus terraces.
The river **Tess** reaches the east sea through a broad delta at Rivergate. Inland, the land dries
quickly and turns to scrub, and then to desert.

## Settlements

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Rivergate** | Tessara's port, with self-steering barges, robotic cargo transfer and a sailors' inn (the Open Tile, 1 mark) |
| **Olvera** | An olive town on the tram line, with a press, a market and a public fabricator that makes anything in the Public Book |
| **Coveport** | A fishing village with a pattern lighthouse that steers ships in by itself, and has started steering them out |
| **The Living Terraces' farms** | Farms that plant, water and harvest themselves, with drones collecting the crops |

## Points of interest

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **The Artificer's Lighthouse** | A lighthouse said to have been built by the Last Artificer. Its pattern has never been read. |

| **The tram line** | The coast tram from Tessara to Olvera, free to anyone |

## Rumours and work

- Coveport's lighthouse has started steering ships out to sea. Its pattern is public; nobody can find what changed.
- A tram has been running at night with nobody on it.
- The Artificer's Lighthouse has started to glow, faintly, since the Registry's envoy arrived in Tessara.
- A farmer in Olvera says her terraces have planted something she didn't ask for.
