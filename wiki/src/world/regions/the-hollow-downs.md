**The Hollow Downs** are rolling chalk hills four days west of the Gilded Ascent, and they are hollow:
beneath them lies [Rillvault](../../cities/rillvault.md), the underground breadbasket whose glowing tunnel farms
feed half the world. On the surface the Downs are sheep country, all wind and grass, but every
sinkhole and cave mouth breathes warm, damp air smelling of mushrooms, and some of them hum.

## Geography

The Downs are smooth green hills of chalk with dry valleys between them, dotted with sinkholes where
the ground has fallen into the caverns. The Root Road crosses them to Rillvault's **Outer Trade
Gate**, a great arch cut into a hillside. The deeper caves lead down past the city into the **Echo
Galleries**, where the springs rise.

## Settlements

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **The Outer Trade Gate** | Rillvault's entrance, where caravans are inspected; a waystation, stables and the Deep Freight Lift head |
| **Chalkmere** | A shepherd village around a dew pond with an inn (the Fleece, 8 chits), a cheese-maker and a Root Ledger seed-lender |
| **The Breathing Holes** | Sinkholes that vent Rillvault's warm air; shepherds winter their flocks around them |
| **Flintwick** | A village of flint-knappers and well-diggers who know every cave mouth on the Downs |

## Points of interest

| Place | What is there |
|---|---|
| **The White Horse** | A giant horse cut into the chalk of a hillside, older than Rillvault. It is not a horse. |
| **Singing Hole** | A cave mouth that sings when the wind is from the west. The note is the same as the Echo Galleries' tuning stone. |
| **The dry springs** | Springs on the surface that have stopped running in the last two years |

## Dungeons and ruins

## Rumours and work

- Chalkmere's dew pond has started filling from below.
- A shepherd fell into a sinkhole and came out a day later in the Lantern Gardens, three kilometres away, singing.
- The Root Ledger wants someone to count the White Horse's legs. There are supposed to be four (see [What the Builders Buried](../../quests/main/what-the-builders-buried.md)).
- Flintwick's well-diggers have found a seamless black stone at the bottom of a new well, and stopped digging.
