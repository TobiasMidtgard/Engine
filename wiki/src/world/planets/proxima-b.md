**Proxima b** circles Proxima Centauri, a small red star in the Alpha Centauri system and the
nearest star to the Sun. It is the closest world of another star you can land on.

## Overview

Proxima b always shows the same face to its star, so on the day side the red sun hangs in one
place in the sky and never sets. Its light is dim and orange, and so is everything it falls
on: plants grow dark to drink in what little light there is, and the land glows copper.

## Surface

Seas and wet ground cover much of the day side. Rock tors and wind-carved formations stand on
the plains.

## Getting there

Cruise from the home world toward Alpha Centauri: the trip takes about a minute and a half
(see [Star travel](../../vehicles/star-travel.md)).
