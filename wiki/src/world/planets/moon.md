**The Moon** is the home world's companion: a grey, airless world of craters and dust under a
black sky. It is the first other world most travellers set foot on.

## Surface

Craters of every size cover the surface, the young ones ringed by bright rays of thrown-out
rock. Without air there is no haze, so shadows are sharp and distant hills look close.

## Getting there

Climb out of the home world's atmosphere and cruise: the Moon passes in seconds (see
[Star travel](../../vehicles/star-travel.md)).
