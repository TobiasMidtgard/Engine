The **home world** is where every journey begins: a planet of oceans and continents, snowy
ranges, forests, farmland and towns. In the game's current test builds it is modelled on Earth,
at full size.

## Overview

You start in [the Valley](../regions/the-valley.md), in the mountains of the northern
hemisphere. The ground there runs on without a seam to the rest of the planet: you can walk,
drive or fly in any direction, climb above the clouds and keep going until the planet curves
away below you.

## Surface

Mountains rise in ridges and peaks with scree slopes and cliffs; rivers wind through valleys to
lakes and the sea. Forests cover the hills, and farmland is laid out in fields around the
towns. From the air, towns and cities show as patches of roofs and streets; at night their
lights spread across the dark land.

## Climate and weather

The planet has seasons, and its sun rises and sets on the true day. Weather moves across it:
clouds that cast shadows on the ground, wind, rain and snow.

You start here. To come back from space, point your ship at the planet and it will fly you home
