**Tools** let you do what your body and your magic can't do alone. Every obstacle in the world has
more than one way past it, and a tool is often the quietest.

## Getting in

| Tool | Rarity | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Lockpicks** | Common | Opens simple locks; better picks open better locks, and break less often |
| **Tension wrench set** | Fine | Lets you hold a lock half-open while you work on its other pins |
| **Wax tablet** | Common | Takes an impression of a key or a lock for a copy |
| **Key blanks and files** | Common | Cut a copy from an impression |
| **Pry bar** | Common | Opens doors, windows and crates, loudly |
| **Glass cutter** | Fine | A neat hole in a window |
| **Ward chalk** | Fine | Shows wards and magical traps; a wardwright's chalk can redirect them |
| **Signature cloth** | Masterwork | Carries a copy of someone's magical identity, for wards that check who you are |
| **Lock-listening horn** | Fine | Hear the pins and wheels inside a lock or a safe |

## Going up, across and down

| Tool | Rarity | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Rope** | Common | Climb, tie, lower, pull. Vantress marsh-fibre rope is waterproof and best |
| **Grapple** | Common | Throw it up, hook it on, climb |
| **Tether bolts** | Fine | Fired from a launcher; sink into stone and hold a line |
| **Zipline trolley** | Fine | Slide down a line across a street or a canal |
| **Climbing claws** | Fine | Hand and boot spikes for stone, bark and ice |
| **Folding glider** | Masterwork | A Sky City criminal's wings: a long, quiet glide from a high place |
| **Hushwalker boots** | Masterwork | Orravell's soft hooked boots for walking under branches |
| **Raft hook** | Common | A Vantress pole for pulling rafts together, or yourself up onto one |

## Tools that are magic

Shaping magic can **conjure** most of these for a while: a lockpick, a rope, a ladder, a light, a
pick or a whole workshop (see [Shaping](../magic/shaping.md#conjuration)). Kinesis can turn the
pins of a lock with a careful push (see [Shaping](../magic/shaping.md#kinesis)). Enchanted tools
keep their magic: a pick that makes no noise, a rope that ties itself, a compass that points to
what you lost (see [Enchanting](../magic/enchanting.md)).
