**Materials** are what everything is made from. Iron, timber, leather and cloth are sold in every
market; the rare materials below come from one place each, and the cities that hold them grew rich
on them. For enchanting, a material's **capacity** is how much magic it can hold (see
[Enchanting](../magic/enchanting.md#vessels)).

## Hushstone

The dark stone of Veyrhold's quarry. Thick, unbroken hushstone weakens any uncontrolled spell that
passes through it; open doorways, cracks and careless digging undo the protection. Long exposure to
magic leaves pale, branching seams in it, and a seamed wall protects nothing. Veyrhold exports raw
hushstone for prisons, vaults and fortifications. A hushstone-lined pocket hides an enchanted item
from detection. A hushstone cell keeps a mage from casting. It holds no enchantment of its own.

## Stormglass

Electrically charged glass, harvested during thunderstorms on the crown of Sky City, where
lightning fuses the sand of the terraces. It holds Storm magic better than anything, and gives it
back as a charge: Sky City's instruments run on it, its duelling sabres have it in their guards,
and a Storm enchantment set in stormglass hardly fades. It is dangerous to carry in a storm.

## Basin glass

Uncut glass from the [Sifting Basin](../cities/sifting-basin.md), where latticebacks concentrate
minerals into their nursery mounds and the city fires them into glass. A flawless piece is the best
vessel for any enchantment, and the Gilded Ascent cuts its Glass Seals from it. A single uncut
piece is worth more than a house, and is easy to carry, which makes it every thief's favourite
prize (see [Before the First Bell](../quests/before-the-first-bell.md)).

## Glow

A sun-charged light refined in Vantress's [Fossil Reach](../cities/vantress/fossil-reach.md). A jar
of glow lights a room for a month without fire. It is also a potent hallucinogen, and the
Gilded Ascent has put it on the Unsealed List. Vantress sells it anyway.

## Brinewing scale

Black, heat-absorbing scales shed by the Brinewings of Keth Veyra. The rookery keepers collect the
shed scales and the city sells a few each year. Armour faced with them shrugs off fire and cold.
Scales taken from a living Brinewing are a crime in Keth Veyra, punished as harshly as stealing
eggs.

## Avarren heartwood

The wood of Orravell's colossal tree, taken only from limbs the tree has shed. It is light, strong
and alive in a way other wood is not: Life enchantments set in it grow stronger with time. The
Crown Ascendant forbids its export except as finished bows and staves, which it sells to allies.

## Common materials

| Material | Used for |
|---|---|
| Iron and steel | Weapons, armour, tools, locks (capacity 2 and 3) |
| Timber | Hafts, bows, shields, rafts (capacity 1) |
| Leather | Armour, harness, straps |
| Marsh fibre | Vantress's waterproof rope and sailcloth |
| Fungal cloth | Rillvault's light, warm cloth |
| Blackwater lacquer | Vantress's coating that stops wood and leather from rotting |
| Herbs and fungi | Potions and poisons (see [Potions and poisons](potions-and-poisons.md)) |
