**Armour** protects each part of the body separately. A helmet does nothing for your knees, and an
arrow that finds the gap at your elbow can lock the joint whatever you are wearing. Heavier armour
stops more, and costs you speed, stamina, silence and the ability to climb.

## Weights of armour

| Weight | Examples | Protects | Costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clothing** | Work clothes, coats, robes | Nothing much | Nothing; the best disguise |
| **Light** | Padded jacks, leather, Hushwalker moss cloaks | Cuts and bruises | Very little noise; you can climb and swim |
| **Medium** | Mail, scale, brigandine, resin plate | Cuts, most thrusts | Some noise; slower climbing; no swimming |
| **Heavy** | Plate, Atlas Bastion force armour | Almost everything but maces and hammers | Loud, slow, tiring; you cannot climb or swim |

Armour and clothing also say who you are. Guards notice armour where it shouldn't be, and a coat of
plate in a ballroom is a confession.

## Numbers

Armour is worn in **pieces**, and each piece protects only the parts of the body it covers. The
numbers below are the **rating** of each piece in Common steel or leather: rating comes off
physical damage, in full against Cut, by half at the gaps against Pierce, and by a quarter against
Crush (see [Damage, armour and injuries](../play/injuries.md#armour)). Fine adds 15% and Masterwork 30%.

| Piece | Covers | Clothing | Light | Medium | Heavy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Head** | Head | 0 (hood) | 5 (leather cap) | 12 (mail coif) | 24 (helm) |
| **Neck** | Neck | 0 | 3 (collar) | 12 (mail coif) | 22 (gorget) |
| **Body** | Chest, belly | 2 (coat) | 6 (jerkin) | 14 (mail shirt); 16 (brigandine) | 28 (cuirass) |
| **Arms** | Upper arm, forearm | 1 | 4 (bracers) | 12 (mail sleeves) | 24 (plate arms) |
| **Hands** | Hands | 0 | 3 (gloves) | 8 (mail mittens) | 18 (gauntlets) |
| **Legs** | Thigh, knee | 1 | 4 (leather leggings) | 12 (mail chausses) | 26 (plate legs) |
| **Feet** | Shin and foot | 1 (shoes) | 4 (boots) | 8 (mail and boots) | 20 (sabatons) |

### What it costs you

| Full set | Poise | Weight | Stamina recovery | Sprint | Climbing | Swimming | Sneak | Spell fizzles | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Clothing** | 0 | 2 to 4 kg | Normal | Normal | Normal | Normal | Normal | Normal | Chits to marks |
| **Light** | +5 | 6 to 10 kg | Normal | Normal | Normal | Slower | −5 | Normal | 3 to 8 marks |
| **Medium** | +15 | 12 to 18 kg | −15% | −10% | Slower; no ledge-hangs over a minute | You sink | −20 | +5% | 15 to 40 marks |
| **Heavy** | +30 | 22 to 30 kg | −30% | −20% | Can't | Can't: you drown | −50 | +15% | 6 to 20 ascents |

The **Heavy armour** skill (trained by [Joska Kroll](../people/joska-kroll.md)) takes a quarter off
these costs at Adept and half at Expert. Strength above 5 lets you carry more before armour slows you
at all. Nothing takes away the noise.

## Disguises

Clothing is a disguise, but a disguise only fixes how you look. To pass as a guard you may also
need the right seal or token, a reason to be where you are, the name of your supervisor, how the
job is done, the right accent and, in some places, the right magical signature. People who know
the person you are pretending to be are much harder to fool. Illusions help with the face, not the
rest (see [Illusion](../magic/illusion.md#phantasm)).

## Magic armour

Clean magic gives you **force armour** that absorbs blows and **bark skin** that hardens your own
(see [Shaping](../magic/shaping.md) and [Vitalism](../magic/vitalism.md#warding)). Dark magic
gives you **hellplate** that burns attackers and **bone armour** grown from your own skeleton (see
[Pactbinding](../magic/pactbinding.md) and [Necromancy](../magic/necromancy.md)). Real armour can
carry enchantments that fire when you block or are struck (see
[Enchanting](../magic/enchanting.md)).
