**Alchemy** turns herbs, fungi, minerals and stranger things into potions, poisons, smokes and
salves. Anyone can brew a simple remedy; a master alchemist can make a drink that stops a heart for
an hour and starts it again. What counts as a medicine, a stimulant, a narcotic or a poison is
decided by [the Root Ledger](../factions/root-ledger.md), and differs from city to city.

## How alchemy works

- **Ingredients** each carry one or two effects. Combine ones that share an effect to make it
  stronger, and ones that clash to make something unpredictable.
- **Dosing** matters: a sedative that sends a guard to sleep can kill a child. A good alchemist
  needs to know who a potion is for.
- **Potions** you drink; **salves** you rub in; **smokes** you burn or throw; **coatings** go on a
  blade or an arrow; **draughts** go in someone else's cup.
- **Brewing** needs an alchemy bench. A field kit lets you brew simple things on the road.
- **Vitalism's [Herbalism branch](../magic/vitalism.md#herbalism)** lets you grow ingredients on the
  spot and brew far stronger potions.

|---|---|---|
| **Mending draught** | Common | Closes small wounds slowly |
| **Healing potion** | Fine | Heals a good share of your health over a few seconds |

| **Clarity** | Fine | Restores mana and steadies your casting |

| **Warmth** | Common | Keeps out cold for an hour |

| **Gill-drink** | Masterwork | Breathe water for a few minutes; a Lowwater crew favourite |

## For thieves

These are the stock of [Meren Vos](../factions/thieves-guild.md#the-bench), the Tangle's apothecary,
and of anyone who works at night:

| Potion | Rarity | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Sleeping smoke** | Fine | A thrown jar that sends a room to sleep; dangerous in small rooms |
| **Choking smoke** | Common | Blinds and coughs; clears a corridor |
| **Sneezing powder** | Common | An irritant for dogs, guards and dinner parties |
| **Lock acid** | Fine | Eats through a lock in a minute, loudly and smokily |
| **Drunkard's draught** | Fine | Makes a sober person seem drunk, or the drinker seem sober |
| **Fever mask** | Masterwork | Makes the drinker look seriously ill for a day: a way out of prison or into a hospital |
| **Death's door** | Masterwork | Stops the heart for an hour, then starts it again. Fools anyone but a necromancer |
| **Truth wine** | Masterwork | Loosens tongues; illegal almost everywhere |

## Poisons

| Poison | Rarity | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Wolfsbane coating** | Common | Weakens and slows; burns werebeasts |
| **Marsh viper venom** | Fine | Paralyses for a short time; Vantress sells the antidote too |
| **Slow grey** | Masterwork | A poison that imitates grey fever; it takes weeks, and looks natural |
| **Nightcap** | Fine | A sedative in a drink; heavy sleep for hours |
| **Blackroot** | Masterwork | Kills within a day. The Root Ledger keeps it on the red pages |
| **Silver dust** | Fine | Burns vampires and werebeasts where it touches them |

Poisoning someone is a crime almost everywhere, and rich households hire tasters once they hear of
a poisoner at work.

## Dark brews

Dark alchemy uses blood, bone and soul as ingredients. **Blood draughts** feed vampires and heal
the drinker from someone else's life; **grave salts** raise a corpse for an hour; **corruption
tinctures** hide the marks of dark magic for a day. All of them are on the Unsealed List, and most
are sold in the Hollow Market of [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md).
