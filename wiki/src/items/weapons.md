**Weapons** come in thirteen kinds, and each kind is a skill of its own: a master swordsman is
still clumsy with a spear. Your skill decides how fast you swing, how often you hit the part of the
body you aim for and which moves you know. Your other hand has its own skill, too, so if your main
hand is injured you can switch, but you fight worse unless you have trained it.

## Kinds of weapon

| Kind | Hands | Good for | Typical example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One-handed blades** | One | Fast cuts and thrusts; pairs with a shield or a spell | Arming sword, sabre, cutlass |
| **Daggers** | One | Speed, stabs at openings, stealth kills; can be thrown | Dirk, stiletto, hook-knife |
| **Axes** | One | Hacking through shields and limbs | Hatchet, bearded axe |
| **Maces** | One | Crushing armour; stuns | Flanged mace, hammer, club |
| **Spears** | One or two | Reach, keeping enemies away, throwing | Short spear, gaff spear, javelin |
| **Two-handed blades** | Two | Wide cuts and great reach | Greatsword, longsword |
| **Great axes and hammers** | Two | Breaking guards, armour and bones; the heaviest blows | Great axe, war hammer, maul |
| **Shields** | One | Blocking, bashing, holding a line | Buckler, round shield, tower shield |
| **Spell foci** | One | Steadier, stronger spells | Wand, rod, orb, ring |
| **Torches** | One | Light, fire, and scaring animals | Pitch torch, glow lantern on a pole |
| **Bows** | Two | Silent killing at range | Short bow, longbow |
| **Firearms** | Two | Punching through armour at range; very loud | Carbine, rifle |
| **Unarmed** | None | Brawling, grappling, takedowns | Fists, knuckles, gauntlets |

Every weapon cuts, pierces or crushes, and armour stops each differently: mail turns cuts but not
thrusts, plate turns most things but not a mace, and nothing much stops a war hammer.

## Numbers

Every number below is for a **Common steel** weapon. Iron does a fifth less; Fine adds 15% and
Masterwork 30%; other metals are on [Smithing](../crafting/smithing.md#metals-and-materials).

### How hard you hit

A hit does the weapon's damage, multiplied by your skill with it and, in melee, by your Strength.
Then the armour on the part of the body you hit takes its share, and that part of the body
multiplies what is left (see [Damage, armour and injuries](../play/injuries.md)).

| Your skill with the weapon | Damage | Swing speed | What else |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Untrained** (0) | ×0.75 | Slow | Every third swing is clumsy and leaves you open |
| **Novice** (1 to 24) | ×1 | Normal | |
| **Apprentice** (25 to 49) | ×1.15 | +5% | You can aim at a limb reliably |
| **Adept** (50 to 74) | ×1.3 | +10% | Stabs at openings; your first perk of the branch |
| **Expert** (75 to 99) | ×1.5 | +15% | You find the gaps in armour (Pierce ignores half the rating more often) |
| **Master** (100) | ×1.75 | +20% | Every swing has a follow-up |
| **Ascended** | ×2, ×2.5, ×3 | +25% and more | Blows that cut what can't be cut |

**Strength** adds 5% to melee damage for each point above 5, and takes 5% away for each point
below. A stab at an opening, or at someone who can't see you, does triple damage with a dagger and
double with anything else.

### Shields

A shield stops part of every blow you block, and its **stability** is how much poise a block costs
you: higher is better.

| Shield | Blocks | Stability | Bash | Weight | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Buckler** | 40% | 20 | 6 Crush; parries are easier | 1.0 kg | 3 marks |
| **Round shield** | 65% | 40 | 8 Crush | 3.5 kg | 4 marks |
| **Kite shield** | 75% | 50 | 8 Crush; covers the legs on horseback | 5 kg | 8 marks |
| **Tower shield** | 90% | 70 | 10 Crush; plant it and fight from behind | 7 kg | 9 marks |

**broadheads** that make wounds bleed. A bow held drawn for too long shakes; Perception and your
Bows skill steady it.

## Conjured and enchanted weapons

Magic makes weapons of its own: arcane blades of hard light that you draw from the air, glintblades
you throw, blades that orbit your head and swords that fight beside you (see
[Shaping](../magic/shaping.md#conjuration)). Dark magic conjures bone blades and pact weapons that
drink life (see [Pactbinding](../magic/pactbinding.md#pact-arms)). Any real weapon can carry an
enchantment that fires on every hit (see [Enchanting](../magic/enchanting.md)).
