Everything you can carry: weapons, armour, potions, crafting materials, tools, valuables and the
few objects in the world that have stories of their own. Every item has a **weight**, a
**condition**, a **value**, an **owner** and, if it is enchanted, a **maker's accent**. All five
matter.

## Rarity

Rarity says how hard an item is to make or find, not how useful it is. A Common rope has saved
more lives than any relic.

| Rarity | What it means | Typical value |
|---|---|---|
| **Common** | Made by anyone with the tools; sold in every market | Chits to a few marks |
| **Fine** | Made well by a trained craftsperson, often with the Makers' Compact's mark | Marks |
| **Masterwork** | Made by a master, beyond what the material usually allows | Tens of marks to ascents |
| **Exotic** | Made from rare materials: stormglass, basin glass, Brinewing scale, Avarren heartwood | Ascents |
| **Storied** | One of a kind, with a name and a history that people know | Priceless, and hard to sell |
| **Divine** | Touched by a god, or made to wound one | Not for sale. Only Divine items can harm a god |

## Condition

Weapons dull, armour dents, rope frays and enchantments fade. An item's **condition** falls with
use and damage and lowers what it does and what it is worth. Repair it yourself with the right
skill and tools, or pay a smith, a weaver or a wardwright. Conjured items have no condition: they
are perfect until they vanish.

## Value and ownership

An item's **value** is what a fair buyer would pay. What you actually get depends on who you sell
to and how the item came to you:

- **Owned items** sell at their full value to any merchant.
- **Stolen items** remember who they belong to. Guards, merchants and the owner may recognise them,
  and the more distinctive an item is, the more likely that is. A fence buys them at a discount
  (see [Money and trade](../lore/money-and-trade.md#selling-stolen-goods)).
- **Contraband** is illegal to carry in some places: dark reagents, glow, blank enchantments and
  unlicensed firearms in [the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md), silvered blades in the
  Eaves of [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md). It sells best in
  [Arena City](../cities/arena-city.md).
- **Information is loot.** Ledgers, letters, keys, floor plans and signet impressions are items
  too, and are often worth more than gold.

## Weight and carrying

Everything has weight, and you can only carry so much. Strength, the Athletics skill and Force
magic all raise how much you can carry (see [Force and movement magic](../magic/force.md)). Heavy
loot slows you down, makes noise and is hard to hide. A thief with clean hands blends into a
crowd; one carrying a strongbox does not.

## Maker's marks and accents

Things made by the [Makers' Compact](../factions/makers-compact.md) carry the lodge's mark beside
the maker's own, which raises their value. Enchanted items carry something harder to fake: the
**accent** of the enchanter, a trace in the magic that skilled investigators can read. An unmarked
enchantment is **blank work**, and is banned in the Gilded Ascent. See
[Enchanting](../magic/enchanting.md).
