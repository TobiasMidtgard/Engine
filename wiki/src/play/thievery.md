There is no thief class. Pickpocketing, burglary, infiltration, cons and piracy are all built from
the same parts: your skills, tools, contacts, disguises and what you know. You get a job, prepare
and improvise, and every job can be done in many ways.

## Every job in five steps

| Step | What you do |
|---|---|
| Discover | Eavesdrop, investigate, bribe, follow, scout, ask the locals, steal documents |
| Get in | An invitation, a disguise, forged authority, stealth, climbing, diving, smuggling, illusion, breaking through |
| Take control | Avoid, distract, recruit, deceive, drug, threaten, trap, knock out or kill |
| Do the job | Steal, copy, swap, plant, destroy, expose, rescue, capture, or transfer it legally |
| Get away and profit | Blend in, run, sail off, hide, launder, ransom, publish or return it |

## Ways to play

Pickpocket, sleight of hand, burglar (ghost, cat burglar, inside hand, locksmith, manipulator,
arcane burglar, brute, removal crew), safecracker, infiltrator, social engineer, con artist,
forger, spy, illusionist, alchemist, saboteur, smuggler, fence, pirate, extractor, bounty hunter,
enforcer, mastermind, scout and tracker, and the people who work against them: security testers,
bodyguards and recoverers of stolen property.

## How it works

- **Jobs have authored routes**, but you are never told them. Opportunities show up as facts about
  the world, such as "the pilot is late", not instructions like "disguise yourself as the pilot".
- **No obstacle has only one answer.** Every one has physical, social, procedural and systemic
  weaknesses, and magic changes the other approaches rather than replacing them. Steal the key,
  trick the keyholder, go round the door, break the wall or take the whole container.
- **Pickpocketing is about possession; sleight of hand is about perception.**
- **A disguise only fixes how you look.** You may still need the access seal, a reason to be there,
  your supervisor's name, the job's knowledge, the accent and the right magical signature.
- **Illusion magic is not invisibility.**
- **Being spotted changes the job; it doesn't end it.** Failures come in degrees, and a loud
  burglary is a plan, not a mistake.
- **Information is loot.**
- **Loot has weight, a look, an owner and a fence value.** One famous treasure can be worth less than
  a bag of things nobody will recognise.
- **What happens next depends on witnesses, evidence and who gets the blame.**
- **Companions help but are never the only key.**

## Piracy

Piracy is the one path big enough for its own faction and questline. A ship is a small moving city,
and taking one means controlling its crew, finding a navigator, selling cargo that everyone can
trace, and paying everyone their share.
