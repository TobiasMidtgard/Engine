You don't have to travel alone. Some people will come with you because you pay them, some because
a guild sends them, and a few because they have decided you are worth following. The last kind are
**companions**: people with their own past, their own opinions and a story of their own that only
you can help finish.

## Followers and companions

| | Followers | Companions |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** | Hired swords, guild partners, a grateful farmer's son | Eight people with their own stories (see [The companions](../people/companions/index.md)) |
| **Why they come** | Money, orders or gratitude | Their own reasons |
| **How long** | For a job, or while you pay | Until you part ways, or they leave you |
| **What they think** | Not much | Everything (see Approval) |

You can have **one** follower or companion at a time to start with, **two** at Presence 7 and
**three** at Presence 10.

## How they fight

Companions think as well as the best enemies on any [difficulty](difficulty.md), and they follow
a few rules that keep them useful:

- They never give you away while you are sneaking. Enemies don't see them until the fighting starts.
- They stay out of your line of fire and on your side of the fight.
- They support you rather than stealing your kills, and get you up when you go down.
- They catch up out of sight if you leave them behind.

You can tell them to **follow**, **wait**, **hold a place**, **attack a target**, **stay hidden**,
**use an ability** or **go home**. Each companion fights in their own style (a tank, a healer, a
thief, a mage), and you can change their gear, but not their nature: a pacifist will not pick up an
axe because you hand them one.

## Approval

Companions notice what you do and what you say, and they have opinions about it. Each one has things
they **approve** of and things they **disapprove** of: mercy, cruelty, theft, honesty, dark magic,
loyalty to a guild, helping the poor, getting paid. You see their reactions in the moment, a nod or
a look, and their approval adds up:

| Approval | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Wary** | They are here for now. They may leave if you push them. |
| **Neutral** | They travel with you and do their job |
| **Friendly** | They tell you about themselves; their personal quest opens |
| **Loyal** | They fight harder for you, and stay with you when it costs them |
| **Devoted** | They will follow you anywhere, including somewhere they shouldn't |

Fall low enough and a companion leaves, sometimes for good, sometimes to join someone you will meet
again.

## Personal quests

Each companion carries an unfinished thing: a debt, a brother, a betrayal, a name. When they trust
you enough, they ask for help with it. How it ends changes them: a companion can come out of their
quest kinder, harder, freed, or gone. Some companions' quests cross each other, and helping one can
cost you another.

## Romance

Some companions can become more than friends, if your approval is high and you say the right
things at the right time. Not every companion is interested, and none of them is interested in
everyone. A romance has its own conversations, its own scenes and its own quarrels, and it can end.

## When they go down

On Easy and Medium a companion who falls is **wounded**, not killed: they get up after the fight,
with an injury that needs treating. On Hard and above they can die, and dead companions stay dead
unless you have the means to bring them back, which is a story of its own.
