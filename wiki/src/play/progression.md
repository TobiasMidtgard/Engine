```meta
summary: From a person who struggles with a house rat to a god who can slay gods.
```

{{draft}}

You start as an ordinary person who struggles to beat a [house rat](../creatures/house-rat.md).
There is no class to pick and no ceiling to hit: you can grow until you stand among the gods.

## Skills grow by use

Every skill improves by doing it: fight to fight better, cast to cast better, sneak to sneak
better. Each skill rises to level 100.

## Ascension

At 100 a skill can **ascend**, past what any mortal can do. Ascended skills keep climbing, and
the change is visible: your blows crack stone and fell trees, your spells grow from sparks to
suns. With every skill ascended, you become a god, strong enough to slay other gods.

## Mastery ranks

| Rank | Where it comes |
|---|---|
| Novice | Your first steps in a skill |
| Apprentice | |
| Adept | Element pairs open in magic |
| Expert | |
| Master | All three elements combine; the strongest spells |
| Ascended | Past level 100 |
| God | Every skill ascended |

## Reflexes and speed

Your level also makes you faster and sharpens your reflexes, so attacks seem to come at you
more and more slowly (see [Movement](movement.md#focus-slowing-time)).
