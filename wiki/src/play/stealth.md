Nobody in this world has a magic sense for players. Guards, servants and animals notice you the
way real people would: they see you in the light, hear you on the boards, wonder why a porter is
upstairs, and tell someone. Stealth is the art of giving them nothing to notice, and nothing to
tell.

## Being seen

Whether someone sees you depends on:

- **Light**: the brighter the light on you, the sooner they see you. Lamps, braziers, moonlight
  and your own torch all count. Douse a lamp and its pool of light goes with it; a Frost bolt puts
  out a candle quietly, a thrown stone less so.
- **Movement**: still and crouched is nearly invisible in shadow; running is visible anywhere.
- **Distance and angle**: people see best straight ahead, worse to the sides, and not at all
  behind. Looking up takes thought; most guards don't, until you teach them to.
- **Cover**: tall grass, crowds, steam, smoke and curtains all hide you. Leaves hide you from the
  side but not from above.
- **What you look like**: dark clothes in the dark, a porter's smock among porters, a noble's coat
  on the Summit. Bright armour in a dark room is a lantern.

Your **visibility** shows as a small eye near the centre of the screen: closed, half open, open.

## Being heard

| Surface | How loud |
|---|---|
| Moss, grass, carpet | Quiet |
| Stone, packed earth | Normal |
| Gravel, dry leaves, sand | Loud |
| Old wooden boards, raft decks | Loud, and they creak when you stop |
| Shallow water | Loud |
| Metal grates, tiles | Very loud |

Armour adds noise (mail jingles; plate clanks), and so does running, landing badly, opening doors,
and fighting. Rain, crowds, waterfalls and machinery cover noise. Sneaking skill, soft boots and
Illusion's Silent Step all lower it.

People hear noise as **a place**, not a person: they go to where the sound was. Throw a stone and
they look where it landed.

## Awareness

| State | What they are doing | How it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Unaware** | Their own business | Nothing |
| **Curious** | Something was off; they look toward it | A faint mark |
| **Searching** | They go to where they think it was | A half-filled mark |
| **Alert** | They know someone is there and look properly | A full mark |
| **Hunting** | They saw you, and call others | A red mark |
| **Lost you** | They search the last place you were, then a wider circle | The mark fades |

People who **lose you** don't forget. A guard who searched for you stays watchful for the rest of
the night, and tells the next shift. A house that was burgled locks its windows next time. On the
harder [difficulties](difficulty.md), searches are organised: guards split up, check the obvious
hiding places first, and look up.

## Bodies and evidence

An unconscious or dead guard who is found raises the alarm. So do open doors that should be shut,
empty cases, footprints in snow or flour, blood, a missing key, and a guard who doesn't report on
time. Hide bodies in shadows, cupboards, water or crates; close what you opened; take your rope
with you. A clean job is one nobody notices until morning.

## Disguises

Wearing someone's clothes makes you look like them from a distance, and lets you walk where they
are allowed. Up close it depends on:

- **Whether they know the person**: a guard knows the faces on his own shift.
- **Whether you act the part**: walk, don't run; carry what they carry; stay where they belong.
- **Your Presence and Disguise skill**: how long you can hold up under a question.

Questions come as **conversations**: answer well and the disguise holds. A disguise that inherits
access (a perk of Disguise) gets you through doors that need a uniform, a seal or a password, until
somebody checks.

## Takedowns

From behind or above, unseen, you can take someone down:

| Takedown | Result | Noise |
|---|---|---|
| **Choke** | Unconscious for hours | Quiet, slow |
| **Knockout** | Unconscious; may wake early with a sore head | A thud |
| **Lethal** | Dead | Quiet with a dagger, loud with anything else |

Some people can't be choked (anyone in a gorget, most monsters, the undead). Killing is quicker
than choking, and it is murder in every city (see [Crime](crime.md)).

## Cities that watch

Each city sees in its own way:

- **[Tessara](../cities/tessara.md)**: Ison watches through the Patternwork. Walls notice. Pattern
  tilers know where its eyes are blind.
- **[Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md)**: nearly every leaf can sense you. Sneaking there is close to
  impossible; being welcome is easier.
- **[The Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md)**: Seal Wardens check seals at every tier gate,
  so you are seen as a number. Without a seal you are a stranger by definition.
- **[Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md)**: districts move, routes open and close with the tide,
  and the guards of one raft can't follow you onto the next.
- **[The Second City](../cities/second-city.md)**: by day the Boulevard watches the street; at night
  the Eaves watch the roofs, and their eyes see in the dark.

## Animals

Animals notice you by smell as well as sight and sound, so come at them from downwind. Dogs bark,
geese honk, horses stamp, and a startled flock of birds tells everyone within a hundred metres that
something is moving. See [Creatures](../creatures/index.md).
