You make your character in four steps: **appearance**, **origin**, **attributes** and a
**keepsake**. None of the choices makes you special. Every origin is a small, ordinary life that
went wrong in a small, ordinary way, and every one of them ends with the same fact: you have no
seal, no record in any registry, and nobody who would notice if you vanished. That turns out to
matter.

## Appearance

Build your body and face in the character designer: height, build and proportions, face shape,
skin, eyes, hair, beard, scars, marks and voice. Everything is adjusted with sliders on the body
itself, so a broad back or long arms show in the way you climb and fight as well as in a mirror.
You can change your hair, paint and clothes later at a barber or tailor; your face and build only
change through magic (Illusion's Glamour or a dark transformation).

gives two skills a head start, decides what you carry at the start, and tells the people of that
city that you are one of their own.

### What each origin was

- **Valley hand**: you worked other people's fields in the Valley for board and a bed, and the
  farm was sold over your head. You know animals, weather and hard work, and nothing else.
- **Raft runner**: you ran messages across the bridges of Vantress, and lost a parcel that turned
  out to belong to the wrong person. You know the rafts move, and when.
- **Lapsed porter**: you carried sacks on the Commons of the Gilded Ascent until the season you
  couldn't pay the one chit your Clay Seal cost. The Registry struck your number. You are still
  there, carrying, but on paper you no longer exist.
- **Pit sweeper**: you swept blood and sand off Arena City's floor after the night bouts, and
  watched the fighters closely enough to copy them in the dark.
- **Hostel child**: you were born in the Returning Yards of Orath to pilgrims who moved on. All
  four faiths fed you, and none of them claimed you.
- **Washed-out recruit**: you trained for the Crown Companies of Orravell and failed the canopy
  test. Your papers say "unfit", and Orravell does not waste food on the unfit.
- **Warren gleaner**: you gathered moss, caps and spores in Rillvault's garden warrens for the
  Root Ledger's stewards, until a bad harvest cut the gleaners first.
- **Rookery groom**: you mucked out the Brinewing rookeries of Keth Veyra, then left the closed
  city, and the gate does not open twice for a groom.
- **Quarry hauler**: you dragged hushstone up Veyrhold's terraces and felt every spell near you go
  quiet. The quarry's mages paid you to stand next to their accidents.
- **Pattern tiler**: you laid and replaced Patternwork tiles in Tessara's walls, a job the Pattern
  Office gives to people it does not expect to promote.
- **Colony keeper's hand**: you fed and counted latticebacks in a colony of the Sifting Basin, and
  the colony was repossessed with you still in it.
- **Lamplighter**: you lit the lamps of the Boulevard at dusk, which is when the Eaves wake up,
  and you have seen things on the third floor you were paid not to see.
- **Dismissed servant**: you served in an estate of Sky City until a guest's ring went missing. It
  wasn't you, which made no difference.

### Origins in play

Your origin is more than a starting kit:

- **Home city**: people from your origin city start at **Known** instead of Unknown (see
  [Reputation](reputation.md)), and some conversations open only to locals.
- **Local knowledge**: one route, shortcut or habit of your home city is marked on your map from the
  start: the raft runner's tide table, the lamplighter's ladders, the porter's back stairs.
- **Old ties**: every origin has someone who remembers you, for better or worse, and a short
  origin quest that starts the first time you go home.

## Attributes

Every attribute starts at **4**, a little below the average adult's 5. Your origin raises two of
them to 5. You then place **two more points** anywhere, up to 6 in one attribute. That is all: you
will earn the rest one level at a time. See [Attributes and vitals](attributes.md).

| Attribute | Governs |
|---|---|
| **Strength** | Melee damage, carrying, poise, grip |
| **Agility** | Attack and climbing speed, dodging, nimble hands |
| **Endurance** | Health, stamina, recovery, resistance to poison and disease |
| **Perception** | Spotting, aim, weak points, noticing what people don't say |
| **Presence** | Persuasion, prices, followers, holding a disguise |
| **Attunement** | Mana, spell stability, sensing magic |

## Keepsake

Choose one small thing you kept from before. Each has a use, and each one has a story someone in
the world can tell you.

| Keepsake | What it does |
|---|---|
| **A letter you never opened** | It names someone you have never met. Open it and a short quest begins in the city it came from. |
| **A lucky chit** | Once a day, a failed haggle or a fizzled spell gets one more try. |
| **Your mother's whetstone** | Your blades dull half as fast, and sharpening one takes a moment instead of a minute. |
| **A Brinewing feather** | Animals start one step calmer around you. |
| **A forged pass** | It gets you through one checkpoint once. Who forged it, and why you have it, is up to you to find out. |
| **Nothing** | You start with 10 extra marks, and the satisfaction of owing the past nothing. |

## After creation

You start at level 1 with about 100 health, as little money as the table above says, and no
reputation outside your home city. A [house rat](../creatures/house-rat.md) is a fair fight. The
story begins in the Valley, whatever your origin: something brought you there, looking for work.
See [Getting started](getting-started.md) and [The Unsealed](../quests/main/index.md).
