At level 60 you reach the end of what a person can become by practice. Experience can no longer
raise your level. To go further you must change what you are, twice: first by **Awakening**, which
breaks the human limit, and then by **Ascending**, which breaks the limits of the world. Neither
can be ground out; both are stories.

## What every change asks

| Part | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Capacity** | You have mastered your current state | Level 60, and real mastery of what you do |
| **Catalyst** | Something rare that makes the change possible | A raw Fundament, an ancient core, a Brinewing bond |
| **Deed** | Proof you can survive what comes next | Living through the crystal's rupture |
| **Price** | A lasting cost, weakness or obligation | A machine you must keep wearing; a debt to a demon |

Failing a trial rarely ends the game. It leaves a scar, a mutation, a debt, a lost catalyst or a
darker road, and you can try again.

## Awakening

There are many ways to break the human limit, and the one you find shapes what you become.

| Road | Catalyst | Where it might be |
|---|---|---|
| **Raw exposure** | Surviving a raw Fundament | A well (see [The Fundaments](../lore/fundaments.md#wells)); the Sunken Works (see [What the Crystal Remembers](../quests/what-the-crystal-remembers.md)) |
| **The bond** | Binding yourself to a supernatural creature | A Brinewing that chooses you; the Chorus of Oruvai |
| **The core** | An ancient magical or mechanical core | Pre-pattern cores in Tessaran ruins; the Last Artificer's work |
| **The return** | Coming back from death with your soul changed | The Returning Road knows the way; the Deep Choir knows the price |
| **The forbidden change** | A transformation no one should survive | Vampirism by inheritance; a demon's gift |
| **The theft** | Stealing an Awakening meant for someone else | Somebody important is about to Awaken; they need not |
| **The gift** | Being remade by a powerful being | An Ascendant, a god, the Chorus |
| **The craft** | Building an artificial Awakening | The Pattern Office and the Spillward, working together, in secret |

Awakening doesn't hand you power; it lets you grow past human limits. From level 61 you can
develop strength beyond muscle, faster perception, unnatural recovery, direct control of magical
energy, resistance to fire, cold and poison, movement that ignores ordinary limits, a
**supernatural ability** of your own, and a first touch of souls and other realms. Your skills can
pass 100 for the first time.

| Levels | What it feels like |
|---|---|
| 61 to 65 | Newly Awakened and unstable: power surges you don't control |
| 66 to 72 | Controlled superhuman ability |
| 73 to 77 | Dominant against any ordinary person |
| 78 to 80 | The known limit of the Awakened |

## Ascension

An Awakened person has gone past human limits. An Ascendant has gone past the limits of the world.
To Ascend you must establish something that is yours alone: a **Legend**, a **Domain**, a
**Principle** or a personal law, and the world must recognise it.

### What you can claim

What you claim grows from how you have played:

| Path | Concepts you might claim |
|---|---|
| Thief | Possession, Secrets, Trespass, Absence, Exchange, Doors, Identity |
| Warrior | Challenge, Victory, Endurance, Severance, Protection, Conquest |
| Mage | Transformation, Distance, Memory, Flame, Pattern, Silence |
| Healer | Mending, Mercy, Return, Growth |
| Leader | Command, Loyalty, Crowds, Law |

Ways to claim one: be the first to do something impossible, defeat or inherit from another
Ascendant, invent a new form of magic, claim an idea nobody owns, go outside reality and come back
unchanged, permanently change part of the world, or be recognised by the world as the embodiment of
something. The great Legends of history each did one of these (see
[Legends and Ascendants](../lore/ascendants.md)).

### Domains and Sovereignties

From level 81 your levels deepen your **authority** rather than your numbers. Your claim becomes a
**Domain**: a set of powers that grow from the same idea.

| Domain | Early | Late |
|---|---|---|
| **Trespass** | Walk into sealed places | Trespass into dreams, memories, other realms and ideas themselves |
| **Possession** | Claim unattended objects; call marked things to your hand | Steal a voice, a memory, a name, a spell, someone's courage |
| **Severance** | Cut what can't be cut: a rope at a distance, a ward | Cut a bond, a contract, a curse, a god's law |
| **The Hunt** | Nothing you hunt can hide its trail | Nothing you hunt can escape |
| **Making** | Make what no one else can make | Make things that make themselves |
| **Endurance** | Wounds close as you fight | You do not fall while the fight goes on |

At the height of a Domain you hold a **Sovereignty**: an area, for a while, where your law is the
world's law. A Sovereign of Trespass can declare a city without borders until dawn; a Sovereign of
Severance can declare that no oath sworn there holds.

> Does this door still have the authority to keep me out?

### Signature techniques

Every Ascendant has **signature techniques**: named moves of their own that the world recognises,
and that other people talk about. You build them from your skills and your Domain, name them, and
NPCs who have seen them will know you by them.

- **King's Empty Vault**: for thirty seconds, every valuable, escape route, patrol and hidden
  mechanism around you is marked, and you are silent while carrying loot.
- **Sever the Moment**: your perception speeds up so far that everything else almost stops.

## After Ascension

Level 100 is not the end. With every skill ascended you reach **God** rank, and the question
becomes whether gods can be made mortal. That is the [Godslayer](progression.md#godslayer) path,
and it has nothing to do with your level.
