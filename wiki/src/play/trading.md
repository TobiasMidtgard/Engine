Everything in the world has an owner and a price, and the price depends on where you are standing.
Fungal flour is cheap in Rillvault and dear in Sifting Basin; basin glass is the other way round.
A trader who understands that can live on the roads alone.

## Prices

What you pay is the item's **value**, changed by:

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Your Trade skill and Presence** | At the start you pay a fifth more than a local and sell for a third of value. A master trader pays value and sells for nearly as much. |
| **Your standing** with the city and the shop | Known: fair. Respected: a little better. Distrusted: a lot worse. |
| **Where it was made** | Things are cheapest where they are made and dearest where they are needed. Each city's exports sell for half again as much in the cities that buy them. |
| **Supply** | Sell ten swords to one smith and the tenth fetches half what the first did. Prices recover over a week. |
| **The season** | Food is cheap after harvest and dear before it; furs in autumn; ice in summer. |
| **The news** | A war, a festival or a failing well moves prices in the cities it touches. |

## Merchants

Every merchant has a **purse**, a limit to what they can pay you in a day, and buys only the kinds
of things they sell. A general trader buys anything cheaply; a smith pays well for weapons and
ore; an alchemist for ingredients and potions; a bank in the Gilded Ascent for gems, bonds and
debts. Purses refill on **market day** (see [Calendar](../lore/calendar.md)), and so do shelves.

| Merchant | Buys | Found |
|---|---|---|
| General trader | Anything, at a discount | Every town |
| Smith | Weapons, armour, ingots, ore | Every city; best in Orravell and Tessara |
| Alchemist and apothecary | Ingredients, potions, poisons (some) | Every city; best in Rillvault and Vantress |
| Enchanter | Enchanted items, vessels, soul gems (where legal) | Veyrhold, Tessara, the Gilded Ascent |
| Tailor and outfitter | Clothes, light armour, cloth, furs | Every city |
| Bookseller | Books, maps, scrolls | The Gilded Ascent, Tessara, the Second City |
| Stable | Horses, tack, feed | Every city with a road gate |
| Fence | Stolen goods, at a fraction | Vantress, Arena City, the Second City's Hollow Market, any guild den |

## Haggling

In conversation with a merchant you can **haggle** once a day (a Trade check, see
[Conversation](dialogue.md)) for a better price on one sale. Succeed and the deal is better; fail
and the merchant's opinion of you drops a little. Some merchants like being haggled with; in the
Gilded Ascent's Weighhouse it is expected, and paying the first price marks you as a stranger.

## Stolen goods

A stolen item stays **stolen** (marked in red) until it is sold through a fence, laundered, or
claimed back. Ordinary shops won't buy it, and a guard who searches you will find it. Fences pay a
share of its value, less the risk (see [Money and trade](../lore/money-and-trade.md#selling-stolen-goods)).
Being Respected by the Tangle, or the Hollow Market's brokers, raises that share.

## Banned goods

Goods on the **Unsealed List** can't be sold in the Gilded Ascent or by any Chartered Road trader,
and carrying them through a Toll Ring is a crime. [Arena City](../cities/arena-city.md) sells and
buys all of them, at prices that pay for the risk.

## Trade runs

Buying where a thing is cheap and selling where it is dear is the oldest job on the continent:

| Run | Buy | Sell | Danger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flour south | Fungal flour and preserves in Rillvault | The Sifting Basin, Arena City | The steppe roads; bandits |
| Glass north | Basin glass in the Sifting Basin | Tessara, Veyrhold, the Gilded Ascent | Sandstorms; glass breaks |
| Hushstone west | Hushstone in Veyrhold | The Gilded Ascent's vault-builders, Orravell's prisons | Weight; it dampens your own spells |
| Lacquer and antivenom | Vantress | The Gilded Ascent, Keth Veyra | Swamp roads; the Tangle's tolls |
| Stormglass down | Sky City | Veyrhold, Tessara | The lifts' fees; the Golden Anchor's tax |
| Pilgrim goods | The Gilded Ascent | Orath at festival time | Crowds; forged relics |

Every run needs a cart or a beast, the right papers or the right smuggler, and a way through the
Toll Rings. With a **Copper Seal** you can trade on the Weighhouse floor; with a **Silver Seal**,
store goods in the Bonded Halls without paying tax until they sell.

## Investing

When you have money, you can make it work:

- **Buy a share in a shop**: the owner pays you a weekly cut, and lowers your prices.
- **Fund a caravan**: a share of what it earns, if it arrives (see
  [Three Accounts of One Caravan](../quests/three-accounts-of-one-caravan.md) for what happens when
  it doesn't).
- **Buy a stall, then a shop, then a trading house** in the Gilded Ascent, climbing the seals as
  you go.
- **Lend money**, at the Fifth Reserve's rates, to people who might not pay it back.

The richest people on the continent never carry a coin. They carry a seal.
