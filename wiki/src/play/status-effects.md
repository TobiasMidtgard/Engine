A **status effect** is anything that changes what a body can do for a while: a burn, a chill, a
spell of fear, a disguise of shadow. Most of them **build up**: one frost bolt chills, three
freeze. A bar under your health (and above an enemy's head, if you can read it) shows how close an
effect is to taking hold.

## Harmful effects

### From the elements

| Effect | Builds from | What it does | Lasts | Ends with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Burning** | Fire | Fire damage every second; panics animals; spreads to cloth, oil, grass | Until put out | Rolling, water, Frost, a cloak smothering it |
| **Chilled** | Frost | Moves and attacks a fifth slower | 6 s | Warmth, Fire |
| **Frozen** | Chilled three times | Can't move. A Crush blow shatters the ice and deals double damage. | 3 s | Breaking out (Strength), Fire |
| **Shocked** | Storm | Twitching: drops held items if hit again; double effect if wet or in metal | 4 s | Time |
| **Wet** | Water, rain, swimming | Can't burn; Storm doubles; Frost freezes faster | Until dry | Fire, sun, time |
| **Oiled** | Oil flasks, Malefice | Fire on you spreads at once and burns twice as long | Until washed | Water |

### From wounds and poison

| Effect | Builds from | What it does | Lasts | Ends with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bleeding** | Cuts and pierces | Health lost every second | Until bound | Bandage, healing |
| **Poisoned** | Poison, Blight, bites | Damage over time, worse with Endurance below 5 | 10 to 60 s | Antidote, Purge, Endurance |
| **Diseased** | Rats, rot, the Waiting Wound's touched | A lasting weakness (see below) | Days | Healers, Purge, the First Spring |
| **Injured** / **crippled** | Damage to one body zone | See [Body zones](injuries.md#body-zones) | Until treated | Treatment |
| **Exhausted** | Running out of stamina twice in a minute | Stamina recovers at half speed | 30 s | Rest |
| **Winded** | A blow to the chest or belly | No sprinting, no heavy attacks | 3 s | Time |

### From force and balance

| Effect | Builds from | What it does | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Staggered** | Poise reaching zero | Open to a finisher; can't block | 1 s |
| **Knocked down** | Heavy blows, Force, being charged | On the ground; next hit is a finisher | Until you get up (Acrobatics shortens it) |
| **Disarmed** | Disarm perks, Shocked, crippled hand | Weapon on the floor | Until you pick it up |
| **Pinned** | Chains, Kinesis, a net | Can't move from the spot | 2 to 5 s, or until broken |
| **Hooked** | Axes, chains | Pulled off balance toward the attacker | Instant |

| Effect | Comes from | What it does | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Frightened** | Fear, howls, Domination's Fear branch | Flees; drops weapons if it gets worse | 5 to 20 s |
| **Calmed** | Illusion | Won't start a fight; joins one only if hit | 30 s |

| **Silenced** | Hushstone, Illusion | Can't cast spells that need words | 5 s |

### Lasting conditions

These last days, not seconds, and need a healer, a cure or a quest:

| Condition | How you get it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Grey fever** | Rillvault's warrens | Endurance counts two lower; see [Not Enough to Go Around](../quests/not-enough-to-go-around.md) |
| **Rot lung** | Rats, the Lowwake Bunks | Stamina a quarter lower |
| **Quarry shakes** | Long work near hushstone | Mana recovers at half speed |
| **Spill-touched** | Standing in a spill | Random small changes to your body; see the [Spillward](../factions/spillward.md) |
| **Corruption** | Dark magic | Five stages; see [Corruption and afflictions](../magic/corruption.md) |
| **Vampirism**, **lycanthropy** | A bite, or a ritual | See [Afflictions](../magic/corruption.md#afflictions) |

## Helpful effects

| Effect | Comes from | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Warded** | Vitalism's Warding, blessed armour | Absorbs the next magical damage |
| **Shielded** | Shields, force shields | Blocks from one side without stamina |
| **Hastened** | Time magic, potions | Moves and attacks a fifth faster |
| **Regenerating** | Vitalism, potions, Wilds | Health returns every second |
| **Bloodlust** | Necromancy | Each kill restores health and stamina |
| **Veiled** | Illusion's Veil | Fades from sight: from slightly harder to see to gone. Your own side still sees a ghost of you. |
| **Rested** | A bed | Skills grow ten percent faster |
| **Well fed** | A good meal | Depends on the dish (see [Cooking](../crafting/cooking.md)) |
| **Inspired** | Illusion, a bard, a speech | Fear can't take hold; poise a fifth higher |
| **Second wind** | Perks, titles | Once a fight: stamina refills when it empties |

## Combinations

Effects react with each other and with the world:

- **Wet and Storm**: the shock arcs to everyone standing in the same water.
- **Oiled and Fire**: a burst of flame, then a long burn.
- **Frozen and Crush**: the ice shatters and the blow lands twice.
- **Chilled and water**: the water freezes around the feet: Pinned.
- **Frightened and cornered**: a frightened animal with nowhere to run fights twice as hard.
- **Burning and Wind**: Force magic fans a fire across a room.

See [Elements and combinations](../magic/elements.md) for the spells that cause these on purpose.
