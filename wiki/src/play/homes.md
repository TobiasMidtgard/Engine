You start with nowhere to sleep. An inn bed costs a few chits a night and leaves you
[rested](attributes.md#hunger-sleep-and-weather); a home of your own is safer, stores what you
can't carry, holds your crafting stations and shows off what you have taken from the world.

## What a home gives you

- **A bed**: rested every time you sleep in it.
- **Storage** that nobody touches (unless you live somewhere people steal).
- **Stations**: room for a forge, an alchemy bench, a tinker's table, an enchanting circle, a
  kitchen.
- **Display**: cases, racks and walls for weapons, relics and trophies, which visitors notice.
- **A door you can lock**, and later a door nobody else can find.

Guilds give their members quarters too: the Slipknot's back rooms for the Tangle, the Undercroft
for the Brass Circuit, the Wardhouse for the Spillward. Guild rooms are free, and the guild knows
everything you keep in them.

## Upgrades

| Upgrade | Cost | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| Lock and bar | 2 ascents | Burglars need skill to get in |
| Forge, alchemy bench or tinker's table | 5 to 15 ascents each | A crafting station (see [Crafting](../crafting/index.md)) |
| Enchanting circle | 25 ascents | Needs a licence in Veyrhold |
| Kitchen | 4 ascents | Cook meals that last longer |
| Garden or grow-bed | 6 ascents | Grow ingredients; in Rillvault, grow rare ones |
| Display cases and racks | 1 to 5 ascents each | Show weapons, relics and trophies |
| A stable | 10 ascents | For a horse; in Keth Veyra, for something larger |
| A hidden room | 20 ascents, and a builder who keeps quiet | Storage nobody finds, a place to hide, a door to the roofs |
| Wards | 15 ascents, or your own Warding | Keep out spirits, demons and Kinesis |
| A servant or steward | 1 ascent a week | Keeps the house, sells what you leave in the sale chest, takes messages |

## Keeping it

Homes in some cities come with obligations: a Clay Seal's chit each season in the Gilded Ascent,
water rights in the Sifting Basin, the Night Council's good opinion in the Eaves. Neglect them and
the home can be taken back. In Vantress, someone may simply change your raft's mooring seal: check
the register now and then.
