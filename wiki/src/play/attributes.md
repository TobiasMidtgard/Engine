Your **attributes** are what your body and mind can do before training: how strong, quick, tough,
sharp, persuasive and magically sensitive you are. Your **skills** are what you have learned to do
with them. A strong person with no training swings a sword hard and badly; a trained one with weak
arms swings it well and lightly. You need both.

## The six attributes

| Attribute | What it is | What it raises |
|---|---|---|
| **Strength** | Muscle and grip | Melee damage (+5% a point above 5), carry weight, poise, how long you can hang from a ledge, heavy weapons and armour without penalty |
| **Agility** | Speed and balance | Attack speed, dodge distance, climbing speed, draw speed with bows, how fast your hands are at a lock or a pocket |
| **Endurance** | Toughness and lungs | Health, stamina, recovery between fights, resistance to poison, disease, cold and heat, how far you can fall |
| **Perception** | Eyes, ears and attention | Spotting hidden people and traps, aim sway, hitting weak points, the **observations** that appear in conversation |
| **Presence** | How people take you | Persuasion and prices, how many followers you can keep, how long a disguise holds under questions |
| **Attunement** | Feel for the Ethereal | Mana, mana recovery, spell stability (less fizzling), sensing spells, wards and spills nearby, enchantment charge |

### What the numbers mean

| Value | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | Ill, old, or very young |
| 3 to 4 | Below average. Where you start. |
| 5 | The average adult |
| 6 to 7 | Clearly gifted, or trained for years |
| 8 to 9 | One of the best in a city |
| 10 | The human apex: the best that a real person can be |
| 11 to 15 | Awakened only. Beyond muscle, beyond nerves. |
| 16 and up | Not numbers any more. See [Ascension](ascension.md#ascension). |

## Raising attributes

Each level gives you **one attribute point**. Raising an attribute costs more the higher it goes:

| Raise | Cost |
|---|---|
| Up to 7 | 1 point |
| 7 to 8, and 8 to 9 | 2 points |
| 9 to 10 | 3 points |
| 10 to 15 (Awakened) | 4 points each |

From 4, reaching 10 in one attribute costs 10 points, so the 60 levels of being human are exactly
enough to bring all six to the human apex, if you spread them evenly. Most people don't: a thief at
level 60 might have Agility and Perception at 10 and Strength at 6.

After [Awakening](ascension.md#awakening), points can take an attribute past 10, to a ceiling of
15. **Ascendant** levels give no attribute points at all: they deepen the law you claimed instead.

## Vitals

| Vital | Starts at | At the human apex | What empties it | What refills it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Health** | About 100 | About 280 | Damage | Rest, food, healing, time out of combat (slowly, and never past an untreated injury) |
| **Stamina** | About 90 | About 160 | Sprinting, climbing, swinging, blocking, dodging | A few seconds of not doing those things |
| **Mana** | About 50 | 200 and more | Casting | Time, rest, potions, standing near a well |
| **Poise** | 40 | 70 to 120 with armour | Being hit, blocking heavy blows | Seconds without being hit |

How they are worked out, for the curious:

- **Health** = 60 + 10 × Endurance + 2 × level
- **Stamina** = 40 + 6 × (Endurance + Agility)
- **Mana** = 20 + 8 × Attunement + your best magic skill
- **Poise** = 20 + 5 × Strength + your armour's poise

Awakening multiplies all four, and keeps multiplying them as you climb toward 80. By then a single
hit that would kill a trained soldier is a scratch.

### Running out

- **No stamina**: you can't sprint, dodge, climb or block until it returns, and your attacks slow
  to a crawl. Hanging from a ledge with no stamina means letting go.
- **No mana**: spells fizzle. Casting with too little mana is possible through **overcasting**: the
  spell draws on your health, and on the Ethereal around you, and may cause a
  [spill](../factions/spillward.md).
- **No poise**: you are **staggered** for a moment, open to a finisher. Enemies are too.
- **No health**: you are **down** (see [Difficulty, defeat and death](difficulty.md)).

## Hunger, sleep and weather

You don't need to eat to live, but food matters. A meal restores health and stamina over a few
minutes, and good cooking adds an effect for hours (see [Cooking](skills.md#craft-skills)). After a
day without sleep you are **tired**: stamina recovers more slowly and your Perception counts one
lower until you rest. A bed you own or rent leaves you **rested** for the next half day: experience
from skills comes ten percent faster.

Cold mountains, desert noon and storms all wear at you unless you dress for them. The snows above
Keth Veyra drain stamina without furs; the glass flats outside the Sifting Basin drain it without a
veil and water. Endurance, the right clothes, Warding spells and a few potions all help.
