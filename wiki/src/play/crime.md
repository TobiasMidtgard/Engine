There is no single law on the continent. A thing that is a trade in Arena City is a hanging matter
in the Gilded Ascent; killing a man is murder on the Boulevard and an expensive purchase three
floors up. Guards enforce the law of their own city, and mostly of their own district, and a
crime is only a crime if someone saw it, or can prove it.

## How a crime is noticed

1. **A witness** sees it, or finds the evidence afterwards (see [Stealth](stealth.md#bodies-and-evidence)).
2. **They decide what to do**, depending on who they are: a guard acts, a servant runs for a guard,
   a thief looks the other way, a child tells everyone. People who like you hesitate; people who
   fear you keep quiet.
3. **Word spreads.** A witness who reaches a guard adds a **bounty** to your name in that city. A
   witness who is never reached, or is talked out of it, or bribed, never does.

Witnesses describe what they saw: your clothes, your weapon, your height, your voice. Change your
clothes and wear a hood and a description stops fitting. Your **face** is what matters most, and
being recognised (somebody who knows you saw you) attaches the crime to your name instead of to a
stranger.

## Crimes and bounties

| Crime | Typical bounty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Trespass | 5 marks | Being somewhere without the right to be there, after a warning |
| Pickpocketing | 10 marks, or the value taken | |
| Theft | The value taken, or 20 marks | Taking from a home or a shop |
| Burglary | Double the value taken | Breaking in to steal |
| Assault | 40 marks | Starting a fight |
| Killing a guard | 500 marks | Always pursued |
| Murder | 1,000 marks | |
| Escaping jail | 100 marks | Plus what put you there |
| Forging papers or seals | City by city | In the Gilded Ascent, the worst crime there is |

Bounties belong to the city where you earned them. Across the continent nobody cares what you did
in Vantress, unless they have been paid to.

## Getting caught

When a guard catches you with a bounty, you can:

- **Pay** the bounty. The stolen goods go back; the bounty is cleared; the guard's opinion of you
  does not improve.
- **Talk your way out**: Persuasion, Deception or Intimidation, harder the larger the bounty (see
  [Conversation](dialogue.md)).
- **Bribe** the guard: double the bounty, half the chance. Easier in Vantress, impossible in
  Tessara.
- **Go to jail**: time passes (a day for every 50 marks), your gear is taken to the evidence room,
  and you come out with a clean name. You can also break out: every jail has a way.
- **Resist** or **run**: the bounty rises and the chase begins. Guards call others, close gates
  and, on the harder [difficulties](difficulty.md), cut off the roofs.

## Clearing your name

- **Guild fixers**: [the Tangle](../factions/thieves-guild.md) can clear bounties in Vantress for a
  fee; the Last Writ can make a Red Writ go away, for much more.
- **Time**: small bounties fade after a few weeks if nobody sees you.
- **Changing the record**: in Vantress you can change who you were. In the Gilded Ascent you can't,
  which is why people try.
- **Favours**: some quests end with an official owing you one.

## Lists that follow you

Two things follow you across the continent:

- **The Unsealed List** of the Gilded Ascent names goods and people that no seal may carry. Being on
  it closes every Chartered Road caravan, bank and Toll Ring to you, in every city they trade with.
- **A Red Writ** bought in the Second City can be carried out anywhere, by the Last Writ or by
  anyone the buyer hires.

## Guards remember

Guards learn. Rob the same district three times and the patrols double; climb the same roofs and
someone posts a watch on them. When your reputation grows, guards who hear your name look at you
twice. When it grows very large, some of them decide they are not paid enough. See
[Reputation](reputation.md).
