Every **skill** grows by doing it: fight with a spear to fight better with a spear, pick locks to
pick them faster, talk your way past guards to talk better. Each skill climbs from 0 to 100 through
five ranks, and every rank opens a **perk** on that skill's tree. Past 100, a skill can
**ascend**, three times, into things no mortal can do.

## How skills grow

- **Use teaches.** A skill rises when you use it against something that tests you. Picking simple
  locks makes you faster; a lock you have never seen teaches you something new; an easy thing done
  a hundred times teaches less each time.
- **Trainers** in the guilds can raise a skill quickly, for a price, but never past what you have
  shown you can do.
- **Breakthroughs** are moments when a skill jumps: robbing a master locksmith, beating a veteran,
  making a masterpiece. Time slows, the old limit appears crossed out, and the screen shows exactly
  what changed (see [Progression](progression.md#what-each-level-gives)).
- **Each hand** has its own skill with each weapon. Your off hand starts weaker and grows when you
  use it.

| Rank | Skill | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Novice** | 0 to 24 | You can do it, badly. A novice spell fizzles a third of the time |
| **Apprentice** | 25 to 49 | You can do it reliably; the first perk opens |
| **Adept** | 50 to 74 | You are good at it; choose one of two perks |
| **Expert** | 75 to 99 | Among the best in a city |
| **Master** | 100 | As good as a person can be; choose one of two perks |
| **Ascended I to III** | Beyond 100 | Past mortal limits; each ascension opens an impossible perk |
| **God** | Every skill ascended | Nothing left that you cannot do |

## Training

Trainers are people: guild masters, old soldiers, a herbalist in a village, a fence who teaches
lockcraft in a back room. Each one trains a few skills **up to their own rank**, and each has
something they want first: a guild rank, a favour, or simply to like you.

| Trainer's rank | Trains up to | Price for one point |
|---|---|---|
| **Apprentice** | 49 | 2 chits for each point of the new level (skill 30 costs 6 marks) |
| **Adept** | 74 | The same (skill 60 costs 12 marks) |
| **Expert** | 99 | The same (skill 90 costs 18 marks) |
| **Master** | 100 | 3 ascents, and a deed they set you |

- You can train **five points per character level**, across all skills.
- Training takes time: an hour a point, during which the trainer is busy and so are you.
- Training never gives a **breakthrough**; you still have to earn those yourself. A trainer can
  tell you where to look for one.
- Guild trainers charge members half, and the guild's best charge nothing to someone they trust.

Trainers are listed on each person's page, and by city on the city pages.

## The tree

Each skill's tree has a perk at every rank. At **Adept** and at **Master** the tree splits and you
choose one of two perks; the other stays closed unless you earn a breakthrough that opens it. So
two masters of the same skill can fight, sneak or cast very differently.

Skills and character levels work together: a skill can't pass 100 until you have
[Awakened](ascension.md#awakening), and can't reach Ascended II until you have
[Ascended](ascension.md#ascension). Training improves your mastery of what you are; it never
changes what you are.

## Combat skills

| Skill | Apprentice | Adept (choose one) | Expert | Master (choose one) | Ascended |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **One-handed blades** | Riposte: a parry opens a free counter | Duellist (faster feints) or Shieldmate (strikes from behind a shield) | Disarm: a strike that knocks the weapon away | Flurry (cancel any swing into the next) or Executioner (finishers on staggered foes) | Your cuts carry through armour into the next body |
| **Daggers** | Quick draw: draw and stab in one move | Backstab (triple damage unseen) or Knife thrower (thrown daggers return to the belt) | Opening: stabs at any gap in a guard | Ghost blade (kills make no sound) or Twin fangs (a dagger in each hand at full skill) | Strike an opening before it opens |
| **Axes** | Hook: pull a shield aside | Cleaver (cuts limbs) or Woodsman (axes break doors, shields and timber) | Hack: each hit lowers armour | Whirl (strike everyone around you) or Throwing axe (throw and recall) | Fell a tree, or a giant, in one blow |
| **Maces** | Stun: heavy blows daze | Bonebreaker (breaks limbs) or Bell-ringer (helmeted heads ring for longer) | Crush: maces ignore half of plate | Earthshaker (a blow to the ground knocks people over) or Penitent (each hit you take makes your next blow harder) | Your blows crack stone and send out shockwaves |
| **Spears** | Reach: keep enemies at a spear's length | Phalanx (stronger beside allies) or Javelin (thrown spears pin) | Vault: pole-vault over a foe or a wall | Wall of points (no one gets close) or Impaler (a thrust through two) | A thrown spear that does not stop |
| **Two-handed blades** | Sweep: wide cuts hit several | Half-sword (grip the blade for thrusts) or Momentum (each swing faster than the last) | Guard break: heavy cuts break blocks | Cleaving arc (cuts through all in an arc) or Sentinel (parry anything, even arrows) | A cut that splits the ground ahead of it |
| **Great axes and hammers** | Wind-up: charged blows hit harder | Breaker (shatters shields) or Juggernaut (you can't be staggered while swinging) | Sunder: armour falls off what you hit | Quake (ground slam) or Executioner's swing (one blow ends a wounded foe) | Shockwaves flatten grass and topple walls |
| **Shields** | Bash: knock a foe back | Bulwark (block from all sides) or Shield-charge (run them down) | Perfect block: stagger attackers | Wall (allies behind you take no arrows) or Rebound (blocked spells fly back) | Nothing can move you |
| **Bows** | Steady aim: draw longer, hit harder | Hunter (silent shots, tracking) or Volley (three arrows at once) | Called shot: hit the part you aim at | Ricochet (arrows bounce) or Eagle eye (zoom and slow time while aiming) | An arrow that crosses a valley |
| **Firearms** | Steady: less kick | Marksman (long shots) or Close quarters (fire on the move) | Quick reload | Piercing round (through cover) or Suppression (enemies keep their heads down) | Shots that bend around cover |
| **Unarmed** | Grapple: hold and throw | Brawler (improvised weapons) or Pressure points (strikes that numb limbs) | Takedown: knock out from behind | Iron body (blows barely hurt you) or Flowing hands (catch and return blows) | Your fists crack stone; caught arrows break |
| **Off hand** | Switch: change hands without fumbling | Ambidextrous (no off-hand penalty) or Parrying hand (a dagger or a torch to parry) | Twin strikes: attack with both at once | Second weapon (dual-wield anything) or Spellhand (cast with the off hand while you fight) | Each hand fights as a master |

## Body skills

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Athletics** | Second wind: stamina comes back faster | Runner (sprint longer) or Porter (carry far more) | Swimmer: swim in armour, and underwater longer | Tireless (stamina barely falls) or Strongback (carry a person at a run) | Run as fast as a horse, for a day |
| **Acrobatics** | Roll: land a fall with a roll | Wall-runner (run along walls) or Tumbler (dodge through attacks) | Safe falls: fall twice as far unhurt | Freerunner (chain every move without slowing) or Catch (grab ledges and ropes in mid-fall) | Land any fall |

| **Light armour** | Fitted: less noise | Skirmisher (move faster in it) or Padded (blunt blows hurt less) | Supple: climb and swim in it | Shadow coat (armour never gives you away) or Deflection (glancing blows slide off) | Light armour stops what plate stops |
| **Heavy armour** | Bearing: less stamina lost | Juggernaut (can't be knocked down) or Fortress (arrows bounce) | Well-fitted: run in it | Unbreakable (armour can't be damaged) or Bastion (allies near you are harder to hit) | Heavy armour weighs nothing |

## Craft skills

The crafts are professions, taught by the [Makers' Compact](../factions/makers-compact.md) and the
guilds, and boosted by the magic branches beside them.

| Skill | Apprentice | Adept (choose one) | Expert | Master (choose one) | Ascended |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Smithing** | Repair: mend your gear on the road | Weaponsmith or Armourer | Exotic metals: work stormglass guards, Brinewing scale and silver | Masterpiece (one item beyond its material) or Forge-line (make many at once, all Fine) | Forge a Divine weapon, with a relic |
| **Enchanting** | Bind: put a Novice spell into an item | Wardwright (wards and runes) or Charmer (worn enchantments) | Two spells: bind two spells into one item | Accent-free (your work leaves no accent) or Great work (enchant basin glass) | Enchant something that cannot be enchanted |
| **Alchemy** | Field kit: brew on the road | Healer (stronger cures) or Poisoner (stronger poisons) | Dosing: know how much is safe for whom | Refiner (potions twice as strong) or Grower (brew from plants you grow on the spot) | A potion that does what magic does |
| **Engineering** | Tinker: fix simple machines | Patternwork (read and lay tiles) or Mechanist (clockwork, traps and launchers) | Firearms and energy weapons: build and mend | Machine-mind (your machines think a little) or Open pattern (anyone can mend what you build) | Build what the Last Artificer built |
| **Animal handling** | Calm: animals stay calm near you | Breeder (better working animals) or Bond (a creature that chooses you) | Veterinary: heal animals' injuries | Pack (lead many animals at once) or Partner (one creature, as strong as you) | Animals of every kind follow you |
| **Cooking** | Rations: food that keeps | Feast (meals that buff a crew) or Chef (meals that open doors) | Preserving: food that crosses a continent | Comfort (meals that heal) or Banquet (meals that win over the powerful) | Food that lifts a city's mood |

## Magic skills

Each of the eight subclasses of magic is a skill of its own, and its four **branches** are its
tree. Rank sets how complex your crafted spells may be (see [Spellcrafting](../magic/spellcrafting.md)),
how often they fizzle and how fast you cast.

| Subclass | Clean or dark | Branches |
|---|---|---|
| [Evocation](../magic/evocation.md) | Clean | Fire, Frost, Storm, Spellblade |
| [Malefice](../magic/malefice.md) | Dark | Blood, Blight, Shadow, Hexblade |
| [Vitalism](../magic/vitalism.md) | Clean | Restoration, Warding, Wilds, Herbalism |
| [Necromancy](../magic/necromancy.md) | Dark | Undeath, Afflictions, Soul, Sacrifice |
| [Shaping](../magic/shaping.md) | Clean | Kinesis, Passage, Conjuration, Artifice |
| [Pactbinding](../magic/pactbinding.md) | Dark | Infernal, Chains, Pact Arms, Gates |
| [Illusion](../magic/illusion.md) | Clean | Veil, Phantasm, Insight, Glamour |
| [Domination](../magic/domination.md) | Dark | Fear, Wrath, Command, Memory |

| Rank | Spell complexity | Fizzles | Time to cast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Novice | 1 | 1 in 3 | Slow |
| Apprentice | 2 | 1 in 7 | |
| Adept | 5 | 1 in 16 | |
| Expert | 8 | 1 in 33 | |
| Master | 12 | 1 in 100 | Fast |
| Ascended I, II, III | 16, 20, 25 | Never | Faster |
| God | 40 | Never | Instant |
