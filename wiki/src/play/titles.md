A **title** is what people start calling you after you have done something enough times, or once
in a way nobody forgets. Titles are not decoration: each one carries a real power, because the
world treats a person with a reputation differently. You can earn many titles, but only **wear** a
few at a time, and more slots open as you grow. Swap them at any rest.

## How titles are earned

- **By repeated deeds.** Slip in and out of guarded places unseen enough times and you become an
  Unseen Guest.
- **By one great deed.** Win a fight against a whole company alone and you are an Army of One.
- **By how you did it.** Two players who rob the same vault can earn different titles: one for
  never being seen, one for leaving the guards tied up in a neat row.

When you earn a title, the **Revelation Screen** names it, shows what it does, and the people around
you react.

## Stealth and thievery

| Title | Earned by | Power |
|---|---|---|
| **Unseen Guest** | Many infiltrations without being identified | Guards are slower to confirm a sighting; servants assume you belong |
| **Vault-Eater** | Robbing a vault everyone called impenetrable | You see the weak points of buildings; security experts fear you |
| **Light Fingers** | A hundred pockets picked unnoticed | Pickpocketing is faster, and marks forget faster |
| **The Ghost of Knot Square** | Escaping a city-wide manhunt in Vantress | Pursuers lose your trail in crowds |
| **Ten Faces** | Passing as ten different people | Disguises hold longer under questioning |
| **Rooftop Saint** | Crossing a city without touching the ground | Climbing and roof runs cost less stamina |

## Combat

| Title | Earned by | Power |
|---|---|---|
| **Army of One** | Defeating a large force alone | Being surrounded hurts you less; weak enemies may flee |
| **The One Who Did Not Fall** | Your first breakthrough beyond the human limit | Falls of 15 metres don't hurt you if you land well |
| **Duellist of the Circuit** | Winning a Brass Circuit tournament | Crowds cheer you; duels are easier to start and end |
| **Shield of the Weak** | Protecting others in a hundred fights | Allies near you take less damage |
| **Bonebreaker** | Breaking a hundred guards | Enemies hesitate before blocking you |
| **Mercy's Hand** | Subduing a hundred enemies without a death | Guards treat you as a lawful fighter; bounties on you fall |

## Magic

| Title | Earned by | Power |
|---|---|---|
| **Spill-walker** | Surviving the inside of a dozen spills | Unstable magic is less likely to hurt you |
| **Lightbringer** | Healing a thousand wounds | Your heals are stronger; the sick seek you out |
| **Stormcaller** | Calling lightning in a hundred storms | Storm spells cost less in a real storm |
| **Grave-friend** | Raising a hundred dead | The undead of others hesitate to attack you |
| **Unwritten** | Erasing yourself from a hundred memories | People forget you a little faster |
| **Pact-breaker** | Breaking a pact and surviving | Demons are wary of bargaining with you |

## The world

| Title | Earned by | Power |
|---|---|---|
| **Breaker of Chains** | Freeing prisoners, slaves or bound creatures | Resist restraints; recognise coercive magic; the oppressed shelter you |
| **Friend of the Chorus** | Earning Oruvai's trust | Animals everywhere are calmer near you |
| **Sealbearer** | Reaching the Glass Seal | Every seal gate in the Gilded Ascent opens without a question |
| **Keeper of the Unbarred Night** | Sheltering strangers in Orath | Hostels and sanctuaries take you in anywhere |
| **Writ-proof** | Surviving three Red Writs on your life | The Last Writ charges triple to accept a writ on you |
| **Godslayer** | Killing a god | Every god knows your name |
