The continent is large, and most of it is not city. Between the great cities lie days of farmland,
forest, steppe, marsh and mountain, with villages, waystations, ruins and things that live off
travellers. You can walk all of it, and at first you will. Later you can pay others to carry you,
or learn to fly.

## The Five Roads

Every great road leads to [the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md). The
[Chartered Road](../factions/chartered-road.md) polices them, tolls them and keeps a waystation a
day's ride apart along each.

| Road | Runs | Through | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Crown Road** | North-west | [Orravell](../cities/orravell.md), then the coast to [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md) | Paved by Orravell's engineers, patrolled by its Companies. Fast and watched. |
| **The Bell Road** | North | The foothills to [Keth Veyra](../cities/keth-veyra.md) | Climbs into the snow. Bargeback trains, avalanches, and a gate at the end that rarely opens. |
| **The Root Road** | West | [Rillvault](../cities/rillvault.md), the edge of [Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md), and the **Salt Spur** south to [Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md) | Ivo Tarrant's road: the busiest, carrying food east and lacquer north |
| **The Glass Road** | South-east | [Orath](../cities/orath.md), then [Tessara](../cities/tessara.md) and the [Sifting Basin](../cities/sifting-basin.md) | Pilgrims, glass carts and sandstorms |
| **The Quarry Road** | East | Hill country to [Veyrhold](../cities/veyrhold.md) | Heavy carts of hushstone and spellwork, and the Spillward's warning posts |

**[Arena City](../cities/arena-city.md)** lies on no road. It is reached by **the Black Track**, a
route across the southern steppe that the Chartered Road does not police, insure or admit exists.
Everything on the Unsealed List travels on it.

## How long it takes

Days from the Gilded Ascent with a caravan; walking takes half as long again, a good horse
two-thirds.

| To | Days | To | Days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sky City | Up the chain: an hour | Tessara | 6 |
| Orath | 3 | Sifting Basin | 6 |
| Rillvault | 4 | Oruvai's edge | 6 |
| Veyrhold | 5 | Vantress | 7 |
| Arena City | 5 (on the Black Track) | Keth Veyra | 8 |
| Orravell | 6 | The Second City | 9 |

## Ways to travel

| Way | Speed | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **On foot** | Slow | Free | You see everything, and everything sees you |
| **Horse** | Fast on roads | 15 ascents, and feed | Needs Riding to fight from; frightened by fire, wolves and Brinewings |
| **Caravan passage** | Fast travel between waystations | 2 chits a day, more with cargo | The caravan can be ambushed; you can be hired to stop it |
| **River barge** | Fast downstream | A few chits | On the great rivers of the west and the Tessaran delta |
| **Coastal ship** | Fast travel between ports | 1 to 5 marks | West sea: the Second City and Vantress. East sea: Veyrhold and Tessara. |
| **Bargeback** | Slow, unstoppable | Hire with a handler | The great pack beasts of the north; they carry a house's worth |
| **Sky City's lifts** | An hour up the Golden chain | 1 mark, and a reason to be there | The Golden Anchor decides who rides |
| **Rillvault's Deep Freight Lift** | Down into the caverns | A few chits | Freight first, passengers if there is room |
| **Brinewing** | Flight | Only for those the Keepers' Road trusts | See [Brinewing](../creatures/brinewing.md) |
| **Magic** | Instant, over short distances | Mana | Shaping's Passage branch: blink, then portals; Pactbinding's Gates: hellgates |

Fast travel always goes **along a route**: you can't jump from a field to a city, but you can take
passage on a caravan, ship or lift between its stops. Time passes while you travel, prices change
while you are gone, and sometimes the journey is interrupted.

In the current test builds, cars, planes and spaceplanes can be driven in [the Valley](../world/regions/the-valley.md); see
[Vehicles](../vehicles/index.md).

## On the road

Roads are where much of the world happens:

- **Waystations** a day apart: a caravanserai with a stable, a trader, a board of notices and a
  fire. Notices are work: escorts, bounties, missing people, wanted posters (sometimes yours).
- **Travellers**: pilgrims to Orath, glass carts, deserters, Spillward runners, a Tangle courier
  pretending to be a pilgrim, a tortoise envoy of Oruvai going somewhere very slowly.
- **Ambushes**: bandits on the Black Track and the Salt Spur, wolves on the Bell Road, worse
  things in the old kingdoms' ruins off the roads (see [Dungeons](../world/dungeons/index.md)).
- **Tolls**: the Toll Rings of the Gilded Ascent, Orravell's checkpoints, the Salt Spur's ferries.
  A seal makes them faster; a smuggler makes them unnecessary.
- **Weather**: snow on the Bell Road, sandstorms on the Glass Road, monsoon floods in the west.

## Getting lost

There is no marker over your goal unless someone gives you one. Directions come from people
("past the burnt mill, then follow the river until the arches"), from maps you buy or draw, and from
the land itself: rivers run to the sea, roads run to the Gilded Ascent, and the spire can be seen
for a day's ride in every direction.
