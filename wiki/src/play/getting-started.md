You begin as an ordinary person in [the Valley](../world/regions/the-valley.md), with no training,
no seal and no reputation. Everything you become, you earn by doing it.

## Before you start

1. **Make your character** (see [Character creation](character-creation.md)): choose a face, an
   origin, where to put your two free attribute points, and a keepsake.
2. **Pick a difficulty** (see [Difficulty, defeat and death](difficulty.md)). Medium is the game as
   it is meant to be played the first time; Hard makes enemies clever.

## Your first hours

1. **Do the job you came for.** The innkeeper of the Valley's tavern has a rat in the cellar. It
   is a fair fight (see [A Rat in the Cellar](../quests/main/a-rat-in-the-cellar.md)).
2. **Learn to move.** The movement course teaches wall-running, climbing and zip lines (see
   [Movement](movement.md)). Climbing out of trouble is often better than fighting it.
3. **Learn to fight.** The arena is a safe place to try [combat](combat.md): aim with your eyes,
   watch your stamina, and parry at the last moment.
4. **Earn your first marks.** The notice board in the village square always has small work:
   wolves near the paddock, a lost goat, a letter to carry.
5. **Take the case.** When a wounded courier stumbles into the village with a sealed case and a
   name, the story of [The Unsealed](../quests/main/index.md) begins.
6. **Take the road.** The Valley's road joins the Bell Road to [the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md),
   where every road on the continent meets (see [Travel](travel.md)).

## Things worth knowing early

- **A guard is dangerous; two are terrifying.** Don't start fights you can walk away from.
- **Sleep in a bed.** You'll be [rested](attributes.md#hunger-sleep-and-weather), and your skills
  grow faster.
- **Eat.** Food heals over time and good food does more.
- **Bandage bleeding at once.** Injuries don't heal on their own (see [Injuries](injuries.md)).
- **Talk to people.** Most problems have a way through that doesn't involve a sword (see
  [Conversation](dialogue.md)).
- **Watch what you carry into the Gilded Ascent.** Some things are banned there (see
  [Crime and the law](crime.md)).

## Trying the test builds

The current test builds also let you drive and fly: a car waits in the race track's pit lane, a
plane on the airfield apron and a spaceplane at the spaceport. Walk up and press **E** (see
[Vehicles](../vehicles/index.md)). Climb above 12 km in the spaceplane and press **J** to cruise to
[the Moon](../world/planets/moon.md) or the stars.

## How you grow

Skills improve as you use them. Fight and you fight better; cast and your magic grows. Levels give
you attribute points and new techniques; past the limit of a mortal, you Awaken, and then you
Ascend. See [Progression](progression.md) and [Skills](skills.md).
