You begin as an ordinary person in [the Valley](../world/regions/the-valley.md), with no
training and no reputation. Everything you become, you earn by doing it.

## Your first hour

1. **Look around.** The village square, the lake and the arena are all close by.
2. **Learn to move.** The movement course teaches wall-running, climbing and zip lines (see
   [Movement](movement.md)).
3. **Pick a fight you can win.** Start small: even a [house rat](../creatures/house-rat.md) is a
   fair fight at first. The arena is a safe place to learn [combat](combat.md).
4. **Try a vehicle.** A car waits in the race track's pit lane, a plane on the airfield apron
   and a spaceplane at the spaceport. Walk up and press **E** (see [Vehicles](../vehicles/index.md)).
5. **Leave the planet.** Climb above 12 km in the spaceplane and press **J** to cruise to
   [the Moon](../world/planets/moon.md) or the stars.

## How you grow

Skills improve as you use them. Fight and you fight better; cast and your magic grows. Past the
limit of a mortal, you ascend. See [Progression](progression.md).
