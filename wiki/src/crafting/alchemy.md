**Alchemy** turns plants, fungi, minerals and the parts of animals into things you can drink, rub in,
throw or slip into someone else's cup. It is the craft most people on the continent know a little
of: every grandmother has a remedy, every soldier a tonic. Doing it well takes knowing what each
ingredient really does, how much of it is safe and for whom, and when it is allowed to be picked.
What you can brew is listed on [Potions and poisons](../items/potions-and-poisons.md); how to make each
one is here.

## How brewing works

Every ingredient carries **up to four effects**. Brew two ingredients that share an effect and you
get a potion of that effect. Add a third that shares it as well and the potion is half again as
strong. Effects the ingredients don't share are lost in the brew, unless they **clash**.

1. **Choose a base.** Water for a drink, wine or spirit to hide the taste in someone's cup, fat or
   oil for a salve or a blade coating, a smoke jar to burn or throw.
2. **Grind** the ingredients in the mortar. Roots and shells take longer; flowers bruise if you
   grind them too hard.
3. **Add them in order**, strongest effect first.
4. **Heat or steep.** Each brew has a window when it is ready. Take it off the flame inside the
   window and the potion is as good as your skill allows; take it off at the very centre of the
   window and it comes out one quality better. Too early and it is weak; too late and it spoils.
5. **Bottle it.** Glass keeps a potion for a season; a cloudwater base and a waxed stopper keep
   it for a year.

**Clashing** happens when you brew ingredients with opposite effects: Heal and Harm, Sleep and
Quicken, Warmth and Cool. The result is a **wild brew** with one random effect, sometimes stronger
than anything you could make on purpose. Wild brews can't be sold under the Root Ledger's seal, and
some alchemists make nothing else.

### Discovering effects

You know an ingredient's **first** effect by tasting it, which is safe for almost everything except
the [poisons](#poisons). Its second effect shows at Apprentice, its third at Adept and its fourth at
Expert. You also learn every effect of every ingredient in a potion you brew, and herbals, trainers
and the Root Ledger's chapters will tell you the rest, for a price (see [Books](../lore/books/index.md)).

### Strength and quality

| Your Alchemy | Strength | Duration | Best quality you can brew |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Novice** (0 to 24) | ×1 | ×1 | Common |
| **Apprentice** (25 to 49) | ×1.25 | ×1.25 | Fine, on a bench |
| **Adept** (50 to 74) | ×1.5 | ×1.5 | Fine |
| **Expert** (75 to 99) | ×1.75 | ×2 | Masterwork, with a rare ingredient |
| **Master** (100) | ×2 | ×2 | Masterwork |
| **Ascended** | ×3 | ×3 | A potion that does what magic does |

Perks change these: **Healer** makes healing and cures half again as strong, **Poisoner** does the
same for poisons and smokes, and **Refiner** doubles the strength of everything. The
[Herbalism branch](../magic/vitalism.md#herbalism) of Vitalism lets you grow a fresh ingredient on the
spot, and a fresh ingredient brews one quality better than a dried one.

## The law

The [Root Ledger](../factions/root-ledger.md) decides what is a medicine, a stimulant, a narcotic and a
poison, and its **harvest calendar** says when wild plants may be picked. Picking out of season, or
anything on its **red pages**, is a crime wherever it has a chapter, and its wardens patrol the rich
harvest grounds. A potion sold under the guild's seal fetches a third more; a potion with no seal
can't be sold in the Gilded Ascent at all.

| Status | Means | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Legal** | Brew, carry and sell it anywhere | Every healing potion, tonics, Warmth, ward chalk |
| **Licence** | Brew and sell it with a Root Ledger licence; carry it without one | Viper coating, sleeping smoke, black powder, nightcap |
| **Unsealed List** | Can't be sold in the Gilded Ascent or by any Chartered Road trader | Glow, truth wine, every dark brew |
| **Red pages** | A crime to pick, brew or carry, everywhere the Root Ledger reaches | Blackroot, slow grey |

Vantress, Arena City and the Hollow Market of the Second City buy anything.
