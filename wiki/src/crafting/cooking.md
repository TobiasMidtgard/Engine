**Cooking** is the craft everyone thinks they know. You don't need to eat to live, but a hot meal
heals you faster than rest, a good one leaves you stronger for hours, and the right dish set in
front of the right person can do more than a bribe. [Rillvault](../cities/rillvault.md) feeds half the
continent, and its cooks will tell you that every war in history was lost by whoever ran out of food
first.

## How cooking works

- A **meal** is one main ingredient and up to two others, cooked at a fire or in a kitchen.
- Eating it restores **health and stamina** over three minutes, faster in a warm place.
- A good meal also leaves you **well fed**: one effect that lasts for hours. You can only be well fed
  by one meal at a time; a second replaces the first.
- Raw food restores a little and spoils in a few days. Cooked food keeps for a week, **rations** for a
  season, and **preserved** food for a year or the length of a continent.
- **Drink** works the same way, faster and weaker. Alcohol adds **tipsy**: Presence +1, Perception −1,
  and a worse aim for every cup after the second.

| Your Cooking | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Novice** | Cook simple meals over a fire. Meals restore their base amount. |
| **Apprentice**, Rations | Make food that keeps for a season. Meals restore a quarter more. |
| **Adept**: Feast or Chef | **Feast**: one pot feeds your whole company and gives everyone its effect. **Chef**: a meal you give someone raises their opinion of you, and some people will tell you things over dinner they never would in an office. |
| **Expert**, Preserving | Food that crosses a continent: smoked, salted, pickled and potted. Well-fed effects last twice as long. |
| **Master**: Comfort or Banquet | **Comfort**: meals heal injuries as well as health. **Banquet**: a dinner you host for the powerful counts as a persuasion in your favour, for everyone at the table. |
| **Ascended** | Food that lifts a city's mood. A meal you cook on a festival day changes what a city thinks of you. |

## Cooking for people

With the **Chef** perk, a meal you cook and give someone raises their opinion of you, once a day;
some people have favourite dishes and will tell you so. [Moss-Under-Stone](../people/companions/moss-under-stone.md)
approves whenever you share food, cooked or not. With **Banquet**, a dinner you host in your
[home](../play/homes.md) or a hired hall can win over a whole table: the Night Council, the Council of
Three Anchors or the keeper families of Keth Veyra. At [the Long Table](../lore/calendar.md#festivals),
Rillvault's autumn feast, the year's best recipe wins a prize and the cook's name is carved in the
Confluence Food Market.
