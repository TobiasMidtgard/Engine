**Smithing** is the craft of metal, and by long habit of leather too, since the smith who makes the
blade usually wraps its grip. Every weapon and piece of armour in the world was made by someone,
and the person who made it decided how good it is. A smith can make you a sword, mend the one you
have, or take a plain sword and make it better than it was.

## How smithing works

1. **Smelt** ore into ingots at a smelter. Two pieces of ore make one ingot; iron and charcoal make
   steel.
2. **Forge** the item at the anvil from ingots and the other parts it needs: a grip, a haft, a
   strap. Your Smithing rank decides what you can make and how good it comes out.
3. **Temper** it at the grindstone (weapons) or the workbench (armour) to raise its quality, one
   step at a time, using more of the material it was made from.
4. **Mark** it. A smith who is a member of the Makers' Compact stamps the lodge's mark beside their
   own, which raises its price by a third and lets enchanted work be sold in the Gilded Ascent.

Forging is a short rhythm at the anvil: heat until the metal glows the right colour, strike on the
beat, quench at the right moment. Getting the rhythm right adds condition and value; getting it
badly wrong cracks the piece and wastes an ingot.

### Quality

| Quality | Damage or armour | Condition | You need | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Common** | As listed on [Weapons](../items/weapons.md#numbers) and [Armour](../items/armour.md#numbers) | 100 | Novice | As listed |
| **Fine** | +15% | 140 | Apprentice; a lodge forge | ×2 |
| **Masterwork** | +30%, and enchantment capacity +2 | 200 | Master, with the **Masterpiece** perk, or a master's forge | ×6 |
| **Storied** | Whatever its history made it | Never breaks | Can't be made, only become | Priceless |

The **Forge-line** perk makes several items at once, all of them Fine. The **Masterpiece** perk lets
one item in each batch go beyond its material, and is the only way to make Masterwork without a
master's forge. A Masterwork item you made yourself, accepted by a lodge, is the **masterpiece** that
makes you a master of the Makers' Compact.

### Condition and repair

Every weapon and piece of armour has **condition**. Blocks, hits, falls and rain wear it down. Below
half, a weapon does a quarter less damage and armour stops a quarter less. At zero it breaks: a
blade snaps, a strap gives, a plate falls off in the middle of a fight.

| Repair | Where | Cost | Restores |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Whetstone and oil** | Anywhere | 2 chits each | A blade to three quarters |
| **Repair kit** | Anywhere, with the **Repair** perk (Apprentice) | 1 mark, five uses | Anything to three quarters |
| **A smith** | Every town | A tenth of the item's value | Full condition |
| **Your own forge** | A forge you own or rent | Materials only | Full condition, and quality kept |
| **Hedda Strom** | The smiths' lodge, Veyrhold | Materials only, for members | Full, and a broken Masterwork mended |

## Leatherwork

Hides go on a **tanning rack** for a day before they can be worked. A tanned hide is **leather**; a
thick hide is **heavy leather**. The workbench beside every forge is enough to make light armour,
straps, grips, sheaths and harness.

## Divine weapons

At **Ascended** rank a smith can forge a weapon around a Divine relic: the only kind of thing that can
wound a god. It takes the relic, a Masterwork weapon to set it in, a forge hot enough (Veyrhold's
Binding Yards, the Maker's own forge in Tessara, or the Lady in the Furnace's) and something more.
See [Making a divine weapon](../items/relics.md#making-a-divine-weapon).
