**Tinkering** is what the Engineering skill makes: small machines that do one thing well. A rattle
that goes off in a minute, somewhere else. A trap that holds a boar. A launcher that sinks a bolt into
stone and holds your weight. A lock nobody in the city can pick. In [Tessara](../cities/tessara.md) it
means something bigger: laying the ceramic tiles of **Patternwork**, which are the city's machines,
and reading the ones somebody else laid.

The skill splits at Adept into two ways of building:

- **Mechanist**: springs, gears, levers and powder. Clockwork, traps, launchers, locks and guns.
  Taught in Vantress, where every raft has something clever holding it together.
- **Patternwork**: ceramic tiles threaded with channels of Fundaments that carry power, motion,
  signals and memory. Taught in Tessara, where every wall is a machine.

## How it works

Everything you build has a **pattern**: a plan you learn from a trainer, a book, or by taking a
working one apart at the tinker's table. Taking a thing apart destroys it and teaches you its
pattern, if your rank is high enough to understand it. With the pattern you can build it from
parts; with the **Open pattern** perk anyone can mend what you build.

| Part | From | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Springs and gears** | Any tinker; salvage from clockwork | 3 chits |
| **Brass fittings** | Smiths; Vantress's markets | 4 chits |
| **Wire** | Smiths | 2 chits |
| **Black powder** | Alchemists (see [Alchemy](alchemy.md#bench-recipes)); licence in most cities | 1 mark a pound |
| **Lantern silk** | [Lantern spiders](../creatures/lantern-spider.md) | 5 chits; the strongest thread there is |
| **Patternwork tiles**, blank | Tessara's Pattern Exchange | 2 marks each; registered to the buyer |
| **Power tiles** | Tessara; charged at any public kiln | 5 marks each |
| **Stormglass cell** | [Sky City](../cities/sky-city.md) | 1 ascent; holds a charge for a season |
| **Floating stones** | [The Deepworks](../world/dungeons/the-deepworks.md) | 2 marks each; they fall upwards |

## Patternwork

> [!NOTE]
> In Tessara, **any machine with power over a person must reveal its pattern**. A hidden pattern is
> a crime (see [Crime and the law](../play/crime.md)).

Patternwork tiles are laid in **chains**. Each tile does one thing; a chain of them is a program you
can hold in your hands. Swap three tiles in a maintenance gallery and a shipment goes somewhere else.
Lay twelve on a wall and the wall tells you when someone touches it.

| Tile | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Power** | Holds and passes a charge |
| **Motion** | Turns a charge into a push, a turn or a lift |
| **Heat** | Warms or cools what it touches |
| **Signal** | Passes a message to the next tile, or to a tile far away |
| **Sense** | Notices touch, weight, light, sound or a magical signature |
| **Memory** | Remembers a pattern, a face or a key |
| **Gate** | Lets a charge through only if another tile says so |

| What you can build | Tiles | Rank | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Read a pattern** | None | Apprentice | See what a machine does, and who laid it |
| **Swap a chain** | 3 | Apprentice | Change where a machine sends things: a door opens for you, a cart goes elsewhere |
| **Alarm wall** | 6 | Adept | A wall that tells you when it is touched, from anywhere in the city |
| **Personal brooch** | 4 | Adept | Translates, guides you, opens public services; Tessara's citizens all wear one |
| **Threadbow chain** | 5 | Expert | Charges each arrow with an effect: a spark, a push, a tether |
| **Aegis emitter** | 8, and a power tile | Expert | Projects force armour (rating 20) and a round shield, until the tile runs down |
| **Vector soles** | 6, and a power tile | Expert | The Iris Striders' boots: a sudden dash, a soft landing, a correction in mid-air |
| **Machine-mind** | 24 | Master, Machine-mind perk | A machine that thinks a little: a door that knows its owners, a cart that finds its way home |

At **Ascended** rank you can build what the Last Artificer built. Nobody alive knows exactly what
that is; the first tile in the Mnemeion (see [Relics](../items/relics.md)) might tell you.

## Mending machines

Every city has machines that break: Keth Veyra's gates, the Gilded Ascent's lifts, Orath's waterworks
and all of Tessara. Engineering at **Apprentice** lets you fix simple ones; at **Adept**, the ones the
city depends on. Mending a public machine is honest work: the Pattern Office in Tessara and
[Ilyan Dareth](../people/ilyan-dareth.md) in Orath pay 1 to 5 marks a job, and posts it on their boards
every market day.
