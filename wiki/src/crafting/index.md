# Crafting

```meta
summary: Alchemy, smithing, tinkering, cooking and enchanting: what you can make, where, and from what.
```

{{stub}}

Crafting happens at **stations**: an alchemy bench, a forge, a tinker's table, a kitchen and an
enchanting circle. Every city has public stations you can rent by the hour, and you can build your
own into a [home](../play/homes.md). The skills that go with them are on
[Skills](../play/skills.md#craft-skills).
