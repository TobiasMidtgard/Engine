**Necromancy** is the dark magic of life. It raises the dead to fight and work, binds and harvests
souls, spreads disease and the afflictions of vampirism and lycanthropy, and heals the necromancer
by taking life from others. It is the dark mirror of [Vitalism](vitalism.md): where Vitalism
gives life, Necromancy takes it and spends it.

## Overview

The only place where Necromancy is lawful is the Eaves of [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md),
where the necromancers of **the Pale Archive** register every corpse, keep the books of who owns
whose body, memories and soul, and question the dead so that murder victims can testify. Their
Registrar sits on the Night Council. Everywhere else, raising the dead is a crime, and the families
of the dead take it very personally.

The Deep Choir of [Orath](../cities/orath.md) teaches that the dead remain part of the community.
It does not mean this.

Necromancy is paid for in corpses, souls and blood as well as mana, and every spell builds
[corruption](corruption.md). Its masters are the most powerful casters of any dark subclass, and
the most hated.

## Undeath

Raising the dead: skeletons, zombies, wraiths and worse. Undead are real bodies with real weight;
they fight, carry and dig until the spell ends or they fall apart.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Speak with dead** | Novice | Touch | A corpse answers three questions |
| **Corpse candle** | Novice | Orbit | A pale light that shows where the dead lie |
| **Raise rat** | Apprentice | Summon | A dead rat gets up and does what it's told |
| **Raise skeleton** | Adept | Summon | A skeleton warrior from a corpse or a grave |
| **Skeletal mage** | Expert | Summon | A skeleton that casts what it cast in life |
| **Bone golem** | Expert | Summon | A giant of fused bones |
| **Raise the fallen** | Expert | Area | Every corpse on the field stands up |
| **Army of the dead** | Master | Area | A legion, from an old battlefield or a graveyard |
| **Bone dragon** | Ascended | Summon | The bones of a great beast, flying again |
| **Kingdom of the dead** | God | Area | A whole land's dead, at your command |

## Afflictions

Disease, curses and the great afflictions. This branch spreads vampirism and lycanthropy, and
its afflicted casters take their forms.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grave chill** | Novice | Touch | A cold that saps strength |
| **Sickness** | Apprentice | Touch | Fever, weakness and a cough that spreads |
| **Wither limb** | Apprentice | Touch | A limb weakens and won't grip |
| **Plague touch** | Adept | Touch | A disease that spreads from body to body |
| **Mist form** | Adept | Self | Become mist (vampires only) |
| **Bat swarm** | Expert | Summon | A storm of bats that blinds and bites |
| **Werebeast form** | Expert | Self | The full change, at will (werebeasts only) |
| **Curse of the line** | Master | Touch | A curse that passes to the victim's children |
| **Plague wind** | Ascended | Area | A disease carried on the wind across a region |

See [Corruption and afflictions](corruption.md#afflictions) for what vampirism and lycanthropy
give and take.

has put its own soul somewhere safe.

| **Soul trap** | Adept | Touch | A soul that dies in it goes into a soul gem |

| **Soul drain** | Expert | Tether | Draws the soul slowly out of a living body |
