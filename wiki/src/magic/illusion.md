**Illusion** is the clean magic of the mind. It hides you, disguises you, shows people things that
are not there, lets you see what is hidden, and calms, inspires and persuades. It is the thief's
magic and the diplomat's, the spy's and the judge's. It never forces anyone to do anything; that
is [Domination](domination.md).

## Overview

Illusion is taught in surprising places. The aristocrats of [Sky City](../cities/sky-city.md) keep
Glamour salons where their children learn to be charming on purpose. The casino illusionists of
Vantress's Lantern Ward teach Phantasm, and the Tangle's wardwrights teach the Veil to anyone the
guild trusts. The Solitary Light's oath courts in [Orath](../cities/orath.md) train their judges in
**truth sense**, and will tell you so before you testify.

Illusion is not an invisibility button. A veil hides how you look, not the sound of your boots, the
smell of the river on you, the door you left open or the magical accent of your spell. Guards who
have been fooled before learn to look for all of those.

| **Blur** | Novice | Self | Slightly harder to see in shadow |
| **Quiet steps** | Novice | Self | Your footsteps make no noise |
| **Hush** | Apprentice | Area | Muffles all sound in a room |
| **Shadow blend** | Apprentice | Self | Almost invisible while you stand still in shadow |
| **Shroud** | Adept | Aura | A cloak of shadow and fog that shortens how far you are noticed |
| **Invisibility** | Adept | Self | Unseen until you strike, cast or touch someone |
| **Silent zone** | Expert | Area | Nothing inside makes a sound, spells included |
| **Mass invisibility** | Expert | Area | Your whole crew, unseen |
| **Perfect invisibility** | Master | Self | Unseen even while you act |
| **Unseen army** | Ascended | Area | An army, unseen |
| **Vanish** | God | Self | Gone from every sense and every memory of where you were |

## Phantasm

Things that are not there: distractions, decoys, disguises and false places. Phantasm changes how
things look, never what they are.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Distraction** | Novice | Rune | A sound or a flicker somewhere else |
| **Small illusion** | Apprentice | Summon | An object that isn't there: a coin, a cat, a closed door |
| **Disguise** | Apprentice | Self | Another face, another coat; you are still you underneath |
| **Mimic voice** | Apprentice | Self | Speak in a voice you have heard |
| **Decoy** | Adept | Summon | A copy of you that runs the other way |
| **Mirror image** | Adept | Orbit | Copies of you circle you, and attackers can't tell which is real |
| **Illusory person** | Adept | Summon | Someone who isn't there, who talks |
| **Hall of mirrors** | Expert | Area | A room nobody can find their way out of |
| **Phantom terrain** | Expert | Area | A bridge where there is a gap, a wall where there is a door |
| **Mirage fortress** | Master | Area | A fortress on a hill, for an army to besiege |
| **Become another** | Ascended | Self | So completely someone else that magic believes it too |
| **Dream a world** | God | Area | A world, for everyone inside it |

## Glamour

Warmth and persuasion: calming, inspiring and befriending. Glamour fades, and when it does, the
target feels it was kind. That is the difference from Domination.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Read mood** | Novice | Self | Know how someone feels about you |
| **Calm** | Apprentice | Touch | Anger drains away; a fight ends |
| **Inspire** | Apprentice | Area | Courage for your allies; the frightened stand |
| **Persuade** | Apprentice | Self | People are a little more willing to agree with you |
| **Befriend** | Adept | Touch | Warmth that fades, and leaves goodwill behind |
| **Mass calm** | Expert | Area | A riot becomes a crowd |
| **Rally** | Expert | Area | A broken army turns and fights |
| **Beloved** | Master | Aura | Everyone who sees you wants to help you |
| **Peace** | Ascended | Area | Two armies lower their weapons |
| **Mask of the gods** | God | Self | People see in you whatever they most hope for |
