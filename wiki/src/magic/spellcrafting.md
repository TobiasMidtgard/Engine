# Spellcrafting

```meta
summary: Build your own spells: effect, cast type, modifiers and power, within your mastery.
```

{{draft}}

At a spellcrafting altar or in your grimoire you build spells yourself, in four steps. Each
spell must fit your **complexity budget**, which grows with your mastery. The crafter shows the
budget, the mana and health cost, any corruption, and a live preview on a practice dummy.

| Mastery | Budget |
|---|---|
| Novice | 1 |
| Apprentice | 2 |
| Adept | 5 |
| Expert | 8 |
| Master | 12 |
| Ascended | 16 to 25 |
| God | 40 |

## 1. Effect

One or more effects from the subclasses you know: fire damage, a heal, a force vector,
invisibility, a conjured blade. Each extra effect costs complexity.

## 2. Cast type

How the spell leaves you.

| Cast type | What it is |
|---|---|
| Touch | On what you touch; cheapest and strongest |
| Imbue | On your weapon or arrows; triggers on each hit |
| Bolt | A projectile in a straight line |
| Lob | A thrown arc that goes over cover |
| Beam | A continuous line while you hold it |
| Cone | A spray in front of you |
| Wave | Travels along the ground and climbs slopes |
| Wall | A line you draw on the ground |
| Area | A circle where you aim |
| Aura | Around you, moving with you |
| Rune | A glyph on a surface that fires when triggered |
| Orbit | Circles you and hits what comes close |
| Tether | Links you to a target in range |
| Chain | Jumps from target to target |
| Rain | Falls from the sky over an area |
| Self | On you |
| Summon | Places a creature, object or construct |

## 3. Modifiers

Each modifier costs complexity.

| Modifier | What it does |
|---|---|
| Blast zone | Explodes on impact |
| Speed, Range, Size | How fast, how far, how big |
| Split | Several projectiles in a fan |
| Fork | Splits into more when it hits |
| Chain | Jumps to more targets |
| Seek | Homes in on targets |
| Pierce | Passes through targets |
| Bounce | Ricochets off walls |
| Boomerang | Flies out and back, hitting both ways |
| Rend | Lodges in the target and stacks; recast to rip them all out |
| Recall | Conjured weapons fly back to your hand |
| Stacking | Something happens at a number of hits: five frost stacks freeze |
| Duration, Linger | How long it lasts; a field left behind |
| Delay, Charge, Channel, Echo | Fire later, hold to grow, hold to sustain, recast once |
| Target filter | Enemies, allies, everyone, or one kind of creature |
| Safeguard | Never harms allies or you; cheap on clean spells, costly on dark |
| On hit, On kill, On expire, On block | Fire another spell when this happens |
| Silent | No sound or light, for stealth |
| Subdue | Knocks out instead of killing (clean only) |
| Lifesteal | Part of the damage heals you (dark only) |
| Blood price | Pay with health instead of mana (dark only) |
| Overcharge | Go past your budget, with a growing chance to backfire |

## 4. Power

How much energy goes in, from charging and from your tier: the same spell grows from a spark
to a sun.

## Keeping spells

Saved spells get a name and an icon and go on your hotbar or radial menu. The Artifice branch
binds them into items: enchantments, scrolls, wands, runestones and turrets.
