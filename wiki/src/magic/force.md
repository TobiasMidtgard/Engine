# Force and movement magic

```meta
summary: One rule for movement magic: a force placed on a target, from a hop to flight.
```

{{draft}}

All movement magic follows one rule: a **force spell is a push, in a direction and with a
strength, placed on a target**. A quick push is an impulse; a held one lasts. Strength comes
from power, so the same spell grows from a hop to flight.

| Force on | Up | Forward or away | Toward you | Down |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Yourself** | Hop, slow fall, hover, levitate, fly | Speed, dash, leap | Grapple to a point | Dive, ground slam |
| **Your pack** | Carry more | | | |
| **An ally** | Lift up a ledge, catch a fall | Throw across a gap | Pull to safety | |
| **An enemy** | Throw into the air, float helpless | Push back, knock over | Pull to you | Force to their knees, pin down |
| **An object** | Lift, telekinesis | Throw | Fetch | Crush, hold a door shut |
| **The ground under you** | Walk on water, run on walls | | | |

## Space magic

- **Blink**: a short teleport to somewhere you can see.
- **Mark and Recall**: mark a place, return to it from far away.
- **Portal**: a two-way gate your party can walk through.
- At Ascended, gates to other planets.

## The dark side

Pactbinding uses the same forces to harm: **chains** drag victims along the ground, pin them and
crush their armour, and at Master break a limb. Dark travel is the **shadow step**, a blink
between shadows, and the **hellgate**, a portal demons can use too.
