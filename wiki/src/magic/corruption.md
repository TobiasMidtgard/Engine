**Corruption** is the mark dark magic leaves on the body that casts it. Dark spells pull the
[Fundaments](../lore/fundaments.md) the wrong way through a living body, taking life to make force
and suffering to make power, and the body remembers. Every dark spell adds a little; big ones,
pacts and dark enchantments add a lot. It shows, it changes how the world treats you, and at its
peak a god starts to listen.

## How it builds

- **Every dark spell** adds corruption by its power: a Novice curse barely marks you, a Master
  ritual leaves a scar.
- **Spells with a dark ingredient** count as dark, even when most of the spell is clean.
- **Pacts, soul gems and dark enchantments** add corruption when you make them, and slowly while
  you use them.
- **Afflictions** such as vampirism hold your corruption at a stage they choose.
- **Time** lowers it, very slowly, if you stop.

## The five stages

| Stage | What shows | How the world reacts |
|---|---|---|
| **Touched** | Faint dark veins at the wrists after casting | Animals grow uneasy around you |
| **Marked** | Eyes darken in dim light; a chill to your touch | Temples refuse you blessings; guards in clean cities search you |
| **Tainted** | Your voice carries a faint echo; wounds close slowly | Healing magic works at half strength on you; shrines turn you away; some towns bar their gates |
| **Hollowed** | Your shadow lags a moment behind you | Children and animals flee; clean healers can't touch you; dark factions open their doors |
| **Claimed** | All of it, and dreams of a door made of skin | [The Waiting Wound](../lore/gods.md#the-waiting-wound) notices you (see [The Wound That Waits](../quests/the-wound-that-waits.md)) |

The changes are visible on your character, in the character creator's body system: the veins, the
eyes, the voice. Disguises, glamours and corruption tinctures can hide them for a while.

## Cleansing

| Where | How | The price |
|---|---|---|
| **Vitalism's Purge** | A spell of the Restoration branch, cast by you or a healer | Lowers corruption a stage; very tiring, and a Tainted caster can't cast it on themselves |
| **The Covenant of Ash's wound-houses** | A week of work tending the sick and the burned in [Orath](../cities/orath.md) or any Covenant hospital | Time, and the work itself |
| **Accord shrines** | Penance set by a Verifier of the [Accord of Four Keys](../factions/accord-of-four-keys.md) | Deeds, not coin |
| **The First Spring** | Bathing in its water, once | All of it, and a vision you won't forget. The Accord rarely allows it |

## Afflictions

Some dark magic changes what you are. **Afflictions** are caught from a bite or taken on by
ritual, and they bring powers and needs together.

### Vampirism

Caught from a vampire's bite, or chosen by an **inheritance agreement** in the Eaves of
[the Second City](../cities/second-city.md).

- **Gifts**: stronger at night; drain health through a bite; turn into a swarm of bats or a mist;
  Blood spells cost less.
- **Needs**: sunlight burns you; thirst grows every night you don't feed; silver hurts.
- **The law**: in the Eaves, feeding on blood you don't own is theft. Elsewhere, being discovered is
  usually the end of you.

### Lycanthropy

Caught from a werebeast's bite, or chosen by ritual with a clan of the Moon Runs.

- **Gifts**: a werebeast form with great strength, speed and regeneration; rage; senses like a wolf's.
- **Needs**: the full moon forces the change, wherever you are; silver and wolfsbane hurt.
- **The law**: the clans of the Second City's Moon Runs live by agreements; elsewhere, werebeasts
  are hunted.

### Demon form

The reward of a pact in the Gates branch of [Pactbinding](pactbinding.md#gates): wings, horns and
hellfire for a while. Your patron asks for something in return, every time.

### Curing an affliction

Afflictions can be cured. Vitalism's Restoration masters know how, the Covenant of Ash will try,
and a questline begins the first time you ask. Some cures cost the gifts as well as the needs; some
cost more.
