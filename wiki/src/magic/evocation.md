**Evocation** is the clean magic of the elements: **Fire**, **Frost** and **Storm**. It is the
magic people picture when they hear the word: a fireball, a wall of ice, a lightning bolt. Clean
damage is built to stop a threat, so Evocation is fast, precise and short-lived, it hurts what you
aim at and little else, and every spell can be cast to **subdue** instead of kill.

## Overview

Evocation is the most widely taught magic on the continent. [Veyrhold](../cities/veyrhold.md)'s
workshops sell evoked spells by the dozen, [Orravell](../cities/orravell.md)'s Crown Companies
train war mages who drop fire from the canopy, and the flying mages of
[Arena City](../cities/arena-city.md) fight with it every night in front of thousands. It is legal
almost everywhere, though Veyrhold requires a Spillward licence to cast it within the quarry.

Each element has its own feel. **Fire** burns and spreads. **Frost** slows, and builds up to a
freeze. **Storm** stuns, and chains from one target to the next. Mastering two elements opens their
combination (see [Elements and combinations](elements.md)), and at the highest ranks Evocation
reshapes the land itself.

Ranks run Novice, Apprentice, Adept, Expert, Master, Ascended and God; see
[Spellcrafting](spellcrafting.md) for how a spell is built and how it grows.

## Fire

Fire burns, spreads to what is flammable and keeps burning after the spell ends. It lights the
dark, scares animals and makes smoke.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flame bolt** | Novice | Bolt | A plum-sized flame; singes, lights a candle or dry grass |
| **Kindle** | Novice | Touch | Lights a torch or a campfire from a few metres |
| **Fireball** | Apprentice | Bolt | A fist-sized ball that sets its target burning |
| **Flame jet** | Apprentice | Cone | A spray of fire while you hold it |
| **Fire wall** | Adept | Wall | A line of flame you draw on the ground |
| **Exploding fireball** | Adept | Bolt | Bursts on impact, throwing burning splashes |
| **Rocket leap** | Adept | Self | A burst of flame under your feet throws you into the air |
| **Flame whip** | Expert | Tether | A lash of fire that pulls what it catches |
| **Fireball volley** | Expert | Bolt | Five fireballs in a fan |
| **Firestorm** | Master | Rain | Fire falls over a whole street |
| **Fire elemental** | Master | Summon | A living blaze that fights for you |
| **Phoenix wings** | Ascended | Self | Wings of flame that carry you, and burn what you pass over |
| **Small sun** | God | Area | A sun, for a moment, where you point |

## Frost

Frost slows. Each hit adds a stack of chill, and at five stacks the target freezes solid; the next
heavy blow shatters it. Frost also makes paths and walls.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Frost touch** | Novice | Touch | Chills a target, frosts a puddle |
| **Cool skin** | Novice | Self | Resist heat for a while |
| **Ice spike** | Apprentice | Bolt | A thrown spike that pierces and chills |
| **Frost armour** | Apprentice | Self | A shell of ice that slows anyone who strikes you |
| **Frost nova** | Adept | Aura | A burst of cold around you that freezes the nearest |
| **Ice bridge** | Adept | Wall | A path of ice over water or a gap |
| **Shardstorm** | Adept | Cone | A blast of ice shards that knocks people back |
| **Ice prison** | Expert | Touch | Encases a target in ice |
| **Deep freeze** | Expert | Area | Cold so deep that time itself stiffens |
| **Blizzard** | Master | Rain | Snow, wind and ice over a wide area |
| **Frost elemental** | Master | Summon | A walking glacier |
| **Frost comet** | Ascended | Rain | A comet of ice from the sky |
| **Eternal winter** | God | Area | Winter that does not end until you allow it |

## Storm

Storm stuns, and chains: lightning jumps to the nearest body, first to anyone wet or already
shocked. Water carries it, metal draws it and nobody sees it coming.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Spark** | Novice | Touch | A static snap that startles and lights a lamp |
| **Static charge** | Novice | Self | The next creature that hits you gets a shock |
| **Shock** | Apprentice | Bolt | A short arc that stuns, and jumps through water |
| **Chain lightning** | Adept | Chain | Leaps from target to target |
| **Lightning trap** | Adept | Rune | A glyph that strikes whoever steps on it |
| **Thunderclap** | Adept | Aura | A shockwave of thunder that stuns everyone near |
| **Ball lightning** | Expert | Orbit | Crackling spheres circle you and strike what comes close |
| **Lightning step** | Expert | Self | Travel the length of a lightning bolt in an instant |
| **Lightning storm** | Master | Rain | Bolts fall across a battlefield |
| **Storm elemental** | Master | Summon | A thundercloud with a temper |
| **Storm call** | Ascended | Area | Calls a real storm, and directs it |
| **Avatar of the storm** | God | Self | You become the storm |

## Spellblade

The spellblade puts the elements on a weapon. Its cast type is **Imbue**: the spell rides on your
blade, your arrows or your fists and lands on everything you strike. Paired with Shaping's
conjured weapons, it makes the sword-and-sorcery fighter of the old songs.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Imbued hand** | Novice | Imbue | Your fist carries a spark of an element |
| **Flame weapon** | Apprentice | Imbue | Your weapon burns, and sets what it strikes burning |
| **Frost brand** | Apprentice | Imbue | Each hit adds a stack of chill |
| **Charged edge** | Adept | Imbue | Each hit shocks, and jumps to the next enemy |
| **Arc cleave** | Adept | Wave | A swing that sends a wave of fire, frost or storm along the ground |
| **Lightning sword** | Expert | Imbue | Storm on a conjured blade: every swing throws a bolt |
| **Meteor punch** | Master | Touch | A blow that lands like a falling star; stone cracks around it |
| **Heaven's spear** | Ascended | Bolt | Your weapon thrown as a bolt of light from the sky |
| **Star forge** | God | Summon | Forge a weapon from the heart of a star |

## Combinations

Two elements mastered to Adept open their pair; all three at Master open Aether. See
[Elements and combinations](elements.md).

| Combination | Makes |
|---|---|
| Fire + Frost | **Steam**: scalding clouds that burn and blind |
| Fire + Storm | **Plasma**: a white-hot charge that cuts armour |
| Frost + Storm | **Tempest**: hail, wind and lightning |
| Fire + Frost + Storm | **Aether**: shifts to whatever a target is weakest to |
