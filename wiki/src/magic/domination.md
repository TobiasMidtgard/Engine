**Domination** is the dark magic of the mind. It terrifies, enrages, compels and possesses, and
it can make people forget, or remember things that never happened. It is the dark mirror of
[Illusion](illusion.md): Illusion persuades, Domination forces.

## Overview

Domination is banned everywhere, and used everywhere. [Orravell](../cities/orravell.md)'s Office
of Civic Integrity is said to keep dominators for its interrogations. The Broker of the Night
Council in [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md) is said to be one. Nobody admits to teaching
it, and its masters tend to have very loyal servants.

Dark social magic is **remembered**. When it wears off, the target knows something was done to
them, and word spreads through everyone they know, unless you also make them forget. Forcing a
guard to open a gate is easy. Making sure nobody finds out is the real work.

## Fear

Terror: fleeing, freezing and despair. Frightened enemies run, drop their weapons and fight badly.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Scare** | Novice | Touch | A weak foe flinches and backs away |
| **Unease** | Novice | Area | Everyone nearby feels watched |
| **Fear** | Apprentice | Bolt | The target flees |
| **Mass fear** | Adept | Area | A crowd scatters |
| **Night terror** | Adept | Touch | Darkness that brings the victim's worst fear (with Malefice's Shadow) |
| **Terror wave** | Expert | Wave | A wave of fear that breaks a line of soldiers |
| **Dread lord** | Master | Aura | Nobody can bring themselves to attack you |
| **Despair of nations** | Ascended | Area | A whole country gives up |

## Wrath

Rage: enraging, frenzy and madness. Turn friends on each other and let them do your fighting.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Taunt** | Apprentice | Bolt | The target must attack you |
| **Rage** | Apprentice | Touch | The target loses their temper |
| **Frenzy** | Adept | Touch | The target attacks the nearest person, friend or foe |
| **Berserk** | Adept | Touch | More strength, no sense |
| **Paranoia** | Expert | Touch | The target believes their friends are plotting against them |
| **Turncoat** | Expert | Touch | For a while, the target fights for your side |
| **Madness** | Master | Touch | A mind breaks; it may not mend |
| **Mind plague** | Ascended | Area | Madness that spreads from person to person |

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Halt** | Novice | Bolt | The target stops for a moment |
| **Nudge** | Novice | Touch | The target takes one small step you choose |
| **Command** | Apprentice | Bolt | One word the target must obey: "Drop", "Sleep", "Open" |
| **Compel** | Apprentice | Touch | One act the target must do |
| **Beast control** | Apprentice | Touch | An animal does what you say |
| **Brief puppet** | Adept | Tether | You move their body for a few seconds |
| **Dominate** | Expert | Touch | The target obeys you for as long as the spell lasts |
| **Possession** | Master | Touch | You leave your body and wear theirs |
| **Thrall legion** | Master | Area | A crowd of servants |
| **Command an army** | Ascended | Area | An army obeys you |

## Memory

Forgetting and false memories: the branch that cleans up after the others.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Doubt** | Novice | Touch | The target is no longer sure what they saw |
| **Forget** | Apprentice | Touch | The last minute is gone |
| **Memory edit** | Adept | Touch | Change one detail of something remembered |
| **False memory** | Expert | Touch | Something that never happened, remembered clearly |
| **Mass forget** | Master | Area | Nobody in a square remembers you were there |
| **New person** | Master | Touch | A whole life, replaced |
| **Rewrite history** | Ascended | Area | A city remembers it differently |
| **Erase from history** | God | Touch | Nobody remembers that it ever existed |

**Erase from history** is how scholars think a god was once killed so completely that nobody
remembers its name (see [Gods and faiths](../lore/gods.md#dead-gods)).
