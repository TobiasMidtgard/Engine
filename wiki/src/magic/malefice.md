**Malefice** is the dark mirror of the elements: **Blood**, **Blight** and **Shadow**. Where
Evocation stops a threat, Malefice punishes it. Its spells linger: wounds that bleed when the
victim moves, rot that eats armour and flesh and stops anything from healing, darkness that brings
terror and eats the light. It is about half again as strong as clean magic for the same mana, and
it costs more than mana: health, blood and [corruption](corruption.md).

## Overview

Malefice is taught nowhere openly. Its masters are the black mages of
[the Second City](../cities/second-city.md)'s Eaves, who keep the wards of the Night Council; its
books, reagents and cursed tools are sold in the Hollow Market; and renegade mages teach it to
anyone who pays. Witnessed dark casting is a crime in almost every city, and guards in clean cities
search anyone who carries its marks.

Dark damage is brutal through the body, not for show. It breaks limbs and locks joints through
injuries, blinds, silences, makes wounds that will not heal, drains health to the caster, spreads
disease, and terrifies, so that the victim flees or drops their weapon. Bystanders are hurt by
default; protecting them costs extra.

Everything with a dark ingredient is dark, so a single Malefice effect in a spell makes the whole
spell dark.

## Blood

Blood drains and bleeds. Its spells move life from the victim to the caster and make wounds that
worsen.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blood spark** | Novice | Bolt | A bolt paid with a drop of your own blood; it hits harder than its size |
| **Leech touch** | Novice | Touch | Drains a little health into you |
| **Siphon** | Apprentice | Bolt | A bolt that drains health back to you along its path |
| **Hemorrhage** | Adept | Bolt | Bleeding that worsens every time the victim moves |
| **Blood boil** | Adept | Touch | Pain that drops a weapon from the victim's hand |
| **Blood bond** | Expert | Tether | While it lasts, damage they take heals you |
| **Exsanguinate** | Master | Tether | Draws the blood from a body, quickly |
| **Blood tide** | Ascended | Wave | A tide of blood that drains everything it passes |

## Blight

Blight rots and poisons. Its wounds cannot heal while it lasts, its rot eats metal, wood and
flesh, and it spreads.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rust rot** | Novice | Touch | Rots a hinge, a chain or a blade's edge |
| **Rot lock** | Apprentice | Touch | Rots a lock or a door away, slowly and silently |
| **Rot** | Apprentice | Bolt | Armour and flesh rot; healing is blocked while it lasts |
| **Poison cloud** | Adept | Area | A green fog that sickens all inside |
| **Slow blight** | Adept | Touch | Rots bone from the inside over days, leaving no wound (see [The Bout That Broke Wrong](../quests/the-bout-that-broke-wrong.md)) |
| **Acid rain** | Expert | Rain | Rain that eats armour and stone |
| **Withering field** | Master | Linger | Ground that rots whatever stands on it |
| **Ash plague** | Ascended | Area | A blight that spreads from crop to crop across a region |

## Shadow

Shadow terrifies, blinds and eats light. It is the assassin's element: silent, invisible and
cruel.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Snuff** | Novice | Aura | Eats the light around you; lamps gutter |
| **Shadow bolt** | Apprentice | Bolt | A bolt of darkness that blinds for a moment |
| **Blind** | Apprentice | Touch | Darkness over the eyes for a minute |
| **Dread** | Adept | Area | Terror that makes the weak flee and the strong fight badly |
| **Night terror** | Adept | Touch | Darkness that brings the victim's worst fear (with Domination's Fear) |
| **Black fog** | Expert | Area | Darkness that no lamp can light |
| **Eclipse** | Master | Area | Turns day to night over a district |
| **Last light** | God | Area | Puts out every light in a city |

## Hexblade

The hexblade puts curses on steel. Its cast type is **Imbue**, like the spellblade's, but what
rides on the blade is suffering. Paired with Pactbinding's pact weapons, it makes the dark knight.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bleeding edge** | Apprentice | Imbue | Every cut bleeds |
| **Hex edge** | Apprentice | Imbue | Every hit rots, and blocks healing |
| **Shadow strike** | Adept | Imbue | A blow struck from darkness lands twice as hard |
| **Cursed wound** | Expert | Imbue | Wounds from your weapon never heal on their own |
| **Doom blade** | Master | Imbue | Carries Doom: bleeding, rot and terror that leap to a new host when the victim dies |
| **Reaper's harvest** | Ascended | Wave | One swing, and everyone wounded in sight bleeds out |

## Combinations

| Combination | Makes |
|---|---|
| Blood + Blight | **Plague**: infectious bleeding that spreads to the next body |
| Blood + Shadow | **Nightfeed**: drains life from anything standing in darkness, unseen |
| Blight + Shadow | **Wither**: ages flesh; the wound cannot be healed |
| Blood + Blight + Shadow | **Doom**: does all three, and leaps to a new host when its victim dies |

Mixed with clean elements they make **boiling blood**, **hellfire**, **black flame**, **frozen
veins**, **frostrot**, **black ice**, **blood lightning**, **acid storm** and **dark thunder**
(see [Elements and combinations](elements.md#clean-and-dark-together)).
