**Pactbinding** is the warlock's magic: the dark mirror of [Shaping](shaping.md). Its power does
not come from study but from a **pact** with a demon of the Hells, who lends it in return for
something. Pactbinders call demons to fight for them, bind victims in chains that crush and drag,
carry weapons and armour made from their pact, and open gates that demons can walk through too.

## Overview

There are no schools of Pactbinding. Every pactbinder learns from their **patron**, the demon they
made a pact with, and every patron teaches what suits it. Summoning circles, pact contracts and the
names of demons are sold in the Hollow Market of [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md), and
very few of them are genuine. Pactbinding is illegal everywhere, including the Eaves, where the
Night Council fears demons more than it fears the Magistracy.

Demons live in **the Hells**, a realm beneath the Ethereal, and they want what the world has:
souls, service, attention and a way in. They are not gods (see
[Gods and faiths](../lore/gods.md#what-is-not-a-god)), but the greatest of them are very old and
very patient.

## Patrons

| Patron | Wants | Gives best |
|---|---|---|
| **Ommath, the Patient Creditor** | Debts: every spell is a loan, and the interest is exact | Infernal summons and gates |
| **The Lady in the Furnace** | To be worshipped, and to burn | Hellfire, hellplate and demon form |
| **Gorrow of a Thousand Chains** | Prisoners: anyone you bind belongs to Gorrow afterwards | Chains |

A patron asks for something every time you reach a new rank: a service, a soul, a secret, a
promise. Refuse, and the pact sours: your spells cost more, and your patron starts sending demons
of its own.

## Infernal

Calling demons: imps, hellhounds and worse. They fight hard and obey badly.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brimstone** | Novice | Bolt | A spat bolt of burning stone |
| **Summon imp** | Apprentice | Summon | A small, clever, spiteful demon that fetches, spies and burns |
| **Hellhound** | Adept | Summon | A burning hound that runs down what flees |
| **Lesser demon** | Expert | Summon | A demon warrior with its own will |
| **Demonic meteor** | Master | Rain | A burning stone from the Hells, with a demon inside |
| **Demon lord** | Ascended | Summon | One of the great ones. It will remember being called |

## Chains

Force turned to harm: chains that drag a victim along the ground, pin them, crush their armour
and break their bones. The dark side of Kinesis.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chain link** | Novice | Touch | A short chain binds two things together |
| **Chain hook** | Apprentice | Bolt | A hooked chain drags the victim to you |
| **Pin** | Apprentice | Touch | Chains rise from the ground and hold a victim down |
| **Drag** | Adept | Tether | Drag the victim behind you, wherever you go |
| **Crushing chains** | Expert | Touch | Chains that tighten until armour buckles |
| **Bone snap** | Master | Touch | Breaks a limb |
| **Thousand chains** | Ascended | Area | Every enemy in sight, bound |

## Pact Arms

Weapons and armour from your pact: soul-bound blades that drink life, and hellplate that burns
whoever strikes you. Pact arms grow **hungry** if they go unused, and a starving weapon feeds on its
owner.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pact blade** | Apprentice | Self | A soul-bound blade in your hand that drinks a little life with each hit |
| **Pact bow** | Adept | Self | A bow whose arrows burn |
| **Hungering axe** | Adept | Self | An axe that grows stronger with every kill, and hungrier |
| **Hellplate** | Expert | Self | Armour of the Hells that burns attackers |
| **Soulbite** | Master | Imbue | Your pact weapon takes a piece of the soul with each hit |
| **Legion arms** | Ascended | Summon | Pact weapons for everyone who fights beside you |

## Gates

form for a while.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shadow step** | Apprentice | Self | A blink from one shadow to another |
| **Hellgate** | Expert | Summon | A portal that you, and demons, can use |
| **Demon wings** | Master | Self | Wings of the Hells |
| **Demon form** | Master | Self | Horns, wings and hellfire for a while; your patron asks something in return |
| **Portal invasion** | Ascended | Summon | Gates open across a battlefield, and the Hells pour out |
| **Open the Hells** | God | Area | A door that does not close |
