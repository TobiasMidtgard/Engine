**Shaping** is the clean magic of matter, force and space. It moves things, including you; it
opens doors to far places; it conjures weapons, tools and walls out of nothing; and it binds magic
into objects. It is the most useful magic there is, and the one thieves love most.

## Overview

Shaping is taught wherever things are made. The engineers of [Tessara](../cities/tessara.md) learn
Artifice beside their Patternwork, [Veyrhold](../cities/veyrhold.md)'s workshops sell conjured
tools and golems, and the pilots and criminals of [Sky City](../cities/sky-city.md) learn Kinesis
and Passage because falling off an island is a long way down. The Makers' Compact's enchanters'
lodge teaches Artifice to anyone who will apprentice for it.

Every movement spell in Shaping follows one rule: a **force placed on a target**, in a direction,
with a strength (see [Force and movement magic](force.md)). The same push that slides a crate
throws a man across a room, and at the top ranks lifts a castle.

Force: pushes, pulls, lifts and throws, on yourself, on others and on objects. Kinesis is also the
thief's quiet friend: a careful push turns the pins of a lock.

| **Quiet unlocking** | Apprentice | Touch | A force that turns a lock's pins, silently |
| **Pull** | Adept | Bolt | Drag an enemy to you |
| **Glide** | Adept | Self | Glide on the air from a high place |
| **Deflect** | Adept | Self | Turn an arrow or a blade aside |
| **Hurl** | Expert | Touch | Throw anything you can lift, very hard |
| **Arrow catch** | Expert | Aura | Arrows stop in the air around you |

| **Kinetic fortress** | Master | Aura | Nothing moving can reach you |
| **Lift the keep** | Ascended | Touch | Lift a building off its foundations |
| **Move a mountain** | God | Touch | As it says |

## Passage

Space: short steps, long jumps and doors. Passage holds the clean side of travel magic, from a
blink across a room to a gate to another world.

| Spell | Rank | Cast | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Water walking** | Apprentice | Self | Walk on water |
| **Blink** | Apprentice | Self | A short teleport to somewhere you can see |
| **Wall running** | Adept | Self | A sideways force that lets you run along walls |
| **Ghost step** | Adept | Self | A blink that leaves no trace (with Illusion's Veil) |
| **Mark** | Adept | Rune | Marks a place to return to |
| **Recall** | Adept | Self | Return to your mark from anywhere on the continent |
| **Short portal** | Expert | Summon | A gate to somewhere you can see, for your whole party |
| **Portal** | Master | Summon | A two-way gate to any place you have marked |
| **Planar door** | Ascended | Summon | A gate to other worlds: [the Moon](../world/planets/moon.md), the red planet and beyond |
| **Star bridge** | God | Summon | A road between stars |

they weigh what they weigh, and they vanish when the spell ends unless you craft them into an
item.
