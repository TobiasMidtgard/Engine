**Vitalism** is the clean magic of life. It heals wounds, mends broken limbs, raises the fallen,
shields friends, calls beasts, takes their shapes and makes plants grow where you stand. It is the
healer's subclass and the tank mage's, the druid's and the herbalist's, and it is the only magic
welcome in every temple on the continent.

## Overview

The best healers on the continent are the Covenant of Ash's physicians in
[Orath](../cities/orath.md), who learned their Restoration in hospitals and on battlefields. The
herbalists of [the Root Ledger](../factions/root-ledger.md) teach Herbalism alongside alchemy, and
the keepers of [the Keepers' Road](../factions/keepers-road.md) learn the Wilds from the animals
themselves. In [Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md) the Chorus teaches beast shapes to the few outsiders
it trusts.

Vitalism also cleanses: its **Purge** washes out [corruption](corruption.md), and its masters can
cure vampirism and lycanthropy. That makes Vitalists the natural enemies of the dark, and very
popular with everyone else.

## Combinations

Vitalism mixes with everything: **Radiance** is Fire with a heal, Frost with Herbalism makes a
preserving frost for medicines, and Storm with the Wilds is the druid's thunder. A heal combined
with Necromancy's drain makes **Transfer**, which moves health from one body to another, and is
dark.
