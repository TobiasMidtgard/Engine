**Gloomwings** are large cave bats, black with grey bellies and a metre and a half across the wings. They hunt moths and lantern spiders, and they defend their roosts in flocks. One is a nuisance; a roost of fifty is dangerous to anyone without a light.

## Behaviour

Gloomwings roost on cave ceilings by day and leave at dusk in a black river of wings. Disturb a roost and the whole flock attacks: they shriek (a stunning noise in an echoing cave), bite at the face and scatter.

## Strategy

Carry light; a torch keeps them off and fire scatters a flock. Fight with your back to a wall. In the Echo Galleries, their shrieks are louder: cover your ears (it's a real button).
