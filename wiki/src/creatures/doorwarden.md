A **doorwarden** is a tall, faceless figure of black stone, without a seam or a joint, standing beside a door of the same stone. The First Builders set one at every door they built over a well, and they wake when anyone comes near who is not written in the Sixth Register. Nobody knows how they move. They have no eyes and see everything.

## Behaviour

A doorwarden doesn't leave its door, doesn't speak and doesn't stop. It can be calmed: the page for its door, held up to it, stills it; the Builders' script, read aloud, can command it. And because you are written nowhere, it can't quite tell whether you are there at all.

## Strategy

Only Crush hurts it much: bring a mace or a hammer. Holding up the right page stops it for five seconds at a time. If you can read the Builders' script (from the Artificer's frame, or the Register), you can tell it to stand aside.
