**Steppe jackals** are lean, dark-furred and long-legged, the colour of the Black Steppe's summer grass, and they live in packs of up to twenty. They follow caravans for days, picking off stragglers. The Wind's Teeth raiders keep them as hounds.

## Behaviour

A pack follows at a distance until a traveller falls behind, then closes in from all sides at once. They bite at the legs to hamstring, and they break off if three of them fall.

## Strategy

Stay with the caravan. Kill the pack leader (the biggest, with a torn ear) and the rest scatter. A fire at night keeps them off. Back to a wall, as with wolves (see [Wolf](wolf.md)).
