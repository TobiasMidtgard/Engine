The **reed stalker** is a grey heron grown to two and a half metres, with a beak half a metre long and the patience of a statue. It stands in the reeds for hours waiting for fish. It will wait for you, too, and its stab aims for the eyes.

## Behaviour

A stalker stands perfectly still in the reeds, nearly invisible, and stabs whatever comes close. It doesn't chase; if a fight goes badly it flies.

## Strategy

Its legs are thin: a mace or a staff to the knee brings it down. Shoot it before you're in reach. Guard your head (it stabs at the eyes).
