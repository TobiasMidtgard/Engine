The **marrow crow** is a large black crow with a grey neck that follows death: battlefields, gallows, caravans in the steppe. It eats the dead, cracking bones for the marrow, and it is clever. It remembers faces, holds grudges, and teaches its young who to avoid.

## Behaviour

Marrow crows gather where something is dying, sometimes before it dies. They mob anyone who hurts one of them, for years. They can be fed and befriended, and a friendly flock warns you of ambushes.

## Strategy

Don't kill one where the others can see. Feed them instead: a flock that likes you follows you and caws when someone hides nearby (it counts as a lookout).
