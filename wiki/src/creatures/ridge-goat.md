The **ridge goat** lives on cliffs that would stop anything else, and it is the reason anything else lives up there at all: lynxes, bears and shepherds all follow the herds. Its wool is the warmest cloth on the continent.

## Behaviour

Herds of a dozen graze the high pastures and flee up the cliffs at any threat. In autumn, the males fight each other with a crack you can hear across a valley, and anything that comes near them then is a rival.

## Strategy

Leave them alone on a ledge, where a ram's charge will send you off it. On open ground they are easy prey for a bow. Shepherds' goats are someone's property.
