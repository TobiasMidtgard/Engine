The **brown bear** is the largest predator of the northern woods: two and a half metres tall standing, and faster than a horse over a short distance. It eats berries, roots, fish and carrion, and it doesn't hunt people. It does defend its cubs, its kill and its winter den, and it doesn't stop until it has won.

## Behaviour

A bear warns before it attacks: it stands, huffs, and slaps the ground. Back away slowly and it usually lets you go. Run and it chases. If it charges, it may stop short (a bluff) or it may not. A mother with cubs never bluffs. In winter, bears sleep in dens; waking one is the worst mistake in the Whitecrowns.

## Strategy

Don't fight a bear in the open. Put a tree between you, use a spear to keep it at length (it impales itself on a braced spear when it charges), and aim for the belly when it rears. Fire makes it hesitate. If it pins you, the only escape is a dagger to the eye or an ally pulling it off.
