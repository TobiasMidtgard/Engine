The **Brinewing** is a dragon-like creature raised by the keepers of
[Keth Veyra](../cities/keth-veyra.md). It looks like a broad, heavily insulated wyvern, with black
heat-absorbing scales along its back, pale feather-fur beneath and wings big enough to ride
violent winds.

## Behaviour

Every Brinewing has its own temperament. They are rare and expensive: Keth Veyra has only four
adults, and raising another takes nearly thirty years. A Brinewing breathes fire by spraying an
oily digestive secretion across mineral plates in its mouth, which bursts into a rolling cloud of
orange fire and black steam. Using it exhausts the animal.

Brinewings fly best in Keth Veyra, where the dam sends up permanent columns of warm air. Away from
the city they are heavier, slower and much harder to launch, which is why the city uses them to
defend itself rather than to conquer its neighbours.

## Uses

Brinewings fire ceramic sluice blocks, clear tangled driftwood, sterilise diseased pens, dry grain
and patrol the reservoir. Their flame lights industrial kilns, and their flight makes them
formidable scouts and defenders.
