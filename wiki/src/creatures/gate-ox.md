**Gate Oxen** turn the capstans that raise and lower the sluices of Keth Veyra's dam. They are bred for size and calm, and they work in teams of eight in the machinery halls of the Gateworks, where the floor shakes and the noise never stops.

## Behaviour

Gate Oxen are calm in noise that would drive a horse mad, and they feel the machinery through their hooves. When a mechanism is failing, they know; when one machinery hall began to fail before the Drowning Year, they panicked, and nobody listened. Calming one (Animal handling) lets you see what frightened it.
