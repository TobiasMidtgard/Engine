The **undead** are corpses raised by [Necromancy](../magic/necromancy.md). They are real bodies,
with real weight and strength, and they do exactly what the necromancer who raised them commands,
no more and no less. Most can't think for themselves; the strongest remember a little of who they
were.

## Behaviour

Undead do not tire, eat, sleep or fear. They follow their last command, so a skeleton told to guard
a door will guard it for a hundred years. They are clumsy with anything their bodies did not know
how to do in life. Kill the necromancer and most undead fall where they stand; a lich's servants
keep going.

In the Eaves of [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md), the dead workforce is registered by the
Pale Archive, and you will see skeletons carrying coal up the stairs.

## Strategy

Maces and hammers shatter bone; blades do less. Fire and Radiance burn them. Silver hurts the
wraiths. Find the necromancer and you end the fight. A lich can't be killed for good until you find
and break its **phylactery**, the vessel that holds its soul.
