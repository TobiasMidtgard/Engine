The **lantern spider** lives in caves, where it glows: a soft gold light from its body that lures cave moths, gloomwings and the curious. Its silk glows too, and a colony's web looks like a hall hung with lamps. The Hollow Downs' shepherds call them cave lamps and leave them alone.

## Behaviour

Lantern spiders hang their webs across cave passages and wait. Anything caught is bitten, wrapped and hung. Their venom slows, then paralyses. Colonies share webs, and a big colony has a **mother** at its heart, much larger than the rest.

## Strategy

Burn the webs before you walk through them; fire spreads along the silk and the spiders with it. A torch or a lantern stops them dropping on you. Carry a clean-blood tonic for the venom. Their glow gives them away in the dark: they are easier to see than you are.
