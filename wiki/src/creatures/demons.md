**Demons** are beings of **the Hells**, a realm beneath the Ethereal. They want what the world has:
souls, service, attention and a way in. They can only enter the world when someone calls them, and
they can only stay as long as a pact holds them. Every demon is looking for a way to make it
permanent.

## Kinds

| Demon | Called by | Danger | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Imp** | Apprentice | 2/5 | Small, clever and spiteful; fetches, spies and throws fire |
| **Hellhound** | Adept | 3/5 | A burning hound that runs down whatever flees |
| **Lesser demon** | Expert | 4/5 | A demon warrior with a will of its own and a long memory |
| **Chain-warden** | Expert | 4/5 | A servant of Gorrow that binds whatever it catches |
| **Demon lord** | Ascended | 5/5 | One of the great ones. It will remember who called it |

## Patrons

The greatest demons are **patrons**, who make pacts with pactbinders and lend them power (see
[Pactbinding](../magic/pactbinding.md#patrons)):

- **Ommath, the Patient Creditor**, who keeps exact accounts of every debt and collects them all.
- **The Lady in the Furnace**, who wants to be worshipped and to burn.
- **Gorrow of a Thousand Chains**, who collects prisoners.

## Behaviour

Demons obey the pact that called them, to the letter, and look for every gap in its wording. An
imp told to fetch a key brings the key and leaves the door open; a hellhound told to guard a gate
guards it against its own master. When a pact breaks, a demon is free until it is sent back, and
free demons do whatever they have wanted to do since they arrived.

## Strategy

Wards hold them, Radiance burns them, and a broken pact sends them home. Find the summoning circle,
the contract or the pactbinder, and break whichever is easiest. Never bargain with a demon yourself
unless you have read the whole contract twice.

## Locations

- Wherever a pactbinder works. Hellgates, if any are open.
