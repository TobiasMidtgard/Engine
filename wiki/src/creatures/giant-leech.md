The **giant leech** lives in still swamp water and in the reeds above it, and it can smell blood and warmth from ten metres. It drops, latches and drinks. A single leech is a nuisance; the swarms of the deep Saltmire can drain a horse in minutes.

## Behaviour

Leeches swarm anything that stands still in the water. They latch onto exposed skin (armour stops them), and a latched leech drains health until it is pulled off or burned.

## Strategy

Wear armour in the swamp, don't stand still in the water, and carry salt: a pinch makes a leech let go at once. Fire on the water clears a swarm.
