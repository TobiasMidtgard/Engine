The **wild boar** is a heavy forest pig that roots through woods and field edges in family
groups. Sows and piglets usually run. An old male, a **tusker**, would rather stand his ground.

## Behaviour

A boar is built like a wedge: a high hump at the shoulders, a back sloping down to a narrow rump,
short stout legs and a big head. Its pale-tipped crest lies flat while it feeds and stands up
when it is roused. A roused tusker lowers its head, trots a few steps and charges.

## Strategy

The crest is your warning. When it stands, step aside from the charge line rather than backing
away: a boar is fast in a straight line and slow to turn.
