A **spillbeast** is an animal that walked through a magical spill and came out changed. No two are the same: a fox with teeth of glass, a hare that blinks a metre at a time, a boar whose bristles smoulder, a stag that moves a moment before you see it. The Spillward hunts them, Ysolde Marr studies them, and the Keepers' Road argues about whose they are.

## Behaviour

A spillbeast keeps most of its old nature (a fox is still a fox) and gains whatever the spill wrote into it. Most are frightened and in pain. Some are not, and those are the dangerous ones.

## Strategy

Read it before you fight it: Perception and Attunement show what the spill changed. Hushstone weakens any spillbeast near it, and a hushstone manacle can hold one for the Spillward or the Keepers' Road, who both pay for live ones.
