The bestiary lists every creature you can meet: where it lives, how it behaves, how
dangerous it is, its level and numbers, and what it leaves behind. Danger runs from one dot
(harmless) to five (run). The same creature can be a threat on your first day and an afterthought
after a hundred levels; the levels say when.

## Numbers

Every creature page has a **Stats** table: level, health, poise, damage and the experience it is
worth. Experience for a kill falls each time you kill the same kind of thing at the same level, and
rises for the first of anything (see [Progression](../play/progression.md#experience)). Health and
damage are on the Medium [difficulty](../play/difficulty.md); Easy lowers enemy damage, Hard and
above raise it and make enemies smarter, not just tougher.
