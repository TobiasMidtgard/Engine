The **latticeback** is a small, social, armoured burrower of the desert. The people of the
[Sifting Basin](../cities/sifting-basin.md) keep whole colonies of them, because their empty nests
are rich in metal.

## Behaviour

Latticebacks sift sand to eat wind-blown seeds, fungal spores and the thin living crust that wakes
after rain. The creatures in their gut separate mineral grains from the grit; they spit out most
of the quartz and bind the minerals into porous honeycomb nurseries that protect their young from
predators, heat and cold. When the young leave, the colony moves on to fresh ground.

A latticeback can only concentrate the minerals already in its sand, so colonies on different
grounds build nests of different metals.
