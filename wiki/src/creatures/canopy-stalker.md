The **canopy stalker** is the old forest's leopard: black-spotted dark brown, three metres from nose to tail, and so heavy it bends branches. It lives in the canopy and comes down only to kill, usually deer, sometimes Crown Companies sentries. The first raiders of Avarren painted it on their shields, and the Bough Cohorts still do.

## Behaviour

A stalker follows its prey through the canopy, silently, for an hour or more, and drops when the prey stops. If it can, it drags what it kills back up into the trees. It hates open ground and avoids roads.

## Strategy

Stay on roads and in clearings. In the forest, listen for falling bark. When it drops, it is on the ground with you, where it is weaker: fight it there, and don't let it get back into the trees with you.
