The **dune runner** is a desert lizard as big as a horse that runs upright on two long legs, balanced by a long tail, faster than any horse over open sand. It is sand-coloured with blue-black stripes, and it hunts in threes, running prey down and taking it from three sides.

## Behaviour

Dune runners hunt in the cool hours, at dawn and dusk, and sleep in the heat and at night, cold and slow. In threes, one drives and two wait. Their kick has a hooked claw that tears.

## Strategy

Don't run from them; you can't outrun them. Fight with your back to rock. Frost slows them badly. Travel at night, when they are cold and asleep, as the desert caravans do.
