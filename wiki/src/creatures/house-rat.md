The **house rat** lives wherever people store food: cellars, granaries, sewers and the holds
of ships. For a hardened adventurer it is a nuisance. For someone who has never held a weapon,
it is a real fight, and the first story every legend tells about themselves.

## Behaviour

House rats keep to the dark and run from light, noise and anything bigger than they are. A rat
that is cornered, or guarding young, turns and bites. Where there is one rat there are usually
more.

## Strategy

At the very start of the game a rat can beat you. Corner it where it can't slip past, keep your
footing and don't chase it into the dark. A few levels later you will barely notice them, and
long after that, the shockwave of a single blow will scatter every rat in the street.

## Locations

Any cellar, storehouse, sewer or dock.

## Lore

People say you can judge a household by its rats: a house with none is either very clean or
very well guarded.
