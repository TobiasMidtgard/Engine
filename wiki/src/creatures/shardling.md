**Shardlings** are crystal creatures that grow from the light of the Sunken Works' Mind crystal: shapes like foxes, birds and people, made of clear, faintly humming crystal, with the crystal's thoughts in them. They are what the crystal reaches out with.

## Behaviour

Shardlings move together, like one mind (they are). Their touch confuses: you forget which way you were going, or who your friends are, for a few seconds. When one breaks, its shards fly.

## Strategy

Maces and hammers shatter them; stand back from the shards. Hushstone stops them, and Illusion's Insight resists the confusion. A companion at Devoted approval can't be turned.
