The **wolf** is a pack hunter of the forests and meadows. A lone wolf is wary; a pack is one of
the most dangerous things a new traveller can meet outside a town.

## Behaviour

Wolves move in packs and hunt together. They stalk low through cover, spread out around their
prey and close in from several sides at once. A resting wolf lies down with its head up; an
alert one stops, raises its head and turns its ears toward the sound. Wolves hear gunfire and
loud fighting from far away and react to it.

## Strategy

Don't let a pack get behind you: put a wall, a rock or water at your back so they can only come
from the front. Watch for the wolf that drops low and stops moving, because that is the one
about to lunge.
