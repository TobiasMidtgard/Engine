**Glass worms** live under the Sifting Desert, swimming through sand as easily as fish through water. They are pale, eyeless, and hot: their bodies are hot enough to fuse sand into glass, and they leave tunnels of green glass behind them that the Basin's lens-makers dig up and sell. They feel footsteps through the sand.

## Behaviour

A glass worm rises under whatever walks above it and bites up. Small ones are the size of a dog. The Great Glass Worm of the Sand-drowned City is forty metres long, and can swallow a person.

## Strategy

Get onto rock, where it can't dive. Walk with an irregular step, or not at all; Sneak makes you harder to feel. Frost cracks its hot skin. Never stand on open sand during a fight.
