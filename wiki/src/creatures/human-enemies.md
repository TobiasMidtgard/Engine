Most of the fights in the world are with **people**. They fight by the same rules you do: they aim at
the body (see [Combat](../play/combat.md#aiming-at-the-body)), they tire, they stagger, they get
injured and they bleed. They also think. They take cover, call for help, flank you, pick up a better
weapon, surrender when they are losing and run when they are beaten, and how well they do all that
depends on the [difficulty](../play/difficulty.md) and on who they are.

Every one of them can be talked to before the fight, and some during it (see
[Conversation and persuasion](../play/dialogue.md)). Most would rather not die.

## Machines

Tessara's machines are open to inspection, by law. Most are harmless. A few go wrong, and a few have
been changed by someone who didn't want them inspected.

| Enemy | Level | Health | Poise | Damage | Tactics | XP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Runaway repair frame** | 40 to 50 | 800 | 300 | 30 to 45 | Mends things that aren't broken, including you | 400 |
| **Black-glazed sentry** | 50 to 60 | 1,000 | 400 | 50 to 70 (energy) | A Closed Kiln guard: its pattern is hidden, so it can't be read or turned off | 600 |
| **Vault construct** | 70 to 80 | 2,000 | Can't be staggered | 70 to 100 | The banks' brass guardians in the Counting Deep: stop only for the right seal | 1,000 |

Read a machine's pattern (Engineering, Patternwork) and you can often turn it off, or tell it to do
something else.

## Fighting people

- **Talk first.** Most fights can be avoided, and most enemies tell you something if you let them.
- **Break their morale.** Kill or disarm the leader, and the rest often break. Bandits flee when
  half are down; soldiers don't, unless their commissar falls.
- **Let them surrender.** A beaten enemy who drops their weapon can be spared, robbed, questioned or
  handed to the law. Companions notice which.
- **Remember them.** People you spared remember it. So do the people you didn't.
