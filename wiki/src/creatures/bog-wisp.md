**Bog wisps** are the marsh lights of the Lantern Fen: soft blue and green flames that drift over the water at night. They are not spirits of the dead, whatever the eel-trappers say. They are small, slow-witted things of raw Life that spill out of the fen, and they feed on the mana and stamina of whatever follows them.

## Behaviour

A wisp drifts just out of reach, and its light pulls at the mind (a Mind effect; high Attunement or Illusion's Insight resists it). It leads its prey into deep water or a leech swarm, then latches on and drains.

## Strategy

Don't follow lights at night. Weapons pass through a wisp; magic doesn't. Frost and Radiance put it out, and a ward stops the drain. Moss-Under-Stone can't be lured.
