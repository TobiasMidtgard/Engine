A **vampire** is a person changed by the affliction of vampirism: stronger, faster and longer-lived
than any human, able to drain the life of others through a bite, and burned by sunlight. Most of
the continent's vampires live in the Eaves of [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md), where
they rule a rooftop city by contract and law.

## Behaviour

Vampires are people, and they behave like people: they trade, argue, scheme, fall in love and hold
grudges for a century. They are most active at night and spend the day under shade, in sealed
rooms or beneath the canvas night-sails of the Eaves. A vampire who has fed recently is calm,
warm-skinned and hard to tell from anyone else. A starving one is pale, cold and dangerous, even to
people it loves.

In the Eaves, vampires feed only on blood they own, buy or win by contract, most of it from the
blood houses of the Red Exchange. Turning someone needs a signed **inheritance agreement**. The
eldest vampire holds the Old Blood's seat on the Night Council. Outside the Second City, vampires
hide, and the ones who don't are hunted.

## Abilities

- **Bite**: drains health from the victim to the vampire. A bite without an agreement can pass on
  the affliction.
- **Night strength**: stronger, faster and quicker to heal after dark.
- **Bat swarm** and **mist form**: older vampires can break into a swarm of bats or a drifting mist.
- **Blood magic**: Malefice's Blood spells cost them less (see [Malefice](../magic/malefice.md#blood)).

## Strategy

Fight them by day if you can, in the open if you can't. Sunlight burns them, fire hurts them, and
silver wounds that won't close quickly. Radiance, the Vitalist spell of sunlight, is the closest
thing to a weapon made for them. Don't let one get close to your neck; a bite drains you and heals
it. Remember that in the Eaves, killing a vampire without a Red Writ is murder.

## Becoming one

You can catch vampirism from a bite or choose it by agreement in the Eaves. See
[Corruption and afflictions](../magic/corruption.md#vampirism).

## Locations

- [The Second City](../cities/second-city.md): the Eaves, the Red Exchange and the Dusk Stair.
