A **werebeast** is a person afflicted with lycanthropy, who can become a huge, beast-like hunter:
most often a wolf on two legs, sometimes a bear, a great cat or something with no name. In human
form they are ordinary people. When the change comes, at will or forced by the full moon, they
become one of the most dangerous things on the continent.

## Behaviour

Werebeasts live in **clans**. The biggest clans run across the rooftops of
[the Second City](../cities/second-city.md), along the long open roofs called the **Moon Runs**, and
their Pack-Speaker holds a seat on the Night Council. Clans settle disputes by racing and fighting
on the Runs, and they control every route across the roofs. Outside the Second City, werebeast
clans live in remote forests, and villages near them lock their doors at the full moon.

A changed werebeast hunts as a pack animal: it runs prey down, flanks it with its clan and leaps
from above. A werebeast forced to change by the moon has little control, and may not recognise its
own family.

## Abilities

- **The change**: great strength and speed, keen senses and fast regeneration, at the cost of
  fine control: no casting, no tools, no lockpicks.
- **Rage**: wounds make them stronger for a while.
- **Howl**: frightens animals and weaker people; calls the clan.
- **The full moon** forces the change, wherever they are.

## Strategy

Silver wounds them and stops their regeneration; wolfsbane on a blade or in smoke weakens them.
Fight with your back to a wall, because they come from several sides, and from above. In daylight,
before the change, a werebeast is just a person, and can be talked to.

## Becoming one

You can catch lycanthropy from a bite or choose it by ritual with a clan. See
[Corruption and afflictions](../magic/corruption.md#lycanthropy).
