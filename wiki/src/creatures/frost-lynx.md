The **frost lynx** is the snow's own hunter: pale grey, tufted, and so still against the snow that you can look straight at one and not see it. It hunts ridge goats and the occasional shepherd from rocks above the passes, dropping onto its prey's back and going for the neck.

## Behaviour

A frost lynx waits above a path, on a rock or a ledge, for as long as it takes. It attacks only from above and behind, and if its first pounce misses, it breaks away and climbs back up to wait again. It avoids groups and fire.

## Strategy

Look up. Perception spots a lynx before it pounces; once it has been seen, it rarely attacks. Carry a torch on the passes. If it lands on you, roll (Acrobatics) to throw it off, then keep your back to rock so it can't get above you again. Guard your neck: it aims there every time.
