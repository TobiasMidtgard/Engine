A **boss** is an enemy who is the point of a place: the thing at the bottom of the dungeon, the person
the story has been walking you towards. Bosses have more health and poise than anything near them,
attacks you won't have seen before, and almost always a weakness that you can find before the fight
if you look. Many of them can be talked to.

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for every boss in the game below.
