The **storm hawk** nests on high cliffs and on the storm-crowns of Sky City, where lightning fuses stormglass, and it hunts in thunderstorms, when everything else is grounded. Its feathers hold a static charge; touch one in a storm and it knocks you down.

## Behaviour

Storm hawks dive from a great height and strike with their talons, discharging their feathers' charge as they hit. They defend their nests from anyone climbing near, including stormglass harvesters.

## Strategy

Wear something that isn't metal in a storm (metal armour conducts). Shoot it in the dive. Grounding magic, or simply standing in water, makes its shock harmless to you.
