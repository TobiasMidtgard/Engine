**Bargebacks** are Keth Veyra's great working animals: broad, flat-backed and slow, with shaggy coats, webbed feet and the patience of a river. They tow barges across the reservoir, carry freight up the passes in trains of twenty, and swim with whole cargoes on their backs. A bargeback can carry a house's worth of goods.

## Behaviour

Bargebacks follow their handlers' signals and nothing else. They do not fight; they refuse. A frightened bargeback stops and won't move until whatever frightened it is gone, and in [The Drowning Year](../quests/main/the-drowning-year.md) the bargebacks refuse to enter a channel for a reason.

## Strategy

Don't fight a bargeback. If one panics, get out of its way. To hire one (with its handler), ask the Keepers' Road; see [Travel](../play/travel.md#ways-to-travel).
