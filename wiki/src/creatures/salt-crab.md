The **salt crab** is a scavenger of the coasts, grey-blue, with a shell like a buckler and one claw much bigger than the other. It eats anything washed up, including people, and at low tide on the Grey Coast whole beaches move with them.

## Behaviour

Salt crabs are slow on sand and fast in water. They block with their shell and pinch with the big claw, and a pinch on the hand can make you drop what you hold. They swarm anything wounded.

## Strategy

Maces and hammers crack the shell; blades skid off it. Flip one (a heavy blow to the edge of the shell) and it's helpless. Watch your hands.
