**Echo-crawlers** live in the deepest caves under Rillvault: pale, eyeless and long-legged, about the size of a large dog, with feathery ears all over their bodies that can hear a heartbeat across a cavern. In the Echo Galleries, where sound carries for kilometres, they can hear you long before you see them.

## Behaviour

Echo-crawlers hunt by sound. Every noise you make tells them where you are: running, fighting, speaking, a dropped torch, a scream. If you stand perfectly still, they can't find you. Their shriek, focused by the galleries, stuns.

## Strategy

Sneak. Walk instead of running, put out your torch if you can see without it, and stand still when they are close. Throw a stone to send a pack the wrong way, or ring a pillar in the Choir of Pillars. Fire scares them.
