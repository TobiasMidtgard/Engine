The **mire eel** is the reason the people of the Saltmire build their villages on stilts. It is black, slick, as long as a rowing boat, and it lives in the channels, waiting for anything that swims or wades. It bites, coils and pulls its prey under, and holds it there. The oldest eels give off a shock.

## Behaviour

Mire eels lie still on the bottom until something disturbs the water above them. Eel-trappers in Reedholm bait cages with fish; the eels have started emptying them.

## Strategy

Don't swim where you can't see the bottom. If one coils you, you have as long as you can hold your breath (Endurance, Athletics) to cut free; stab at the head. Out of the water, an eel is slow and helpless.
