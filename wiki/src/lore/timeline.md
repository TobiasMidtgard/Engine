The history of the continent, oldest first. Each event links to the places, people and factions it
shaped. Dates count back from the year the story begins; the further back they go, the less
anybody agrees on them.

## The ages

| Age | When | What it was |
|---|---|---|
| **Before names** | Time out of mind | People without cities. The First Builders and their black stone. |
| **The old kingdoms** | Until about 420 years ago | Many small kingdoms rule the continent, its roads, its wars and its dams |
| **The age of roads** | About 420 to 170 years ago | Trade outgrows the kings; the great cities rise around their trades while the kingdoms shrink |
| **The age of guilds** | The last 170 years | Holy war ends in Orath; the kingdoms that remain are small, and the guilds are stronger than any crown |

## The old kingdoms

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| Long ago | **The Great Burning**: Orath burns, but the spring keeps flowing beneath the ruins, and the Covenant of Ash is born. |
| Centuries ago | A great flood strands the animals of the western valley on its last dry islands. Their cooperation through the vines becomes the Chorus of [Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md). |
| Centuries ago | The first raiders of the great tree Avarren learn that rulers will pay them to fight: the beginning of [Orravell](../cities/orravell.md). |
| Centuries ago | The smith Ferris draws a tempered hand from the Maker's forge, and the Maker's priesthood begins. |

## The age of roads

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| About 420 years ago | Five rival caravan houses meet at the Middle Well, a crossroads bought by [the Crownless King](ascendants.md#the-crownless-king), and strike the first seals. The [Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md) and the [Chartered Road](../factions/chartered-road.md) begin. |
| About 350 years ago | A drifting island is caught on the winds east of the Gilded Ascent, chained, and settled by the rich: [Sky City](../cities/sky-city.md). |
| About 300 years ago | Abandoned dam-builders stay through the first monsoon and found [Keth Veyra](../cities/keth-veyra.md). |
| About 300 years ago | [The Beast Without Chains](ascendants.md#the-beast-without-chains) bonds with a Brinewing as an equal. |
| About 250 years ago | A failed enchantment in a quarry shed shows that the dark stone drinks stray magic: [Veyrhold](../cities/veyrhold.md) becomes a city of mages. |
| About 230 years ago | [The Last Artificer](ascendants.md#the-last-artificer) lays the first cores of the Mnemeion in [Tessara](../cities/tessara.md), and then vanishes inside it. |
| About 220 years ago | A spill that hushstone cannot hold destroys a cutting shed in Veyrhold. The workers who go back in found [the Spillward](../factions/spillward.md). |
| About 200 years ago | The keepers of Rillvault's seed archive begin lending to the farmers above: the start of [the Root Ledger](../factions/root-ledger.md). |
| About 190 years ago | The forbidden market grows on a platform in the south, selling what the Gilded Ascent bans: [Arena City](../cities/arena-city.md). |

## The age of guilds

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| 168 years ago | The last holy war of Orath. Rescuers of all four faiths open the Hollow Crown to the civilians against orders; the [Accord of Four Keys](../factions/accord-of-four-keys.md) follows. |
| About 150 years ago | The quiet war between the Boulevard and the Eaves of [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md) ends in the Sunset Compact. [The Empty Blade](ascendants.md#the-empty-blade) founds [the Last Writ](../factions/last-writ.md). |
| About 120 years ago | **The Long Strike**: the smiths of a dozen cities lay down their hammers at once, and three kingdoms find they have no new swords. [The Makers' Compact](../factions/makers-compact.md) signs the peace. |
| About 100 years ago | Dockside brawlers start fighting on the forbidden market's floor at night, and [the Brass Circuit](../factions/brass-circuit.md) is born. |
| 83 years ago | **The Drowning Year**. In a catastrophic flood, the council of [Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md) cuts a working-class district loose; its survivors, declared dead, become [Ward Null](../cities/vantress/ward-null.md). |
| 83 years ago | The same monsoon, in Keth Veyra: merchants try to steal Brinewing eggs, and the Night of Cut Harnesses drowns a lower neighbourhood. The city closes itself to outsiders. |
| About 40 years ago | The Sunken Works, a magical research facility east of Veyrhold, collapses into its own foundations. The Spillward seals it. |
| Recent years | A long regional peace ruins [Orravell](../cities/orravell.md)'s war economy, and Cael Ordan rises to power promising renewal through expansion. |
| Recent years | The Gilded Ascent's last Restriking. The next is due within the year. |

## The present

The story begins. Orath's First Spring has started to weaken. Orravell is quietly planning to take
Kestrel Vale. The Sunset Compact of the Second City is cracking. And somewhere, an ordinary person
who struggles to beat a house rat is about to find work.
