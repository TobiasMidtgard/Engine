The history of the world, oldest first. Each event links to the places, people and factions it
shaped. Dates count back from the year the story begins.

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| Before any faith | Travellers leave offerings beside the First Spring of [Orath](../cities/orath.md). Unknown builders lay the black foundations of the Hollow Crown. |
| Centuries ago | A great flood strands the animals of the western valley on its last dry islands. Their cooperation through the vines becomes the Chorus of [Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md). |
| Long ago | The Great Burning: Orath burns, but the spring keeps flowing beneath the ruins, and the Covenant of Ash is born. |
| About 300 years ago | Abandoned dam-builders stay through the first monsoon and found [Keth Veyra](../cities/keth-veyra.md). |
| 168 years ago | The last holy war of Orath. Rescuers of all four faiths open the Hollow Crown to the civilians against orders; the Accord of Four Keys follows. |
| 83 years ago | In a catastrophic flood, the council of [Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md) cuts a working-class district loose. Its survivors, declared dead, become [Ward Null](../cities/vantress/ward-null.md). |
| 83 years ago | The Night of Cut Harnesses in Keth Veyra: merchants try to steal Brinewing eggs, and a lower neighbourhood drowns. The city closes itself to outsiders. |
| Recent years | A long regional peace ruins [Orravell](../cities/orravell.md)'s war economy, and Cael Ordan rises to power promising renewal through expansion. |
