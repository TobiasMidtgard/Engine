# Glossary

```meta
summary: The game's words in one place: from ascension to subdue.
```

{{draft}}

Accent
: The trace an enchanter leaves in their work, as individual as handwriting. See [Enchanting](../magic/enchanting.md#the-accent).

Ascension
: The second transformation, from level 81, when you claim a Legend, Domain or law of your own. A skill also ascends when it goes past 100. See [Progression](../play/progression.md).

Ascent
: The gold coin, five-sided, worth ten marks. See [Money and trade](money-and-trade.md).

Awakening
: Breaking the human limit at level 60, through a major quest and something rare. See [Progression](../play/progression.md).

Blank work
: An enchanted item without a registered maker's mark; banned in [the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md).

Boughborn
: The registered descendants of the founders of [Orravell](../cities/orravell.md), the city's privileged class.

Branch
: One of the four mastery trees of a magic subclass; it decides how your spells grow.

Brass seal
: The numbered mooring seal on every building in [Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md). It decides who owns the building, what it pays and who guards it.

Chit
: The copper coin, square with a hole; ten make a mark.

Chorus
: The shared mind of [Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md), carried by vines, birdsong, pollinators and herds.

Clean magic
: Magic that defends: fast, efficient, and able to knock out instead of kill. See [Magic](../magic/index.md).

Complexity budget
: How much a spell you craft can hold, set by your mastery. See [Spellcrafting](../magic/spellcrafting.md).

Corruption
: The mark dark magic leaves on your body and your name, in five stages. See [Corruption](../magic/corruption.md).

Counting Deep
: The vaults beneath [the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md#the-counting-deep), the deepest older than the city.

Cruise
: Fast travel between planets and stars in a spaceship. See [Star travel](../vehicles/star-travel.md).

Dark magic
: Magic that harms: it lingers, drains and blocks healing, and costs corruption.

Eaves
: The rooftop city of vampires and werebeasts above the streets of [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md).

Ethereal
: The unseen medium that carries the Fundaments' currents; mana is the Ethereal a body holds. See [The Fundaments](fundaments.md#the-ethereal).

Five Seals
: The trading seals of [the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md#the-five-seals): Clay, Copper, Silver, Gold and Glass, one for each tier of the market spire.

Focus
: Seeing danger in slow motion; how slow depends on your reflexes. See [Movement](../play/movement.md).

Fundaments
: The seven deep currents beneath the world: Energy, Force, Life, Matter, Mind, Space and Time. See [The Fundaments](fundaments.md).

Glow
: A sun-charged light refined in Vantress's [Fossil Reach](../cities/vantress/fossil-reach.md). It is also a potent hallucinogen.

Godslayer
: Someone who has learned how to kill a god for good. It is earned, not a level. See [Progression](../play/progression.md#godslayer).

Hells
: The realm of demons, beneath the Ethereal. See [Demons](../creatures/demons.md).

Hushstone
: The dark stone of [Veyrhold](../cities/veyrhold.md)'s quarry, which swallows stray magic.

Legend
: What an Ascendant claims and the world recognises: Exchange, Trespass, Severance. See [Legends and Ascendants](ascendants.md).

Listener bloom
: A flowering tendril visitors wear in [Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md); it turns the Chorus into feelings, images and words.

Mark
: The silver coin, worth ten chits; a day's wages for a skilled worker.

Marrowfruit
: The dense red fruit of [Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md), which feeds even its carnivores.

Night Council
: The five seats that rule the Eaves of [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md#the-night-council).

Patron
: The demon a pactbinder makes a pact with. See [Pactbinding](../magic/pactbinding.md#patrons).

Patternwork
: [Tessara](../cities/tessara.md)'s ceramic tiles whose channels carry power, water, magic and information. Laid together, they form machines.

Red Writ
: A bought licence to kill one named person in the Eaves, verified by [the Last Writ](../factions/last-writ.md).

Restriking
: Every seventh year in the Gilded Ascent, when every Copper and Silver Seal is struck again.

Spill
: Magic that went somewhere it wasn't meant to, and changed what it found. See [the Spillward](../factions/spillward.md).

Stormglass
: Electrically charged glass harvested during thunderstorms above [Sky City](../cities/sky-city.md).

Subclass
: The clean or dark half of a magic class, levelled like any skill. See [Classes and subclasses](../magic/classes.md).

Subdue
: Bringing an enemy down without killing them; clean magic only.

Sunset Compact
: The peace between the two layers of [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md#the-sunset-compact).

Title
: A name the world gives you for your deeds, with a real power. See [Titles](../play/titles.md).

Unsealed
: Stripped of every seal in the Gilded Ascent and barred from trading; the city's harshest punishment.

Unsealed List
: The goods no seal may carry in [the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md); Arena City sells them.

Well
: A place where a Fundament rises unfiltered. See [The Fundaments](fundaments.md#wells).

White Seal
: A temporary seal issued for one job, recorded three times. See [the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md#the-five-seals).
