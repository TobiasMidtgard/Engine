Every city has its own feast days, but contracts, loans and caravans all run by one calendar: the
one the Registry of Seals keeps in [the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md). A debt falls due
on the same day in Vantress and the Second City, and that is reason enough for everyone to keep it.

## The week

Five working days are named for the five seals, from the lowest to the highest, followed by a
market day and a rest day.

| Day | Named for | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Clayday** | The Clay Seal | The week's first work. Porters are hired at dawn. |
| **Copperday** | The Copper Seal | Weighing day in the Weighhouse |
| **Silverday** | The Silver Seal | Bonded goods are counted |
| **Goldday** | The Gold Seal | Loans and contracts are signed |
| **Glassday** | The Glass Seal | The Court of Seals sits |
| **Market day** | | Shelves and purses refill; every town holds a market |
| **Rest day** | | Shops shut, temples open. Thieves work. |

## The months

| Season | Months | The land |
|---|---|---|
| **Spring** | Thaw, Sowing, Greening | Snow melts on the Bell Road; Rillvault plants its surface terraces; Orravell's campaign season opens |
| **Summer** | Highgrass, Longday, Haying | Caravans everywhere; sandstorms on the Glass Road; monsoon rains in the west |
| **Autumn** | Reaping, Russet, Fogfall | Harvests, fairs, and the year's great trade runs |
| **Winter** | Frostmark, Deepnight, Coldwake | The north closes; Vantress's floods; the Second City's longest nights |

After Coldwake come the **Five Quiet Days**, one for each seal, which belong to no month. Debts
are not collected in them, contracts are not signed, and in the Gilded Ascent nobody may be
unsealed. They are the only days in the year when the Lowmarket outside the walls trades freely
inside them.

Years are counted from the founding of the Gilded Ascent, about 420 years ago. Most people just say
"the year of" something: the year of the long frost, the year the caravan didn't come.

## Time of day

Bells keep time in every city: **first bell** at sunrise, **noon bell**, **dusk bell** at sunset and
**last bell** at midnight. Shops open at first bell and shut at dusk; guards change at first and
last bell; in the Second City the dusk bell is when the Eaves wake and the Boulevard goes home.
