Money on the continent is struck in one place: the **Mint of Seals** in
[the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md). Its three coins are taken in every city, by every
guild and on every road, which is one more reason the world's roads all lead to the same spire.

## Coins

| Coin | Metal | Worth | What it buys |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chit** | Copper, square, with a hole | The smallest coin | A loaf, a cup of ale, a ferry across a canal |
| **Mark** | Silver, round, stamped with a seal | 10 chits | A night at a decent inn, a day's wages for a skilled worker |
| **Ascent** | Gold, five-sided, one side for each tier of the spire | 10 marks | A month's rent in a good street, a young horse's first saddle |

Every coin carries the mark of the tier that struck it, and the Assayers of the Weighhouse check
them by weight. Clipped and shaved coins are weighed, not counted.

Other money exists, too. [Vantress](../cities/vantress/index.md) runs on **bearer notes** from
the Gilded Ledger and **silver trade weights**; [Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md) does not use money at
all and trades by meeting visible needs; in [the Second City](../cities/second-city.md)'s Eaves the
real currency is blood.

## Banned goods

The Gilded Ascent's **Unsealed List** names the goods that may never carry a seal: dark magic
reagents and soul gems, glow, unregistered enchantments ("blank work"), unlicensed firearms,
Brinewing eggs, marrowfruit cuttings, human beings, and debts sold with a person attached. Banned
in the Gilded Ascent does not mean unavailable: [Arena City](../cities/arena-city.md) was founded
to sell exactly these things, and most of them are legal somewhere.

## Selling stolen goods

Stolen goods sell for less than they are worth, and the more recognisable they are, the less you
get. A **fence** buys them and takes on the risk. The Tangle's fences in Vantress use a simple
reckoning:

> Fence value = sale price − laundering cost − heat − damage − partners' shares

A silver trade weight sells almost at its full value; a famous jewel may not sell at all. See
[Items and equipment](../items/index.md#value-and-ownership).
