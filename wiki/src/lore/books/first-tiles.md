***First Tiles*** is the book every child in [Tessara](../../cities/tessara.md) learns to read machines
from. [The Pattern Office](../../factions/pattern-office.md) gives it away, and revises it every year
after the Open Measure, when the citizens vote on Ison's charter. It explains the seven kinds of tile
(power, motion, heat, signal, sense, memory and gate), how a chain of them makes a program, and how
to read any machine you meet (see [Tinkering](../../crafting/tinkering.md#patternwork)).

## From the book

> A tile does one thing. A chain of tiles does what the tiles do, in order. If a machine does
> something you didn't expect, it is not magic, and it is not the machine's fault. Find the tile.

> Every machine with power over a person must show its pattern. If a door will not tell you why it
> is shut, it is breaking the law, and so is whoever laid it.

## The last page

The last page of every edition is blank, for the reader to lay their first chain on: four tiles,
drawn in, that make a lamp brighten when you come near. This year's edition has a different last
page in some copies: a chain of nine black-glazed tiles that nobody at the Pattern Office can read,
and an advertisement for one of the Closed Kilns (see [The Black Pattern](../../quests/the-black-pattern.md)).
