***The Charter of the Road*** is the agreement the five caravan houses swore at the Middle Well,
the day the Crownless King bought it for them. It is the founding law of [the Chartered Road](../../factions/chartered-road.md),
and in a sense of the whole continent's trade: how a caravan is insured, how goods are weighed, how
a debt passes to an heir, and what happens to a road that robs its travellers. The original is kept
in the Charter House on the Ledger Floor. It has been amended 316 times, and every amendment is in a
different hand.

The oldest copy anyone knows of is painted on the keep wall of [the Old Toll Fort](../../world/dungeons/the-old-toll-fort.md),
from before the first amendment. It is shorter, and it says some things the Charter House copy
doesn't.

## From the charter

> **The first article.** No house shall own a road. A road is owned by those who walk it, while they
> walk it, and by nobody when it is empty.

> **The ninth article.** A caravan robbed on a chartered road shall be made whole by the Road, and
> the Road shall find who robbed it, and the Road shall not rest.

> **The fortieth article, as amended.** A traveller without a seal may walk a chartered road under
> the protection of the Road, provided a Charter Captain vouches for them.

## What the old wall says

The painted copy at the Old Toll Fort has the first article differently: *No house shall own a
road, nor a well, nor a name.* The words "nor a name" were struck out of the Charter House copy in
the second amendment, about four hundred years ago. The amendment is initialled by the first
Registrar of Seals. [Ivo Tarrant](../../people/ivo-tarrant.md) would very much like to see a rubbing.
