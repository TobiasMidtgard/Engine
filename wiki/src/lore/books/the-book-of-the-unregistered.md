> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for Teodor Voss's personal quest below.

***The Book of the Unregistered*** is the book [Teodor Voss](../../people/companions/teodor-voss.md)
writes if you help him **lay to rest** the 212 people shelved in
[the Catacombs of the Pale Archive](../../world/dungeons/pale-archive-catacombs.md). Every one of them
was wearing a seal of the Gilded Ascent; none of them had been in the Registry's books, because the
pages with their names had been cut out. Teodor finds the missing pages, and writes down every name,
and everything he can learn about each person: where they lived, what they did, who missed them.

## From the book

> Number forty-one. Brannoc Tull, a lamplighter of the Eaves. Forty years old. He lit the lamps on
> the Dusk Stair for nineteen years and was never late. His sister says he was afraid of the dark,
> and lit the lamps so that nobody else would have to be.

> They wanted them to be numbers. I have given them names, and I have given them each a page, and
> the pages are not for sale.

## Where it goes

Teodor gives the first copy to the Pale Archive, and sends a copy to every temple in
[Orath](../../cities/orath.md) with a letter asking the priests to read one name a day. Some of them
do. If you carry a copy into the Pale Archive's catacombs, the dead there let you pass.
