People write things down. Every city has booksellers (the best in the Gilded Ascent, Tessara and
the Second City), every guild has its manuals, and every ruin has someone's last journal in it.
Books are found on shelves, in desks, on bodies and in the libraries of dungeons, and they are
worth reading.

## What books do

- **Manuals teach.** The first time you read a manual, the skill it covers rises by two points.
  Some also teach a recipe, a pattern or a spell effect.
- **Herbals and bestiaries reveal.** Reading one shows you the effects of the ingredients, or the
  weaknesses of the creatures, it describes (see [Alchemy](../../crafting/alchemy.md#discovering-effects)).
- **Histories explain.** They tell you what happened in a place before you came, and often where
  something was left.
- **Journals and letters are evidence.** Many of them open a quest, change one, or give you a new
  thing to say to someone.
- **Some books are written because of you.** A companion who finishes their story may write it down,
  and the book appears in shops across the continent.

Books you have read are kept in your journal, so you can read them again without carrying them.
