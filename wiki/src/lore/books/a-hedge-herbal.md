***A Hedge Herbal*** is the plant book of the continent. **Marit Fell** walked the hedgerows of the
Tollands and the Valley for forty years as a gatherer of [the Root Ledger](../../factions/root-ledger.md),
and wrote down what every plant on them did to her. The Root Ledger has printed it ever since, and
every apothecary keeps a copy under the counter, foxed and stained and annotated by three
generations of apprentices.

Reading it reveals the second effect of woundwort, healing moss, sneezewort, thyme, saltreed,
snowbell, dusk poppy and wolfsbane (see [Alchemy](../../crafting/alchemy.md#ingredients)).

## From the book

> **Woundwort.** Grows where people walk, as if it knew they would need it. Bruise a leaf and lay it
> on a cut and the cut closes cleaner. Brew it and it does the same from the inside. Too much and
> you will be sick in the hedge you picked it from, which I have been, so you need not be.

> **Dusk poppy.** Pick it at dusk, as the name says, when the petals close. Picked in the full sun it
> is half the plant it should be, and I have watched a whole chapter's harvest wasted by a sower who
> liked to sleep in the evenings. A spoon of the syrup sends a grown man to sleep. Two spoons send a
> child somewhere you cannot fetch her back from. Write the dose on the jar.

> **Wolfsbane.** Pretty, and it knows it. Wear gloves. The hill people say a werewolf cannot cross a
> line of it, which is not true, but it burns them where it touches, which is better.

## Notes in the margins

Every copy is different, because every owner writes in it. The copy in the Lowmarket's unlicensed
apothecary in the Gilded Ascent has a recipe for a mending draught that works without a bench,
in a hand nobody recognises. The copy in Ottilie Brand's still-room in Vantress has every dose in it
crossed out and doubled.
