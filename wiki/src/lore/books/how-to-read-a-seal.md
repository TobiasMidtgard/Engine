***How to Read a Seal*** is the primer the Weighhouse gives every trader who applies for a Copper
Seal, and sells to everybody else. It is dry, exact and much more interesting than it looks, because
it is the only book that explains in plain words how the seals of [the Gilded Ascent](../../cities/gilded-ascent.md)
work. A new edition comes out after every Restriking; this year's is overdue.

## What it teaches

| Seal | Struck in | What the primer says it lets you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Clay** | Fired clay, one chit a season | Sell from a stall; sleep in the Ring; be counted |
| **Copper** | Copper, after a year's clean trading | Trade by registered weight; hire porters; borrow |
| **Silver** | Silver, for wholesalers and consuls | Store goods bonded; import in bulk; sit in a trade hall |
| **Gold** | Gold, for bankers, insurers and notaries | Lend, insure, witness; enter the Registry |
| **Glass** | Basin glass, about sixty holders | Set the laws |

## From the book

> A true seal is heavier than it looks, warm a moment longer than it should be, and numbered on the
> rim in the Registry's own hand. A forged seal is usually too perfect. The Registry has never made a
> perfect seal in four hundred years, and does not intend to start.

> Never tell a Seal Warden that you have lost your seal. Tell them it is being restruck. A lost seal
> is a missing person; a seal being restruck is paperwork.

## Between the lines

The edition after the coming Restriking has already been printed, and is waiting in a locked room
of the Weighhouse. Its first page says something new about who must carry a seal, and where. A clerk
in the Weighhouse will sell you a look at it for 5 marks, and is very frightened about having
offered (see [The Restriking Is Called](../../quests/main/the-restriking-is-called.md)).
