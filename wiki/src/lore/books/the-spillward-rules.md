***The Spillward Rules*** is the handbook of [the Spillward](../../factions/spillward.md). Every
warden carries one, and the good ones never need to open it. It explains what a **spill** is (magic
that didn't go where it was meant to), how to grade one, how to hold it, and when to run. This
edition was revised by Inspector [Halvard Cray](../../people/halvard-cray.md), whose preface is three pages
long and mostly about licences.

## The grades of spill

| Grade | What you see | What the rules say |
|---|---|---|
| **One** | Milk sours, doors stick, a candle burns blue | Note it. Find whoever cast nearby, and teach them. |
| **Two** | Furniture moves, rain falls indoors, voices from the wrong room | Clear the building. Ward the doors. Send for a warden. |
| **Three** | The street changes: glass flows, stone breathes, people forget their names | Clear the street. Hold it with hushstone. Send for three wardens and the Spillmaster. |
| **Four** | The district is somewhere else | Do not enter. Nobody who has entered a grade four has been the same. |

## From the book

> **The first rule.** In the first minute of a spill, do nothing. Most spills end in a minute. Most
> wardens who die, die in the first minute, doing something.

> **The ninth rule.** A spill is not a crime. Causing one is. Causing one with unlicensed magic is a
> worse one. (Preface, Cray: the ninth rule shall be enforced.)

[Tamsin Reeve](../../people/companions/tamsin-reeve.md)'s copy has the ninth rule scratched out and
"teach them better" written in.
