***The Long Strike*** is the story of the year the hammers stopped, told by **Oda Strom**, a striker
at Orravell's forges who was there. About a hundred and twenty years ago the smiths of a dozen cities
laid down their hammers on the same morning, and the armies of three kingdoms found, by autumn, that
they had no new swords. Two of those kingdoms are gone now. [The Makers' Compact](../../factions/makers-compact.md)
signed the peace, and the day is still its holiday (see [Peace Day](../calendar.md#festivals)).

Oda Strom was the great-grandmother of [Hedda Strom](../../people/hedda-strom.md), steward of the
smiths' lodge, who keeps the first copy in her forge in Veyrhold and reads from it every Peace Day.

## From the book

> They told us the kingdoms would starve us out. They forgot who makes ploughs. By the second month
> the farmers were feeding us, because nobody else would mend their tools, and by the fourth the
> generals were coming to our yards to ask, very politely, what we wanted.

> We wanted to own our forges. We wanted to set our own prices, and to teach whom we liked. We wanted
> nobody to be made to strike a blade for a war they had not chosen. We got the first two.

## Why it matters now

The Compact's smiths in Orravell are being asked to work for the Bough Cohorts' next campaign, and
some of them have started carrying *The Long Strike* to work.
