***Free Hearths*** is the book [Tamsin Reeve](../../people/companions/tamsin-reeve.md) writes if her
classes in [Veyrhold](../../cities/veyrhold.md) stay free at the end of her quest. It is every cantrip
she knows, written plainly, with the mistakes beginners make and how she cleaned up after hers. Its
first page says anyone may copy it and nobody may sell it, and within a season it is being copied by
hand in every city on the continent.

## What it teaches

Reading it teaches six Novice spell effects: a frost bolt that slows, a spark that lights oil, a
kitchen ward, a light that follows you, a breeze to clear smoke and a warmth to dry wet clothes (see
[Evocation](../../magic/evocation.md) and [Vitalism](../../magic/vitalism.md)).

## From the book

> Nobody owns this. If somebody sold it to you, go back and ask for your money, and then come to the
> Hearths and I'll teach you the rest for nothing.

> Every spill I have ever cleaned up was somebody's first spell. That's not a reason to stop people
> casting. It's a reason to teach them properly.

> The ward on page nine is the one Pim Arden cast in the Binding Yards. He cast it right. Read the
> chapter on the cheap stone before you cast it near a workbench.

## Who minds

[Halvard Cray](../../people/halvard-cray.md) has one on his desk. The Registry of Seals has put it on
the Unsealed List, which has done wonders for its circulation.
