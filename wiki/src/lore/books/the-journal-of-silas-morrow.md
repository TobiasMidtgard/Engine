***The Journal of Silas Morrow*** lies open on the bed in the lantern room of
[the Weeping Tower](../../world/dungeons/the-weeping-tower.md), beside the great lens. **Silas Morrow**
was a necromancer of the Pale Archive whose wife drowned off Fogwick thirty years ago, and who built
the tower on the Weeper to bring her back. He has written in the journal every night since. The first
pages are in a neat archivist's hand. The last are hard to read.

## From the journal

> The first night. The boat came in without her. They say the sea off Fogwick keeps what it takes.
> I am an archivist of the dead. I know where things are kept.

> The third year. She came up the causeway with the tide. She knew my name. She did not know her own.
> I will mend it. Everything that is broken can be mended if you are patient enough, and I am very
> patient.

> The eleventh year. I have stopped eating. It saves time. The lens keeps her here as long as it is
> lit, and I do not sleep any more, so it is always lit.

> Last week. Someone has seen the light from the coast. Good. Let them come. Let them tell me what I
> should have done instead.

## What it tells you

The journal makes clear that the lens holds his wife in the tower, and that it holds Morrow too.
Break it and both of them can rest (see [Silas Morrow](../../world/dungeons/the-weeping-tower.md#silas-morrow)).
Read it before the fight and you can say her name to him, which stops him for a moment, and offer
mercy, which he may take. [Teodor Voss](../../people/companions/teodor-voss.md) will want to read it
afterwards, and is quiet for a day.
