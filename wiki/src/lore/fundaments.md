The **Fundaments** are the seven deep currents that run beneath the world: **Energy**, **Force**,
**Life**, **Matter**, **Mind**, **Space** and **Time**. Everything that burns, moves, grows, holds
its shape, thinks, stands apart or changes does so because a Fundament runs through it. Magic is
the art of pulling on them.

## The seven currents

| Fundament | What it carries | Turned one way | Turned the other |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Energy** | Heat, light and lightning | Heat and fire | Cold and dark |
| **Force** | Motion and weight | Push | Pull |
| **Life** | Growth and healing | Grow | Wither |
| **Matter** | Substance and shape | Form | Unform |
| **Mind** | Thought, sense and will | Reveal and bind | Veil and break |
| **Space** | Distance and place | Bring near | Send far |
| **Time** | Change and speed | Hasten | Slow |

No spell uses only one current. A fireball is Energy pushed by Force; a heal is Life drawn into a
wound; a blink is Space folded with a little Mind to know where you land. The four classes of
[magic](../magic/index.md) are four ways of learning the same seven currents.

## The Ethereal

The currents do not touch the world directly. They move through **the Ethereal**, an unseen medium
that soaks everything like water in soil. A caster draws on the Ethereal around them, holds some of
it in their own body, and shapes it into a spell. What a body can hold is called **mana**. Most
people hold barely enough to light a candle; trained mages hold enough to light a street.

The engineers of [Tessara](../cities/tessara.md) send **Ethereal signals** through it, carrying
messages along their Patternwork faster than any rider. Spells that are cast too hard, or that
fail, leave the Ethereal disturbed, and the disturbance has to go somewhere: that is what the
[Spillward](../factions/spillward.md) calls a **spill**.

## Wells

In a few places a Fundament rises into the world unfiltered, the way a spring rises from the rock.
These are **wells**, or raw Fundaments, and they change everything near them.

| Well | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **The First Spring** | [Orath](../cities/orath.md) | Its water heals, and the Spillward suspects raw Life beneath it. The Accord of Four Keys does not like the suggestion. |
| **The Deepworks well** | Inside [Sky City](../cities/sky-city.md)'s rock | A well of raw Force, pushing upwards. It is why the island floats, and why its chains are always taut. |
| **The storm crowns** | The air above Sky City | Lightning strikes the island's crown again and again, and the sand of its terraces fuses into stormglass. |
| **The Sunken Works** | East of [Veyrhold](../cities/veyrhold.md) | Not a natural well: a fragment of raw Mind made by people, and sealed (see [What the Crystal Remembers](../quests/what-the-crystal-remembers.md)). |

Surviving raw exposure to a well is one of the known roads to
[Awakening](../play/ascension.md#awakening). Most people who try it do not survive.

## Materials that remember

Some materials hold onto a current long after it has passed through them:

- **[Hushstone](../items/materials.md#hushstone)**, the dark stone of Veyrhold, may once have lain
  over a well. It drank the current so long that now it drinks any stray magic that touches it.
- **[Stormglass](../items/materials.md#stormglass)** holds the Energy of the lightning that made it,
  and gives it back as a charge.
- **[Basin glass](../items/materials.md#basin-glass)** from the Sifting Basin, concentrated by
  latticebacks, holds any current well, which is why it carries the strongest enchantments.
- **Glow**, refined in Vantress, holds sunlight, and gives it back as light and as visions.

## Corruption and the dark side

Dark magic pulls on the same currents, but it pulls them **the wrong way through a living body**:
it takes life to make force, drains one body to fill another, and uses suffering as fuel. The
currents remember, and so does the caster's body. That is [corruption](../magic/corruption.md).
