**Gods** are real. They are not everywhere and they do not see everything: each is a vast,
ancient being bound to one idea, one kind of place or one kind of act, and most of them pay no
attention to people at all. They cannot be killed by strength. Each god lives by a **law**, a
condition that keeps it immortal for as long as the condition holds, and the people who learn how
to break one are called **Godslayers**.

## What a god is

Scholars of the Spillward, priests of the Maker and the reciters of the Deep Choir agree on very
little, but they agree on this much:

- **A god is bound to an idea.** The Maker is bound to things that work, the God of Locked Doors
  to thresholds, the Waiting Wound to suffering that never heals.
- **A god grows with its idea.** The more locks there are in the world, the stronger the God of
  Locked Doors; the more wounds go unhealed, the hungrier the Wound.
- **A god's law protects it.** Every god has one condition that keeps it immortal. Some laws are
  known; most are secrets that the god's own priests do not know.
- **Gods rarely act directly.** They work through priests, whisperers, omens and the people who
  serve their idea without knowing it. When a god does act, people die.

Gods can die. The Godslayer path in [Progression](../play/progression.md#godslayer) is the long work
of learning how.

## The known gods

### The Maker

The god of craft and of things that work. Its priests are **Ferris's Tempered Hand** in
[Tessara](../cities/tessara.md), named for Ferris, a smith who put their hand into a forge at the
Maker's command and drew it out as tempered steel. They teach that building something without
teaching others to repair it is sacrilege. Smiths, engineers and the Makers' Compact's lodges keep
the Maker's mark over their workshop doors. The Maker is the most loved god on the continent, and
the least demanding.

### The God of Locked Doors

The god of thresholds, secrets and keeping. Nobody builds it temples any more, but every master
locksmith keeps a tiny shrine to it somewhere in their workshop, and vault-keepers, gaolers and
the keepers of secrets pray to it quietly. Its old temple is lost. Its law, known only to those who
have looked for it, is that it **cannot be harmed while any door in its temple stays closed**.
See [The Temple of Locked Doors](../quests/the-temple-of-locked-doors.md).

### The Waiting Wound

A dark god of suffering that never heals. It has no temples; its whisperers carry open wounds
they keep from healing for years. Every dark spell that makes a wound that cannot close feeds it,
and when a caster's [corruption](../magic/corruption.md) reaches its last stage, the Wound begins
to call. See [The Wound That Waits](../quests/the-wound-that-waits.md).

### The Antlered Hunt

The god of pursuit: of every chase, every hunt and every fight that ends with one side running.
Hunters leave the first blood of a kill for it, pit fighters touch the ground before a bout, and
[Sabeth Rook](../people/sabeth-rook.md) of the Last Writ keeps its shrine. Those who have
seen it describe a stag taller than a tree, whose antlers are made of the bones of things it has
caught. People say you must never run from it.

## The four revelations of Orath

[Orath](../cities/orath.md) holds four faiths around one sacred spring, and none of them names its
god. Some believers say the four revelations are four faces of one god; some that they are four
gods; some that they are no god at all, only four ways of living well.

| Faith | Believes | Emblem |
|---|---|---|
| **The Solitary Light** | One divine presence lights every person | A sunrise through an arch |
| **The Deep Choir** | The dead remain part of the community | An open mouth singing |
| **The Returning Road** | The divine once came to Orath as a stranger nobody recognised | An open door |
| **The Covenant of Ash** | Surviving catastrophe creates a duty to others | A bowl over a fire |

## Dead gods

> [!WARNING]
> Spoilers for the world's mysteries below.

Gods have died before. The black foundations beneath the Hollow Crown in Orath and beneath the
Counting Deep in [the Gilded Ascent](../cities/gilded-ascent.md) were laid by builders nobody can
name, and some scholars believe the Hollow Crown was the temple of a god whose death was erased
from history, so thoroughly that even its name is gone. If they are right, someone once learned how
to kill a god, and then made sure nobody would remember how.

## What is not a god

- **The Chorus of [Oruvai](../cities/oruvai.md)** is a shared mind of animals and plants, not a god,
  whatever pilgrims say. The Chorus finds the question funny.
- **[Ison](../people/ison.md)**, the machine mind that governs [Tessara](../cities/tessara.md), is not a god either,
  although some of the Maker's priests are not so sure.
- **Ascendants** are people who have gone beyond the limits of the world. Some have been mistaken
  for gods, and some have not corrected the mistake (see [Legends and Ascendants](ascendants.md)).
- **Demons** are not gods. They live in the Hells and bargain with people who call them (see
  [Pactbinding](../magic/pactbinding.md)).
